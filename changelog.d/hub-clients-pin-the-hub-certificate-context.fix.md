`vibey hub pair`, `devices` and `revoke`, and `vibey mcp` / `vibey acp`, now reach a hub that serves TLS with a TLS context whose only authority is the hub's own certificate (`<state_dir>/hub-cert.pem`), instead of passing the file's path to httpx, which deprecates it. They still trust that certificate and nothing else, and now also refuse it unless it is a regular file of yours alone (never a symbolic link, never readable by other accounts); `vibey mcp` and `vibey acp` say "the hub's certificate could not be read" and send nothing, where before an unreadable certificate was a traceback.
