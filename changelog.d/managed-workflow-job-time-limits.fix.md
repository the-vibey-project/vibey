**Every job in the managed workflows now declares a time limit.** A job with no `timeout-minutes` runs under GitHub's 360-minute default, so one hung job holds a runner for six hours, and a repository that adopts the templates under `vibey_gh/templates/workflows/` inherited that for every job. 29 jobs in 14 managed workflows had none; each now carries `timeout-minutes`, set at the template and re-rendered into both copies, the repository root's and the one in `src/vibey_tools/gh`. 22 of the limits come from measured run times, at twice the job's p90 and never within half again of its slowest run: the 300 newest runs for `pr-review`, `pr-evaluate`, `release-surfaces` and `github-release`, and the 20 newest for the other workflows. The other 7 jobs have no run history and are labelled `unmeasured`: four that only run git and `gh` calls get 30 minutes, and the three model-action jobs (`review`, `repair` and `resolve-conflict`) get 120, the default of `pr_af.timeout_minutes`. The placeholder-driven `timeout-minutes` on `review-pr-af`, `review-pr-af-host` and `repair-sovereign` is unchanged, and the job-limits census in `tests/meta/workflow_job_limits.toml` now has a ceiling of 0, so a new job without a limit fails the build.
