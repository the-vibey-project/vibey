The loop capability bundle has a schema: a `[loops]` table in your own `vibey.toml` can name marketplaces, plugins (`name@marketplace`), MCP servers (`local` stdio with a `command`, or `remote` over `https://`) and skills, and `disable` switches any of them off. Every key is checked and a mistake is refused naming its key path (for example `loops.mcp_servers[0].url`). With no table you get the research-methodology plugins from the marketplace that ships with vibey. A project's `vibey.toml` may only `disable`; any other `[loops]` key it carries is ignored and named in a notice. Nothing applies the bundle to a loop yet.
