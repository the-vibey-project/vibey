`vibey serve` now binds and listens on its port before it writes its runtime record (`serving.json`), hands uvicorn duplicates of those sockets (uvicorn no longer binds its own), and lets the port go only after clearing the record, also while it waits for open connections at stop. A record therefore never names a port another process could hold: before, another local account that bound the hub's port could receive a token sent between the record and uvicorn's failed bind, or while a stopping hub waited for its connections. A host name still binds every address it resolves to (`localhost`: 127.0.0.1 and ::1), on one port. A hub that cannot bind its port now writes no record, opens nothing and exits 1 with "could not listen on <host>:<port>: <reason>", and a hub asked for port 0 records the port the system picked. `vibey hub start`, when it finds a hub starting that has not yet written its pid, now says at once that the hub exited if that hub dies, instead of waiting out its timeout and saying it was still starting.
