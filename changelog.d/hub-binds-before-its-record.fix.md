`vibey serve` now binds and listens on its port before it writes its runtime record (`serving.json`), then hands that socket to uvicorn, which no longer binds one of its own. A record therefore never names a port another process holds: before, another local account that had bound the hub's port could receive a token sent in the milliseconds between the record and uvicorn's failed bind. A hub that cannot bind its port now writes no record, opens nothing and exits 1 with "could not listen on <host>:<port>: <reason>", and a hub asked for port 0 records the port the system picked. `vibey hub start`, when it finds a hub starting that has not yet written its pid, now says at once that the hub exited if that hub dies, instead of waiting out its timeout and saying it was still starting.
