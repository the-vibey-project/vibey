**The krypton app can share a project** (#1698, part of #1665; the Share slice). Each project card on Home has a **Share** button that opens the system share sheet with one sentence, for example "Greeter is in BUILD, cycle 2 of 5." On a browser with no share sheet it copies that text and says so. What may leave the device is decided in one place, `ShareComposer`, which reads the project's name, phase and cycle by name and nothing else: a test hands it an object carrying an id, a token, a path and a cost and proves none of them reaches the message, and another proves the share sheet is called with only that text. **Not done:** a project's final summary is not shared (the app does not hold one), what else may be shared is the operator's call (the issue's open question 2, answered here by the plan's default), and the notification actions, App Shortcut and widget of #1698 are separate slices.
