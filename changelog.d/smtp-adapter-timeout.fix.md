**The SMTP email adapter bounds every connection** (#730). `ForwardEmailAdapter` opened `smtplib.SMTP` and `SMTP_SSL` with no timeout, so a relay that accepted the connection and never answered blocked the sending thread for ever. It now takes a keyword-only `timeout` (default `DEFAULT_SURFACE_ADAPTER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`) and passes it to both; a connection that exceeds it raises `TimeoutError`.
