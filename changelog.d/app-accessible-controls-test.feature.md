**The krypton app now has a test that fails when a control has no accessible name** (#1698, part of #1665; the first of that issue's slices). `test/ui/accessibility.test.tsx` renders every screen with data that makes it show its controls and reads the tree for what a screen reader would meet: every pressable, text input and switch must have a name and every pressable a role, and a screen that shows fewer controls than it should fails too. The checker is itself tested on deliberately broken trees, and removing a switch's label makes the suite fail. Two real gaps are fixed: a loading spinner now says "Loading", and the tab bar's glyphs are hidden from the screen reader so each tab is read once, by its title. The screens already named their controls; this makes it stay true. **Not done:** nobody has walked the app with VoiceOver or TalkBack (rows marked "not run" in the new `clients/app/ACCESSIBILITY.md`), and the notification actions, Share, App Shortcut and widget of #1698 are separate slices.
