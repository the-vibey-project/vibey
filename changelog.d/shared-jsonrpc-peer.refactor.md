The MCP server now shares a stdlib JSON-RPC codec with a new bidirectional asyncio peer (`vibey.infrastructure.jsonrpc`), the base for the Agent Client Protocol agent. `vibey mcp` replies are byte-identical, pinned by a golden transcript. The peer answers every request, including ones with a non-finite id or an unserialisable result, drains in-flight handlers for a configurable grace at end of input, and refuses oversized lines without losing the next one.
