`EmailPort.send_email` takes an optional `idempotency_key` naming one logical message. `ForwardEmailAdapter` derives a stable `Message-ID` from it (`<vibey.<32 hex>@<from domain>>`) as a courtesy to receivers; SMTP cannot deduplicate, so the surface lane guards the send. `InMemoryEmail` records each key in `sent_keys`. `ForwardEmailAdapter` also refuses a non-positive `timeout` with `ValueError("timeout must be positive")`.
