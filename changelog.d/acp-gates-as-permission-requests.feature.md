`vibey acp`: open gates are now put to the editor as permission requests. After each prompt turn, the open gates the hub lists that the session has not seen yet (oldest first, at most `[acp] max_gate_prompts_per_turn`, each once per session, and only in `drive` mode) appear as a tool call with one `session/request_permission`: an option per choice (labels in `[acp] reject_words` offered as refusals), "Accept the defaults" for a design question, "Acknowledge" for a gate that takes any answer, and "Leave it open". The pick answers the gate through the hub as the scoped agent principal. A cancelled, failed, timed-out or unknown answer, or "Leave it open", answers nothing. Spend gates (`budget_exhausted`, `deploy_*`, or any the hub flags as spending) are never offered: you are told to answer them on the host or in the desktop or mobile app. `session/cancel` resolves a permission request still out at once. `/answer 1` and a bare number now mean in an ACP session what they mean at the hub, and a bare number is shown as a gate answer until the hub says what it ran.
