**A krypton notification about a question can now be answered from the notification** (#1698, part of #1665; the notification-actions slice). A review (`approval`, `deploy_demo_review`) or a choice with up to three options arrives with those options as buttons, and the design interview's question arrives with "Accept the suggested answers"; pressing one sends the same answer the Gates screen would, once, and a notification says it was answered. On iOS a button works only once the phone is unlocked and does not open the app. **A gate that spends never gets a button**: `NotificationPlanner.actionsFor` returns none for `budget_exhausted` and every `deploy_*` kind, and `answerFor` checks the gate's kind again when a button is pressed, so even a forged or stale notification cannot answer one; its notification only opens the app, where the answer is re-verified on the device as before (ADR-0068). If the hub is away or refuses, a notification says "Your answer was not sent" and opens Gates on a tap, so nothing fails silently. **Not done:** this works while the app is open or recently in the background, as notifications always have; a closed app still hears nothing (that needs the push relay of #1585), and on Android the unlock requirement is not enforced by the platform. The App Shortcut and the widget of #1698 are separate slices.
