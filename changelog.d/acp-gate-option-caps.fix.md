`vibey acp` no longer offers the editor a choice or verdict gate with an unbounded number of options or an unbounded label: a gate that lists more options than the new `[acp] gate_options_max` (default 20, 1 to 1000) or any label longer than `gate_label_chars_max` (default 200 characters, 1 to 10000) is not offered and is said, like a gate whose labels could be misread, with where to answer it on the host. Both keys are yours; a project's `vibey.toml` may only lower them.
