**The real-binary conformance test no longer fails intermittently inside the pre-push full-suite run** (`tests/live/test_scripted_binary_conformance.py`). Its `--version` probe had a fixed 10 s budget, which a busy machine can exceed, and a probe that gives up reports no version, which conformance reads as "below the minimum". `LoopProcessAdapter` now takes `version_timeout` (default unchanged at 10 s, like `doctor_timeout`), and that test widens it and the verdict poll to 60 s without loosening any assertion.
