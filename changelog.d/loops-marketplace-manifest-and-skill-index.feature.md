Marketplaces and their skills can be read without leaving the marketplace. A marketplace's `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` is read with a 2 MiB cap and checked field by field; a plugin whose source is absolute, climbs out with `..`, or resolves through a symlink to a folder outside the marketplace makes the whole marketplace refused, and none of its files are read. Skills are indexed from each plugin's `skills/*/SKILL.md` frontmatter (name and description only): a file over 64 KiB, one that is not UTF-8, a symlink leading out of its plugin or a malformed frontmatter is skipped with its reason named, never a crash. Nothing reads a marketplace during a run yet.
