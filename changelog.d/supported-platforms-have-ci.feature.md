**A declared Linux distro now has to be proven by CI, or say it is not** (`scripts/minimum_specs.toml`, `tests/meta/test_supported_platforms_have_ci.py`; first slice of #1116). Each `[minimum_specs.linux.distros.*]` entry names the `ci.yml` `desktop` cell that proves it, or carries an explicit `pending` marker, and the new meta test holds that to the workflow: a missing cell fails, a Linux cell no distro names fails, and a release-numbered distro must say whether its cell is pinned, a claim the test checks against the runner and container. Today that is honest rather than flattering: `ubuntu-24.04` runs on the floating `ubuntu-latest`, so it is declared `pinned = false`, and `ubuntu-26.04` is `pending` until its pinned CI rows exist.
