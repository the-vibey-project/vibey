* **docs:** the ledger explorer (`/explorer/`), an Etherscan-style view into a published ledger: search, plain-language
  summaries, a bar showing what was published and what was withheld, and a record page whose digest your browser
  recomputes. Static, reads what `vibey ledger site` writes, linked from the home page, the README, three guides
  and the navigation (kept on the published sites by `channel.js`). Authored in TypeScript.
