# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free regardless of identity,
experience, disability, appearance, nationality, ethnicity, age, religion, or technology
choice.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, protect private
information, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence. Harassment, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to `adam@matthewsteinberger.com`. Reports will be reviewed
promptly, confidentially, and without retaliation. Maintainers may edit, reject, mute,
ban, or otherwise moderate contributions proportionately. This policy applies in project
spaces and when representing the project publicly.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
