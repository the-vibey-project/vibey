`[version] max_major` caps the major the version deriver may reach: a breaking-change marker that would carry the version past it derives a minor instead, and the reason names the cap. While it is set, nothing is derived from or raised to a version above it: a working version already above the cap is refused with a reason that names both, a staged version above it is refused rather than kept, and a staged version at or under it beside a released one above it (`4.6.0` released, `0.3.0` staged, cap `0`) is kept as a renumber. `vibey-gh promote` refuses to promote, and the merge train holds a promotion, whose version is above the cap. `max_major = 0` keeps a project pre-1.0 until the key is deleted; unset, a break is a major bump as before.
