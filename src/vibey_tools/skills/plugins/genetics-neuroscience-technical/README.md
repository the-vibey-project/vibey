# Genetics and Neuroscience Plugin

The technical layer of genetics and neuroscience treated as one connected reference: molecular genetics and the central dogma stated accurately, gene regulation and epigenetics, mutation and inheritance, population and quantitative genetics including the most misused number in biology, genome engineering and the CRISPR toolbox, delivery and screens, neuron biophysics and synapses, plasticity, neural coding, circuit architectures, neuromodulation, development and glia, and the neural engineering and stimulation methods.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **neurogen-molecular-genetics-and-regulation** — Molecular Genetics, Gene Regulation, and Inheritance (§0–§3): Routing; Molecular Genetics; Gene Regulation and Epigenetics; Mutation, Variation, Inheritance.
- **neurogen-population-genetics-and-genome-engineering** — Population Genetics, Genome Engineering, and Synthetic Biology (§4–§6): Population and Quantitative Genetics; Genome Engineering; Delivery, Screens, Synthetic Biology.
- **neurogen-neuron-biophysics-plasticity-and-coding** — Neuron Biophysics, Plasticity, and Neural Coding (§7–§9): Neuron Biophysics and Synapses; Plasticity; Neural Coding.
- **neurogen-circuits-neuromodulation-and-neural-engineering** — Circuit Architectures, Neuromodulation, and Neural Engineering (§10–§14): Circuit Architectures; Neuromodulation; Development and Glia; Neural Engineering Methods; Stimulation and Intervention.
- **neurogen-reference** — Misconceptions, Numbers, and the Frontier (§15–§20): Misconceptions; Numbers; Frontier — What Actually Moved; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
