# Biomedical Engineering Plugin

The technical layer of biomedical engineering: physiological signal processing and the filtering constraints specific to the domain, medical imaging modality physics and the DICOM internals that produce silent errors, clinical data structures, the technicals of clinical machine learning, bioinformatics, structural and systems biology, PK/PD and physiological modelling, biomechanics, biomaterials and tissue engineering, neural interfaces and prosthetics, lab automation, and the biostatistics that determine whether a result holds up.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **biomed-clinical-data-ml-and-bioinformatics** — Clinical Data, Clinical ML, and Bioinformatics (§3–§5): Clinical Data Structures; Clinical ML Technicals; Bioinformatics.
- **biomed-structural-systems-biology-and-pharmacology** — Structural Biology, Systems Biology, PK/PD, and Physiological Models (§6–§9): Structural Biology; Systems Biology; PK/PD; Physiological Models.
- **biomed-biomechanics-devices-and-biostatistics** — Biomechanics, Biomaterials, Devices, Lab Automation, and Biostatistics (§10–§15): Biomechanics; Biomaterials; Tissue Engineering; Neural Interfaces and Prosthetics; Lab Automation and Instrumentation; Biostatistics.
- **biomed-reference** — Misconceptions, Numbers, and Canon (§16–§20): Misconceptions; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
