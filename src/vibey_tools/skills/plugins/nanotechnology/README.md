# Nanotechnology Plugin

Nanotechnology for technical readers: the scaling laws and which forces actually matter as things get small, quantum confinement and tunnelling, top-down fabrication and lithography, bottom-up synthesis and self-assembly, the semiconductor process and what a node name really means, characterization methods, nanomaterials, DNA nanotechnology, nanomedicine, computational nanoscience from DFT through machine-learned interatomic potentials, and nanoparticle safety and environmental behaviour.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **nano-fabrication-and-semiconductor-process** — Top-Down Fabrication, Self-Assembly, and the Semiconductor Process (§3–§5): Top-Down Fabrication; Bottom-Up Synthesis and Self-Assembly; Semiconductor Process.
- **nano-characterization-materials-and-nanomedicine** — Characterization, Nanomaterials, DNA Nanotechnology, and Nanomedicine (§6–§9): Characterization; Nanomaterials; DNA Nanotechnology; Nanomedicine.
- **nano-computational-methods-and-safety** — Computational Nanoscience and Safety (§10–§11): Computational Nanoscience; Safety and Environmental Behaviour.
- **nano-reference** — Misconceptions, Numbers, What Moved, and Canon (§12–§17): Misconceptions; Numbers; What Actually Moved; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
