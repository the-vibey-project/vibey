# Fundraising Fundamentals Plugin

Raising capital across the whole range, for founders, operators and non-profit leaders: what fundraising actually is, the universal structure and narrative, running a process, the capital ladder, the instruments and the dilution and cap-table mathematics behind them, term sheet economics including liquidation preference, control and governance, non-dilutive and debt options, diligence, valuation, secondaries and exits, the public markets from IPO mechanics to post-IPO raising and public debt, securities regulation, and the non-profit side from the donor pyramid and grants through capital campaigns and the overhead myth. The securities and tax material is orientation, not legal or financial advice.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
