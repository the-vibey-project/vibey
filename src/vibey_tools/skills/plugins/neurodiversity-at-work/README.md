# Neurodiversity at Work Plugin

Workplace communication and accommodation for neurodivergent professionals (autistic, ADHD, AuDHD or otherwise) and for the managers, colleagues and HR teams who work with them: the evidence on autistic burnout, masking and the double empathy problem and why writing preferences down lowers load; a reusable library of low-ambiguity scripts for stating your own preferences, asking others theirs, clarifying vague instructions, protecting focus, feedback, disagreement, repair, deadlines, change and capacity; a How I Work one-pager; and, for when everyday wording is not enough, the evidence on disclosure and accommodations, the Job Accommodation Network (JAN) menu, the US ADA process, a staged disclosure ladder, template letters and interview accommodations.

Two skills, so a task loads only the part it needs. Research citations, JAN material and the US legal framework are as of October 2026; laws, agency guidance and filing deadlines change, so confirm the version in force. Evidence strength is marked where it is known, and ⚠️ flags claims that are contested, mixed or rest on a single study.

## ⚠️ Scope

Educational. **Not medical, clinical, psychological or legal advice**, and nothing here diagnoses anyone. The legal and hiring material describes the **United States** (ADA, EEOC, JAN) only; other jurisdictions work differently. Before relying on any protection — especially when facing discipline, a performance process or dismissal — talk to an employment lawyer, a union or an official advice service where you work.

## Skills

- **nd-workplace-communication-scripts** — Communication Scripts and the How I Work One-Pager (§1–§15): why writing preferences down works; how to use the library; stating your own preferences; asking others theirs; getting instructions clear and in writing; time, focus and meetings; boundaries, feedback, disagreement and repair; deadlines, status, change and handoffs; social energy and capacity signals; the How I Work one-pager; a staged rollout; a weekly burnout self-check; notes for managers and colleagues.
- **nd-accommodation-and-disclosure** — Accommodations and Disclosure (§1–§10): what the evidence says about disclosure and accommodations, including the documented risks of disclosing; the JAN accommodation menu and its low-cost data; the US ADA framework in brief; scripts from no disclosure to soft disclosure; staged disclosure on the job; formal letters and follow-ups; disclosure and interview accommodations in hiring; how managers and HR should receive a request.
