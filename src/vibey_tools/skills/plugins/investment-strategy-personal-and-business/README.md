# Gold Standards for Investment Strategy Plugin

Investment mechanics for individuals and for businesses, with the evidence stated plainly and the marketing stripped out. What comes before investing at all, risk and return, compounding, diversification, and market efficiency assessed honestly; asset classes, factors, costs, tax concepts, allocation, rebalancing and vehicle selection; accumulation, decumulation and the behavioural failures that dominate real outcomes; then how business investment differs — capital budgeting, cost of capital, capital structure, treasury, valuation and raising capital; plus fraud red flags, alternatives, and advisor conflicts. This is a mechanics reference, not investment advice, and it recommends no strategy expected to be profitable.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (checked August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
