# Consumer Bankruptcy Plugin

Consumer bankruptcy for someone weighing or preparing a filing: whether Chapter 7 is the chapter to ask about, the means test and the six-month look-back that can make the filing date matter, what a filing costs and how fee waivers and installments work, how bankruptcy attorneys are paid, the timeline from credit counselling to discharge and the automatic stay, surrendering a house or car with little or no equity, what must be disclosed, the state's exemptions, what is not discharged, what to avoid before filing, credit and long-term effects, the alternatives, and the questions to take to an attorney.

The first jurisdiction covered is South Carolina. Procedure is stable; dollar figures (means-test medians, court fees, exemptions) are current as of October 2026 and must be confirmed against the figures in effect on the filing date. Claims resting on conflicting or general sources are marked ⚠️. A research reference for preparing a consultation with a licensed bankruptcy attorney, not legal advice.
