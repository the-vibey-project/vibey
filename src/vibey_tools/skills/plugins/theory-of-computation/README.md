# Theory of Computation Plugin

Theory of computation as working knowledge for practicing software engineers: what is computable and what isn't, automata and the Chomsky hierarchy as a practical design tool, regular languages and the ReDoS failure mode, parsing and grammar classes, complexity classes and NP-completeness recognition, what to actually do when your problem is NP-hard, SAT and SMT solvers, fine-grained complexity, space complexity and streaming, the distributed computing impossibility results, type systems and Curry–Howard, and randomness and approximation.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **toc-automata-regex-and-parsing** — Automata, Regular Expressions, and Parsing (§0–§3): Routing; Automata and the Chomsky Hierarchy; Regular Expressions in Practice; Parsing and Grammars.
- **toc-computability-and-complexity** — Computability, Complexity Classes, NP-Hardness, and Solvers (§4–§7): Computability — What You Cannot Do; Complexity Classes; Your Problem Is NP-Hard. Now What?; SAT, SMT, and Solvers.
- **toc-beyond-np-space-and-distributed-limits** — Beyond NP, Space and Streaming, and Distributed Impossibility Results (§8–§11): Beyond NP; Fine-Grained Complexity; Space and Memory; Distributed Computing Impossibility Results.
- **toc-type-systems-and-randomization** — Type Systems, Logic and Verification, and Randomness and Approximation (§12–§13): Type Systems, Logic, and Verification; Randomness, Approximation, and Heuristics.
- **toc-reference** — Anti-Patterns, Contested Questions, Currency, and Canon (§14–§19): Anti-Patterns; Contested Questions; Currency Snapshot; The Canon; Quick Reference; Sources and Method.
