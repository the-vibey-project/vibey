# Building a Homestead Plugin

A house from the ground up, animals that earn their keep, and ground that grows food —
detailed enough to actually go out and do it.

One reference, split into 8 skills, so a task loads only the part it needs.

**The thesis the building half opens on**, and it is worth reading before any of the detail:

> Most building failures are **not engineering failures**. They are coordination failures at the
> boundaries between disciplines, and between design and construction.

The farming half has a matching one: the cheap, subjective, weekly measurement — body condition
score, a FAMACHA card, a spade in the soil — beats the expensive annual one, because it is the
measurement you will actually take.

Section numbers are **shared across the set**: a reference written as `§N → skill` points into that
sibling skill.

## ⚠️ What this reference is not

Building work is governed by **local codes and inspection**, and livestock by **local animal-health
rules**. This tells you what to design for and what to ask — not what your jurisdiction permits.
A structural element, a foundation on expansive clay, a fire separation or a drug withdrawal period
is a question for an engineer, an inspector or a vet, not for a reference.

One thing to know before you use §12: **the source document's FAMACHA colour thresholds are
internally inconsistent** — it gives the same colours for "anemic, treat" and "healthy, don't treat".
The pack reproduces the text and flags it rather than silently picking a side, because getting the
direction backwards means treating healthy animals and leaving anemic ones to die. Read the
thresholds off an official FAMACHA card or from your vet.

## Neighbours in this marketplace

Several plugins cover this ground as **disciplines**; this one covers it as **a thing you are
building**:

- `designing-and-building-buildings` — the professional building lifecycle: procurement, BIM, cost
  estimating, commissioning, why projects fail
- `hunting-botany-livestock-agriculture` — botany, livestock science and soil as fields of study
- `cooking-cleaning-waste-sciences` — food safety and waste handling downstream of the harvest
- `refrigeration-ac-climate-control-food-water` — cold chain, water storage and treatment
- `working-with-wood-and-metal` and `making-fabric-clothing-furniture-laundering` — the shop skills
- `survival-skills` — the same terrain when nothing is built yet
