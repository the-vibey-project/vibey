# Power Engineering Plugin

Electric power systems for software engineers who have to build against them: AC power fundamentals including the real-versus-reactive distinction software people get wrong and frequency as the balance signal, generation, transmission and distribution, power system analysis, protection and relaying, SCADA, EMS and DMS, the protocols, inverters and the inertia problem, storage, markets and dispatch, writing grid software and why OT is not IT, and datacenter power.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **power-ac-fundamentals-generation-and-grid** — AC Power Fundamentals, Generation, and Transmission and Distribution (§0–§3): Routing; AC Power Fundamentals; Generation; Transmission and Distribution.
- **power-system-analysis-and-protection** — Power System Analysis and Protection and Relaying (§4–§5): Power System Analysis; Protection and Relaying.
- **power-scada-ems-and-protocols** — SCADA, EMS and DMS, and the Protocols (§6–§7): SCADA, EMS, and DMS; Protocols.
- **power-inverters-storage-markets-and-datacenters** — Inverters and Renewables, Storage, Markets, Grid Software, and Datacenter Power (§8–§12): Inverters, Renewables, and the Inertia Problem; Storage; Markets and Dispatch; Writing Grid Software; Datacenter Power.
- **power-reference** — Anti-Patterns, Numbers, What Moved, and Canon (§13–§18): Anti-Patterns; Numbers; What Actually Moved; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
