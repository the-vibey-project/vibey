# Cooking, Cleaning and Waste Sciences Plugin

The chemistry behind cooking, cleaning and waste handling, at domestic and commercial scale: heat transfer and what it does to proteins, browning, starch and gluten, emulsions, brining, acid, fermentation and leavening; the food-safety layer of pathogens and temperature, HACCP, and cross-contamination and allergens; kitchen organization, restaurant economics and inspection; cleaning chemistry including surfactants, water hardness, the cleaning-versus-sanitizing-versus-disinfecting distinction and the chemical combinations that are genuinely dangerous; and wastewater treatment, landfills, recycling assessed honestly, and composting.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **foodenv-food-science** — The Food Science (§0–§11): Routing; What Kind of Claim Is This?; ⚠️ Heat Transfer; Proteins and Meat; Browning; Starch, Gluten and Gels; Fats and Emulsions; Salt, Water and Brining; Acid and pH; Flavour and Seasoning; Fermentation; Baking and Leavening.
- **foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service** — Food Safety, HACCP, and Running a Kitchen (§12–§18): ⚠️ Pathogens and Temperature; HACCP; Cross-Contamination and Allergens; Kitchen Organization; Restaurant Economics; Menu Engineering; Regulation and Inspection.
- **foodenv-cleaning-chemistry** — Cleaning Chemistry, Sanitizing, and the Mixing Dangers (§19–§23): What Cleaning Actually Is; ⚠️ Surfactants and Soil Types; Water Hardness; ⚠️ Cleaning vs Sanitizing vs Disinfecting — and the Mixing Dangers; Laundry.
- **foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management** — Wastewater Treatment, Landfills, Recycling, and Composting (§24–§28): Wastewater Treatment; The Waste Hierarchy; Landfills; ⚠️ Recycling, Honestly; Composting and Organics.
- **foodenv-reference** — What's Live, Misconceptions, and Numbers (§29–§34): What's Live; Misconceptions; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
