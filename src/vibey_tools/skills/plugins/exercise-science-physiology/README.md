# Exercise Science and Physiology Plugin

Exercise science and physiology with the evidence gradient stated up front — a field with a strong mechanistic core and an unusually noisy application layer. Muscle structure and energy systems, cardiovascular, respiratory, endocrine and neural response, thermoregulation; training principles and the mechanisms of strength, hypertrophy, endurance and power; programming variables, periodization, recovery and aging; fuelling and the supplement evidence tiers alongside under-fuelling, RED-S and overtraining; injury and load management; and testing. A technical reference, not medical, dietary or personal training advice.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
