# Newtonian Mechanics Plugin

Classical mechanics for people who have to compute with it: kinematics, Newton's laws stated precisely and the misconceptions they generate, free-body diagrams, the forces, work and energy and why conservation laws run deeper than the laws themselves, momentum and collisions, rotation, rigid bodies and gyroscopes, oscillations and resonance, central forces and orbits, non-inertial frames, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, fluids in brief, chaos and the limits of prediction, and numerical integration including why symplectic methods matter.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
