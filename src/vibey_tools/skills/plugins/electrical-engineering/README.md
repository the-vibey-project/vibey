# Electrical Engineering Plugin

Practical electrical engineering for people who have to make real hardware work: circuit fundamentals, real component behaviour and parasitics, DC and AC analysis, filters, semiconductors, op-amps, digital logic and interfacing, power and regulators, signal integrity and grounding, EMC and EMI, PCB design, test equipment and how not to lie to yourself with an oscilloscope, datasheets and part selection, electrical safety, and a hardware debugging method.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **ee-fundamentals-components-and-circuit-analysis** — Fundamentals, Real Components, and DC and AC Analysis (§0–§4): Routing; Fundamentals; Real Components; DC Analysis; AC, Impedance, Filters.
- **ee-semiconductors-op-amps-logic-and-power** — Semiconductors, Op-Amps, Digital Interfacing, and Power (§5–§8): Semiconductors; Op-Amps; Digital Logic and Interfacing; Power.
- **ee-signal-integrity-emc-and-pcb-design** — Signal Integrity, Grounding, EMC, and PCB Design (§9–§11): Signal Integrity and Grounding; EMC and EMI; PCB Design.
- **ee-test-selection-safety-and-debugging** — Test Equipment, Part Selection, Safety, and Debugging (§12–§15): Test Equipment; Datasheets and Selection; Safety; Debugging Hardware.
- **ee-reference** — Anti-Patterns, Numbers, and Canon (§16–§20): Anti-Patterns; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
