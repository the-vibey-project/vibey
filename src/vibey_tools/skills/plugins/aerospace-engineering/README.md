# Aerospace Engineering Plugin

Atmospheric flight engineering for technical readers: aerodynamics fundamentals and an honest account of how lift works, airfoils and wings, the drag breakdown, compressible and supersonic flow, performance, stability and control, air-breathing propulsion, structures and materials, aeroelasticity, flight controls and avionics, drones and UAS, launch vehicles, flight test and certification, and the design process.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **aero-aerodynamics-airfoils-and-compressible-flow** — Aerodynamics Fundamentals, Airfoils and Wings, Drag, and Compressible Flow (§0–§4): Routing; Aerodynamics Fundamentals; Airfoils and Wings; Drag; Compressible and Supersonic Flow.
- **aero-performance-stability-and-propulsion** — Performance, Stability and Control, and Air-Breathing Propulsion (§5–§7): Performance; Stability and Control; Air-Breathing Propulsion.
- **aero-structures-aeroelasticity-and-avionics** — Structures and Materials, Aeroelasticity, and Flight Controls and Avionics (§8–§10): Structures and Materials; Aeroelasticity; Flight Controls and Avionics.
- **aero-drones-launch-vehicles-flight-test-and-design** — Drones and UAS, Launch Vehicles, Flight Test and Certification, and the Design Process (§11–§14): Drones and UAS; Launch Vehicles; Flight Test and Certification; The Design Process.
- **aero-reference** — Misconceptions, What Moved, Numbers, and Canon (§15–§20): Misconceptions; What Moved; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
