# Family and Community Building Plugin

Family and community research with its evidence quality attached: how to read a claim in a field crowded with advocacy, attachment, what actually predicts couple stability, conflict and repair, transitions and dissolution, family systems and life stages, child development and parenting, behaviour genetics and what it does to naive parenting inferences, discipline, siblings, family structure as genuinely contested territory, adversity and resilience, caregiving and aging, social capital, networks and belonging, loneliness, third places, voluntary associations, community organizing, collective efficacy, and program evaluation.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **family-reading-claims-attachment-and-couple-relationships** — Reading Claims, Attachment, Couple Stability, Conflict, and Dissolution (§0–§5): Routing; ⚠️ How to Read Claims in This Field; Attachment; Couple Relationships: What Predicts Stability; Conflict and Repair; Transitions and Dissolution.
- **family-systems-child-development-and-parenting** — Family Systems, Roles and Life Stages, Child Development, and Parenting (§6–§9): Family Systems; Family Roles and Life Stages; Child Development; Parenting.
- **family-behaviour-genetics-structure-and-caregiving** — Behaviour Genetics, Discipline, Siblings, Family Structure, Resilience, and Caregiving (§10–§15): ⚠️ Behaviour Genetics; Discipline; Siblings and Birth Order; ⚠️ Family Structure — Contested Territory; Adversity and Resilience; Caregiving and Aging.
- **family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation** — Social Capital, Networks, Loneliness, Third Places, Organizing, and Program Evaluation (§16–§23): Social Capital; Networks and Belonging; ⚠️ Loneliness and Social Isolation; Third Places; Congregations and Voluntary Associations; Community Organizing; Collective Efficacy; ⚠️ Does the Program Actually Work?.
- **family-reference** — What's Live, Misconceptions, and Canon (§24–§29): What's Live; Misconceptions; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
