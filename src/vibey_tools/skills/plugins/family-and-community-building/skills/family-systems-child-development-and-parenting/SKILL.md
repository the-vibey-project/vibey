## §6. Family Systems

**⚠️ The founding insight (Bowen, Minuchin, Satir): a family is an interdependent system,
so a symptom in one member is often a property of the SYSTEM, not of the person.**
```
HOMEOSTASIS       ⚠️ systems resist change, including beneficial change
⚠️ IDENTIFIED PATIENT  the person carrying the symptom, who may be expressing
                  a system-level strain. ⚠️ "Fix the problem child" reliably fails
BOUNDARIES        ⚠️ enmeshed ←→ clear ←→ disengaged
TRIANGULATION     ⚠️ a third party (often a child) pulled into a dyad's tension
DIFFERENTIATION   ⚠️ Bowen — capacity to be a self WHILE staying connected.
                  Neither fused nor cut off
MULTIGENERATIONAL TRANSMISSION  patterns repeating across generations
```
**⚠️ Honest assessment**: **family systems theory is clinically influential and
empirically softer than it appears** — ⚠️ **much of it is hard to operationalize and test,
and specific constructs like differentiation have mixed measurement support.** **What
survives well is the core reframe: interdependence is real, and intervening on one member
in isolation usually fails.**

## §7. Family Roles and Life Stages

**Family life cycle stages** (⚠️ **a useful heuristic, and note it assumes a particular
normative sequence that many families don't follow**): **formation, childbearing,
school-age, adolescence, launching, later life.**
**⚠️ Transitions are where strain concentrates** — **new baby, adolescence, launching,
caregiving onset (§15 → `family-behaviour-genetics-structure-and-caregiving`), retirement, bereavement.**
**⚠️ Roles and division of labour**: **the "second shift" and the persistent gap in
household and care work is well documented and remains** — ⚠️ **the 2025 American Time Use
Survey continues to show a chore gap.** **⚠️ Perceived FAIRNESS predicts satisfaction
better than measured equality does, which is a genuinely interesting finding — the
negotiation matters as much as the split.**
**⚠️ Family rituals and routines have surprisingly good evidence**: **predictable
mealtimes, bedtimes, celebrations and traditions are associated with better child
adjustment, family cohesion and resilience under stress.** ⚠️ **This is the §-level
"structure beats willpower" point applied domestically — routines do work that intention
does not.**

## §8. Child Development

**⚠️ Stages are approximate and variation is wide** (see a psychology reference §9 — Piaget
directionally right, ages wrong).
```
⚠️ SERVE AND RETURN  responsive back-and-forth interaction is the core
   mechanism of early brain development. Well supported, and it's what
   "sensitivity" (§2) looks like moment to moment
⚠️ LANGUAGE     enormous variation in early exposure; ⚠️ the famous "30 million
   word gap" figure has failed replication and is now disputed — the
   underlying point that interaction quality matters survives, the number
   does not
ATTACHMENT (§2) · SELF-REGULATION · THEORY OF MIND (~4 yrs) ·
EXECUTIVE FUNCTION (⚠️ develops into the mid-20s)
⚠️ ADOLESCENCE  reward and social systems mature earlier than control
   systems. Real, and popularly overstated into determinism
```
**⚠️ Sensitive periods are real and are usually not "windows that slam shut."** ⚠️ **Language
and vision have genuine sensitive periods; most cognitive and social capacities remain
plastic well beyond early childhood.** **The "first three years determine everything"
framing oversells the science and produces unnecessary parental anxiety.**
**⚠️ Play is not optional**: **unstructured and pretend play supports self-regulation,
language and social skill**, ⚠️ **and its decline in favour of structured activity is a
documented and plausible concern.**

## §9. Parenting

**⚠️ Baumrind's typology remains the organizing frame, on two axes — DEMANDINGNESS and
RESPONSIVENESS:**
```
                 High responsiveness    Low responsiveness
High demand   ⚠️ AUTHORITATIVE          AUTHORITARIAN
Low demand       PERMISSIVE             NEGLECTFUL
```
**⚠️ Authoritative parenting — warm AND structured, high expectations with high support —
is associated with better outcomes across a wide range of measures.** ⚠️ **This is among
the more robust findings in the field**, **with two honest caveats: ⚠️ the associations are
correlational and partly confounded by heredity** (§10 → `family-behaviour-genetics-structure-and-caregiving`), **and ⚠️ the optimal balance
varies by cultural context — authoritarian-looking parenting has different meanings and
different associated outcomes in different communities, which is well documented.**
**⚠️ What has decent evidence, specifically:**
- **⚠️ Parent training programs work** — **behavioural parent training (Incredible Years,
  Triple P, PCIT and similar) has among the strongest evidence in this whole domain,
  including randomized trials, particularly for child conduct problems.**
- **Warmth and responsiveness; consistency and predictability; scaffolded autonomy.**
- **⚠️ Reading with children and quality of verbal interaction.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — parenting effects are real and smaller than the parenting industry
> implies, and the reason is §10 → `family-behaviour-genetics-structure-and-caregiving`.** ⚠️ **Much of the parent-child correlation is genetic
> confounding — parents pass on genes AND environment — and studies that control for this
> find substantially reduced effects.** **⚠️ This is not licence to disengage: the
> well-evidenced effects concentrate in avoiding harm (abuse, neglect, severe harshness),
> in the relationship itself, and in specific domains like language and education.**
> **The takeaway is calibration, not fatalism — and it should reduce parental guilt about
> ordinary variation in style.**
