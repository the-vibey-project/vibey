# Survival Skills Plugin

Wilderness and emergency survival grounded in what actually kills people rather than in what looks dramatic — exposure and bad decisions, not predators or starvation. Survival psychology and priority ordering, thermoregulation and shelter, water, fire and why food comes last; navigation when lost, signalling and rescue, field medical priorities, heat illness and animals in proportion; the environment-specific hazards of cold, desert, tropical, maritime and urban disaster settings; preventive planning, kit that earns its weight and practising; and the survival myths that get people killed. Orientation grounded in the evidence, not a substitute for hands-on training or for calling emergency services.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
