# Space Exploration Plugin

Spacecraft and mission engineering for technical readers: mission architecture and the design cascade in which everything is mass, trajectory and mission design, power, thermal control, communications and the link budget including light-time consequences, navigation and autonomy, attitude control and in-space propulsion, entry, descent and landing and surface operations, human physiology and its hard limits, life support and ISRU, radiation environments and shielding, instrumentation, planetary protection, and reliability and margins.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **space-mission-architecture-and-trajectory** — Mission Architecture and Trajectory Design (§0–§2): Routing; Mission Architecture; Trajectory and Mission Design.
- **space-power-thermal-comms-and-navigation** — Power, Thermal Control, Communications, and Navigation (§3–§6): Power; Thermal Control; Communications; Navigation and Autonomy.
- **space-attitude-propulsion-and-edl** — Attitude Control, In-Space Propulsion, and Entry, Descent and Landing (§7–§8): Attitude Control and In-Space Propulsion; EDL and Surface Operations.
- **space-human-factors-life-support-and-reliability** — Human Physiology, Life Support and ISRU, Radiation, Instruments, and Reliability (§9–§14): Human Physiology — The Hard Limits; Life Support and ISRU; Radiation Environments and Shielding; Instrumentation; Planetary Protection; Reliability and Margins.
- **space-reference** — Misconceptions, Contested Questions, and the Open Frontier (§15–§20): Misconceptions; Contested; What's Actually Open; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
