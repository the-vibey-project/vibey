## §12. ⚠️ Documents and Entry

> **⚠️ Check with the destination's official government source and your airline. Third-party
> visa sites, including ones that look official, frequently charge for free processes.**
```
⚠️ PASSPORT VALIDITY  ⚠️ many countries require 6 MONTHS validity
   BEYOND your intended departure, and some count from ENTRY.
   ⚠️ This turns people away at check-in constantly
⚠️ BLANK PAGES  some countries require them
⚠️ VISA vs TRAVEL AUTHORIZATION  ⚠️ different things. ESTA (US),
   eTA (Canada), ETA (UK), ⚠️ ETIAS (EU, forthcoming — §26.1)
   are AUTHORIZATIONS, not visas, and are not guarantees of entry
⚠️ TRANSIT  ⚠️ some countries require a transit visa even airside.
   Check for every country you touch, not just the destination
⚠️ ONWARD/RETURN TICKET  frequently required and enforced at check-in
⚠️ SCHENGEN 90/180  ⚠️ a ROLLING window, not a calendar one, and it is
   now automatically calculated (§26.1)
⚠️ NAME MATCHING  ⚠️ the ticket must match the passport
```
**⚠️ Practical hygiene**: ⚠️ **photograph or scan your passport, visas, insurance policy and
key bookings, and store them somewhere accessible offline AND in the cloud.** **⚠️ Leave a
copy with someone at home.** **⚠️ Know where your country's embassy is.**
**⚠️ Also verify**: ⚠️ **customs restrictions (food, medication, cash declaration
thresholds), and whether your PRESCRIPTION MEDICATION is legal at the destination —
several common medications are controlled or banned in some countries, and this catches
people out badly.**

## §13. ⚠️ Travel Insurance

**⚠️ What it's actually for: the catastrophic case, not the inconvenient one.**
```
⚠️ THE COVERAGE THAT MATTERS MOST  ⚠️ MEDICAL and MEDICAL EVACUATION.
   ⚠️ Evacuation from a remote area or by air ambulance can run into
   six figures. This is the reason to have insurance
TRIP CANCELLATION and INTERRUPTION · baggage · ⚠️ personal liability
⚠️ WHAT COMMONLY ISN'T COVERED
   ⚠️ Pre-existing conditions unless declared (⚠️ and the declaration
      window is often short after first booking)
   ⚠️ Alcohol-related incidents · "hazardous activities" (⚠️ which can
      include motorcycling, skiing off-piste, diving, trekking above
      an altitude threshold) · ⚠️ travel against official advisories ·
      ⚠️ known events (a named storm, a pre-existing strike)
   ⚠️ "Cancel for any reason" is a separate, more expensive add-on
      and usually reimburses only a percentage
```
**⚠️ Check what you already have**: ⚠️ **credit card travel coverage is real but usually
narrower than assumed and often requires paying for the trip with that card; and some
national health arrangements (⚠️ the EHIC/GHIC in Europe) cover state healthcare but NOT
repatriation.**
**⚠️ The practical test**: ⚠️ **read the exclusions before the benefits, and check the
medical and evacuation LIMITS rather than the headline price.**

## §14. Health

**⚠️ Check requirements and recommendations well ahead** — ⚠️ **some vaccines need multiple
doses over weeks, and yellow fever certificates are a legal entry requirement for some
routes.**
**⚠️ Malaria prophylaxis where relevant, and ⚠️ bite avoidance matters at least as much as
tablets** — **it also addresses dengue and chikungunya, for which there's no prophylaxis.**
**⚠️ Medication**: ⚠️ **carry in original labelled packaging, in hand luggage, with a
doctor's letter for anything unusual or controlled, and ⚠️ take more than the trip length
in case of delay.** **⚠️ Check legality at the destination** (§12).
**⚠️ Altitude**: ⚠️ **ascent RATE is the risk factor, not fitness; and descent is the
treatment for serious altitude illness.**
**⚠️ Ordinary risks dominate exotic ones**: ⚠️ **road traffic injury, falls, sunburn,
dehydration and existing conditions flaring account for far more traveller harm than
tropical disease** (§15).

## §15. ⚠️ Safety, In Proportion

> **⚠️ Traveller risk perception is systematically miscalibrated, and correcting it
> improves decisions in both directions.**
```
⚠️ WHAT ACTUALLY HARMS TRAVELLERS, roughly in order
   ⚠️ ROAD TRAFFIC — by a wide margin. ⚠️ Wear the seatbelt, avoid
      night driving on unfamiliar rural roads, wear a helmet,
      and be sceptical of informal transport
   ⚠️ Drowning · falls · existing medical conditions · alcohol
      (⚠️ a multiplier on all of the above) · petty theft
⚠️ WHAT PEOPLE FEAR  terrorism, kidnapping, violent crime against
   tourists — ⚠️ real but statistically rare relative to the above
```
**⚠️ Practical measures that generalize**: ⚠️ **situational awareness at transit hubs and
ATMs; separating cash and cards across locations; a "throwaway" wallet in some contexts;
⚠️ not resisting a robbery; sharing your itinerary with someone; registering with your
foreign ministry where that service exists; and ⚠️ knowing the local emergency number.**
**⚠️ Scams follow patterns rather than places**: ⚠️ **the unusually helpful stranger, the
"attraction is closed today" redirect, the taxi meter that's broken, the friendship
bracelet, the spilled substance and the helpful cleaner, and ⚠️ anything with artificial
time pressure.** **The common structure is a stranger controlling your attention and your
schedule.**
**⚠️ Solo travel** — ⚠️ **and specifically solo travel as a woman, or as a visible minority,
or as an LGBTQ+ traveller — carries destination-specific considerations that are worth
researching from sources written by people in that position**, **because generic advice
misses them.**

## §16. Money Abroad

```
⚠️ CARDS  ⚠️ notify the bank or use a card that doesn't need it;
   carry TWO cards on different networks in different places
⚠️ FOREIGN TRANSACTION FEES  ⚠️ a no-FX-fee card pays for itself fast
⚠️ DYNAMIC CURRENCY CONVERSION  ⚠️ when a terminal or ATM offers to
   charge you in YOUR currency, ALWAYS DECLINE. The exchange rate is
   deliberately poor. ⚠️ Choose the LOCAL currency, every time
⚠️ ATMs  ⚠️ bank-operated ones over standalone machines in tourist
   areas; decline the conversion offer; check the withdrawal fee
⚠️ CASH  ⚠️ still essential in many places. Research the norm —
   card penetration varies enormously
AIRPORT EXCHANGE  ⚠️ consistently the worst rate available
⚠️ TIPPING  ⚠️ varies from expected to insulting. Research it
```
**⚠️ Have a backup**: ⚠️ **a card frozen for suspected fraud in a country where you have no
cash is a genuinely bad afternoon.** **Redundancy is the whole strategy.**
