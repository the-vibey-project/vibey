# Trip Planning and Travel Strategies Plugin

Planning and executing travel as a craft with real mechanics behind it: matching the trip type to the purpose, choosing a destination, constraints-first planning, seasonality and budgeting; how airfare pricing actually works, flight routing strategy, points and miles assessed honestly, accommodation, ground transport and the booking order that preserves optionality; passports, visas, insurance, health, safety in proportion and money abroad; itinerary pacing, packing, jet lag, connectivity and cultural navigation; and the skill that matters most in practice — handling disruption when the plan breaks.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **travel-pacing-packing-jet-lag-connectivity-and-culture** — Itinerary Pacing, Packing, Jet Lag, Connectivity and Navigation, Language and Culture, and Food and Water (§17–§22): ⚠️ Itinerary Pacing; Packing; Jet Lag; Connectivity and Navigation; Language and Culture; Food and Water.
