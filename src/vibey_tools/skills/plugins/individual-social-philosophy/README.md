# Individual Social Philosophy (Plugin)

A marketplace plugin providing an end-to-end philosophical, theological, and practical system for individual social navigation.

Political philosophy governs collective states and legal codes. Cultural philosophy examines macro-sociological norms. Individual social philosophy provides the micro-sociological principles, cognitive filters, and boundary rules for a single person living in a fallen world.

## Structure

This plugin contains five modular skill slices:

1. **`foundations-and-methodology`**: The ontological baseline of human fallenness (*homo incurvatus in se*), the Christological precedent of guarded association ([John 2:24-25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+2%3A24-25&version=ESV)), and the methodological distinction between doctrine, history, culture, and social life.
2. **`theological-and-historical-sources`**: Primary textual synthesis across Catholic deuterocanonical wisdom (Sirach), scholastic virtue ethics (Aquinas on *prudentia* and *amicitia*), monastic probation (St. Aelred of Rievaulx), Eastern Orthodox asceticism (*nepsis*, *diakrisis*, Desert Fathers), and Protestant ecclesiology (Bonhoeffer on community, C.S. Lewis on the "Inner Ring").
3. **`concentric-spheres-and-asymmetry`**: The five-tier concentric architecture (Tiers 0 to 4), the mathematical hysteresis of trust (arithmetic accumulation coupled with geometric collapse), and the presentation spectrum (public decorum at perimeters and vulnerability at the covenantal core).
4. **`objective-risk-calculus`**: The formal Social Risk Index ($R_s = \frac{E \cdot H \cdot C}{I \cdot A \cdot S}$), parameter definitions and scoring metrics, and the six empirical observation vectors for evaluating counterparts with objective discipline.
5. **`operational-playbook`**: The real-world behavioral guide: peripheral entry and polite affability, St. Aelred's micro-probing protocol, the immediate revocation protocol, the Matthew 18 process, and the distinction between Christian forgiveness and earned trust.
