# Debt Consolidation, Ramsey and Bankruptcy Plugin

Weighing debt consolidation against Dave Ramsey's 7 Baby Steps and bankruptcy, as of October 2026 and written for South Carolina: the five different tools that share the name "consolidation" and which two actually reduce what you owe, balance transfers, personal loans, secured and retirement-funded options, nonprofit debt management plans, settlement, South Carolina collection law, Chapter 7 and Chapter 13, the Baby Steps and the evidence for and against them, EntreLeadership and solo-LLC mechanics, and a decision framework with the questions to ask each professional. General information, not legal, tax or financial advice.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its Parts so a task loads only the part it needs. Cross-references into a sibling skill are written as text, not links.

## Refresh policy

The structure of the decision is stable; the numbers are not. Update the rates and fee ranges in `debtplan-consolidation-tools-and-costs` first, then the South Carolina median-income figure and statutes in `debtplan-collection-law-and-bankruptcy`, then Ramsey Solutions' published steps and prices in `debtplan-ramsey-baby-steps`. Anything in the open-questions section of `debtplan-reference-open-questions-and-sources` is unverified and should be confirmed before reuse.
