# Biology and Chemistry Foundations Plugin

The chemistry and biology a technical reader actually needs, organized so the reasoning transfers rather than the facts alone: atoms and periodicity, bonding and geometry, intermolecular forces and solutions, thermodynamics, kinetics and equilibrium, redox and electrochemistry, organic chemistry and mechanism, analytical methods and spectroscopy, biomolecules and enzymes, bioenergetics and metabolism, membranes and transport, cell architecture and signalling, and evolution.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **biochem-thermodynamics-kinetics-and-equilibrium** — Thermodynamics, Kinetics, Equilibrium, and Electrochemistry (§4–§7): Thermodynamics; Kinetics; Equilibrium and Acid-Base; Redox and Electrochemistry.
- **biochem-organic-chemistry-and-analytical-methods** — Organic Chemistry and Analytical Methods (§8–§9): Organic Chemistry; Analytical Methods and Spectroscopy.
- **biochem-biomolecules-cells-and-evolution** — Biomolecules, Enzymes, Metabolism, Cells, and Evolution (§10–§16): Biomolecules; Enzymes; Bioenergetics and Metabolism; Membranes and Transport; Cell Architecture; Cell Cycle and Signalling; Evolution.
- **biochem-reference** — Misconceptions, Numbers, and Canon (§17–§20): Misconceptions; Numbers; Books and Quick Reference; Method.
