# Christian Pastoral Care Plugin

Pastoral care written from inside evangelical Christian conviction, for a believer — or someone caring for one — who is working through burnout, shame or trauma and wants to bring the theology of grace together with what clinical research says about self-compassion.

## ⚠️ Scope

**Pastoral and educational. Not therapy, counselling or medical advice**, and not a substitute for licensed clinical care. The reference states the theological tradition's case on that tradition's own terms and reports the clinical evidence with its strength and its gaps; §9 of the skill covers when and how to work alongside a licensed clinician, and where there is any risk of suicide or self-harm, act now: contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the United States, call or text 988). The theology is stable; the clinical evidence (self-compassion research, religiously adapted therapy, adult neurodivergence) is young and moving, and is current as of October 2026.

## Skills

- **faith-integrated-self-compassion** — Grace, Shame and Recovery (§0–§12): how to read it; the theology of grace; gospel self-grace and secular self-compassion; shame and guilt, conviction and condemnation; identity in Christ as a counter-narrative to shame; spiritual disciplines as means of grace; what the emerging theology says about neurodivergent believers; what the clinical evidence shows and where it runs out; working alongside professional care; pastoral failure modes; an optional pacing outline; a quick reference.
