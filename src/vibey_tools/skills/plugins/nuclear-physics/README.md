# Nuclear Physics Plugin

Nuclear physics and nuclear engineering for technical readers: nuclear structure and the binding energy curve, radioactive decay, reactions and cross sections, radiation and dose, fission physics, reactor physics including why delayed neutrons make reactors controllable at all, reactor types, the fuel cycle and waste, reactor safety, fusion physics and both confinement approaches, an honest account of why fusion is hard to engineer, and detection and measurement.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **nuclear-fusion-confinement-and-detection** — Fusion Physics, Magnetic and Inertial Confinement, and Detection (§10–§14): Fusion Physics; Magnetic Confinement; Inertial Confinement; Why Fusion Is Hard to Engineer; Detection and Measurement.
- **nuclear-reference** — Misconceptions, What Moved, Numbers, and Canon (§15–§21): Misconceptions; What Moved; Scope Note; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
