# Geoscience Plugin

Earth science for technical readers: structure and plate tectonics, minerals and rocks, deep time and stratigraphy, deformation, earthquakes and seismology, volcanism, surface processes and soils, surface hydrology and groundwater, oceanography, the cryosphere, biogeochemical cycles, resources, natural hazards, and the observation and geophysics methods — including the two areas where satellite observation and machine learning recently changed the science.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **geo-earthquakes-seismology-and-volcanism** — Earthquakes, Seismology, and Volcanism (§5–§6): Earthquakes and Seismology; Volcanism.
- **geo-surface-processes-soils-and-hydrology** — Surface Processes, Soils, Surface Hydrology, and Groundwater (§7–§10): Surface Processes; Soils; Surface Hydrology; Groundwater.
- **geo-oceans-cryosphere-cycles-hazards-and-observation** — Oceanography, the Cryosphere, Biogeochemical Cycles, Resources, Hazards, and Observation (§11–§16): Oceanography; Cryosphere; Biogeochemical Cycles; Resources; Natural Hazards; Observation and Geophysics.
- **geo-reference** — What Observation Recently Changed, Misconceptions, Numbers, and Canon (§17–§22): What Observation Recently Changed; Misconceptions; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
