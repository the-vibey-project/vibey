# Pipeline Novel Conversion
## Content Engineering Pipeline — Phases 6 and 7

This skill governs Phases 6 and 7 of the Content Engineering
Pipeline. Phase 6 converts a
publication-ready non-fiction manuscript into a complete novel
architecture. Phase 7 executes that architecture as prose.

---

## YOUR GOVERNING POSTULATES

These operate as axioms across all work in this pipeline. They apply
to the novel exactly as they apply to the research book. The novel
does not permit violation of these postulates on the grounds that it
is "fiction."

They are the postulates the author stated before Phase 1 — see
*Before Phase 1 — State Your Governing Postulates* in
`pipeline-thesis-development`, which also carries a worked example.
The rules this skill applies under the Third Postulate are the
method's **limits on means**: they stand on their own, whatever words
the author's own Third Postulate uses, and that postulate may tighten
them but never loosen them.

In the novel context, the limits on means specifically prohibit:
emotional manipulation substituted for earned dramatic tension;
antagonists who are evil without comprehensible motive; false
resolutions that do not cost the protagonist what genuine
transformation requires; and villainizing readers' actual positions
through the protagonist's antagonist rather than engaging those
positions honestly through the antagonist's own logic.

---

## THE CONVERSION RATIONALE

A research book argues. A novel proves. Both can arrive at the same
destination — a reader whose understanding of the world has
permanently shifted — but they take radically different roads.

The specific move this conversion enables is what C.S. Lewis called
**"stealing past the watchful dragons"** — the cultural defensiveness
and intellectual fatigue that inoculates many readers against explicit
argument, but that cannot resist a story that is genuinely,
beautifully, emotionally true. The research book's findings do not
disappear in conversion. They go underground. They become the
architecture beneath the story's surface — the controlling idea,
the character's want and need, the stakes of the central conflict,
the emotional arc, the eucatastrophic turn. The reader does not
receive the findings as propositions. They receive them as experience.

Jonathan Haidt's research on moral elevation confirms why this
matters: witnessing a story that proves a moral truth produces
stronger attitude change, deeper memory encoding, and higher sharing
behavior than reading an argument for the same truth. The novel is
not a weaker form of the research book. It is a more powerful one —
provided the architecture is built with full intellectual seriousness
before the first word of prose is written.

**Architecture before prose. This principle is as inviolable in the
novel phases as in the non-fiction phases.**

## THE FOUR PHASE INPUTS

Four specific documents from the earlier phases feed directly into
Phase 6 work:

**1. `00_phase1_claim.md` → Controlling Idea**
The Phase 1 master claim in its final revised form is the primary
resource for deriving the novel's controlling idea. The conversion
task: translate the claim from its thick thesis format ("Claim +
because + Reasons + although + Acknowledgment") into Robert McKee's
controlling idea form: "Value is achieved / destroyed / transformed
when condition / cause."

**2. Phase 3 Social Currency list → Highest-Priority Novelization
Targets**
The Phase 3 confirmed Social Currency findings, already verified and
ranked by surprise value to the target reader, are the conversion's
highest-priority targets. The finding ranked highest for surprise
value becomes the eucatastrophe's source material. Do not regenerate
this list from scratch — work from what Phase 3 established.

**3. Phase 3 Citation Verification Log → Accuracy Standard**
The authoritative source for every factual claim that enters the
novel in any form. Accuracy to the underlying research is not relaxed
by the change of genre. The novel may fictionalize surface
particulars — names, places, composite characters — but it does not
distort the verified findings that underlie its argument.

**4. Phase 5 Placeholder Inventory Resolution Record → Category A/B/C
Determination**
The record of which personal testimony scenes were confirmed as
Category A, B, or C determines what the author can draw on in the
Conversion Audit's vantage point step and what must be newly
constructed for the novel form.

## PHASE 6 — NOVEL ARCHITECTURE

### Before You Write a Word

Answer all five questions in writing before any architecture work
begins. These are the architectural anchors of the entire novel and
will be consulted at every point of uncertainty during Phase 7.

**Question 1: What is the controlling idea the novel exists to prove?**
Not the research book's thesis restated. A controlling idea in McKee's
sense: a single sentence expressing a value and its cause, provable
through a chain of fictional events. It must be capable of being
demonstrated through what happens to characters — not stated through
what characters say to each other.

Conversion task: take the Phase 1 thick thesis and translate it. A
research book's claim that "strategic competence without character
formation produces cultural failure, because strategies are adopted
by institutions but formation changes people" becomes, for example,
"the person who changes a community does so not through strategy but
through the quality of their presence, earned through suffering they
did not seek." Apply the same translation to your own Phase 1 thick
thesis.

Test: if the controlling idea cannot generate a conflict — a force
that would argue the opposite — it is not yet a controlling idea.
Keep reducing until it can be argued against.

**Question 2: Who is the protagonist, and what are their want and
need?**
- **Want** = the conscious desire that drives external action;
  the goal the protagonist pursues throughout the plot
- **Need** = what they actually require to grow; the inner wound
  or false belief the story will force them to confront
- These must be in productive tension: the want is the lie the
  character believes will satisfy them; the need is the truth they
  are resisting

The want and need must be direct translations of the research book's
central argument: the protagonist's want is what the book's target
reader currently believes; their need is what the book exists to
change in that reader. The reader's transformation happens by
inhabiting the protagonist's transformation.

**Question 3: Which archetype does the source material most naturally
inhabit?**
Select from the fifteen archetypes in the Craftsman's Manual. This
is the most important single decision in the conversion, because it
determines the emotional arc, the structural beats, the type of
antagonist, the nature of the climax, and the register of the
resolution. Different archetypes deliver the same intellectual content
with fundamentally different physiological impact. A Rebirth Arc
delivers the same truth as a Sacrifice Narrative but targets
oxytocin and elevation differently. A Quest generates different
cortisol and awe patterns than Overcoming the Monster.

You are not choosing a genre. You are choosing a neurological
delivery mechanism.

**Question 4: What is the eucatastrophe?**
Tolkien's term for the sudden joyous turn that "denies universal
final defeat" without denying the reality of suffering — the moment
when the story's central truth becomes experiential rather than
conceptual.

The Phase 3 confirmed Social Currency list is the direct source for
this identification — specifically, the finding ranked highest for
surprise value to the target reader. In the novel, this content must
be delivered not as a finding but as an event: a plot turn, a
revelation, a character choice that enacts the truth rather than
states it.

The eucatastrophe simultaneously fires dopamine (the better-than-
predicted outcome), oxytocin (restoration of bonds or safety), and
endorphin (the release of sustained tension). Identify this moment
before building any architecture. It is the destination. The entire
novel is the road toward it.

**Question 5: What is the novel's opening image and closing image?**
Following Blake Snyder's Save the Cat architecture:
- **Opening image** (at the 1% mark) — the "before" snapshot; the
  world, protagonist, and status quo before the story begins;
  should embody the condition the research book diagnoses
- **Closing image** (at the 99–100% mark) — the "after" snapshot,
  mirroring the opening to show how completely the world has
  changed; should embody the condition the research book argues is
  possible

Write both images as single vivid paragraphs before proceeding to
the Conversion Audit.

### The Conversion Audit

Before any architectural decisions, every element of the source
research book must be mapped to its narrative equivalent. Do not
skip this. Moving directly from the research book's chapter structure
into architecture produces didactic fiction — illustrated lectures
rather than lived experience.

**Step 1: Thesis-to-controlling-idea translation**
Use the McKee lens to convert the Phase 1 thick thesis into a
controlling idea: "Value is [achieved / destroyed / transformed]
when [condition / cause]." The sentence should make the research
book's author say: "Yes, that is what I was arguing — I just argued
it with data rather than drama."

**Step 2: Social Currency audit**
The Phase 3 confirmed Social Currency list is the direct input. For
each confirmed finding, identify which of three narrative mechanisms
will carry it:

- **Option A: Scene revelation** — the finding is dramatized as a
  plot event the protagonist witnesses or experiences; highest
  impact for findings about human behavior or consequence
- **Option B: Character-embodied truth** — the finding is
  incarnated in a character whose way of being demonstrates what
  the research proved; the reader encounters it as a person before
  understanding the person's coherence; highest impact for findings
  about character formation, moral psychology, or relational dynamics
- **Option C: Setting-as-argument** — the finding is built into
  the physical and sensory world; highest impact for findings about
  systems, cultures, or institutions

**Step 3: Chapter-to-act mapping**
Lay the research book's chapters alongside the three-act structure.
Identify which chapters contain Act One material (diagnosis, inciting
condition, problem space), Act Two material (complications,
escalating failure of conventional approaches, the dark night), and
Act Three material (synthesis, resolution, transformed understanding).
Research books often have their most important content in the middle
chapters, while novels require their highest-stakes material near
the end. The conversion frequently requires restructuring this
sequence entirely.

**Step 4: Case studies as plot events**
Every case study, practitioner account, and documented instance is
raw material. Classify each as: (a) a plot event that can be
fictionalized and placed in the novel's timeline, (b) a character's
history or backstory that informs who they are without being narrated
directly, or (c) a story-within-a-story carried through embedded
narrative. The emotional and structural truth of each case study
must be preserved even as surface particulars are changed.

**Step 5: Author's vantage point as narrative consciousness**
Translate from the credentialing move of the research book ("I am
the right person to tell you this because...") to the identification
move of the novel ("This is a person whose inner experience I
recognize as my own"). The Phase 5 Placeholder Inventory resolution
record governs this translation: Category A and B testimony can be
drawn on freely; Category C testimony presents a distinct challenge
requiring the fictionalization protocol below.

For confirmed Category C scenes: extract the emotional and moral
truth and reconstruct it through a fictional scene with different
surface particulars — different names, changed contexts, composite
characters — while preserving the specific quality of experience
that made the original testimony load-bearing. The limits on means
govern this reconstruction: the fictional scene must be emotionally
and morally true to what was actually experienced, not amplified for
dramatic effect or softened to protect anyone's reputation.
Fictionalization is not license to improve on what happened. It is
the craft of translating what happened into a form that a reader
who was not there can inhabit.

**Step 6: Trust sequence arc mapping**
Before the scene map is built, the novel's relational deepening arc
must be mapped separately from its plot arc. Three research frameworks
govern this mapping:

- **Altman and Taylor's Social Penetration Theory** — relationships
  begin at surface pleasantry and public information, progress
  through personal opinions and preferences, into personal fears
  and vulnerabilities, then into core identity. Rate of progression
  is governed by reciprocity. A character who discloses more deeply
  than the relationship has earned creates the discomfort of violated
  intimacy norms. Refusing to advance inward stalls the relationship
  permanently at acquaintanceship regardless of time spent together.
- **Aron's closeness research** — time together is less important
  than depth of mutual disclosure; two characters in genuinely
  escalating vulnerability for an afternoon will feel closer at
  its end than characters who have spent months in surface contact
- **Brown's trust sequence reversal** — vulnerability does not
  follow trust; it precedes it. Trust is built through repeated
  cycles of choosing to be vulnerable before sufficient evidence
  of safety exists, and having that vulnerability honored. The
  character who waits until they feel safe before disclosing
  never becomes close to anyone.

Answer these questions in writing before building the scene map:
Which relationship is the primary relational arc? At what Social
Penetration layer does it begin, and at what layer does it end?
Which specific scene represents each inward movement through
the disclosure layers? Which scene is the vulnerability-before-trust
leap — the moment where one character discloses before they have
sufficient evidence of safety? What is the oxytocin loop moment —
the specific scene where that vulnerability is honored and the
deepening becomes irreversible? Which scene is the rupture, and
which is the repair?

### Architecture Before Prose — The Five Required Documents

Do not begin Phase 7 drafting until all five architecture documents
are complete.

**Step 1: The Archetype Blueprint (`00b_archetype_blueprint.md`)**
Write out the full construction blueprint for the selected archetype,
populated with the specific content of this novel. Do not use a
generic archetype template. Name the specific ordinary world, the
specific call and its source, the specific mentor and what they
carry, the specific ordeal and what it costs, the specific
resurrection and what it proves. Two to four pages. This is the
most important document in the project and will be consulted at
every point of uncertainty during Phase 7.

**Step 2: The Scene Map (`00e_scene_map.md`)**
Plot every scene before writing any of them. For each scene, write
one sentence answering: "What changes in this scene?" Not what
happens — what changes. If nothing changes (in character knowledge,
relationship dynamic, power balance, protagonist's understanding),
the scene should not exist.

Apply the therefore/but test from Trey Parker and Matt Stone: every
scene transition connects through either "therefore" (direct
consequence) or "but" (obstacle to the character's goal). "And then"
is chronological sequence, not causation. Remove every "and then"
and replace it with "therefore" or "but," or cut the scene.

Trust sequence arc scenes are labeled by function: Social Penetration
layer advance / vulnerability leap / oxytocin loop / rupture /
repair. These labels ensure the relational arc is built into the
scene structure rather than assumed to emerge from proximity.

**Step 3: The Emotional Arc Blueprint (in
`00_conversion_architecture.md`)**
Plan the emotional arc scene by scene using Reagan et al.'s six
fundamental arc shapes. Name the target physiological state for
every narrative beat: cortisol engagement, oxytocin investment,
dopamine anticipation, endorphin release, awe activation. Plan
contrast effects deliberately: the moment of warmth that must
precede the devastating reversal, the comedy or tenderness that
must appear before the ordeal.

**Step 4: Character Design (`00c_character_designs.md`)**
Five required elements for every major character:

1. **Controlling belief** — the core conviction about themselves
   or the world, formed from a wound or formative experience, that
   drives all behavior; every significant decision must be traceable
   to this belief
2. **Want and need** — as defined above; secondary characters'
   wants and needs should create genuine thematic complication
3. **Voice** — specific vocabulary, rhythm, sentence length,
   preoccupations, and speech habits; write three lines of dialogue
   for each character before drafting any scenes
4. **Arc** — the specific transformation, if any; every step must
   be causally produced by the story's events, not asserted
5. **Attachment operating system** — the most consistently omitted
   design element in general craft guidance; as load-bearing as
   any of the four above it

The four attachment styles and their scene-level behavioral signatures:

- **Secure** — comfortable with both intimacy and autonomy;
  approaches rather than flees or freezes under threat; functions
  best as secondary characters modeling what the protagonist needs
  to become, or as the partner whose secure presence provides the
  corrective experience enabling transformation
- **Anxious-preoccupied** — hypervigilant to relational threat;
  bid escalation when bids go unanswered; difficulty tolerating
  ambiguity; rejection sensitive dysphoria (intense physiological
  pain in response to perceived rejection at an intensity
  neurotypical people find difficult to credit); reads between lines
  that may or may not contain what they find there
- **Dismissive-avoidant** — learned not to need others; experiences
  emotional bids as intrusions; stonewalling as self-protection
  experienced by the partner as abandonment; minimizes emotional
  expression; genuine puzzlement at why relationships require so
  much maintenance; deep unexamined belief they are not worth
  staying for
- **Disorganized** — approach and avoidance systems activate
  simultaneously; craves and fears intimacy in the same moment;
  moves toward connection and then sabotages it; requires careful
  handling under the limits on means, which prohibit writing
  disorganized attachment without the inner experience that makes
  the behavior coherent from inside the character's own logic

For neurodivergent characters, the five standard design elements are
supplemented with neurological profile design:

- **Autistic characters** — the masking-unmasking dynamic: sustained
  performance of neurotypical social behavior producing connection
  at the cost of being known, versus the terrifying risk of dropping
  the mask. Design: what does the mask look like in performance?
  what does the unmasked self look like? which character is first
  to encounter the unmasked self? where in the arc does the
  unmasking happen? Milton's double empathy framework: autistic
  relational difficulty is located in the mismatch between two
  different but equally valid social operating systems, not in
  the autistic person's deficits alone.
- **ADHD characters** — rejection sensitive dysphoria (RSD) trigger
  map: the intense, physiologically overwhelming pain many adults
  with ADHD report in response to perceived or anticipated
  rejection (⚠️ RSD is a clinical description, not a DSM
  diagnosis, and the often-quoted "99%" is a clinician's estimate,
  not a measured prevalence; verify before citing a figure); design
  the specific RSD triggers before drafting. Executive function failure patterns as relational
  ruptures: the ADHD character forgot the anniversary not because
  they do not care but because working memory failure cannot be
  overridden by caring. Hyperfocus arc: the absorbed attentiveness
  of ADHD relational hyperfocus in early connection is real, not
  performed; when novelty fades and hyperfocus shifts, the contrast
  is stark enough to constitute a plot event.
- **AuDHD characters** — the interaction between the profiles, not
  their simple sum: the autistic drive for routine and the ADHD
  drive for novelty create internal conflict. The masking-exhaustion-
  shutdown cycle is often more severe. Design the specific load
  conditions: what depletes them, what restores them, what the
  external behavioral signature of each state looks like to other
  characters who lack the explanatory framework.

**Step 5: The Setting Bible (`00d_setting_bible.md`)**
For every major location, complete a setting design using the
thematic setting principle. Every location must do at least one of:
externalize the emotional truth of the scenes within it, argue
thematically for the novel's controlling idea through its physical
properties, or create the specific sensory world that activates the
reader's parahippocampal simulation network and places them physically
inside the scene.

For each location: dominant sensory register across all five senses
(not just visual), the thematic argument the space is making, and
the single most important physical detail that will recur as a motif.

The Setting Bible also contains the **synchrony scene plan**: which
synchrony-producing scene type is assigned to each primary
relationship arc, and which location hosts it. The synchrony scene
toolkit includes: shared meals (activating multiple bonding systems
simultaneously), synchronous physical activity (activating mirror
neuron resonance and the cooperative trust response), shared
adversity (the most powerful bonding mechanism — the relationship
bond that years of pleasant interaction cannot produce), communal
singing or rhythmic movement (activating the social endorphin system
that bonds large groups), and family storytelling (activating the
relational continuity mechanism that produces felt security).

## PHASE 7 — NOVEL DRAFT

### What Phase 7 Actually Is

Phase 7 converts the complete Phase 6 architecture documents into
prose. The architecture documents do not recede in authority once
prose begins. Every scene is measured against the scene map. Every
character choice is measured against the character design. Every
relational moment is measured against the trust sequence arc.

The Phase 6 architecture is not a plan that the novel grows beyond —
it is the structure the novel inhabits. Departure from it requires
documented justification and revision of the architecture first.

The Phase 3 Citation Verification Log continues to govern accuracy.
The novel may fictionalize surface particulars but does not distort
the verified findings underlying its argument.

### The Limits on Means Applied to Drafting

The limits on means govern the specific temptation drafting
introduces: using narrative power in excess of what the evidence
supports. A compelling scene can make a finding feel more certain
than it is. A dramatic framing can make an argument's limitations
invisible. Every drafting decision that inflates a claim beyond
what Phase 3 investigation supported is a withdrawal from the
book's long-term authority.

### The Ten Mechanics as Drafting Tools

> See also: `writing-craft:narrative-structure` (the hero's journey and
> story architecture) and `entertainment-comedy-drama-storytelling`'s
> `story-rules-character-structure-and-scene` and
> `story-dialogue-point-of-view-theme-exposition-and-endings`, which
> treat the same craft on its own terms.

**Mechanic One: Setting**
The Setting Bible from Phase 6 contains the full sensory design.
Phase 7 executes those designs with five-sense specificity that
creates full neurological simulation rather than symbolic processing.

The synchrony scene toolkit (from Phase 6's Setting Bible) is
executed here at full dramatic weight:
- Shared meals — activate physical co-presence, oxytocin-triggering
  eye contact, synchronized consumption, and ritualized reciprocity
- Synchronous physical activity — activates mirror neuron resonance
  and cooperative trust; two characters building something together
  are doing relational work that talking about their relationship
  cannot do
- Shared adversity — the most powerful bonding mechanism; the
  relationship bond that years of pleasant interaction cannot produce
- Communal worship, singing, or rhythmic movement — activates the
  social endorphin system; the neurological explanation for why
  communal worship across every human culture has included sung
  corporate response
- Family storytelling — activates the bonding mechanism that
  produces felt security through relational continuity

**Mechanic Two: Theme**
The controlling idea must be discoverable from what happens to the
characters — not from what they say to each other. When a character
voices the theme directly in dialogue, the novel has failed the
conversion. The theme should be felt before it is understood, and
understood before it is named. If it is named at all, name it only
after it has been demonstrated through irreversible plot consequence.

**Mechanic Three: Plot Architecture**
Blake Snyder's fifteen-beat Save the Cat structure was mapped onto
the research book's logical sequence in Phase 6. Draft in scene map
order throughout: the Setup embodies the research book's diagnosis;
the Catalyst delivers the inciting condition; the Break into Two
commits the protagonist to the book's central question; the All Is
Lost delivers the moment when every conventional approach has failed
and the book's most counterintuitive argument becomes the only
remaining option; the Break into Three delivers the synthesis; the
Finale demonstrates that synthesis in action.

**Mechanic Four: Character Development**
The protagonist was designed with a wound that makes the research
book's central question personal and unavoidable. Phase 7 ensures
findings are things the protagonist discovers through lived
experience, not through reading or being told. Every major finding
must be earned by the protagonist before the reader receives it.

The antagonist embodies the position the research book most seriously
contends with, drawn from Phase 2's steelmanned opposing arguments.
The antagonist is not wrong in every way; is wrong about the one
thing that matters most; is wrong for reasons the reader can
understand.

**Mechanic Five: Conflict**
All five conflict types should appear in a full-length conversion:
person-versus-person (the antagonist who embodies the opposing
position), person-versus-self (the protagonist's internal resistance
to the book's central truth), person-versus-society (the
institutional forces protecting the status quo), person-versus-nature
(environmental stakes appropriate to the setting), and if the book's
argument touches on anything transcendent, person-versus-fate-or-God
(the protagonist's honest wrestling with what the book ultimately
asks of them).

**Mechanic Six: Point of View**
Close third-person limited POV performs best for most conversions —
emotional proximity of first person without the credibility limitation
of a narrator who knows too much too early. The protagonist is the
focalizing consciousness, because the reader's identification arc
must track the protagonist's discovery arc.

**Mechanic Seven: Dialogue**
The research book's arguments must never appear as dialogue. The
most common failure mode in research-to-novel conversion: a character
delivers a speech that sounds like a book chapter, and the fiction
collapses into illustrated lecture. The research book's central
claims become subtext — the reality characters navigate without
being able to name.

Dialogue must accomplish at least two of McKee's three functions
simultaneously: reveal character, advance plot, or deepen conflict.

Relational conflict dialogue deploys Gottman's Four Horsemen with
precision:
- **Criticism** — attacks the person's character rather than a
  specific behavior; sounds like cold judgment, not heat
- **Contempt** — the single strongest predictor of relationship
  failure; communicates fundamental disrespect; cold and dismissive,
  not explosive; "I see what you are, and what you are is beneath
  me"
- **Defensiveness** — responds to feedback with counter-attack or
  victim posturing; every line redirects away from the matter at
  hand; produces conversation that spirals rather than progresses
- **Stonewalling** — withdrawal; monosyllabic or absent; the
  cortisol-flooded brain's shutdown when physiological arousal
  exceeds the threshold for prefrontal cortex function; write from
  the other character's experience — what it feels like to speak
  into a silence that used to speak back

Each horseman has a specific antidote governing the novel's repair
scenes: gentle startup (criticism), culture of appreciation
(contempt), taking responsibility (defensiveness), physiological
self-soothing with honest naming of flooding and request for time
(stonewalling — the twenty-minute minimum is not arbitrary).

The Gottman 5:1 ratio applies to the novel's overall relational arc:
the relational bank account that allows a relationship to survive
conflict and repair is built outside of conflict through accumulated
warm interactions. A novel whose relational arc consists primarily
of conflict is a novel whose relationship the reader will not believe.

**Mechanic Eight: Pacing**
The most common pacing error in novel conversion: treating every
finding as equally Scene-worthy. Reserve full scene treatment for
genuine turning points. Compress supporting material to summary,
dialogue, or setting detail. At the sentence level: short sentences
for urgency, shock, or intense action; long cadenced sentences for
contemplation, grief, or the slow dawning of understanding.

**Mechanic Nine: Literary Devices**
- **Foreshadowing** — every major finding should be planted as a
  seed early in the novel that will mean something different the
  second time the reader encounters it
- **Chekhov's Gun** — every piece of research-derived material
  introduced creates a promise of significance; do not include a
  finding that does not ultimately matter to the plot
- **Dramatic irony** — the primary device for carrying the research
  book's argument without stating it; the reader sees what the
  characters cannot yet see
- **Motif** — the recurring image, phrase, or symbol accumulating
  meaning across the novel; design it to carry the research book's
  most important thread; gather all the novel's evidence onto itself
  by the final page

**Mechanic Ten: Emotional Arc**
The emotional arc planned in Phase 6 is executed here at the scene
level, maintaining the contrast effects designed deliberately. The
cortisol peak of the crisis lands harder after the oxytocin warmth
of the preceding connection scene. The dopamine release of the
eucatastrophe fires more completely after the sustained despair of
the dark night. These are not aesthetic choices. They are the
engineering of the reader's physiological experience.

### The Draft Sequence

Draft in this order without exception. The sequence is governed by
the logical dependencies of the material.

**Step 1:** Phase 6 gate confirmation — all five architecture
documents must be complete; if any Phase 6 gate condition is
unchecked, return to Phase 6

**Step 2:** Opening and closing images — both before writing
anything in between; the architectural anchors of the entire
emotional arc

**Step 3:** The eucatastrophe scene — write the climactic scene
before the scenes leading to it; the destination must be written
before the road; writing it first also ensures that the emotional
arc of preceding scenes is calibrated correctly

**Step 4:** Act One scenes, in sequence — the ordinary world must
be established with sufficient warmth and specificity that the reader
invests in it before the disruption begins; the ordinary world is
not filler — it is the emotional foundation that gives every
subsequent loss its weight

**Step 5:** Act Two scenes, in sequence — the escalation of conflict
must build causally; non-linear drafting in Act Two produces scenes
that feel disconnected because the characters' psychological states
do not accumulate as they should

**Step 6:** Act Three scenes, in sequence — the emotional payoff of
each beat depends on the accumulated weight of all previous beats

**Step 7:** Scene openings, revised — after drafting the full novel,
return to the opening of each scene; scene openings are almost always
wrong on first draft because you do not yet know precisely what the
scene proves

**Step 8:** The first chapter, revised last — must earn, set up,
and promise everything the novel delivers; can only be written
accurately against a completed manuscript; must establish all five
sensory registers of the dominant setting, introduce the protagonist
in their controlling belief and want, plant the need without naming
it, and create the emotional baseline all subsequent contrast effects
will measure against

**Step 9:** Revision passes, in sequence with single focus per pass

### Quality Standards

**The Conversion Integrity Test** — applied to every scene:
- Could this scene exist in a novel not based on the source research
  book? If yes, confirm it is earning its place through narrative
  function, not merely thematic relevance
- Does this scene state the research book's findings or prove them
  through action? More than four consecutive lines of dialogue that
  could appear without significant alteration in the source book
  means the scene has collapsed into illustrated lecture
- Is the research book's intellectual architecture visible through
  the novel's surface? If a knowledgeable reader would immediately
  recognize the chapter-by-chapter correspondence, the conversion
  has not gone deep enough

**The Archetype Integrity Test:**
- Is every major structural beat of the selected archetype present?
  Three most commonly missing beats: Refusal of the Call, Mentor's
  withdrawal, and the full Resurrection beat
- Does the protagonist make choices rather than have things happen
  to them? Research books describe things that happen to people;
  every major plot turn must be produced by a choice the protagonist
  makes, even under constraint
- Is the protagonist genuinely different at the end from the
  beginning, and is that difference the direct and earned emotional
  result of what the story put them through?

**The Relational Arc Test:**
- Does the primary relational arc follow the trust sequence mapped
  in Phase 6 Conversion Audit Step 6?
- Does the vulnerability-before-trust leap exist, with genuine
  stakes, and is the response to the vulnerability the turning
  point the arc requires?
- Is at least one rupture and repair cycle present? A relational
  arc without rupture and repair is a relationship that has not
  been tested and cannot carry the weight of the novel's central
  claim
- Does the relational bank account exist? Substantially more
  positive interaction scenes than conflict scenes

**The Neurodivergent Authenticity Test** — applied to every scene
with a neurodivergent character:
- Does the neurodivergent character's behavior follow from their
  designed neurological profile or from neurotypical relational
  defaults?
- For autistic characters: is the masking dynamic present in every
  neurotypical-majority scene, even as background cost?
- For ADHD characters: when executive function fails, is it written
  from inside the character's experience of the gap between knowing
  and doing? Is it written as not resolvable by motivation alone?
- Is the double empathy principle honored in cross-neurotype
  conflict scenes? Both sides of the communication failure present?

**The Craft Standard Checklist** — before finalizing any chapter:
- Does the chapter opening arrest within the first sentence?
- Is the destination named within the first 500 words?
- Does every scene connect to the next through "therefore" or "but"?
- Is the chapter's primary emotional target different from the
  preceding chapter's?
- Is there at least one moment in the chapter at which the attentive
  reader would feel awe — not at the craft but at the reality the
  craft is pointing toward?
- Has the chapter cost the protagonist something real?
- Does the chapter's final sentence redirect toward the next chapter
  with sufficient forward momentum?

### Production Awareness

Three Phase 7 drafting decisions have direct downstream consequences
for Phase 14 KDP submission:

1. Use heading styles consistently throughout — Heading 1 for
   chapter titles, Heading 2 for section breaks — rather than
   manually applying formatting; heading styles generate the
   navigational table of contents in the Kindle EPUB
2. Do not hard-code page numbers anywhere in the manuscript; page
   numbers are only finalized after the interior PDF is generated
   at the correct trim size in Phase 14
3. If the novel includes images, source or create them at a minimum
   of 300 DPI from the start

## THE STANDARD OF CHARTRES

The medieval stonemasons who carved the highest vaults of Chartres
Cathedral carved them perfectly, knowing no human eye would ever see
the work up close. The converted novel holds this same standard.

The research book's intellectual architecture — the precise
correspondence between its argument and the novel's plot, the careful
translation of each finding into a scene designed to deliver it at
the exact moment of maximum emotional readiness — will never be
consciously visible to the reader. They will not know that the
eucatastrophe was positioned where it is because the cortisol of
the preceding crisis maximizes the dopamine release of the turn.
They will only know that something happened to them on that page
that they cannot stop thinking about, and that they need to tell
someone.

That is the goal. That is the standard. That is entirely achievable
by any writer who knows the research well enough to love it, who
knows the craft well enough to hide it, and who trusts that a truth
told beautifully is more enduring than a truth argued brilliantly.

## OPERATING PRINCIPLES FOR THIS SKILL

When a user invokes this skill, determine which phase they are
working on (Phase 6 architecture or Phase 7 drafting) and which
specific document or step they need.

1. Phase 6 does not begin without the Phase 5 Publication Readiness
   Declaration signed and every Phase 5 exit condition met
2. Phase 7 does not begin without all five Phase 6 architecture
   documents complete and the Phase 6 quality gate fully cleared
3. Hold the Phase 6 architecture documents as the governing standard
   throughout Phase 7; the architecture is not abandoned when prose
   begins — it is the structure the prose inhabits
4. The Phase 3 Citation Verification Log continues to govern
   accuracy even in the novel; the change of genre does not relax
   the accuracy standard
5. The limits on means prohibit using narrative power in excess of
   what the evidence supports; a compelling scene is not evidence;
   emotional momentum does not resolve unresolved concessions
6. Apply the craft standard throughout: work executed with full
   seriousness whether or not any reader will consciously notice it
7. Hold the C.S. Lewis principle as the conversion's governing
   purpose: the novel enables truths to steal past the watchful
   dragons of cultural defensiveness and intellectual fatigue;
   it serves this purpose only if the architecture is built with
   full intellectual seriousness and the prose is executed at the
   level of craft the truths deserve
