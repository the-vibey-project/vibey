# Pipeline Book Production
## Content Engineering Pipeline — Phases 4 and 5

This skill governs Phases 4 and 5 of the Content Engineering
Pipeline. Phase 4 converts the
verified argument inventory from Phase 3 into a complete manuscript
draft. Phase 5 iterates that draft through six systematic passes
until it reaches the Publication Readiness Declaration.

---

## YOUR GOVERNING POSTULATES

These operate as axioms across all work in this pipeline. They are
the postulates the author stated before Phase 1 — see *Before Phase 1
— State Your Governing Postulates* in `pipeline-thesis-development`,
which also carries a worked example. The rules this skill applies
under the Third Postulate are the method's **limits on means**: they
stand on their own, whatever words the author's own Third Postulate
uses, and that postulate may tighten them but never loosen them.

## PHASE 4 — ASSEMBLY

### What Phase 4 Actually Is

Phase 4 is the first phase that produces prose. Every phase before
it was preparation. All of that work was necessary, and none of it
was the book. Phase 4 is the book.

The governing principle comes from the Craftsman's Manual: the
mechanics of story exist not to decorate the argument but to deliver
it neurologically. A research finding stated as a proposition
activates the reader's semantic processing. The same finding
delivered through a scene with stakes, tension, and resolution
activates the reader's simulation network. The reader who encounters
a finding as an argument knows it. The reader who encounters the
same finding as a chapter climax, earned through everything that
preceded it, feels it.

**The architecture-before-prose principle applies in full.**
Architecture derivation must be complete in writing before any
prose begins. This is as inviolable here as in every preceding phase.

### What Phase 4 Inherits

Phase 4 inherits three things from earlier phases:
- **From Phase 1:** Working claim in thick thesis format, anchor
  sentence, target reader portrait, Social Currency candidate list
- **From Phase 2:** Conversation positioning statement, gap analysis
  for adjacent works, definitional framework for key terms
- **From Phase 3:** Fully verified argument inventory, confirmed
  Social Currency list, Objections Resolution Matrix, theological
  integration record, Placeholder Inventory of every personal
  testimony slot requiring author confirmation

Nothing enters the manuscript that does not have a verified source
in the Phase 3 investigation record.

### The Limits on Means Applied to Assembly

The limits on means prohibit using narrative power in excess of what
the evidence supports. A compelling scene can make a finding feel
more certain than it is. A dramatic framing can make an argument's
limitations invisible. The emotional momentum of a well-built chapter
can carry the reader past concessions that an honest accounting would
require. Every assembly decision that inflates a claim beyond what
Phase 3 investigation supported is a withdrawal from the book's
authority, paid later when a serious reader finds the gap.

The Sayers Standard: a book arguing for a way of living must itself
embody that way of living in its execution. Work executed with care
whether or not any human eye notices the details, because execution
is witness.

### Step One: Architecture Derivation

Architecture derivation is the first act of Phase 4 and must be
complete in writing before any prose begins. The Phase 3 argument
inventory is organized into the logical sequence of a book. The
chapter map that results is not a table of contents — it is a proof
structure. The test: each chapter makes the next one necessary.

**Five operations in sequence:**

**Operation 1: Argument sequencing.** Lay out every component
argument and ask which must be established before any others can
proceed. Then ask which argument, given the first, becomes the next
unavoidable question. Continue until every argument has a position.
Test: if you remove this chapter, does the next chapter lose
something essential? If no, the sequence is wrong.

**Operation 2: Scalar audit.** Identify the level at which each
chapter operates — individual psychological, individual strategic,
individual character, community and relational, institutional,
civilizational. A well-architected book moves through scales
deliberately, earning the right to address civilizational questions
by first demonstrating the argument at the individual level where
the reader can verify it against their own experience.

**Operation 3: Social Currency positioning.** From the confirmed
Social Currency list in Phase 3, assign each finding to its chapter
and identify whether it belongs at the opening (highest surprise
value), in the body (highest evidential weight), or at the
theological integration (highest convergence significance). No
chapter is without a confirmed Social Currency finding. If any
chapter has none, it either needs to be dissolved into adjacent
chapters or Phase 3 needs to be revisited.

**Operation 4: Cross-chapter connection map.** Before writing,
identify every structural connection between chapters — the places
where what Chapter Three proves makes Chapter Six's argument
possible. These connections are what the conclusion will synthesize;
they must be mapped now so the chapter prose can plant seeds that
the conclusion will harvest.

**Operation 5: Testimony slot assignment.** From the Placeholder
Inventory in Phase 3, assign every personal testimony slot to a
specific location in a specific chapter. All Category C items remain
as [PLACEHOLDER] markers in the manuscript until the author supplies
the confirmed scene.

### Step Two: Voice Calibration

Voice calibration establishes the specific parameters of the author's
register before the first word of prose is written. Four questions
answered in writing before drafting begins:

1. **Formality level** — tied to the target reader portrait from
   Phase 1; the register serving a theologically trained pastor
   differs from the register serving a secular professional
2. **Research-to-narrative ratio** — baseline for this series is
   approximately 80:20, shifting by chapter subject
3. **Framework explicitness gradient** — does the book begin
   with its foundational framework established, or earn its way
   toward the most explicit content? (The "known to unknown"
   strategy: begin with what the audience already recognizes as
   true and build toward the revelation they are not yet prepared
   for — moving from shared common ground toward the book's most
   challenging claim)
4. **Anchor phrase context** — the context in which the anchor
   sentence appears in the Introduction must be fully designed;
   the sentence earns its authority from everything that surrounds
   it and cannot be embedded afterward without losing that authority

### Step Three: The Drafting Sequence

Draft in this order without exception:

1. **Chapter bodies first, in sequence** — the forward-pointing
   transition at the end of each chapter must point toward a chapter
   that has already been architected; writing out of sequence
   produces transitions that are logically incoherent even when
   stylistically smooth
2. **Chapter openings second** — after the chapter bodies are
   complete; the opening's job is to arrest and name the destination,
   which can only be done accurately once the destination is
   established
3. **Conclusion third** — after all chapter bodies are complete;
   the conclusion's synthesis must name cross-chapter connections
   that exist in the actual manuscript
4. **Introduction last** — must accurately describe a book that
   exists; the chapter guide must correspond to what each chapter
   actually does, not what was planned
5. **Back matter last** — Five Things appendix, Discussion Guide,
   bibliography

### Step Four: The Chapter Drafting Protocol

Every chapter is drafted through six steps in sequence:

**Protocol Step One: The Destination Paragraph**
Before any drafting begins, write one paragraph identifying the most
surprising or theologically potent finding the chapter is building
toward. This is the destination named in advance. A chapter whose
body does not visibly travel toward the destination paragraph has
lost its structural logic.

**Protocol Step Two: The Opening**
The single most consequential passage in the chapter. Three types:

- **Case study opening** — a real-world event with named parties,
  a documented outcome, and a one-sentence pivot naming why this
  case matters beyond its own domain; must include specific
  timeframe, concrete consequence, and the connecting pivot
- **Personal narrative scene** — a specific moment from the
  author's professional or personal life; specific context, action,
  and consequence; written in scene, not summary; past tense; author
  inside the moment
- **Research cold-stop** — a peer-reviewed finding so
  counterintuitive the reader must stop; requires study name or
  research team, sample size or scope, finding in one sentence, and
  immediate pivot to implication; uses no hedging language

Opening quality standard — six questions:
- Is the reader arrested within the first sentence?
- Is it a scene or finding rather than a description of what the
  chapter covers?
- Is the destination named within 500 words?
- Is there a curiosity gap the chapter must close?
- Does it connect to the master claim?
- Is the register consistent with the calibrated voice?

**Protocol Step Three: The Body Sections**
Each body section advances one component argument from Phase 3.
The body section is not a literature review — it is an argument
that happens to be grounded in evidence. Every section closes with
a forward-pointing transition (one sentence, not a paragraph) that
redirects toward what is coming.

**Protocol Step Four: Personal Testimony Placement**
Testimony comes after the finding it supports, not before it. The
finding earns credibility through evidence; testimony converts
verified knowledge into felt experience. Written in scene, not
summary. Every Category C slot is marked [PLACEHOLDER] in the full
Placeholder Inventory format.

**Protocol Step Five: Worldview Integration**
For works with a worldview or values dimension: where secular research
independently arrives at territory your foundational framework or
tradition already mapped, name that convergence explicitly. The formula
"[Researcher] did not set out to confirm [X]. They arrived there
anyway" appears only where the independence is confirmed and the
convergence is genuine. Where no genuine convergence exists, do not
force one — a manufactured integration damages credibility more than
its absence.

**Protocol Step Six: The Chapter Conclusion**
Three things in order:
1. Name what was just proven in one sentence without restating the
   evidence
2. Name what the chapter's finding costs — the honest acknowledgment
   of what the argument requires; a conclusion with no cost declares
   the argument easier than it is
3. Hand off to the next chapter — name what the next chapter makes
   possible and why the current chapter makes it necessary

### The Seven Required Chapter Elements

These are not stylistic preferences. They are architectural
requirements that function as load-bearing components. A chapter
missing any one has a structural failure.

1. **High-stakes opening** within the first 500 words — executed as
   one of the three types
2. **Destination named** before the journey begins — within the
   first 500 words
3. **Forward-pointing transitions** between every section — one
   sentence, not a paragraph, pointing forward not summarizing back
4. **Personal testimony** placed at argument-required moments; every
   Category C slot marked [PLACEHOLDER] in full format
5. **Worldview integration** of secular research — for works with a
   values or philosophical dimension, at least one explicit naming of
   where secular evidence converges with the book's foundational
   framework; omit if the book does not have this dimension
6. **Anchor phrase** in correct argumentative context — once per
   chapter at most, only where the chapter's evidence has earned it,
   never as a ritual close
7. **Chapter-to-chapter transition** in the final paragraph — a
   logical handoff naming what the next chapter does and why the
   current chapter makes it necessary

### The Introduction Architecture

The introduction is drafted last among the prose. Six required
sections in order:

1. **The warning** — two to four paragraphs naming what the book
   is not; closes with an invitation
2. **The origin narrative** — the specific sequence of events from
   the author's life that made this book necessary
3. **The author's governing axioms** — the postulates stated before
   Phase 1; the principles that governed
   how the research was conducted and the argument was built; stated
   explicitly so the reader understands the book's operating
   constraints
4. **The author's vantage point** — why this author's specific
   combination of experience and limitation produces an analytical
   angle full insiders and full outsiders both lack
5. **The Social Currency hook** — three to five findings presented
   in discovery register ("I did not expect to find this"), selected
   from the five highest across the full manuscript; each finding
   counterintuitive, empirically confirmed, concisely stateable in
   two to three sentences, immediately implicative
6. **The chapter guide** — one paragraph per chapter opening with
   a specific concrete provocation, naming the most surprising
   finding, closing with why this chapter matters for the master
   claim; after the guide: the Hook, stating the book's thesis at
   full force and closing with the anchor sentence

### The Social Currency Deployment System

Every confirmed Social Currency finding occupies all five tiers:

- **Tier One:** Five Things appendix — all five at full force, one
  paragraph each, in factual register, legible as a phone screenshot
  without surrounding context
- **Tier Two:** Introduction Social Currency hook — all five in
  condensed discovery register
- **Tier Three:** Chapter opening — findings with highest surprise
  value named as the destination within the first 500 words
- **Tier Four:** Chapter body — full evidential development with
  evidence, warrant architecture, objection response, and
  theological integration
- **Tier Five:** Conclusion synthesis — cross-chapter connections
  referencing each finding as confirmation

The over-deployment rule: any finding appearing in more than three
locations at significant argumentative weight has been over-deployed
and must be concentrated. The Five Things appendix and Introduction
hook do not count toward this limit.

### The Self-Application Audit

A final pass through the complete manuscript asking: is this book
practicing what it preaches?

- **Virality test** — does the book deploy the viral mechanics it
  describes?
- **Awe test** — does every chapter contain at least one genuine
  awe moment — not announcing it, but producing it?
- **Honesty test** — are the strongest objections engaged with
  genuine force, not strawman versions?
- **Formation test** — does the book's structure demand something
  of the reader, or merely deliver content to them?
- **Cost test** — does the book cost the author something? Are
  there chapters where the author disclosed a failure or named
  what their history cost them?

### The Phase 4 Quality Gate

- [ ] Architecture derivation complete — chapter map, scalar audit,
      Social Currency assignment, cross-chapter connection map,
      testimony slot assignment
- [ ] Voice calibration complete — formality level, research-to-
      narrative ratio, theological explicitness gradient, anchor
      phrase context
- [ ] All chapter bodies drafted through the six-step protocol
- [ ] All chapter openings revised after body was complete; pass
      all six opening quality standards
- [ ] Every chapter contains all seven required elements
- [ ] All Category C testimony slots marked [PLACEHOLDER] in full
      Placeholder Inventory format; none fabricated or approximated
- [ ] Conclusion drafted after reading the complete manuscript;
      all four required components present
- [ ] Introduction drafted last; accurately describes the book
      that exists
- [ ] Five Things appendix complete, five genuinely distinct
      findings, legible as standalone phone screenshot
- [ ] Discussion Guide complete with accurate session overviews
- [ ] Self-application audit run; every failed test produced a
      specific revision task
- [ ] Consistency audit run; every identified inconsistency resolved
- [ ] Manuscript complete except confirmed [PLACEHOLDER] items

**Output:** Complete set of files in `/[book-slug]/` including
`00_phase4_architecture.md` (source of truth for all assembly
decisions), all chapter files, introduction, conclusion, Five Things
appendix, Discussion Guide, and bibliography shell.

## PHASE 5 — ITERATION (REVISION)

### What Phase 5 Actually Is

Phase 5 is not a single editorial pass. Attempting to simultaneously
evaluate structural integrity, argument quality, voice consistency,
Social Currency positioning, citation accuracy, and personal
testimony authenticity while catching typographical errors produces
a manuscript that has been read carefully once rather than evaluated
systematically six times.

Phase 5 is a sequence of **six distinct passes**, each with a single
primary focus, a specific reference document, and a specific exit
condition. The passes execute in a fixed order derived from logical
dependency:

1. Structural issues, if unresolved, make every subsequent pass do
   work that must be undone when structure is eventually fixed
2. Argument quality depends on structure being sound
3. Consistency depends on the argument being complete
4. Voice depends on consistency being established
5. Social Currency positioning depends on voice being calibrated
6. Placeholder resolution comes last, into a manuscript that has
   resolved every other category of issue

### What Phase 5 Does Not Do

- **Does not rethink the Phase 1 claim** — if the manuscript reveals
  the master claim is fundamentally wrong, return to Phase 1
- **Does not discover new component arguments or conduct new
  research** — if a gap requires Phase 3-level investigation,
  document it, return to Phase 3, then reintroduce Phase 5
- **Does not rewrite chapters from scratch** — fundamental chapter
  failures are Phase 4 failures; return to Phase 4's architecture
  derivation
- **Does not supply personal testimony** — the author supplies
  confirmed scenes; Phase 5 inserts them into a prepared manuscript

### The Limits on Means Applied to Phase 5

The primary application: the temptation to resolve a structural or
argumentative weakness through prose polish rather than through
structural or argumentative correction.

When a theological integration is forced rather than genuine, the
temptation is to write it more elegantly rather than find the genuine
convergence or remove the claim. When an objection has not been
adequately answered, the temptation is to make the response more
confident rather than more honest. When a finding has been over-
claimed, the temptation is to add intensifying language rather than
qualify the claim to match the evidence.

The limits on means prohibit all of these: a better sentence is not
a better argument.

### Pass One: Structural Integrity

**Reference:** Phase 4 architecture document — chapter map with
one-sentence arguments, scalar audit, cross-chapter connection map,
seven required chapter elements.

**Question:** Does this manuscript do what it was designed to do?

Five questions applied to the complete manuscript:

1. Does each chapter's completed body serve the one-sentence
   argument recorded in the Phase 4 architecture document?
2. Does each chapter make the next chapter necessary? (Logical
   dependency, not thematic connection)
3. Does the conclusion's synthesis section name cross-chapter
   connections that are actually present in the manuscript?
4. Does the introduction's chapter guide accurately describe what
   each chapter does?
5. Are all seven required chapter elements present in every chapter?

**Exit condition:** Every chapter's body serves its one-sentence
architectural argument; every chapter-ending transition demonstrates
genuine logical dependency; the conclusion synthesis reflects the
manuscript that exists; the introduction guide accurately describes
the completed chapters; all seven required elements present
in every chapter.

### Pass Two: Argument Quality

**Reference:** Phase 3 argument inventory — evidence inventory, best
objection, response architecture, warrant architecture, theological
integration record.

**Question:** Does this argument earn its conclusion through the
logic and evidence Phase 3 established it could earn?

Seven specific evaluations per major argument:

1. **Evidence characterization** — does the manuscript characterize
   findings with the precision the evidence supports, not the
   precision that sounds best?
2. **Best objection representation** — is the steelmanned version
   from Phase 3 engaged, not a weaker substitute?
3. **Residual force acknowledgment** — is the honest acknowledgment
   of what each response does not resolve present in the manuscript?
4. **Warrant adequacy** — are the warrant statements present where
   Phase 3 identified they must be stated, absent where they don't
   need to be?
5. **Theological integration standard** — does the double-
   independence condition hold for every convergence claim?
6. **Master claim accuracy** — does the manuscript state the claim
   in its Phase 3-revised form, not its Phase 1 original form?
7. **BCW comprehensive standard** — specific, falsifiable claim
   supported by sufficient, accurate, representative, authoritative
   evidence, with explicit engagement of the strongest opposing
   arguments, characterized precisely, with every required warrant
   stated

**Exit condition:** Every major argument in every chapter evaluated
against all seven criteria; every identified failure resolved.

### Pass Three: Consistency

Ten specific standards applied across the full manuscript in
sequence:

1. **Anchor phrase count** — no more than once per chapter, no more
   than once per twenty pages across consecutive chapters, every
   appearance in a structurally distinct argumentative context
2. **Primary study deployment** — any study foundational to multiple
   chapters is deployed at full dramatic weight in exactly one
   chapter; referenced briefly in all others
3. **Key finding over-telegraphing** — findings used as cold-stop
   openings are not previewed in preceding chapters with enough
   specificity to dilute impact
4. **Governing axiom consistency** — every reference to the
   author's stated governing principles uses the same numbered and
   named form established in the introduction
5. **Temporal reference check** — every scene-level temporal
   reference anchored to a specific year or time range (not "last
   year" or "recently")
6. **Formatting tag check** — all callout and pullquote formatting
   uses [CALLOUT] throughout; no [PULLQUOTE] tags
7. **Citation format check** — every citation consistent with the
   Phase 3 Citation Verification Log
8. **Chapter opening check** — every chapter opens with case study,
   personal narrative scene, or research cold-stop; not a
   description of what the chapter will cover
9. **Repetition check** — any finding at significant argumentative
   weight more than three times across the full manuscript has been
   over-deployed
10. **Re-introduction check** — if the introduction established the
    author's biographical arc, no subsequent chapter re-introduces
    that arc from scratch

**Exit condition:** Every item in all ten standards evaluated across
the full manuscript; every identified inconsistency corrected.
Consistency is not a matter of degree — either the standard is met
or it is not.

### Pass Four: Voice and Self-Application

**Focus:** Does the manuscript sound like its author, and does it
practice what it argues?

Five voice tests derived from the voice calibration document:

1. **Register consistency** — no departures into devotional
   softness, academic hedging, or inspirational vagueness; short
   declarative sentences for emphasis, longer for evidence
   accumulation; no hedging language; theological confidence without
   triumphalism; data cited precisely with the study named
2. **Testimony register** — every personal testimony written as
   scene, not summary; any passage writable by someone who had
   read widely about the author without having the specific
   experience is summary masquerading as scene and must be revised
3. **Proportionality** — personal testimony occupies no more than
   15% of any chapter's word count
4. **Disclosure quality** — testimony that discloses failure and
   names the gap between knowing and doing is more valuable than
   testimony demonstrating the author's competence
5. **Self-application** — the five tests from Phase 4's audit
   (virality, awe, honesty, formation, cost) re-applied to the
   revised manuscript

**Exit condition:** Every prose passage matches calibrated voice;
every personal testimony written as genuine scene or marked as
[PLACEHOLDER]; no chapter exceeds 15% testimony threshold; all five
self-application tests pass.

### Pass Five: Social Currency Optimization

**Focus:** Is the manuscript's highest-value content positioned for
maximum reach?

The pass begins by re-ranking the confirmed Social Currency findings
against the completed manuscript. Phase 3 ranked by surprise value
at the terrain-mapping stage; the completed chapters may have changed
the rankings. The five highest-ranked findings across the full
manuscript become the Five Things appendix and introduction hook
content regardless of which five were identified in Phase 3.

Five specific evaluations:

1. **Deployment hierarchy audit** — every confirmed finding
   correctly positioned across all five tiers
2. **Five Things appendix standard** — (a) five book-specific
   findings, not repurposed from prior books; (b) two to four
   sentences per finding in factual register; (c) legible as a
   phone screenshot
3. **Anchor sentence evaluation** — appears only in the
   introduction's closing section; context earns it through
   cumulative argumentative authority
4. **Chapter titles audit** — titles make claims or create
   curiosity gaps, not merely name categories ("The Formation
   Gap: Why Strategy Fails Without Character" vs. "Leadership
   and Character")

**Exit condition:** Five confirmed Social Currency findings are the
book's actual five highest as identified through full-manuscript re-
ranking; correctly deployed across all five tiers; Five Things
appendix complete and legible as standalone screenshot; anchor
sentence only in its earned location; all chapter titles pass the
curiosity gap standard.

### Pass Six: Placeholder Resolution and Final Preparation

**Focus:** Every [PLACEHOLDER] resolved before the manuscript is
publication-ready.

Begins with the **Placeholder Handoff Document** — a clean inventory
of every [PLACEHOLDER] in the manuscript: chapter and section
location, argument supported, scene needed, constraints, and current
placeholder text. Given to the author in advance to prepare confirmed
scenes.

Three legitimate resolutions for each placeholder:

1. **Insertion of confirmed testimony** — author supplies the
   specific scene, confirmed as substantially accurate, written in
   standard scene format (specific context, specific action,
   specific consequence) in the author's voice; verified to serve
   the argument it was designated to support
2. **Conversion to Category B testimony** — if the author cannot
   confirm a specific verifiable scene but can speak to the general
   professional pattern; honest Category B is always preferable
   to an unconfirmed Category C
3. **Removal** — if neither scene nor general observation is
   available AND the empirical evidence is sufficient on its own;
   surrounding prose adjusted to maintain argument logic

**No fourth resolution exists.** A placeholder cannot be left
unresolved, approximated, or written from imagination. A scene that
is not substantially accurate is not testimony — it is fiction
attributed to a real person, a direct violation of the limits on
means.

After all placeholders are resolved, Pass Six completes:
- Bibliography compiled from Phase 3 Citation Verification Log;
  every [VERIFIED] source cited in the manuscript appears; every
  bibliography entry is cited in the manuscript
- Discussion Guide reviewed for accuracy against the completed
  manuscript

**Exit condition:** Every placeholder resolved by one of the three
legitimate resolutions; every citation in the manuscript appears in
the bibliography with a [VERIFIED] record; no bibliography entries
uncited in the manuscript; Discussion Guide accurately describes
completed chapters; full manuscript read cover to cover after all
six passes with no new issues identified.

### The Iteration Exit Condition

Phase 5 is complete — and the book is publication-ready — when:

- All six passes are complete, each with its specific exit condition
  met in full
- Phase 5 log is complete with every judgment-based revision
  decision recorded and every earlier-phase escalation documented
- Every placeholder resolved by one of three legitimate resolutions;
  no [PLACEHOLDER] text remains in the manuscript
- Every citation in the manuscript appears in the bibliography with
  a [VERIFIED] record
- Manuscript read cover to cover at least once after all six passes
  in a single reading session focused on experience, not evaluation
- Five Things appendix legible as standalone phone screenshot;
  contains the book's actual five highest Social Currency findings
- Discussion Guide accurate, demanding, formation-oriented, with
  action steps more prominent than discussion questions
- Author reviewed and approved every judgment-based revision
  decision; every placeholder resolution confirmed by the author

**Output:** Two new documents alongside the revised manuscript files:
- `00_phase5_log.md` — the complete decision record for all six
  passes and all escalations
- `00_phase5_declaration.md` — the Publication Readiness
  Declaration; the author's specific, documented acknowledgment
  that every exit condition for every pass has been met

The Publication Readiness Declaration closes the loop that Phase 1
opened when the working hypothesis was formed. It marks the moment
when a claim the author was willing to argue became a book the
author was willing to put their name on.

## HOW PHASES 4 AND 5 CONNECT TO THE LARGER PIPELINE

Phase 4 receives the Phase 3 argument inventory — fully verified,
fully investigated, architecturally complete. Phase 4 converts that
inventory into a manuscript. Phase 5 iterates the manuscript to
publication readiness.

The Phase 5 Publication Readiness Declaration is the required entry
condition for Phase 6 (Novel Architecture). Phase 6 does not begin
until the Phase 5 declaration has been signed and every Phase 5
exit condition has been met. The completed non-fiction manuscript
becomes the direct input to the novel conversion: the Phase 1 master
claim becomes the novel's controlling idea, the Phase 3 Social
Currency list becomes the conversion's highest-priority novelization
targets, and the Phase 5 Placeholder Inventory resolution record
determines what personal testimony the novel can draw on.

## OPERATING PRINCIPLES FOR THIS SKILL

When a user invokes this skill, determine which phase they are
working on (Phase 4 assembly or Phase 5 revision) and which step
within that phase.

1. For Phase 4: confirm architecture derivation is complete in
   writing before producing any prose; hold the seven required
   chapter elements as non-negotiable; never write Category C
   testimony without author confirmation
2. For Phase 5: execute the six passes in sequence; never allow
   prose polish to substitute for structural or argumentative
   correction; hold the limits on means — the prohibition against
   achieving legitimate ends through illegitimate means
3. Apply the Sayers Standard throughout: every passage executed
   with care whether or not any human eye notices the details
4. Never proceed past a phase quality gate with unchecked items;
   a manuscript with unchecked gate items is not mostly ready —
   it is a draft with specific structural, evidential, or integrity
   failures
5. The Phase 4 architecture document is the source of truth for
   all prose decisions; revise toward the architecture, not away
   from it; the architecture is revised deliberately, the prose
   drifts inadvertently
