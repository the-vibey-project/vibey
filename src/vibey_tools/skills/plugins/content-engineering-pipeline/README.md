# Content Engineering Pipeline

A sequential 14-phase method that converts a thesis into a book, novel, screenplay, AI film, and distribution campaign. Every phase has entry conditions, named output documents and a quality gate; architecture is written before prose at every conversion.

Before Phase 1 the method asks you to state your own governing postulates — what you hold to be true, the ethic your work serves, and your limits on means. Every later reference to "the postulates" means yours; `pipeline-thesis-development` carries one author's postulates as a worked example. The method's limits on means — no predetermined conclusions, no fabricated testimony, no narrative power in excess of the evidence, no manipulative distribution — stand whatever postulates you state. Vendor, platform and Amazon KDP specifics are marked ⚠️ as volatile facts, dated September 2026; verify them before relying on them.

## Skills

- **pipeline-thesis-development** — Before Phase 1: your governing postulates; Phases 1–3: topic → falsifiable thesis → terrain mapping → argument investigation with verified evidence chains
- **pipeline-book-production** — Phases 4–5: research book assembly from investigated arguments and iterative revision to publication readiness
- **pipeline-novel-conversion** — Phases 6–7: converting non-fiction to novel (C.S. Lewis 'watchful dragons'); architecture before prose
- **pipeline-screenplay-conversion** — Phase 8: novel → screenplay architecture; visual storytelling; 100-120 page compression
- **pipeline-ai-film-production** — Phases 10–13: AI film production (Sora 2, ElevenLabs, Kling AI — dated tool stack) and five-spoke transmedia campaign (TikTok/LinkedIn/X/Instagram/email)
- **pipeline-distribution** — Phase 14: Amazon KDP submission, hub-and-spoke digital deployment, in-person delivery formats
