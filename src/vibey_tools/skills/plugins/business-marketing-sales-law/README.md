# Business, Marketing, Sales and Law Plugin

The commercial disciplines a technical founder or engineering leader actually runs into, with explicit attention to what the evidence supports: business models and unit economics, strategy, operations and financing; marketing fundamentals, what the advertising evidence actually shows, brand, measurement and attribution, and channels; the sales process, qualification, negotiation and sales management; and a deliberately scoped commercial-law layer on contracts, IP, employment, privacy, consumer protection and disputes. The legal sections are an orientation to the questions worth putting to counsel, not legal advice.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **biz-models-strategy-operations-and-financing** — Business Models and Unit Economics, Strategy, Operations, and Financing (§0–§4): Routing; Business Models and Unit Economics; Strategy; Operations and Organization; Financing.
- **biz-marketing-evidence-brand-and-attribution** — Marketing Fundamentals, the Evidence, Brand and Advertising, Attribution, and Channels (§5–§9): Fundamentals; ⚠️ What the Evidence Actually Shows; Brand and Advertising; ⚠️ Measurement and Attribution; Channels.
- **biz-sales-process-qualification-and-negotiation** — The Sales Process, Qualification, Negotiation, and Sales Management (§10–§13): The Sales Process; Qualification; Negotiation; Sales Management.
- **biz-legal-contracts-ip-employment-and-privacy** — Commercial Law — Contracts, IP, Employment, Privacy, and Disputes (§14–§21): ⚠️ Scope: What This Section Is and Isn't; How Legal Risk Actually Materializes; Contracts; Intellectual Property; Employment; Privacy and Data; Consumer Protection and Marketing Law; Disputes.
- **biz-reference** — What Moved, Misconceptions, and Canon (§22–§26): What Moved; Misconceptions; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
