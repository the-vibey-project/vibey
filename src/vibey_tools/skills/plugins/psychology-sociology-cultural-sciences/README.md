# Psychology, Sociology and Cultural Sciences Plugin

The behavioural and social sciences presented with their evidence quality attached: it opens with the epistemic situation after the replication crisis, the reliability gradient, and how to read a claim in these fields, then covers perception, memory, learning, cognition and bias, emotion, development, personality, motivation, social psychology, clinical psychology and what actually works, intelligence and neurodiversity, sociological thinking and the theory traditions, stratification, networks, institutions, deviance, religion, culture and anthropology, and the WEIRD problem. Findings are reported with their replication status, not as settled facts.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
