# Rocket Science Plugin

The physics and engineering mathematics of rocketry, for when you need the actual equations, the derivations behind them, or the numbers that constrain real vehicles: the rocket equation and staging optimization, nozzle thermodynamics and isentropic flow, combustion chamber physics, turbomachinery and cycle thermodynamics, regenerative cooling, propellant chemistry, orbital mechanics through patched conics and perturbations, ascent trajectory optimization, aerodynamic loads, thin-walled tank structures, guidance and control, reentry heating, and failure physics.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **rocket-turbomachinery-cooling-and-propellants** — Turbomachinery and Cycles, Heat Transfer and Cooling, and Propellants (§4–§6): Turbomachinery and Cycles; Heat Transfer and Cooling; Propellants.
- **rocket-orbital-mechanics-and-ascent** — Orbital Mechanics and the Ascent Trajectory (§7–§8): Orbital Mechanics; Ascent Trajectory.
- **rocket-aerodynamics-structures-guidance-and-reentry** — Aerodynamics and Loads, Structures, Guidance and Control, Reentry, and Failure Physics (§9–§13): Aerodynamics and Loads; Structures; Guidance and Control; Reentry Physics; Instabilities and Failure Physics.
- **rocket-reference** — Numbers, Misconceptions, Contested Questions, and the Open Frontier (§14–§20): Numbers; Misconceptions; Contested; What's Actually Open; Textbooks; Quick Reference; Method.
