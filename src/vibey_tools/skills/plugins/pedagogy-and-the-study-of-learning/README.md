# Pedagogy and the Study of Learning and Teaching Plugin

How learning works and how teaching is studied, in a field with an unusually wide gap between what is well established and what is widely believed and practised — some findings here are among the most robust in psychology, others in the same textbooks failed replication entirely, and a third group were never research findings at all. The reliability gradient, memory and cognitive load, the robust effects of spacing, retrieval and interleaving, motivation and metacognition; explicit instruction and the discovery debate, worked examples, questioning, feedback, assessment and curriculum sequencing; reading and mathematics instruction, differentiation and educational technology; and how to read education research critically, including effect sizes and the zombie ideas that refuse to die.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
