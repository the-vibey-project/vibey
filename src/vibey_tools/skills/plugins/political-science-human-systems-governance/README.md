# Political Science, Human Systems and Governance Plugin

Political science as a body of findings with its confidence levels attached, starting from what the field can and cannot establish. Power, authority and legitimacy, the state and the major theory traditions; collective action problems, the commons and Ostrom's correction to the tragedy story, social choice and Arrow's theorem, and voting systems; constitutional design, electoral systems in practice, executives, legislatures, federalism and courts; bureaucracy, parties, voter behaviour, public opinion measurement and polarization; regimes and transitions, state capacity, corruption, international relations theory, conflict and cooperation, the policy process — and the field's own replication and measurement problems.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (checked August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
