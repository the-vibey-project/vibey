# Botany, Agriculture, Livestock and Hunting Plugin

Plant science through to working agriculture, livestock and wildlife: plant structure, photosynthesis and the C3/C4/CAM pathways, water relations and nutrition, pollination, ecology and succession, identification and the hard limit on identifying by resemblance, pathology and weeds; soil, fertility, crop production, IPM, irrigation, breeding, and farming systems assessed honestly; ruminant digestion, livestock nutrition, reproduction, herd health and biosecurity, genetics, grazing and welfare; and wildlife management plus the law, ethics, safety and meat-handling side of hunting.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **agri-botany-and-plant-science** — Plant Science, Identification, Pathology, and Weeds (§0–§9): Routing; Plant Structure; Photosynthesis and the Three Pathways; Water Relations; Plant Nutrition; Reproduction and Pollination; Ecology and Succession; ⚠️ Identification — and a Hard Limit; Plant Pathology; Weeds.
- **agri-soil-fertility-and-crop-production** — Soil, Fertility, Crop Production, IPM, and Farming Systems (§10–§16): ⚠️ Soil; Fertility Management; Crop Production; Integrated Pest Management; Water and Irrigation; Breeding and Genetics; Farming Systems, Honestly.
- **agri-livestock-nutrition-health-and-welfare** — Ruminant Digestion, Livestock Nutrition, Health, Genetics, Grazing, and Welfare (§17–§23): ⚠️ Ruminant Digestion; Livestock Nutrition; Reproduction; Herd Health and Biosecurity; Livestock Genetics; Grazing Management; Welfare.
- **agri-wildlife-management-and-hunting** — Wildlife Management, Hunting Law, Ethics and Safety, and Game Meat (§24–§27): Wildlife Management; ⚠️ Hunting: Law, Ethics, Safety; Shot Placement and Recovery; ⚠️ Field Care and Game Meat Safety.
- **agri-reference** — What's Live, Misconceptions, and Numbers (§28–§33): What's Live; Misconceptions; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
