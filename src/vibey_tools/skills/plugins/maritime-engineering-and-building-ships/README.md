# Maritime Engineering and Building Ships Plugin

Naval architecture and shipbuilding from first principles to the yard: the design spiral, buoyancy and hydrostatics, stability and the metacentric height that decides whether a ship rights itself, hull form, resistance and powering, propulsion, seakeeping and manoeuvring; structure and longitudinal strength, materials and corrosion, machinery, systems and ship types; the shipyard, build planning, welding and fabrication, launch, outfitting and trials; then class, flag and IMO, safety regulation, small craft, operation and drydock, and a direct account of why ships are lost.

One reference, split into 6 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (checked August 2026) flags what goes stale first.
