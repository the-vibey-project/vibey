# Media Engineering Plugin

Music, audio, video, TV, film and podcast engineering for software engineers building, debugging, or making architectural decisions about media systems: the fundamentals of digital media, containers and codecs as engineering choices, audio and video production, colour and timecode, loudness standards, transcoding and ABR packaging, streaming delivery including Media over QUIC, DRM, captions and accessibility, podcast infrastructure and dynamic ad insertion, metadata and rights identifiers, and the AI generation layer with its live licensing fight.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **media-fundamentals-containers-and-codecs** — Fundamentals, Containers and Muxing, and Codecs (§0–§3): Routing; Fundamentals; Containers and Muxing; Codecs.
- **media-production-and-loudness** — Audio Production, Video Production, and Loudness (§4–§6): Audio Production; Video Production; Loudness.
- **media-transcoding-streaming-and-drm** — Transcoding and Packaging, Streaming Delivery, and DRM (§7–§9): Transcoding and Packaging; Streaming Delivery; DRM.
- **media-captions-podcasts-rights-and-qc** — Captions, Podcast Infrastructure, Rights, AI Generation, and QC (§10–§14): Captions and Accessibility; Podcast Infrastructure; Metadata, Identifiers, and Rights; AI Generation and the Licensing Fight; Testing and QC.
- **media-reference** — Anti-Patterns, Contested Questions, Currency, and Canon (§15–§20): Anti-Patterns; Contested Questions; Currency Snapshot; The Canon; Quick Reference; Sources and Method.
