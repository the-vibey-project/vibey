# Economics, Accounting and Tax Plugin

Economics, accounting and tax for technical readers who have to read a financial statement or an economic claim critically: how economics knows what it claims and how strong that evidence is, microeconomics and market structure, market failure, macroeconomics, money, fiscal policy, trade and growth, behavioural findings; double-entry, the three statements, accrual accounting and revenue recognition, the judgement-heavy areas, ratio analysis, GAAP versus IFRS and audit; and a deliberately general tax layer on book-tax differences, deferred tax and entity choice.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **econ-methodology-and-microeconomics** — How Economics Knows What It Claims, Microeconomics, Market Structure, and Market Failure (§0–§4): Routing; ⚠️ How Economics Knows What It Claims; Microeconomics; Firms and Market Structure; Market Failure.
- **econ-macroeconomics-money-and-behavioural** — Macroeconomics, Money and Monetary Policy, Fiscal Policy, Trade, and Behavioural Findings (§5–§9): Macroeconomics; Money and Monetary Policy; Fiscal Policy and Debt; Trade and Growth; Behavioural Findings.
- **econ-accounting-statements-accrual-and-ratios** — Double-Entry, the Three Statements, Accrual Accounting, and Ratio Analysis (§10–§14): Double-Entry; The Three Statements; Accrual Accounting and Revenue Recognition; The Judgement-Heavy Areas; Ratio Analysis.
- **econ-gaap-ifrs-audit-and-tax** — GAAP versus IFRS, Audit and Controls, and Tax (§15–§19): GAAP vs IFRS; Audit and Controls; ⚠️ Tax Fundamentals and Why This Section Is Deliberately General; Book-Tax Differences and Deferred Tax; Entity Choice.
- **econ-reference** — Misconceptions, What Moved, and Canon (§20–§24): Misconceptions; What Moved; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
