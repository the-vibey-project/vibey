# Evangelism, Apologetics and Church Planting Plugin

Sharing the faith, defending it, preaching it, teaching it, and planting the community that
carries it — practical enough to actually go out and do it.

One reference, split into 10 skills, so a task loads only the part it needs.

**The thesis the reference opens on**, and every other part of it follows from this one sentence:

> Evangelism is the communication of the gospel message with the intent to invite someone to follow
> Jesus Christ. It is both a privilege and a responsibility, and **it is also a communication task**
> — which means the evidence on what actually persuades, what builds trust, and what constitutes
> ethical influence applies directly.

The ethics half has a matching one, and it is the sentence the whole of §5 is the working-out of:

> Evangelism that bypasses a person's reasoning to produce a decision is manipulation, not
> persuasion, and it produces decisions that do not last.

## ⚠️ What this reference is and is not

This is **a practitioner reference written from inside Christian conviction**, and it does not
pretend otherwise. Three things follow that a reader should have before they open it.

**The arguments for God are contested philosophy, not settled results.** §6 presents the
cosmological, teleological, moral and ontological arguments the way the source presents them, and
then names, in a column flagged as coming from the wider literature rather than from the source, the
objection each one actually faces — the causal principle may not transfer to the beginning of time
itself, fine-tuning's probability claim needs a measure that may not normalise and the multiverse
exchange is unsettled, secular moral realism denies the moral argument's first premise, and the
ontological argument's possibility premise is the whole disagreement. The source's own framing is
that the case is **cumulative rather than demonstrative** and that no single
argument carries the weight alone; the pack keeps that framing rather than upgrading it. §7's
minimal-facts resurrection argument is likewise **one specific apologetic method, given with the
criticisms it actually faces** — the source names no critic and the pack supplies no name it was not
given — and its table of alternative explanations is reproduced as given, not strengthened.

**The empirical claims are unquantified.** The persuasion findings in §1, the curse of knowledge in
§13, learning as a change in long-term memory in §18 and onboarding as the growth constraint in §21
are real claims about real literatures, and the source supplies no studies, no sample sizes and no
numbers. The pack states them as the tendencies they are and invents nothing to fill the gap — where
you need a figure, you need a citation this reference does not have.

**Some of this is law, not judgement.** §21's safeguarding material says out loud that the duties,
reporting thresholds and mandatory-reporter rules for any community involving children or vulnerable
adults are **set by the law where you are** — a formal obligation, not a matter of leadership
discretion or community feeling. This tells you the question exists and that you must have answered
it before you need it; your jurisdiction, insurer or denomination tells you the answer.

One thing the pack does carry as load-bearing rather than as an appendix: **§5, the ethics of
evangelistic influence**. The disclosure test there governs everything else in the set, including
the sermon aimed at a decision in §12 and the altar call in §5 itself — which the source does not
rule for or against, and neither does this pack.

## Neighbours in this marketplace

Three plugins cover the same human ground as **secular disciplines**; this one covers it as
**ministry**, and cross-references rather than duplicates them:

- `speaking-influence-and-community` — the closest neighbour by far. Public speaking, listening,
  rapport, what actually persuades, the ethics of influence, facilitation, organizing, volunteers
  and burnout, and community conflict, treated as a craft with an evidence base and with the famous
  claims that failed to replicate flagged as such. Where that pack tags each claim's durability and
  carries a dated currency snapshot, this one is faithful to a single source document and says so
  when the source declines to quantify something. Read it for the evidence behind §1, §14 and §21.
- `family-and-community-building` — family and community research with its evidence quality
  attached: social capital, networks and belonging, third places, congregations and voluntary
  associations, community organizing, collective efficacy, and whether a program actually works.
  That is the research literature underneath §21's onboarding, norms and volunteer sections.
- `writing-craft` — prose mechanics, technical prose, narrative structure, white papers and legal
  briefs. §12–§15 is writing for the ear and for a listener who cannot re-read; that pack is writing
  for a reader who can.

Two more sit one step further out and are worth knowing about:

- `pedagogy-and-the-study-of-learning` — the learning science §18 gestures at, with the reliability
  gradient made explicit: spacing, retrieval and interleaving as among the most robust effects in
  psychology, and the zombie ideas in the same textbooks that were never findings at all.
- `negotiation-and-conflict-resolution` — interests versus positions, mediation and the ADR
  spectrum, behind §21's conflict material.
