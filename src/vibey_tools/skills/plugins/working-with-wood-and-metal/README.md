# Working with Wood and Metal Plugin

Shop craft in wood and metal, organised around what changes when the material changes: reading and selecting stock, layout, hand and power tools, sharpening, joinery, glue-ups and finishing; then metal cutting, filing, drilling and tapping, manual machining, the welding processes, brazing, forming, forging and heat treatment; plus fits and measurement, shop setup, dust and fume control, and the safety practices that actually prevent injuries — kept alongside the operations they apply to rather than filed away at the end.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

- **shop-woodworking-tools-sharpening-joinery-and-finishing** — Layout and Marking, Hand Tools, Sharpening, Machines, the Table Saw, Joinery Technique, Glue-Ups, and Wood Finishing (§5–§12): Layout and Marking; Hand Tools; ⚠️ Sharpening; Machines; ⚠️ The Table Saw; Joinery Technique; Glue-Ups; Wood Finishing.
- **shop-metalwork-cutting-drilling-and-machining** — Metal in the Shop, What Changes When You Work Metal, Marking Out, Cutting Metal, Drilling and Tapping, and Manual Machining (§13–§18): Metal in the Shop; What Changes When You Work Metal; Marking Out; Cutting Metal; Drilling and Tapping; Manual Machining.
- **shop-welding-forging-heat-treatment-and-shop-setup** — Welding Processes, Brazing and Soldering, Forming and Forging, Heat Treatment, Metal Finishing, Fasteners and Fits, Measurement, and Shop Setup with Dust and Fume Control (§19–§26): ⚠️ Welding Processes; Brazing and Soldering; Forming and Forging; Heat Treatment; Metal Finishing; Fasteners and Fits; Measurement; Shop Setup, Dust and Fume.
- **shop-reference** — What's Live, Misconceptions, Numbers, and Resources (§27–§32): What's Live; Misconceptions; Numbers; Learning and Resources; Quick Reference; Method.
