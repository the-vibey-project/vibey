# Weather Science Plugin

Atmospheric science and forecasting for technical readers: the structure of the atmosphere, radiation and the energy budget, thermodynamics and stability, moisture and clouds, the dynamical balances, the general circulation, synoptic meteorology and where mid-latitude weather comes from, thunderstorms and severe weather, tropical cyclones, the boundary layer, observation, numerical weather prediction including the underrated data-assimilation half, forecast verification, predictability limits, and machine-learning weather prediction with its live scientific dispute.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **weather-atmosphere-radiation-thermodynamics-and-moisture** — The Atmosphere, Radiation and the Energy Budget, Thermodynamics, and Moisture (§0–§4): Routing; Structure of the Atmosphere; Radiation and the Energy Budget; Thermodynamics and Stability; Moisture and Clouds.
- **weather-dynamics-circulation-and-synoptic** — The Dynamical Balances, the General Circulation, and Synoptic Meteorology (§5–§7): Dynamics — The Balances; General Circulation; Synoptic Meteorology.
- **weather-severe-storms-cyclones-and-boundary-layer** — Thunderstorms and Severe Weather, Tropical Cyclones, and the Boundary Layer (§8–§10): Thunderstorms and Severe Weather; Tropical Cyclones; Boundary Layer and Local Effects.
- **weather-observation-nwp-verification-and-machine-learning** — Observation, Numerical Weather Prediction, Verification, Predictability, and Machine Learning (§11–§16): Observation; Numerical Weather Prediction; Forecast Verification; Predictability and Chaos; Machine Learning Weather Prediction; Weather vs Climate.
- **weather-reference** — Misconceptions, Numbers, and Canon (§17–§21): Misconceptions; Numbers; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
