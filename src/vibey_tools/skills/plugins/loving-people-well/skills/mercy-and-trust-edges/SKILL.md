# Mercy and Trust with Edges

## Overview
This skill operationalizes Level 4 of the reverse architecture: the dual discipline of unconditional forgiveness and bounded trust. It equips practitioners to absorb personal offenses without malice while maintaining firm, truthful boundaries that protect relational integrity and prevent exploitation.

## Invocation Triggers
Invoke this skill when:
- Resolving relational offenses, hurt feelings, or broken agreements.
- Burning private grievance ledgers to practice seventy times seven forgiveness.
- Distinguishing between unilateral forgiveness and earned bilateral trust.
- Confronting cowardly agreeableness, people-pleasing, or silent resentment.
- Setting protective boundaries while maintaining genuine Christian charity.

## Governing Principles

### 1. Unilateral Forgiveness (Seventy Times Seven)
Jesus commanded forgiving a brother seventy times seven (Matthew 18:21-22). Unilateral forgiveness is an act of sovereign grace. The practitioner tears up the internal ledger of grievances, releases personal claims to vengeance, and prays for the counterpart's good. Withholding forgiveness poisons the soul with chronic bitterness.

### 2. Bilateral Trust as an Earned Account
Forgiveness is an unconditional gift of mercy; trust is an earned account accumulated penny by penny across years of verified honesty. Trust requires demonstrated integrity and collapses upon covenantal betrayal. Extending automatic trust to an unrepentant or deceitful actor constitutes moral negligence.

### 3. Truthfulness over Cowardly Agreeableness
A true friend speaks the truth with calm affection (Ephesians 4:15). Proverbs 27:6 teaches that the wounds of a friend can be trusted, refusing deceitful kisses. Nodding along to keep a superficial peace harms both parties. Truthfulness represents the kinder action.

## Operational Checklist
- [ ] Identify every recorded grievance and burn the internal scorekeeping ledger.
- [ ] Declare unconditional forgiveness before God, clearing bitterness from the heart.
- [ ] Differentiate forgiveness from access; evaluate whether the counterpart has earned operational trust.
- [ ] Speak necessary, uncomfortable truths with calm gentleness, rejecting cowardly agreeableness.
- [ ] Maintain firm structural boundaries to ensure personal safety and relational honor.

## Failure Modes
- Chronic Scorekeeping: Nursing silent resentment and deploying past failures as weapons in arguments.
- Gullible Negligence: Handing keys back to an untrustworthy actor without verified behavioral change.
- Cowardly Agreeableness: Concealing truth and enabling destructive behavior to avoid immediate tension.
