# Speaking, Influence and Community: What Actually Persuades, and the Ethics of Influence

> **Part 4 of 6** of the *Public Speaking, Influence and Community* reference (plugin `speaking-influence-and-community`), covering §18–§19. Sibling skills: `speaking-what-the-field-knows-anxiety-audience-and-structure` (§0–§5), `speaking-language-delivery-visual-aids-and-practice` (§6–§11), `speaking-listening-rapport-difficult-conversations-and-negotiation` (§12–§17), `speaking-groups-community-facilitation-organizing-and-conflict` (§20–§26), `speaking-reference` (§27–§32). Section numbers are shared across the set; a reference written as §N → `skill` points into that sibling skill.
>
> **Currency:** The craft is old and stable. Two areas have moved. See §27 → `speaking-reference` for the status of the famous body-language claims, and what the persuasion evidence now shows.

> **⚠️ A domain with an unusually bad ratio of confident advice to actual evidence.** ⚠️ **A
> great deal of what is taught in communication training traces to small 1960s studies,
> misread, or to findings that failed to replicate — and is still being repeated in
> material published this year** (§27.1 → `speaking-reference`).
>
> **⚠️ This file tries to separate three things: what is well supported, what is craft
> wisdom that experienced practitioners agree on without formal evidence, and what is
> folklore.**
>
> **⚠️ GOTCHA** boxes mark where the standard advice is wrong or overstated.
>
> **The three ideas that organize this document:**
> 1. **⚠️ SUBSTANCE IS THE TECHNIQUE** (§4 → `speaking-what-the-field-knows-anxiety-audience-and-structure`, §18, §27.2 → `speaking-reference`). **The persuasion evidence points
>    consistently at content — relevant facts, specific evidence, real engagement with the
>    other person's actual position — rather than at delivery tricks. This is both the most
>    reassuring finding for nervous speakers and the least commercially attractive one for
>    the training industry.**
> 2. **⚠️ INFLUENCE WITHOUT A LEGITIMACY CHECK IS MANIPULATION** (§19). **The techniques in
>    this file work on people who don't want them used. The distinction between persuasion
>    and manipulation is not a matter of degree or intent — it has a testable structure, and
>    it belongs at the front, not in an appendix.**
> 3. **⚠️ COMMUNITIES ARE MAINTAINED, NOT FOUNDED** (§21 → `speaking-groups-community-facilitation-organizing-and-conflict`, §24 → `speaking-groups-community-facilitation-organizing-and-conflict`). **Almost all the failure
>    modes are in sustaining: the founder bottleneck, volunteer burnout, unhandled conflict,
>    and the transition from a group that knows each other to one that cannot.**

## §18. ⚠️ What Actually Persuades

> **⚠️ §27.2 → `speaking-reference` has the current evidence. This is the durable frame.**
```
⚠️ CLASSICAL  ⚠️ ETHOS (credibility), LOGOS (argument), PATHOS
   (emotion) — ⚠️ still the best organizing triad, and note that
   ethos is largely established BEFORE you speak
⚠️ ELABORATION LIKELIHOOD  ⚠️ central route (argument quality,
   when people are motivated and able to think) vs peripheral
   (cues and heuristics). ⚠️ Central-route change is more durable
   and more resistant to counter-argument
⚠️ ⚠️ THE HEURISTICS people cite (Cialdini's reciprocity,
   commitment, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity, unity)
   ⚠️ are real and are PERIPHERAL-ROUTE. ⚠️ They shift behaviour
   in low-stakes moments and are exactly the machinery of
   manipulation when used against someone's interests (§19)
⚠️ ⚠️ WHAT THE EVIDENCE ACTUALLY EMPHASIZES (§27.2)
   ⚠️ RELEVANT FACTS AND EVIDENCE — ⚠️ information density is
      repeatedly the strongest identified lever
   ⚠️ ⚠️ CONVERSATION beats broadcast, substantially
   ⚠️ ⚠️ LISTENING FIRST — eliciting the other person's view
      before advancing yours — produces DURABLE change
   ⚠️ MORAL REFRAMING — arguing from THEIR values rather than
      yours. ⚠️ Difficult, effective, and rarely done because it
      requires understanding values you may not share
⚠️ ⚠️ WHAT PERSUADES LESS THAN ASSUMED  ⚠️ more forceful
   assertion · ⚠️ facts alone against identity-linked beliefs ·
   ⚠️ psychographic MICROTARGETING (§27.2 — the evidence is
   genuinely conflicting and the effects found are small)
⚠️ ⚠️ ATTITUDE CHANGE AND BEHAVIOUR CHANGE ARE DIFFERENT THINGS
   and may not correlate (§27.2). ⚠️ Winning the argument is
   not the same as changing what someone does
```

## §19. ⚠️ The Ethics of Influence

> **⚠️ Load-bearing, and placed here deliberately: everything before this works, and works
> on people who have not consented to it.**
```
⚠️ ⚠️ THE STRUCTURAL DISTINCTION — not a matter of degree
   ⚠️ PERSUASION  ⚠️ engages the other person's REASONING. It
      works BECAUSE they evaluated it. ⚠️ It survives disclosure
   ⚠️ MANIPULATION  ⚠️ BYPASSES reasoning — exploiting bias,
      emotion, time pressure, social cost or incomplete
      information. ⚠️ It stops working if the target sees the
      mechanism
   ⚠️ COERCION  removes the option
⚠️ ⚠️ THE DISCLOSURE TEST, and it is a good one
   ⚠️ "If I told them exactly what I was doing and why, would
   this still work?" ⚠️ A sound argument survives. Manufactured
   scarcity, false urgency, and engineered guilt do not
⚠️ THE SUPPORTING QUESTIONS
   ⚠️ Am I giving them what they need to decide, or what makes
      them decide my way?
   ⚠️ ⚠️ Would I be comfortable if the roles were reversed?
   ⚠️ Am I exploiting a state — grief, fear, exhaustion,
      loneliness, faith, hope — rather than an argument?
   ⚠️ ⚠️ Is there a POWER ASYMMETRY? ⚠️ The same technique is
      different from a manager, a pastor, a doctor, a landlord
      or a creditor than between equals
   ⚠️ Do they have a real exit?
⚠️ ⚠️ THE HARD CASES ARE HARD  ⚠️ persuading someone toward their
   OWN stated goals · fundraising for genuine need · public
   health messaging · a sermon. ⚠️ The test still applies:
   engage reasoning, do not manufacture pressure
⚠️ ⚠️ AND SEE §27.2. ⚠️ Highly effective personalized persuasion
   is now available at scale and at near-zero cost, which
   changes the ethics from a personal question to a structural
   one
```
