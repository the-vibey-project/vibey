# Social Media Engineering Plugin

Social media for developers in both senses: building social systems — feed and timeline architecture, social graphs, ranking, notifications, media handling and moderation at scale — and integrating with platform APIs while surviving their pricing and policy changes. Also covers the open protocols (ActivityPub and AT Protocol) and what federation actually costs, the regulatory layer (DSA, Online Safety Act, age assurance) that now determines what you may ship, spam and abuse engineering, and — separately — a developer's own presence and how to do it without it eating your life.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **social-platform-apis-and-open-protocols** — Platform APIs and the Open Protocols (§0–§2): Routing; Platform APIs; Open Protocols.
- **social-feed-graph-ranking-and-notifications** — Feed Architecture, the Social Graph, Ranking, and Notifications (§3–§6): Feed and Timeline Architecture; The Social Graph; Ranking and Recommendation; Notifications and Real-Time.
- **social-moderation-abuse-and-regulation** — Moderation at Scale, Spam and Abuse, and Regulation (§7–§9): Moderation at Scale; Spam, Bots, and Abuse; Regulation and Compliance.
- **social-media-analytics-privacy-and-presence** — Media Handling, Analytics, Privacy, and Your Own Presence (§10–§13): Media Handling; Analytics and Experimentation; Privacy and Data; Your Own Presence as a Developer.
- **social-reference** — Anti-Patterns, Contested Questions, Currency, and Resources (§14–§19): Anti-Patterns; Contested Questions; Currency Snapshot; Resources; Quick Reference; Sources and Method.
