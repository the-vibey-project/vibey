# christian-marriage-and-family

A comprehensive skills plugin providing theological foundations, sacrificial male headship, executive domestic governance, authoritative parenting, and filial piety triangulated across Protestant, Catholic, and Greek Orthodox traditions.

**Author:** Adam Matthew Steinberger  
**Email:** `adam@matthewsteinberger.com`  
**Version:** 0.1.0  

## Member Skills

| Skill | Description |
|---|---|
| `covenantal-marriage-theology` | Theological foundations of Christian matrimony across Protestant, Catholic, and Greek Orthodox traditions, covering the creation order, ontological equality, Augustinian tria bona, and Ephesians 5 sacramental covenant. |
| `sacrificial-male-headship` | Biblical patriarchal leadership and kenotic male headship, establishing the husband's higher hierarchical office over the wife, sacrificial responsibility ($R_H \gg R_W$), priestly intercession, and domestic protection. |
| `feminine-honor-and-homemaking` | Biblical calling of the Christian wife as the executive heart of the home, covering domestic governance (*oikodespotein*), Proverbs 31 enterprise, reverence (*phobos*), and building an ecclesiola. |
| `biblical-parenting-and-discipline` | Authoritative Christian parenting and child formation, integrating the Deuteronomy 6 circadian Shema liturgy, calm non-reactive discipline, and maternal emotional nurture. |
| `filial-piety-and-family-order` | The Fourth Commandment and filial piety, outlining the transition from prompt childhood obedience to lifelong generational honor (*kavod*) and domestic elder care. |

## Doctrinal Anchors

- **Scripture Canon**: Genesis 1-2, Exodus 20, Deuteronomy 6, Proverbs 31, Ephesians 5-6, Colossians 3, 1 Timothy 5, 1 Peter 3, Sirach 3.
- **Patristic & Scholastic**: St. John Chrysostom (*Homilies on Ephesians*), St. Augustine (*De Bono Coniugali*), St. Thomas Aquinas (*Summa Theologiae*).
- **Magisterial Encyclicals**: Pope Leo XIII (*Arcanum Divinae Sapientiae*), Pope Pius XI (*Casti Connubii*).
- **Reformation Casuistry**: William Perkins (*Christian Oeconomie*), Richard Baxter (*A Christian Directory*), Matthew Henry (*Commentary on Genesis 2*).
