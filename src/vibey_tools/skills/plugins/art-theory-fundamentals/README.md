# Art Theory Fundamentals Plugin

Art theory for people who want to look at and talk about art precisely: it opens by distinguishing what kind of claim is being made — descriptive, evaluative or interpretive — then covers the formal elements, value, colour, composition and pictorial space, the philosophical questions from beauty and the sublime through the definition problem and art and ethics, a historical skeleton, the canon problem, iconography, semiotics and the critical lenses, intention and the author, a practical method for looking, and the art world and market.

One reference, split into 5 skills along its section groups so a task loads only the part it needs. Section numbers (§N) are shared across the set and cross-references into a sibling skill are written as §N → `skill`. Reference, not tutorial: sections are independent, every claim is tagged by how durable it is (stable fundamentals vs. versioned specifics vs. genuinely contested questions), and a currency snapshot (verified August 2026) flags what goes stale first.

## Skills

- **art-claims-elements-colour-and-composition** — What Kind of Claim, the Elements, Colour, Composition, and Materiality (§0–§7): Routing; ⚠️ What Kind of Claim Is This?; The Elements; Value; Colour; Composition; Pictorial Space; Medium and Materiality.
- **art-aesthetics-and-philosophy-of-art** — The Philosophical Questions, Beauty, Taste, Theories of Art, and Ethics (§8–§13): The Questions; Beauty and the Sublime; Taste and Judgement; Theories of Art; ⚠️ The Definition Problem; Art and Ethics.
- **art-history-modernism-and-the-canon** — A Historical Skeleton, Modernism to Contemporary, and the Canon Problem (§14–§16): A Skeleton; Modernism to Contemporary; ⚠️ The Canon Problem.
- **art-interpretation-criticism-and-the-art-world** — Iconography, Semiotics, Critical Lenses, Intention, Looking, and the Art World (§17–§22): Iconography and Iconology; Semiotics; Critical Lenses; Intention and the Author; How to Actually Look; The Art World and Market.
- **art-reference** — What's Live, Misconceptions, and Canon (§23–§27): What's Live; Misconceptions; Books; Quick Reference; Method.
