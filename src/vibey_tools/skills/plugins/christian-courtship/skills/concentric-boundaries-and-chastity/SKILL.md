# Concentric Boundaries and Chastity

## Overview
This skill operationalizes the boundary topology of Christian courtship. It defines five discrete concentric spheres governing relational access, information disclosure, and emotional vulnerability, enforcing the absolute prohibition of premarital sexual union as the paramount safety guardrail.

## Invocation Triggers
Invoke this skill when:
- Establishing relational boundary layers between parties.
- Defining physical purity protocols and accountability structures.
- Preventing premature emotional or physical intimacy.
- Transitioning between community friendship, deliberate courtship, and betrothal.
- Safeguarding cognitive clarity against neurochemical bonding illusions.

## The Five Concentric Spheres

### Sphere 0: Civic Perimeter
- Counterparts: General acquaintances, neighbors, civic contacts.
- Posture: Universal civility, politeness (*amicitia*), professional excellence.
- Information Flow: Public matters only. Zero private disclosure.

### Sphere 1: Community Friendship
- Counterparts: Brothers and sisters in the local church congregation.
- Posture: Shared worship, collective service, transparent public fellowship.
- Information Flow: Spiritual encouragement and general life updates. Zero romantic exclusivity.

### Sphere 2: Deliberate Courtship
- Counterparts: Suitor and pursued undergoing exclusive marital evaluation.
- Posture: Intentional vetting under pastoral and family oversight.
- Information Flow: Deep doctrinal, financial, and personal history disclosures. Emotional vulnerability calibrated strictly to progressive commitment.

### Sphere 3: Solemn Betrothal
- Counterparts: Formally engaged couple preparing for holy matrimony (*sponsalia de futuro*).
- Posture: High public constraint, marital counseling, household preparation.
- Information Flow: Full legal, logistical, and domestic integration. Complete emotional dedication.

### Sphere 4: Covenant Marriage
- Counterparts: Husband and wife in an indissoluble one-flesh union (*matrimonium*).
- Posture: Total mutual self-gift (*communio personarum*).
- Information Flow: Complete vulnerability under God, shared bed, shared name, shared life.

## The Paramount Invariant: Absolute Premarital Chastity

The governing physical law of courtship is absolute sexual abstinence prior to covenant marriage:

Sex(t) = 0 for all t prior to marriage

Premarital sexual intercourse introduces severe operational pathologies:
1. Neurochemical Distortion: Sexual intimacy releases oxytocin, dopamine, and vasopressin, generating synthetic emotional bonding that blinds cognitive evaluation and obscures character defects.
2. Sliding Inertia: Sexual entanglement accelerates relationship velocity, forcing couples into premature cohabitation and shared financial constraints before dedication is verified.
3. Spiritual Violation: Scripture commands believers to flee fornication (*porneia*, 1 Corinthians 6:18) and possess their vessels in sanctification and honor (1 Thessalonians 4:3-5). The marriage bed remains undefiled exclusively within holy matrimony (Hebrews 13:4).

## Operational Checklist
- [ ] Maintain courtship interactions in public, church, and family settings.
- [ ] Eliminate secluded domestic environments and isolated overnight travel.
- [ ] Establish explicit verbal agreement defining physical boundaries: zero sexual contact before the wedding altar.
- [ ] Appoint mature third-party mentors (pastor, elder, married couple) with authority to audit boundary adherence.

## Failure Modes
- The Test Drive Illusion: Believing physical intimacy is required to determine compatibility.
- Boundary Creep: Gradual escalation of late-night physical affection eroding self-control.
- Premature Cohabitation: Sharing living quarters prior to the public exchange of marital vows.
