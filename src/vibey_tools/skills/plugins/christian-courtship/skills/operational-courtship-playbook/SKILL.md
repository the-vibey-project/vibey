# Operational Courtship Playbook

## Overview
This skill translates biblical covenant theology and empirical commitment science into concrete, situational action protocols. It provides behavioral scripts, financial audit checklists, pastoral counsel workflows, and guidelines for navigating previous divorce and abandonment as inverted pedagogical anti-material.

## Invocation Triggers
Invoke this skill when:
- Executing a formal transition from community friendship to deliberate courtship.
- Conducting comprehensive premarital financial disclosure and debt auditing.
- Navigating previous divorce and parental responsibilities under 1 Corinthians 7:15.
- Establishing formal premarital counseling with church elders or pastors.
- Preparing for solemn betrothal (*sponsalia de futuro*) and wedding liturgies.

## Situational Action Protocols

### Protocol 1: Transitioning from Friendship to Courtship
1. Pre-Transition Verification: Both parties must complete at least six months of public observation within church community life (Sphere 1).
2. The Directional Conversation: Suitor initiates an explicit, sober conversation defining intent:
   - Script: "I admire your godly character and Christian maturity. I desire to initiate intentional, exclusive courtship with you to evaluate marriage under church and family counsel."
3. Pastoral Blessing: Meet with the pastor or church elders to register courtship intent and request formal mentors.

### Protocol 2: Financial and Asset Transparency Audit
Prior to transitioning from Courtship (Sphere 2) to Betrothal (Sphere 3), both parties execute total financial disclosure:
1. Debt Disclosure: Produce certified credit reports and itemize all outstanding student loans, credit card debts, vehicle notes, and personal obligations.
2. Asset and Income Accounting: Review bank statements, monthly cash flow, business ledgers, and retirement savings.
3. Household Budget Integration: Draft a projected post-wedding monthly budget, allocating funds for tithes, housing, savings, and debt retirement.

### Protocol 3: Navigating Past Divorce as Inverted Pedagogy
When a candidate has experienced a previous divorce:
1. Pastoral Inquiry: The church consistory verifies that the divorce occurred under legitimate biblical grounds: willful desertion by an unbelieving spouse (1 Corinthians 7:15) or unrepentant sexual immorality (Matthew 19:9).
2. Resolution of Grief and Bitterness: The candidate must demonstrate complete spiritual repentance, freedom from malice toward the former spouse, and emotional healing.
3. Protection of Children: Full transparency regarding custody agreements, child support, visitation schedules, and parental obligations. The new partner must actively embrace the child as a divine blessing.
4. Inverted Learning: Analyze the failure modes of the previous union (such as sliding, unaddressed red flags, or lack of spiritual unity) to establish strict, protective guardrails in the current courtship.

### Protocol 4: The Physical Boundary Covenant
1. Public Locus: All dates and meetings take place in open, public, church, or family environments.
2. Zero Private Seclusion: Eliminate isolated overnight trips, secluded hotel stays, and private apartment visits after dark.
3. The Verbal Covenant: Formulate an explicit mutual commitment: "We preserve physical intimacy exclusively for our wedding night, honoring God and protecting our judgment."

## Operational Checklist
- [ ] Complete six months of community friendship observation before declaring courtship intent.
- [ ] Meet with pastor and elders to initiate formal premarital counseling.
- [ ] Exchange certified credit reports and itemized debt ledgers.
- [ ] Enforce strict public and family environments for all dates.
- [ ] Complete pastoral review of any prior marital dissolution under 1 Corinthians 7:15.

## Failure Modes
- Ambiguous Dating: Drifting along for years without explicit directional intent toward marriage.
- Financial Secrecy: Concealing debts or financial obligations until after the wedding ceremony.
- Isolated Courtship: Withdrawing from the local church into private emotional seclusion.
