# Risk Calculus, Character Verification, and Quarantine Protocols

## Overview
This skill operationalizes interpersonal risk analysis and game-theoretic verification in Christian courtship. Operating under realistic baseline assumptions of human fallenness: recognizing that people lie, conceal liabilities, break trust, and commit infidelity: this skill provides formal formulas, diagnostic observation vectors, quarantine safety valves, and revocation triggers.

## Invocation Triggers
Invoke this skill when:
- Calculating the Social Risk Index of escalating intimacy with a suitor.
- Auditing longitudinal character metrics across moral and behavioral domains.
- Responding to detected deceit, concealed debts, boundary transgressions, or infidelity.
- Deploying the 90-day probationary Quarantine Valve.
- Executing an immediate Revocation Trigger and hard state reset.

## Mathematical Formulations

### 1. The Social Risk Index (R_s)
The objective risk index determines operational safety:

R_s = (E times H times C) / (I times A times S)

Threshold Classifications:
- Critical Hazard Zone (R_s >= 3.0): Zero private disclosures. Enforce strict public decorum and formal boundaries.
- Guarded Civility Zone (1.0 <= R_s < 3.0): Polite affability. Maintain systematic, low-stakes testing.
- Secure Covenant Zone (R_s < 1.0): Verified character enables progressive emotional and spiritual vulnerability.

### 2. Character Verification Loss Function
Character score V_c(t) accumulates longitudinally across M weighted domains:

V_c(t) = sum from k=1 to M of w_k times integral from 0 to t of mu_k(tau) exp(-gamma(t - tau)) dtau

where w_k denotes domain weight, mu_k(tau) represents verified performance, and gamma penalizes recent behavioral instability.

### 3. Six Diagnostic Behavioral Observation Vectors
1. Speech Governance: Observe how the candidate speaks of former partners, employers, and church leaders in their absence (Sirach 19:8).
2. Adversity Response: Behavioral stability during financial strain, illness, or public embarrassment.
3. Inner Ring Defense: Degree of susceptibility to social flattery, elite status seeking, or exclusionary cliques.
4. Cross-Context Consistency: Uniformity of moral character across diverse settings (church, workplace, home, traffic).
5. Incentive Geometry: Identifying whether the counterpart gains advantage from the agent's degradation.
6. Reaction to Boundaries: Candidate's response when presented with an unequivocal, polite "No." Boundary resentment indicates an exploitative posture.

## Quarantine and Revocation Circuit Breakers

### The Quarantine Valve (Q_p)
- Trigger: Discovery of an unverified inconsistency, hidden financial strain, or emotional manipulation during Sphere 2 or 3.
- Protocol: Relational escalation halts instantly. Interaction drops to public settings under pastoral supervision for a ninety-day probationary window.
- Restoration: Advancing requires complete restitution, transparent accounting, and unanimous mentor clearance.

### The Immediate Revocation Trigger (R_t)
- Trigger: Active deceit, physical boundary violation, sexual infidelity, or unrepentant betrayal.
- Protocol: Immediate hard reset to Sphere 0. All courtship privileges terminate unconditionally.
- Forgiveness versus Trust: Spiritual forgiveness releases bitterness and surrenders vengeance to God (Romans 12:19); trust requires verified repentance and future reliability.

## Operational Checklist
- [ ] Calculate the Social Risk Index prior to transitioning into deliberate courtship.
- [ ] Audit candidate across all six diagnostic observation vectors over at least six months.
- [ ] Verify financial bank statements and credit reports before solemn betrothal.
- [ ] Execute immediate quarantine or revocation upon detection of deceit or boundary violations.

## Failure Modes
- Naive Credulity: Accepting verbal declarations of piety without longitudinal behavioral evidence.
- Sunk Cost Rationalization: Overlooking red flags because significant time or emotion has been invested.
- Conflating Grace with Access: Allowing an untrustworthy person into intimate spaces under the guise of Christian forgiveness.
