# How hard it works

**krypton: Choose the effort** sets how hard the engine works: auto, TRIVIAL, LOW, STANDARD,
HIGH, MAX or ULTRA.

- **auto** starts at the base effort, LOW, and climbs one step each time a task fails.
- **ULTRA** has no turn limit. A finished pass becomes a checkpoint and the next improvement
  pass begins. It stops only when you press **Stop**, or when a budget you declared is
  reached. It is shown in its own magenta, with a flame.

On the paid loop, running with no dollar cap takes two warnings and the typed phrase
`I accept unlimited spending`. **krypton: End unlimited spend** ends it in one step.
