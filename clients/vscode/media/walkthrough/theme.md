# Light, Dark or System

**krypton: Choose the theme** offers three modes.

- **System**, the default, follows the editor's own colour theme, and switches the moment it
  changes. The panels sit on the editor's surfaces, so they look native in any theme.
- **Light** and **Dark** keep krypton's own palette, whatever the editor uses.

Your choice is the `vibey.theme` setting, and it is remembered on this device. Motion follows
your reduced-motion preference in every mode.
