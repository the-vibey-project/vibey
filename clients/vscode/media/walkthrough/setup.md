# Check your setup

One click runs **krypton: Check my setup**. It looks at each piece krypton needs, in order:

- the storm home, where each task's copy of your folder is kept;
- git;
- gptossloop, the engine that runs on your computer;
- vibey;
- Ollama, and the model (gpt-oss:20b unless you chose another) with its context window.

Each line says ok, warn or FAIL, and every FAIL says what to do next. Nothing leaves your
computer: there is no account and no cloud.
