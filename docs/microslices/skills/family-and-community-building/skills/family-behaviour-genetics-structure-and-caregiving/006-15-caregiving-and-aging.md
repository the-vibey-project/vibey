## §15. Caregiving and Aging

**⚠️ The sandwich generation and the growing caregiving burden is one of the major
demographic realities of the next decades**, **as populations age and family sizes fall.**
**⚠️ Caregiver strain is measurable and consequential** — **health effects, employment
disruption, financial cost, and social isolation** (§18 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`) — ⚠️ **and caregivers systematically
under-report and under-seek support.** **Respite care has decent evidence; so does
caregiver skills training.**
**⚠️ Gendered distribution is stark and persistent**: **the majority of intensive family
caregiving is done by women**, **with corresponding career and retirement-income effects.**
**⚠️ Advance planning is the highest-leverage and most-avoided intervention**: **advance
directives, powers of attorney, explicit conversations about preferences, and financial
planning.** ⚠️ **Families that have had the conversation before a crisis make better
decisions and experience less conflict and guilt afterward** — **and the conversation is
much easier before it's urgent.**
**⚠️ Aging in place, intergenerational households and the social infrastructure of aging**
(§19 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`) — **and note that ⚠️ social connection is among the stronger predictors of healthy
aging** (§18 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`).
