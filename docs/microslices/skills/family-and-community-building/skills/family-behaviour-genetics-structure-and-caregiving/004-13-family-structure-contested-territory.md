## §13. ⚠️ Family Structure — Contested Territory

> **⚠️ I'm going to lay out what's established, what's disputed, and why — rather than
> take a position. This is one of the most politically loaded areas in social science and
> the reader deserves the actual state of the argument.**

**⚠️ Not seriously disputed:**
- **Children in single-parent households show worse average outcomes on several measures
  — educational attainment, income, some behavioural measures.** ⚠️ **The ASSOCIATION is
  well replicated.**
- **⚠️ Household income and economic stability explain a substantial share of that gap.**
- **⚠️ Family INSTABILITY — the number of transitions in household composition — appears to
  matter independently of structure at any one time.**
- **⚠️ Conflict exposure matters, and high-conflict intact households are not protective**
  (§5 → `family-reading-claims-attachment-and-couple-relationships`).

**⚠️ Genuinely disputed:**
- ⚠️ **How much is causal versus selection.** **Parents who marry and stay married differ
  systematically beforehand.** **Studies using sibling comparisons, natural experiments
  and policy variation reduce estimated effects substantially — how much is contested.**
- ⚠️ **How much is mediated by money.** **Estimates vary widely, and the answer drives very
  different policy conclusions.**
- ⚠️ **Whether marriage per se, or stability and resources, does the work.**
- ⚠️ **What policy follows even from agreed facts** — **a values question, not an empirical
  one, and both sides frequently present it as empirical.**

**⚠️ Where the evidence is clearer than the public argument suggests**: **research on
children of same-sex parents has consistently found outcomes comparable to those of
children with different-sex parents when other factors are accounted for**, **and this is
the position of major professional bodies.**
**⚠️ Read any research in this area for who funded it and what the comparison group is.**
⚠️ **Advocacy organizations across the spectrum produce reports here, and the same
underlying data supports very different-sounding summaries depending on controls.**
