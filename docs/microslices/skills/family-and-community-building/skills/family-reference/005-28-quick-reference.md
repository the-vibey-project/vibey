### 28.2 If you're trying to build connection
- [ ] ⚠️ **Pick something RECURRING over something impressive** (§17 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Optimize for repeated unplanned contact, not for events** (§17 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`, §19 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`)
- [ ] Shared activity or purpose beats socializing as the goal (§18 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Expect it to take dozens of hours. There's no shortcut** (§17 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Bridging ties are worth deliberate effort — they don't form by default** (§16 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`)
- [ ] Rituals and routines carry families through strain (§7 → `family-systems-child-development-and-parenting`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Structure beats willpower — for families and communities alike**
