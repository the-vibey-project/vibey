## §27. Books

| Author | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Putnam** | ***Bowling Alone*** / *Our Kids* | ⚠️ **The foundational social capital work (§16 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`, §24.2)** |
| **Klinenberg** | ***Palaces for the People*** / *Heat Wave* | ⚠️ **§19 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`. Social infrastructure, argued through evidence** |
| **Oldenburg** | *The Great Good Place* | §19 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`'s origin |
| **Sampson** | *Great American City* | ⚠️ **§22 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`. Collective efficacy, rigorously** |
| **Harris** | ***The Nurture Assumption*** | ⚠️ **§10 → `family-behaviour-genetics-structure-and-caregiving`, provocative and important — read with the critiques** |
| **Plomin** | *Blueprint* | ⚠️ **Behaviour genetics from an advocate; read alongside critics** |
| **Masten** | *Ordinary Magic* | ⚠️ **§14 → `family-behaviour-genetics-structure-and-caregiving`. The best resilience treatment** |
| **Gottman** | *The Seven Principles* | ⚠️ **Useful observations; discount the prediction claims (§3 → `family-reading-claims-attachment-and-couple-relationships`)** |
| **Doherty** | *The Intentional Family* | §7 → `family-systems-child-development-and-parenting`'s rituals and routines |
| **Alinsky** | *Rules for Radicals* | ⚠️ **§21 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`, and read a critique alongside** |
| **Kretzmann & McKnight** | *Building Communities from the Inside Out* | ABCD (§21 → `family-social-capital-community-and-program-evaluation`) |
| **Vaillant** | *Triumphs of Experience* | ⚠️ **The Harvard longitudinal study — relationships and aging** |
