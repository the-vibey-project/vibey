## §4. Conflict and Repair

**⚠️ Conflict is not the problem; unrepaired conflict is.** ⚠️ **All couples fight, and
Gottman's estimate that a large majority of couple conflicts are PERPETUAL — rooted in
enduring personality and value differences rather than solvable — reframes the goal from
resolution to dialogue.**
```
⚠️ SOFT STARTUP    how a conversation begins strongly predicts how it ends
⚠️ REPAIR ATTEMPTS bids to de-escalate. ⚠️ Whether they're ACCEPTED matters
                   more than whether they're skillfully made
⚠️ FLOODING        physiological arousal that makes productive talk impossible.
                   ⚠️ Take a real break — 20+ minutes — and RETURN
DEMAND-WITHDRAW    ⚠️ the most-documented destructive pattern. One pursues,
                   one retreats, each escalating the other
```
**⚠️ Accountability without self-abasement is the useful repair posture**: **acknowledge
the specific thing, don't perform contrition, don't counter-attack.** ⚠️ **Over-apology and
defensiveness fail for the same reason — both move attention off the actual issue.**
**⚠️ On violence, stated plainly**: ⚠️ **intimate partner violence is not a communication
problem and couples counselling is generally contraindicated where it is present.** **It
is a safety matter requiring specialized resources.**
