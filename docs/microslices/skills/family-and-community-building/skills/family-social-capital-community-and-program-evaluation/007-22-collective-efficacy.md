## §22. Collective Efficacy

**⚠️ Sampson's construct — social cohesion PLUS shared willingness to intervene for the
common good — is one of the better-supported findings in community research.**
⚠️ **It predicts lower neighbourhood violence and better outcomes even controlling for
poverty and demographic composition.**
**⚠️ The key insight is that cohesion alone is insufficient**: ⚠️ **you need both trust AND
the expectation that neighbours will act** — **that someone would say something if children
were skipping school or a fight broke out.** **Warm but passive neighbourhoods don't get
the effect.**
**⚠️ What builds it**: **residential stability (⚠️ high turnover undermines it
mechanically), physical spaces that support encounter (§19), local organizations, and
homeownership or long tenancies.** ⚠️ **Which means displacement and housing instability
have community-level costs beyond the individual household.**
**⚠️ Broken windows is the adjacent theory and is much weaker** (see a psychology reference
§21) — ⚠️ **the collective efficacy research suggests informal social control by residents,
not disorder policing, is what does the work.**
