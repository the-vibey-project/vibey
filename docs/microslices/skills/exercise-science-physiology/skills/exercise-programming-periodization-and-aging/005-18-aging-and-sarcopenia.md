## §18. Aging and Sarcopenia

**⚠️ Sarcopenia — age-related loss of muscle mass and strength — begins measurably in
midlife and accelerates.** ⚠️ **STRENGTH declines faster than mass, and POWER faster than
strength** — **which is why power and rate of force development matter most for
function.**
**⚠️ What follows practically**: ⚠️ **resistance training is effective at every age tested,
including in the very old and frail** — **this is one of the better-established findings
in the field.** **Older adults show anabolic resistance (⚠️ requiring a larger protein dose
per meal to produce the same muscle protein synthesis response), so protein distribution
matters more with age.**
**⚠️ Falls are the outcome that matters**: **balance training, power work and lower-limb
strength reduce fall risk, and multicomponent programmes have the best evidence.**
**⚠️ Bone**: **loading is the stimulus — ⚠️ impact and heavy resistance loading maintain
bone mineral density; swimming and cycling do not, which is worth knowing for anyone
whose only training is non-weight-bearing.**
