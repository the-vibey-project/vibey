## §20. ⚠️ Supplements — Evidence Tiers

> **⚠️ The supplement industry is large, lightly regulated in the US (⚠️ marketed largely
> without pre-market efficacy or safety approval), and funds much of its own supporting
> research** (§1 → `exercise-physiology-muscle-energy-and-body-systems`). **⚠️ Contamination with banned substances is a documented and recurring
> problem — third-party testing (NSF Certified for Sport, Informed Sport) matters for
> anyone subject to testing.**

```
⚠️ STRONG EVIDENCE — small number of things
  CREATINE MONOHYDRATE  ⚠️ the best-evidenced ergogenic aid there is.
     Improves high-intensity repeated-effort performance and training
     adaptations; extremely well studied for safety; ⚠️ cheap; ⚠️ monohydrate
     is as good as any expensive form. ⚠️ Does not "damage kidneys" in
     healthy people — that claim has been examined repeatedly
  CAFFEINE      ⚠️ robust performance benefit; tolerance and sleep costs are real
  PROTEIN POWDER ⚠️ food in convenient form. Works if it closes a real gap
  BETA-ALANINE  ⚠️ modest, specific to ~1–4 min high-intensity efforts
  NITRATE/BEETROOT  ⚠️ modest endurance benefit, mainly in less-trained people
  BICARBONATE   ⚠️ real for specific efforts; GI distress is common

⚠️ WEAK, MIXED, OR CONTEXT-DEPENDENT
  HMB · BCAAs (⚠️ largely pointless with adequate total protein) ·
  most "testosterone boosters" · most pre-workout blends beyond their
  caffeine content · glutamine · tart cherry (⚠️ modest for soreness —
  and see §13's blunting concern) · collagen (⚠️ some tendon evidence)

⚠️ VITAMINS AND MINERALS
  ⚠️ Correct a DEFICIENCY and you'll see benefit; supplement beyond
  sufficiency and you generally won't. ⚠️ Vitamin D and iron are the
  common real deficiencies — ⚠️ test rather than guess, and iron
  supplementation without deficiency is harmful
```
**⚠️ The ordering point from §16 → `exercise-programming-periodization-and-aging` stands: supplements are the last few percent, and they
only exist as a percentage of a foundation that's already there.**
