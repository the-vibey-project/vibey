## §7. ⚠️ Road Safety

> **⚠️ §1 → `infra-geometric-design-pavement-drainage-and-traffic`'s third organizing idea, and the area where the paradigm genuinely changed.**
```
⚠️ ⚠️ THE PHYSICS  ⚠️ kinetic energy = ½mv². ⚠️ Energy scales
   with the SQUARE of speed, while human injury tolerance is a
   FIXED biological limit
   ⚠️ ⚠️ THE APPROXIMATE SURVIVAL THRESHOLDS that drive design
   ⚠️ Pedestrian struck by a car: survivable around 30 km/h
      (20 mph); ⚠️ fatality risk rises steeply above it
   ⚠️ Side impact: around 50 km/h · Head-on: around 70 km/h
   ⚠️ ⚠️ THEREFORE SPEED LIMITS SHOULD FOLLOW THE CRASH TYPES
   POSSIBLE ON THAT ROAD. ⚠️ Where vehicles and people mix,
   30 km/h is not arbitrary caution — it is the biomechanical
   threshold
⚠️ ⚠️ THE SAFE SYSTEM APPROACH — the paradigm shift
   ⚠️ OLD MODEL  ⚠️ crashes are caused by bad drivers; fix the
      driver through education and enforcement
   ⚠️ ⚠️ SAFE SYSTEM  ⚠️ HUMANS WILL MAKE MISTAKES; the system
      should be designed so that mistakes are not fatal.
      ⚠️ Responsibility is SHARED between users and those who
      design and operate the system
   ⚠️ THE PILLARS  safe speeds · safe roads · safe vehicles ·
      safe users · post-crash care
   ⚠️ ⚠️ AND THE ETHICAL PREMISE: NO DEATH IS ACCEPTABLE as the
      cost of mobility. ⚠️ Vision Zero follows from this
⚠️ ⚠️ SELF-ENFORCING DESIGN beats enforcement  ⚠️ if the geometry
   invites 50 mph, a 30 mph sign does very little (§2).
   ⚠️ Lane narrowing, deflection, vertical deflection and visual
   friction change behaviour continuously
⚠️ THE COUNTERMEASURE EVIDENCE  ⚠️ roundabouts (§6) · median
   barriers · rumble strips · ⚠️ ROAD DIETS (4 lanes to 3 with a
   turn lane — well-evidenced crash reductions, frequently
   contested politically) · lighting · protected cycle
   infrastructure
⚠️ ⚠️ CRASH MIGRATION AND REGRESSION TO THE MEAN are the two
   evaluation traps — ⚠️ sites are selected because they had a
   bad year, so they improve anyway. ⚠️ Serious before/after
   studies use comparison groups
```
