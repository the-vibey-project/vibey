## §8. Construction and Maintenance

**⚠️ Work zones** are their own safety problem — ⚠️ **for workers and drivers, and temporary
traffic control is a designed system with its own standards.**
**⚠️ Staging and phasing** determine whether a project is a nuisance or a catastrophe for
the corridor, ⚠️ **and night work, accelerated construction and full closure with detour
are the trade being made between duration and disruption.**
**⚠️ ACCELERATED BRIDGE CONSTRUCTION** — ⚠️ **prefabricated elements, self-propelled modular
transporters, slide-in replacement — moves work off the critical path of traffic and is one
of the genuine recent advances** (§9 → `infra-bridges-types-loads-failure-modes-and-inspection`).
**⚠️ Quality assurance**: ⚠️ **materials testing, density and smoothness specifications, and
increasingly performance-based specifications that pay for outcomes.**
**⚠️ Maintenance activities** — ⚠️ **crack sealing, patching, striping, sweeping, vegetation,
winter operations — are unglamorous, chronically underfunded, and where §21 → `infra-procurement-cost-asset-management-funding-and-equity`'s arithmetic
actually plays out.**
