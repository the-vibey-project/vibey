## §15. ⚠️ Willpower

```
⚠️ ⚠️ EGO DEPLETION — ⚠️ the claim that self-control draws on a
   limited resource that depletes with use, and that glucose
   restores it — WAS THE DOMINANT MODEL FOR TWO DECADES
⚠️ ⚠️ IT LARGELY FAILED TO REPLICATE. ⚠️ Large preregistered
   multi-lab studies found effects near zero, and the glucose
   version fared particularly badly
⚠️ ⚠️ WHAT SEEMS TO REMAIN  ⚠️ effort has real subjective costs
   and motivational and attentional accounts fit the data
   better than a depleting fuel does. ⚠️ People shift what they
   want to do rather than becoming unable to do it
⚠️ ⚠️ THE PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE IS ACTUALLY LIBERATING
   ⚠️ If self-control were a fuel tank, the strategy would be
      rationing and building capacity
   ⚠️ ⚠️ IF IT IS ABOUT MOTIVATION, ATTENTION AND SITUATION, the
      strategy is DESIGNING SITUATIONS THAT REQUIRE LESS OF IT
      (§14) — ⚠️ and the trait-self-control research supports
      this: ⚠️ people who score high on self-control report
      resisting FEWER temptations, not winning more fights.
      They have arranged their lives to avoid the fights
⚠️ ⚠️ THEREFORE  ⚠️ "I lack discipline" is usually a
   misdiagnosis of an environment problem
```
