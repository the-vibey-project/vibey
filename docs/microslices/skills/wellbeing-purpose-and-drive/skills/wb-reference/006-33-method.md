## §33. Method

**§2–§27 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`, `wb-what-holds-up-relationships-sleep-and-attention`, `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`, `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`, `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique` rests on a research literature that I have deliberately reported with its error
bars visible** — **which is itself the main editorial decision here.** ⚠️ **A confident,
actionable version of this document would be easy to write and would misrepresent the
evidence.**

**Two searches were run in August 2026**, on **positive psychology intervention effect
sizes** and **social connection** — ⚠️ **the first because this field's headline numbers are
the ones most likely to be inflated and I wanted the reanalyses rather than the originals,
the second because §8 → `wb-what-holds-up-relationships-sleep-and-attention` is the strongest claim in the document and deserved primary
sourcing.**

**⚠️ The contrast between the two searches is the most useful thing here.** ⚠️ **§28.1 is a
literature where the effects shrank every time someone looked harder — smaller studies
giving bigger effects, low study quality, control-group choices inflating results.**
⚠️ **§28.2 is a large effect appearing consistently across many large populations, from a
three-year WHO commission, without depending on small intervention trials.** ⚠️ **Both are
"the science says," and they are not the same kind of claim.**

**Confidence.** **High** in §6 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`, §15 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower` and §26 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`, which are the sections I'd most want read.
⚠️ **§6 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication` because knowing which specific findings failed — ego depletion, power posing's
hormonal claims, the positivity ratio, most of grit's promise — is what lets you read the
rest of the genre.** ⚠️ **§15 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower` because the replacement model is genuinely more useful: people
with high self-control report facing FEWER temptations, not winning more fights, which
relocates the work from character to environment.**
⚠️ **§26 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique` because the field's characteristic failure is treating structural conditions as
mindset problems — and because the clinical threshold is a real line where this material
becomes the wrong tool rather than a weaker one.**

**Moderate** on §4 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`'s heritability figures and on most point estimates throughout —
⚠️ **flagged in §30 and worth holding loosely as a general policy in this domain.**

**High** on §18 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`'s meaning-happiness divergence, which is the single most useful distinction
here and has held up: ⚠️ **meaning correlates with giving, with connecting past to future,
and with MORE stress and struggle — so the most meaningful things people do often reduce
their moment-to-moment happiness, and "will this make me happy?" is the wrong question for
most large decisions.**
