## §30. Numbers

```
⚠️ ⚠️ HOLD ALL EFFECT SIZES LOOSELY (§6). Directions are more
   reliable than magnitudes
⚠️ Wellbeing heritability  ⚠️ often ~30-50% of variance —
   ⚠️ a population statistic, NOT an individual ceiling
⚠️ Income  ⚠️ LOGARITHMIC — proportional increases, not absolute
⚠️ Habit formation  ⚠️ median ~66 days, range ~18-254

   than previously reported"
⚠️ 2025 mega-analysis  ⚠️ 198 meta-analyses, >500,000
   participants → small-to-medium

⚠️ ⚠️ Quality signal  ⚠️ larger effects in smaller, lower-quality
   studies — what publication bias looks like from inside
⚠️ ⚠️ LONELINESS  ⚠️ 1 in 6 worldwide (~16%, 2014-2023, WHO)
⚠️ ⚠️ Attributed deaths  ⚠️ ~871,000/year, ~100/hour (WHO estimate)

   older men just under 10%
⚠️ WHO report  ⚠️ 30 June 2025 · WHA resolution May 2025
```
