## §32. Quick Reference

### 32.1 Picker
| Question | Where |
|---|---|
| Will this decision make me happy? | ⚠️ **Wrong question. Ask about meaning too** (§18 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`) |
| Why can't I make myself do it? | ⚠️ **Emotion, not discipline. Change the situation** (§15 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`, §17 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`) |
| I lack willpower | ⚠️ **Probably an environment problem** (§14 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`, §15 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`) |
| Should I take the higher-paying job? | ⚠️ **Logarithmic, and security matters more than level** (§5 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`) |
| Is this claim about wellbeing true? | ⚠️ **Check sample size, control group, preregistration** (§6 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`, §28.1) |
| I'm burned out | ⚠️ **Look at workload, control, fairness — not resilience** (§24 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`) |
| How do I motivate someone? | ⚠️ **Autonomy, competence, relatedness. Support internalization** (§11 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`) |
| Nothing feels meaningful | ⚠️ **Coherence, purpose, or mattering — which one?** (§18 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`) |
| How do I build connection as an adult? | ⚠️ **Repeated unplanned contact and recurring structure** (§8 → `wb-what-holds-up-relationships-sleep-and-attention`) |
| Should I turn my hobby into a business? | ⚠️ **Overjustification is real; calling is exploitable** (§12 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`, §20 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`) |
| Is this beyond self-help? | ⚠️ **If it's your life not a topic — get a professional view** (§26 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`) |

### 32.2 A calibrated starting list
- [ ] ⚠️ **Sleep first — it's upstream of nearly everything else** (§9 → `wb-what-holds-up-relationships-sleep-and-attention`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **One recurring structure that puts you near the same people repeatedly** (§8 → `wb-what-holds-up-relationships-sleep-and-attention`)
- [ ] Movement, at a modest dose you'll actually sustain (§9 → `wb-what-holds-up-relationships-sleep-and-attention`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Design the environment rather than relying on discipline** (§14 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`, §15 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`)
- [ ] Implementation intentions for anything that keeps not happening (§14 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **For a stuck task: shrink the first step until it isn't aversive** (§17 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Check autonomy, competence and relatedness where you're flat** (§11 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`)
- [ ] Ask what's meaningful separately from what's enjoyable (§18 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Name which constraints are structural — those need different action** (§26 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Don't measure and optimize your own wellbeing continuously** (§27 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`)
