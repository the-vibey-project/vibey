## §7. ⚠️ What Actually Holds Up

**⚠️ The list is shorter and duller than the bookshelf suggests, which is itself
informative.**
**⚠️ Reasonably robust:**
- ⚠️ **SOCIAL CONNECTION as a major factor in both wellbeing and physical health** (§8,
  §28.2 → `wb-reference`) — ⚠️ **the strongest single finding in the field.**
- ⚠️ **SLEEP, and its bidirectional relationship with mood** (§9).
- ⚠️ **PHYSICAL ACTIVITY, with small-to-moderate effects on mood and depressive symptoms**
  (§9).
- ⚠️ **The impact bias in affective forecasting** (§4 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`).
- ⚠️ **Logarithmic income effects** (§5 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`).
- ⚠️ **Unemployment's large and poorly-adapted-to negative effect** (§4 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication`, §26 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`).
- ⚠️ **Autonomy, competence and relatedness as motivational needs, with a large and
  reasonably well-replicated literature** (§11 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`).
- ⚠️ **Implementation intentions for behaviour change** (§14 → `wb-self-determination-motivation-goals-habits-and-willpower`).
- ⚠️ **Procedural fairness and control at work** (§20 → `wb-flow-procrastination-meaning-purpose-and-adversity`, §24 → `wb-interventions-burnout-limits-and-the-industry-critique`).

**⚠️ Real but smaller than advertised**: ⚠️ **gratitude practices, mindfulness, savouring,
acts of kindness — ⚠️ these do something, and the something is modest** (§28.1 → `wb-reference`).
**⚠️ Weak, contested or failed**: ⚠️ **the §6 → `wb-traditions-measurement-adaptation-money-and-replication` list.**
