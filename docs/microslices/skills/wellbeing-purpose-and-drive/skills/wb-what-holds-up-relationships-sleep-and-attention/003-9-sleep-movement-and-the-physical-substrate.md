## §9. Sleep, Movement and the Physical Substrate

**⚠️ Sleep** is bidirectional with mood — ⚠️ **poor sleep worsens mood, low mood worsens
sleep — and sleep deprivation experiments show reliable effects on emotional regulation and
on the negative interpretation of ambiguous events.**
**⚠️ CBT-I (cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia)** has a strong evidence base and is
⚠️ **the recommended first-line treatment for chronic insomnia ahead of medication.**
**⚠️ Physical activity** shows ⚠️ **small-to-moderate effects on depressive symptoms and mood
across many trials, with the honest caveats that publication bias applies here too, that
blinding is impossible, and that effect sizes shrink in higher-quality studies.**
⚠️ **The dose that has evidence is modest; the "exercise is better than antidepressants"
claim outruns it.**
**⚠️ Daylight, particularly morning light,** for circadian regulation (see a
thermodynamics/biology reference on circadian mechanisms).
**⚠️ Alcohol** is worth naming plainly: ⚠️ **it is a depressant, it degrades sleep
architecture even at doses that do not feel disruptive, and using it for mood regulation has
a well-documented trajectory.**
