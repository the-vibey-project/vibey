## §18. ⚠️ Meaning and Happiness Come Apart

> **⚠️ The third organizing idea above, and the most useful distinction in this document.**
```
⚠️ ⚠️ THEY CORRELATE AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME. ⚠️ Baumeister
   and colleagues' work identified systematic divergences
   ⚠️ HAPPINESS was associated with ⚠️ getting what you want,
      health, present-focus, and being a TAKER of benefits
   ⚠️ ⚠️ MEANING was associated with ⚠️ connecting past, present
      and future; ⚠️ being a GIVER; ⚠️ self-expression and
      identity; ⚠️ AND with more stress, worry and struggle
⚠️ ⚠️ THEREFORE SOME OF THE MOST MEANINGFUL THINGS PEOPLE DO
   RELIABLY LOWER MOMENT-TO-MOMENT HAPPINESS. ⚠️ Raising small
   children is the canonical example, and it holds up: parents
   often report lower daily affect and higher meaning
   ⚠️ ⚠️ SO "WILL THIS MAKE ME HAPPY?" IS THE WRONG QUESTION FOR
   MOST LARGE LIFE DECISIONS
⚠️ ⚠️ THE THREE COMPONENTS OF MEANING, in the standard account
   ⚠️ COHERENCE  ⚠️ life makes sense; events fit a narrative
   ⚠️ PURPOSE  ⚠️ direction, valued goals, a future orientation
   ⚠️ ⚠️ SIGNIFICANCE / MATTERING  ⚠️ my existence matters.
      ⚠️ Arguably the one most people are actually missing, and
      the one least addressed by advice about goals
⚠️ ⚠️ MEANING IS MORE STABLE THAN HAPPINESS  ⚠️ it survives
   adversity better, and it can be present during suffering,
   which is why §21 is not a paradox
⚠️ ⚠️ EXPERIENTIAL APPRECIATION has been proposed as a fourth
   component — ⚠️ meaning found in the moment, not in the
   narrative — which addresses a real gap in the other three
```
