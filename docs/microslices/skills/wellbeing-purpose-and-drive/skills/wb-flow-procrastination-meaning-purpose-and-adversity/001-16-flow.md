## §16. Flow

**⚠️ Csikszentmihalyi's construct**: ⚠️ **complete absorption in an activity, with the
conditions being clear goals, immediate feedback, and a match between challenge and skill.**
**⚠️ The phenomenology** — ⚠️ **loss of self-consciousness, altered time perception, and the
activity feeling autotelic — is consistently reported and is a genuine contribution.**
**⚠️ The evidential status is mixed**: ⚠️ **the descriptive account is well grounded in
experience sampling; the causal claims about flow producing wellbeing are harder to
establish, and the challenge-skill balance model has been questioned in detail.**
**⚠️ The practical value is real regardless**: ⚠️ **the conditions are actionable — make the
goal clear, get feedback fast, adjust difficulty, remove interruption.**
**⚠️ Note that flow is not the same as enjoyment**, ⚠️ **and people frequently report flow in
activities they would not choose again — which connects to §18.**
