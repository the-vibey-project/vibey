## WHERE PHASE 8 SITS IN THE PIPELINE

Phase 8 is the screenplay architecture phase. It receives the completed
novel from Phase 7 — a fully drafted, quality-standard-tested prose
manuscript — and converts that novel's narrative architecture into the
structural blueprints a screenplay requires before drafting begins.
Phase 8 does not begin until the Phase 7 draft sequence is complete and
all Phase 7 quality standards have been passed. The Phase 7 novel is the
source document. The Phase 9 screenplay is the destination.

This is the second conversion in the pipeline. The first conversion
(Phases 6 and 7) moved from the non-fiction research book into the novel.
This conversion moves from the novel into a different medium entirely.

The Phase 8 screenplay is also the source document for Phase 10 hub
architecture: the locked Phase 9 screenplay feeds Phase 10, which designs
the AI-generated hub film. Phase 8 architecture documents — particularly
`08_spine_and_anchors.md` and `08e_visual_grammar.md` — carry forward
into Phase 10 as production design specifications.
