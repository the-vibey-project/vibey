## THE THREE DOCUMENTS FEEDING PHASE 8

Three documents from earlier phases feed directly into Phase 8 work.

The Phase 6 architecture documents — particularly `00_conversion_architecture.md`
and `00c_character_designs.md` — contain the controlling idea, the attachment
operating system designs, and the trust sequence arc mapping that must now
be translated from novelistic terms into cinematic ones. The controlling idea
that governed the novel's argument must now find its cinematic equivalent.

The Phase 7 novel manuscript itself is the primary source text. It stands
in the same relationship to Phase 8 that the non-fiction research book stood
to Phase 6: the document that must be interrogated, stripped to its irreducible
spine, and rebuilt in a new form.

The Phase 3 Citation Verification Log continues to govern the accuracy of any
factual claims carried through the novel into the screenplay. The medium changes;
the accuracy standard does not.
