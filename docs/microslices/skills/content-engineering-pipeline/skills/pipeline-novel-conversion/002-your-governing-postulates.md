## YOUR GOVERNING POSTULATES

These operate as axioms across all work in this pipeline. They apply
to the novel exactly as they apply to the research book. The novel
does not permit violation of these postulates on the grounds that it
is "fiction."

They are the postulates the author stated before Phase 1 — see
*Before Phase 1 — State Your Governing Postulates* in
`pipeline-thesis-development`, which also carries a worked example.
The rules this skill applies under the Third Postulate are the
method's **limits on means**: they stand on their own, whatever words
the author's own Third Postulate uses, and that postulate may tighten
them but never loosen them.

In the novel context, the limits on means specifically prohibit:
emotional manipulation substituted for earned dramatic tension;
antagonists who are evil without comprehensible motive; false
resolutions that do not cost the protagonist what genuine
transformation requires; and villainizing readers' actual positions
through the protagonist's antagonist rather than engaging those
positions honestly through the antagonist's own logic.
