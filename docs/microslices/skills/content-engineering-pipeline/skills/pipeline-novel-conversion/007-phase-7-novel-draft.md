## PHASE 7 — NOVEL DRAFT

### What Phase 7 Actually Is

Phase 7 converts the complete Phase 6 architecture documents into
prose. The architecture documents do not recede in authority once
prose begins. Every scene is measured against the scene map. Every
character choice is measured against the character design. Every
relational moment is measured against the trust sequence arc.

The Phase 6 architecture is not a plan that the novel grows beyond —
it is the structure the novel inhabits. Departure from it requires
documented justification and revision of the architecture first.

The Phase 3 Citation Verification Log continues to govern accuracy.
The novel may fictionalize surface particulars but does not distort
the verified findings underlying its argument.

### The Limits on Means Applied to Drafting

The limits on means govern the specific temptation drafting
introduces: using narrative power in excess of what the evidence
supports. A compelling scene can make a finding feel more certain
than it is. A dramatic framing can make an argument's limitations
invisible. Every drafting decision that inflates a claim beyond
what Phase 3 investigation supported is a withdrawal from the
book's long-term authority.

### The Ten Mechanics as Drafting Tools

> See also: `writing-craft:narrative-structure` (the hero's journey and
> story architecture) and `entertainment-comedy-drama-storytelling`'s
> `story-rules-character-structure-and-scene` and
> `story-dialogue-point-of-view-theme-exposition-and-endings`, which
> treat the same craft on its own terms.

**Mechanic One: Setting**
The Setting Bible from Phase 6 contains the full sensory design.
Phase 7 executes those designs with five-sense specificity that
creates full neurological simulation rather than symbolic processing.

The synchrony scene toolkit (from Phase 6's Setting Bible) is
executed here at full dramatic weight:
- Shared meals — activate physical co-presence, oxytocin-triggering
  eye contact, synchronized consumption, and ritualized reciprocity
- Synchronous physical activity — activates mirror neuron resonance
  and cooperative trust; two characters building something together
  are doing relational work that talking about their relationship
  cannot do
- Shared adversity — the most powerful bonding mechanism; the
  relationship bond that years of pleasant interaction cannot produce
- Communal worship, singing, or rhythmic movement — activates the
  social endorphin system; the neurological explanation for why
  communal worship across every human culture has included sung
  corporate response
- Family storytelling — activates the bonding mechanism that
  produces felt security through relational continuity

**Mechanic Two: Theme**
The controlling idea must be discoverable from what happens to the
characters — not from what they say to each other. When a character
voices the theme directly in dialogue, the novel has failed the
conversion. The theme should be felt before it is understood, and
understood before it is named. If it is named at all, name it only
after it has been demonstrated through irreversible plot consequence.

**Mechanic Three: Plot Architecture**
Blake Snyder's fifteen-beat Save the Cat structure was mapped onto
the research book's logical sequence in Phase 6. Draft in scene map
order throughout: the Setup embodies the research book's diagnosis;
the Catalyst delivers the inciting condition; the Break into Two
commits the protagonist to the book's central question; the All Is
Lost delivers the moment when every conventional approach has failed
and the book's most counterintuitive argument becomes the only
remaining option; the Break into Three delivers the synthesis; the
Finale demonstrates that synthesis in action.

**Mechanic Four: Character Development**
The protagonist was designed with a wound that makes the research
book's central question personal and unavoidable. Phase 7 ensures
findings are things the protagonist discovers through lived
experience, not through reading or being told. Every major finding
must be earned by the protagonist before the reader receives it.

The antagonist embodies the position the research book most seriously
contends with, drawn from Phase 2's steelmanned opposing arguments.
The antagonist is not wrong in every way; is wrong about the one
thing that matters most; is wrong for reasons the reader can
understand.

**Mechanic Five: Conflict**
All five conflict types should appear in a full-length conversion:
person-versus-person (the antagonist who embodies the opposing
position), person-versus-self (the protagonist's internal resistance
to the book's central truth), person-versus-society (the
institutional forces protecting the status quo), person-versus-nature
(environmental stakes appropriate to the setting), and if the book's
argument touches on anything transcendent, person-versus-fate-or-God
(the protagonist's honest wrestling with what the book ultimately
asks of them).

**Mechanic Six: Point of View**
Close third-person limited POV performs best for most conversions —
emotional proximity of first person without the credibility limitation
of a narrator who knows too much too early. The protagonist is the
focalizing consciousness, because the reader's identification arc
must track the protagonist's discovery arc.

**Mechanic Seven: Dialogue**
The research book's arguments must never appear as dialogue. The
most common failure mode in research-to-novel conversion: a character
delivers a speech that sounds like a book chapter, and the fiction
collapses into illustrated lecture. The research book's central
claims become subtext — the reality characters navigate without
being able to name.

Dialogue must accomplish at least two of McKee's three functions
simultaneously: reveal character, advance plot, or deepen conflict.

Relational conflict dialogue deploys Gottman's Four Horsemen with
precision:
- **Criticism** — attacks the person's character rather than a
  specific behavior; sounds like cold judgment, not heat
- **Contempt** — the single strongest predictor of relationship
  failure; communicates fundamental disrespect; cold and dismissive,
  not explosive; "I see what you are, and what you are is beneath
  me"
- **Defensiveness** — responds to feedback with counter-attack or
  victim posturing; every line redirects away from the matter at
  hand; produces conversation that spirals rather than progresses
- **Stonewalling** — withdrawal; monosyllabic or absent; the
  cortisol-flooded brain's shutdown when physiological arousal
  exceeds the threshold for prefrontal cortex function; write from
  the other character's experience — what it feels like to speak
  into a silence that used to speak back

Each horseman has a specific antidote governing the novel's repair
scenes: gentle startup (criticism), culture of appreciation
(contempt), taking responsibility (defensiveness), physiological
self-soothing with honest naming of flooding and request for time
(stonewalling — the twenty-minute minimum is not arbitrary).

The Gottman 5:1 ratio applies to the novel's overall relational arc:
the relational bank account that allows a relationship to survive
conflict and repair is built outside of conflict through accumulated
warm interactions. A novel whose relational arc consists primarily
of conflict is a novel whose relationship the reader will not believe.

**Mechanic Eight: Pacing**
The most common pacing error in novel conversion: treating every
finding as equally Scene-worthy. Reserve full scene treatment for
genuine turning points. Compress supporting material to summary,
dialogue, or setting detail. At the sentence level: short sentences
for urgency, shock, or intense action; long cadenced sentences for
contemplation, grief, or the slow dawning of understanding.

**Mechanic Nine: Literary Devices**
- **Foreshadowing** — every major finding should be planted as a
  seed early in the novel that will mean something different the
  second time the reader encounters it
- **Chekhov's Gun** — every piece of research-derived material
  introduced creates a promise of significance; do not include a
  finding that does not ultimately matter to the plot
- **Dramatic irony** — the primary device for carrying the research
  book's argument without stating it; the reader sees what the
  characters cannot yet see
- **Motif** — the recurring image, phrase, or symbol accumulating
  meaning across the novel; design it to carry the research book's
  most important thread; gather all the novel's evidence onto itself
  by the final page

**Mechanic Ten: Emotional Arc**
The emotional arc planned in Phase 6 is executed here at the scene
level, maintaining the contrast effects designed deliberately. The
cortisol peak of the crisis lands harder after the oxytocin warmth
of the preceding connection scene. The dopamine release of the
eucatastrophe fires more completely after the sustained despair of
the dark night. These are not aesthetic choices. They are the
engineering of the reader's physiological experience.

### The Draft Sequence

Draft in this order without exception. The sequence is governed by
the logical dependencies of the material.

**Step 1:** Phase 6 gate confirmation — all five architecture
documents must be complete; if any Phase 6 gate condition is
unchecked, return to Phase 6

**Step 2:** Opening and closing images — both before writing
anything in between; the architectural anchors of the entire
emotional arc

**Step 3:** The eucatastrophe scene — write the climactic scene
before the scenes leading to it; the destination must be written
before the road; writing it first also ensures that the emotional
arc of preceding scenes is calibrated correctly

**Step 4:** Act One scenes, in sequence — the ordinary world must
be established with sufficient warmth and specificity that the reader
invests in it before the disruption begins; the ordinary world is
not filler — it is the emotional foundation that gives every
subsequent loss its weight

**Step 5:** Act Two scenes, in sequence — the escalation of conflict
must build causally; non-linear drafting in Act Two produces scenes
that feel disconnected because the characters' psychological states
do not accumulate as they should

**Step 6:** Act Three scenes, in sequence — the emotional payoff of
each beat depends on the accumulated weight of all previous beats

**Step 7:** Scene openings, revised — after drafting the full novel,
return to the opening of each scene; scene openings are almost always
wrong on first draft because you do not yet know precisely what the
scene proves

**Step 8:** The first chapter, revised last — must earn, set up,
and promise everything the novel delivers; can only be written
accurately against a completed manuscript; must establish all five
sensory registers of the dominant setting, introduce the protagonist
in their controlling belief and want, plant the need without naming
it, and create the emotional baseline all subsequent contrast effects
will measure against

**Step 9:** Revision passes, in sequence with single focus per pass

### Quality Standards

**The Conversion Integrity Test** — applied to every scene:
- Could this scene exist in a novel not based on the source research
  book? If yes, confirm it is earning its place through narrative
  function, not merely thematic relevance
- Does this scene state the research book's findings or prove them
  through action? More than four consecutive lines of dialogue that
  could appear without significant alteration in the source book
  means the scene has collapsed into illustrated lecture
- Is the research book's intellectual architecture visible through
  the novel's surface? If a knowledgeable reader would immediately
  recognize the chapter-by-chapter correspondence, the conversion
  has not gone deep enough

**The Archetype Integrity Test:**
- Is every major structural beat of the selected archetype present?
  Three most commonly missing beats: Refusal of the Call, Mentor's
  withdrawal, and the full Resurrection beat
- Does the protagonist make choices rather than have things happen
  to them? Research books describe things that happen to people;
  every major plot turn must be produced by a choice the protagonist
  makes, even under constraint
- Is the protagonist genuinely different at the end from the
  beginning, and is that difference the direct and earned emotional
  result of what the story put them through?

**The Relational Arc Test:**
- Does the primary relational arc follow the trust sequence mapped
  in Phase 6 Conversion Audit Step 6?
- Does the vulnerability-before-trust leap exist, with genuine
  stakes, and is the response to the vulnerability the turning
  point the arc requires?
- Is at least one rupture and repair cycle present? A relational
  arc without rupture and repair is a relationship that has not
  been tested and cannot carry the weight of the novel's central
  claim
- Does the relational bank account exist? Substantially more
  positive interaction scenes than conflict scenes

**The Neurodivergent Authenticity Test** — applied to every scene
with a neurodivergent character:
- Does the neurodivergent character's behavior follow from their
  designed neurological profile or from neurotypical relational
  defaults?
- For autistic characters: is the masking dynamic present in every
  neurotypical-majority scene, even as background cost?
- For ADHD characters: when executive function fails, is it written
  from inside the character's experience of the gap between knowing
  and doing? Is it written as not resolvable by motivation alone?
- Is the double empathy principle honored in cross-neurotype
  conflict scenes? Both sides of the communication failure present?

**The Craft Standard Checklist** — before finalizing any chapter:
- Does the chapter opening arrest within the first sentence?
- Is the destination named within the first 500 words?
- Does every scene connect to the next through "therefore" or "but"?
- Is the chapter's primary emotional target different from the
  preceding chapter's?
- Is there at least one moment in the chapter at which the attentive
  reader would feel awe — not at the craft but at the reality the
  craft is pointing toward?
- Has the chapter cost the protagonist something real?
- Does the chapter's final sentence redirect toward the next chapter
  with sufficient forward momentum?

### Production Awareness

Three Phase 7 drafting decisions have direct downstream consequences
for Phase 14 KDP submission:

1. Use heading styles consistently throughout — Heading 1 for
   chapter titles, Heading 2 for section breaks — rather than
   manually applying formatting; heading styles generate the
   navigational table of contents in the Kindle EPUB
2. Do not hard-code page numbers anywhere in the manuscript; page
   numbers are only finalized after the interior PDF is generated
   at the correct trim size in Phase 14
3. If the novel includes images, source or create them at a minimum
   of 300 DPI from the start
