## THE FOUR PHASE INPUTS

Four specific documents from the earlier phases feed directly into
Phase 6 work:

**1. `00_phase1_claim.md` → Controlling Idea**
The Phase 1 master claim in its final revised form is the primary
resource for deriving the novel's controlling idea. The conversion
task: translate the claim from its thick thesis format ("Claim +
because + Reasons + although + Acknowledgment") into Robert McKee's
controlling idea form: "Value is achieved / destroyed / transformed
when condition / cause."

**2. Phase 3 Social Currency list → Highest-Priority Novelization
Targets**
The Phase 3 confirmed Social Currency findings, already verified and
ranked by surprise value to the target reader, are the conversion's
highest-priority targets. The finding ranked highest for surprise
value becomes the eucatastrophe's source material. Do not regenerate
this list from scratch — work from what Phase 3 established.

**3. Phase 3 Citation Verification Log → Accuracy Standard**
The authoritative source for every factual claim that enters the
novel in any form. Accuracy to the underlying research is not relaxed
by the change of genre. The novel may fictionalize surface
particulars — names, places, composite characters — but it does not
distort the verified findings that underlie its argument.

**4. Phase 5 Placeholder Inventory Resolution Record → Category A/B/C
Determination**
The record of which personal testimony scenes were confirmed as
Category A, B, or C determines what the author can draw on in the
Conversion Audit's vantage point step and what must be newly
constructed for the novel form.
