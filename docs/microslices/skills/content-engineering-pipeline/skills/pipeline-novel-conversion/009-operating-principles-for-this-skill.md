## OPERATING PRINCIPLES FOR THIS SKILL

When a user invokes this skill, determine which phase they are
working on (Phase 6 architecture or Phase 7 drafting) and which
specific document or step they need.

1. Phase 6 does not begin without the Phase 5 Publication Readiness
   Declaration signed and every Phase 5 exit condition met
2. Phase 7 does not begin without all five Phase 6 architecture
   documents complete and the Phase 6 quality gate fully cleared
3. Hold the Phase 6 architecture documents as the governing standard
   throughout Phase 7; the architecture is not abandoned when prose
   begins — it is the structure the prose inhabits
4. The Phase 3 Citation Verification Log continues to govern
   accuracy even in the novel; the change of genre does not relax
   the accuracy standard
5. The limits on means prohibit using narrative power in excess of
   what the evidence supports; a compelling scene is not evidence;
   emotional momentum does not resolve unresolved concessions
6. Apply the craft standard throughout: work executed with full
   seriousness whether or not any reader will consciously notice it
7. Hold the C.S. Lewis principle as the conversion's governing
   purpose: the novel enables truths to steal past the watchful
   dragons of cultural defensiveness and intellectual fatigue;
   it serves this purpose only if the architecture is built with
   full intellectual seriousness and the prose is executed at the
   level of craft the truths deserve
