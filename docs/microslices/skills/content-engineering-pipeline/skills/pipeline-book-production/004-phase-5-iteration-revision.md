## PHASE 5 — ITERATION (REVISION)

### What Phase 5 Actually Is

Phase 5 is not a single editorial pass. Attempting to simultaneously
evaluate structural integrity, argument quality, voice consistency,
Social Currency positioning, citation accuracy, and personal
testimony authenticity while catching typographical errors produces
a manuscript that has been read carefully once rather than evaluated
systematically six times.

Phase 5 is a sequence of **six distinct passes**, each with a single
primary focus, a specific reference document, and a specific exit
condition. The passes execute in a fixed order derived from logical
dependency:

1. Structural issues, if unresolved, make every subsequent pass do
   work that must be undone when structure is eventually fixed
2. Argument quality depends on structure being sound
3. Consistency depends on the argument being complete
4. Voice depends on consistency being established
5. Social Currency positioning depends on voice being calibrated
6. Placeholder resolution comes last, into a manuscript that has
   resolved every other category of issue

### What Phase 5 Does Not Do

- **Does not rethink the Phase 1 claim** — if the manuscript reveals
  the master claim is fundamentally wrong, return to Phase 1
- **Does not discover new component arguments or conduct new
  research** — if a gap requires Phase 3-level investigation,
  document it, return to Phase 3, then reintroduce Phase 5
- **Does not rewrite chapters from scratch** — fundamental chapter
  failures are Phase 4 failures; return to Phase 4's architecture
  derivation
- **Does not supply personal testimony** — the author supplies
  confirmed scenes; Phase 5 inserts them into a prepared manuscript

### The Limits on Means Applied to Phase 5

The primary application: the temptation to resolve a structural or
argumentative weakness through prose polish rather than through
structural or argumentative correction.

When a theological integration is forced rather than genuine, the
temptation is to write it more elegantly rather than find the genuine
convergence or remove the claim. When an objection has not been
adequately answered, the temptation is to make the response more
confident rather than more honest. When a finding has been over-
claimed, the temptation is to add intensifying language rather than
qualify the claim to match the evidence.

The limits on means prohibit all of these: a better sentence is not
a better argument.

### Pass One: Structural Integrity

**Reference:** Phase 4 architecture document — chapter map with
one-sentence arguments, scalar audit, cross-chapter connection map,
seven required chapter elements.

**Question:** Does this manuscript do what it was designed to do?

Five questions applied to the complete manuscript:

1. Does each chapter's completed body serve the one-sentence
   argument recorded in the Phase 4 architecture document?
2. Does each chapter make the next chapter necessary? (Logical
   dependency, not thematic connection)
3. Does the conclusion's synthesis section name cross-chapter
   connections that are actually present in the manuscript?
4. Does the introduction's chapter guide accurately describe what
   each chapter does?
5. Are all seven required chapter elements present in every chapter?

**Exit condition:** Every chapter's body serves its one-sentence
architectural argument; every chapter-ending transition demonstrates
genuine logical dependency; the conclusion synthesis reflects the
manuscript that exists; the introduction guide accurately describes
the completed chapters; all seven required elements present
in every chapter.

### Pass Two: Argument Quality

**Reference:** Phase 3 argument inventory — evidence inventory, best
objection, response architecture, warrant architecture, theological
integration record.

**Question:** Does this argument earn its conclusion through the
logic and evidence Phase 3 established it could earn?

Seven specific evaluations per major argument:

1. **Evidence characterization** — does the manuscript characterize
   findings with the precision the evidence supports, not the
   precision that sounds best?
2. **Best objection representation** — is the steelmanned version
   from Phase 3 engaged, not a weaker substitute?
3. **Residual force acknowledgment** — is the honest acknowledgment
   of what each response does not resolve present in the manuscript?
4. **Warrant adequacy** — are the warrant statements present where
   Phase 3 identified they must be stated, absent where they don't
   need to be?
5. **Theological integration standard** — does the double-
   independence condition hold for every convergence claim?
6. **Master claim accuracy** — does the manuscript state the claim
   in its Phase 3-revised form, not its Phase 1 original form?
7. **BCW comprehensive standard** — specific, falsifiable claim
   supported by sufficient, accurate, representative, authoritative
   evidence, with explicit engagement of the strongest opposing
   arguments, characterized precisely, with every required warrant
   stated

**Exit condition:** Every major argument in every chapter evaluated
against all seven criteria; every identified failure resolved.

### Pass Three: Consistency

Ten specific standards applied across the full manuscript in
sequence:

1. **Anchor phrase count** — no more than once per chapter, no more
   than once per twenty pages across consecutive chapters, every
   appearance in a structurally distinct argumentative context
2. **Primary study deployment** — any study foundational to multiple
   chapters is deployed at full dramatic weight in exactly one
   chapter; referenced briefly in all others
3. **Key finding over-telegraphing** — findings used as cold-stop
   openings are not previewed in preceding chapters with enough
   specificity to dilute impact
4. **Governing axiom consistency** — every reference to the
   author's stated governing principles uses the same numbered and
   named form established in the introduction
5. **Temporal reference check** — every scene-level temporal
   reference anchored to a specific year or time range (not "last
   year" or "recently")
6. **Formatting tag check** — all callout and pullquote formatting
   uses [CALLOUT] throughout; no [PULLQUOTE] tags
7. **Citation format check** — every citation consistent with the
   Phase 3 Citation Verification Log
8. **Chapter opening check** — every chapter opens with case study,
   personal narrative scene, or research cold-stop; not a
   description of what the chapter will cover
9. **Repetition check** — any finding at significant argumentative
   weight more than three times across the full manuscript has been
   over-deployed
10. **Re-introduction check** — if the introduction established the
    author's biographical arc, no subsequent chapter re-introduces
    that arc from scratch

**Exit condition:** Every item in all ten standards evaluated across
the full manuscript; every identified inconsistency corrected.
Consistency is not a matter of degree — either the standard is met
or it is not.

### Pass Four: Voice and Self-Application

**Focus:** Does the manuscript sound like its author, and does it
practice what it argues?

Five voice tests derived from the voice calibration document:

1. **Register consistency** — no departures into devotional
   softness, academic hedging, or inspirational vagueness; short
   declarative sentences for emphasis, longer for evidence
   accumulation; no hedging language; theological confidence without
   triumphalism; data cited precisely with the study named
2. **Testimony register** — every personal testimony written as
   scene, not summary; any passage writable by someone who had
   read widely about the author without having the specific
   experience is summary masquerading as scene and must be revised
3. **Proportionality** — personal testimony occupies no more than
   15% of any chapter's word count
4. **Disclosure quality** — testimony that discloses failure and
   names the gap between knowing and doing is more valuable than
   testimony demonstrating the author's competence
5. **Self-application** — the five tests from Phase 4's audit
   (virality, awe, honesty, formation, cost) re-applied to the
   revised manuscript

**Exit condition:** Every prose passage matches calibrated voice;
every personal testimony written as genuine scene or marked as
[PLACEHOLDER]; no chapter exceeds 15% testimony threshold; all five
self-application tests pass.

### Pass Five: Social Currency Optimization

**Focus:** Is the manuscript's highest-value content positioned for
maximum reach?

The pass begins by re-ranking the confirmed Social Currency findings
against the completed manuscript. Phase 3 ranked by surprise value
at the terrain-mapping stage; the completed chapters may have changed
the rankings. The five highest-ranked findings across the full
manuscript become the Five Things appendix and introduction hook
content regardless of which five were identified in Phase 3.

Five specific evaluations:

1. **Deployment hierarchy audit** — every confirmed finding
   correctly positioned across all five tiers
2. **Five Things appendix standard** — (a) five book-specific
   findings, not repurposed from prior books; (b) two to four
   sentences per finding in factual register; (c) legible as a
   phone screenshot
3. **Anchor sentence evaluation** — appears only in the
   introduction's closing section; context earns it through
   cumulative argumentative authority
4. **Chapter titles audit** — titles make claims or create
   curiosity gaps, not merely name categories ("The Formation
   Gap: Why Strategy Fails Without Character" vs. "Leadership
   and Character")

**Exit condition:** Five confirmed Social Currency findings are the
book's actual five highest as identified through full-manuscript re-
ranking; correctly deployed across all five tiers; Five Things
appendix complete and legible as standalone screenshot; anchor
sentence only in its earned location; all chapter titles pass the
curiosity gap standard.

### Pass Six: Placeholder Resolution and Final Preparation

**Focus:** Every [PLACEHOLDER] resolved before the manuscript is
publication-ready.

Begins with the **Placeholder Handoff Document** — a clean inventory
of every [PLACEHOLDER] in the manuscript: chapter and section
location, argument supported, scene needed, constraints, and current
placeholder text. Given to the author in advance to prepare confirmed
scenes.

Three legitimate resolutions for each placeholder:

1. **Insertion of confirmed testimony** — author supplies the
   specific scene, confirmed as substantially accurate, written in
   standard scene format (specific context, specific action,
   specific consequence) in the author's voice; verified to serve
   the argument it was designated to support
2. **Conversion to Category B testimony** — if the author cannot
   confirm a specific verifiable scene but can speak to the general
   professional pattern; honest Category B is always preferable
   to an unconfirmed Category C
3. **Removal** — if neither scene nor general observation is
   available AND the empirical evidence is sufficient on its own;
   surrounding prose adjusted to maintain argument logic

**No fourth resolution exists.** A placeholder cannot be left
unresolved, approximated, or written from imagination. A scene that
is not substantially accurate is not testimony — it is fiction
attributed to a real person, a direct violation of the limits on
means.

After all placeholders are resolved, Pass Six completes:
- Bibliography compiled from Phase 3 Citation Verification Log;
  every [VERIFIED] source cited in the manuscript appears; every
  bibliography entry is cited in the manuscript
- Discussion Guide reviewed for accuracy against the completed
  manuscript

**Exit condition:** Every placeholder resolved by one of the three
legitimate resolutions; every citation in the manuscript appears in
the bibliography with a [VERIFIED] record; no bibliography entries
uncited in the manuscript; Discussion Guide accurately describes
completed chapters; full manuscript read cover to cover after all
six passes with no new issues identified.

### The Iteration Exit Condition

Phase 5 is complete — and the book is publication-ready — when:

- All six passes are complete, each with its specific exit condition
  met in full
- Phase 5 log is complete with every judgment-based revision
  decision recorded and every earlier-phase escalation documented
- Every placeholder resolved by one of three legitimate resolutions;
  no [PLACEHOLDER] text remains in the manuscript
- Every citation in the manuscript appears in the bibliography with
  a [VERIFIED] record
- Manuscript read cover to cover at least once after all six passes
  in a single reading session focused on experience, not evaluation
- Five Things appendix legible as standalone phone screenshot;
  contains the book's actual five highest Social Currency findings
- Discussion Guide accurate, demanding, formation-oriented, with
  action steps more prominent than discussion questions
- Author reviewed and approved every judgment-based revision
  decision; every placeholder resolution confirmed by the author

**Output:** Two new documents alongside the revised manuscript files:
- `00_phase5_log.md` — the complete decision record for all six
  passes and all escalations
- `00_phase5_declaration.md` — the Publication Readiness
  Declaration; the author's specific, documented acknowledgment
  that every exit condition for every pass has been met

The Publication Readiness Declaration closes the loop that Phase 1
opened when the working hypothesis was formed. It marks the moment
when a claim the author was willing to argue became a book the
author was willing to put their name on.
