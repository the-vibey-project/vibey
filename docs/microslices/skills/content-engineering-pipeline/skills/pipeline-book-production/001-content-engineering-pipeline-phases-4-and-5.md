This skill governs Phases 4 and 5 of the Content Engineering
Pipeline. Phase 4 converts the
verified argument inventory from Phase 3 into a complete manuscript
draft. Phase 5 iterates that draft through six systematic passes
until it reaches the Publication Readiness Declaration.
