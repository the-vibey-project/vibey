### 6.2 Smaller discrepancies

- **Trademark pendency:** the USPTO dashboard gives 4.3 months to first action and 10.4 months total (October 1, 2026; 4.45 and 10.03 in the first half of fiscal 2026). The USPTO application-timeline page says 6 to 9 months for a 1(a) application. The dashboard figures are used in the tables and the page's wording is noted here.
- **Patent first-action pendency:** 20.6 months (dashboard) versus 22.6 months (secondary FY2025). The dashboard is used.
- **Small-entity filing total:** $800 (live fee page as read through UpCounsel) versus $730 (Neustel). $800 is used.
- **DMCA restoration:** statute says 10 to 14 business days; GitHub says 10 to 14 days.
- **Copyright Office examiner emails:** reported as cop-ad@loc.gov (an @loc.gov domain) versus scam guidance to look for @copyright.gov. Both are noted.
- **Appendix C inconsistency:** the Copyright Office's proposed schedule shows GRNW at $350 in the appendix and $275 in the text.
- **Madrid basic fee:** the notes' CHF 653/903 is a 2023 figure and stale; use WIPO's current table.
- **PCT fee reductions:** the 90 percent reduction does not apply to US applicants (applicants in listed developing countries only).
