## Glossary

- **ADS (Application Data Sheet):** USPTO form giving inventor, applicant, correspondence and priority data.
- **AFCP 2.0:** After-Final Consideration Pilot; ended December 14, 2024.
- **Alice:** Supreme Court case setting the two-step test for abstract-idea ineligibility.
- **CCB:** Copyright Claims Board, a small-claims forum.
- **Claim (patent):** the numbered sentence defining the legal scope of the invention.
- **CLA / DCO:** Contributor License Agreement; Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off.
- **CPC:** Cooperative Patent Classification.
- **Deposit (copyright):** the copy of the work submitted with the application.
- **DMCA:** Digital Millennium Copyright Act; Section 512 sets takedown and counter-notice procedure.
- **EDR:** effective date of registration (copyright).
- **eCO / Registration Portal:** the Copyright Office's online filing system.
- **ID Manual:** USPTO list of accepted goods and services descriptions; using entries avoids surcharges.
- **IDS:** Information Disclosure Statement (patent).
- **ITU / 1(b):** intent-to-use trademark filing basis.
- **Limitation of Claim:** copyright form section excluding old or third-party material.
- **Madrid Protocol:** international trademark filing system run by WIPO.
- **Micro / small / large entity:** USPTO fee tiers with 80%, 60% and 0% discounts.
- **NOA / SOU:** Notice of Allowance; Statement of Use (trademark).
- **Office action:** examiner's written refusal or request.
- **PCT:** Patent Cooperation Treaty.
- **Provisional application:** unexamined placeholder filing valid for 12 months.
- **PTAB / TTAB:** Patent Trial and Appeal Board; Trademark Trial and Appeal Board.
- **PTRC:** Patent and Trademark Resource Center.
- **RCE:** Request for Continued Examination.
- **Rule of Doubt:** Copyright Office acceptance of a registration despite doubt (used for object-code-only deposits).
- **Section 101:** patent statute defining eligible subject matter.
- **SME declaration (SMED):** Rule 132 declaration on subject-matter eligibility.
- **Specimen:** proof of trademark use submitted with a use-based application.
- **TSDR:** Trademark Status and Document Retrieval.
- **UDRP:** domain-name dispute policy.
- **Work made for hire:** work whose copyright vests in the employer or commissioning party.
