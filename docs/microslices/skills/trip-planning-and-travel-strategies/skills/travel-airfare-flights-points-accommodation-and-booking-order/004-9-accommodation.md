## §9. Accommodation

**⚠️ Match the type to the trip (§1 → `travel-trip-type-destination-constraints-and-budget`):**
```
HOTEL       ⚠️ predictable, serviced, recourse when things go wrong
APARTMENT/RENTAL  ⚠️ space, kitchen, laundry — genuinely better for
   families and long stays; ⚠️ variable, less recourse, and check
   local regulation (some cities restrict short-term rentals)
HOSTEL      ⚠️ social, cheap; private rooms exist and are underrated
GUESTHOUSE / RYOKAN / B&B  ⚠️ often the most local experience
```
**⚠️ Location dominates almost every other factor** — ⚠️ **and "distance to the centre" is
less useful than "distance to the transit line you'll actually use."** **Check the walk at
night, the noise (⚠️ a lively square is charming at 6pm and not at 2am), and the actual
route from the airport.**
**⚠️ Reading reviews**: ⚠️ **read the 3-star reviews, not the 1s or 5s — they're the most
informative.** **⚠️ Filter for recent, and for reviewers like you.** **⚠️ Repeated complaints
about the same specific thing are signal; a single furious review usually isn't.**
**⚠️ Booking direct vs aggregator**: ⚠️ **direct often gets better treatment when something
goes wrong, and sometimes better cancellation terms.**
