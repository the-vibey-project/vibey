## §10. Ground Transport

**⚠️ Rail** (see a rail reference for the mechanics): ⚠️ **often faster than flying
city-centre to city-centre under roughly 700 km once you count airport time; ⚠️ passes
versus point-to-point depends heavily on the country, and passes are frequently NOT the
cheaper option; ⚠️ and reservations are mandatory on many high-speed services even with a
pass.**
**⚠️ Driving**: ⚠️ **check whether an International Driving Permit is required; understand
insurance excess and what your card actually covers; ⚠️ note urban restricted zones (ZTL
in Italy, low-emission zones) where fines arrive months later; and ⚠️ parking is often the
real cost and constraint in cities.**
**⚠️ Local transit**: ⚠️ **learn the fare system BEFORE arriving — day passes, contactless
tap, stored-value cards — because getting this wrong at the airport at midnight is a
predictable annoyance.**
**⚠️ Ride-hailing and taxis**: ⚠️ **know the norm and the approximate fare before you need
one, and know which apps work locally.**
