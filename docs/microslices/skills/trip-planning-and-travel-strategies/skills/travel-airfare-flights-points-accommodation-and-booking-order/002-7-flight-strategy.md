## §7. Flight Strategy

**⚠️ Routing**: ⚠️ **nonstop reduces disruption risk substantially — every connection is
another chance to fail.** **⚠️ If connecting, mind MINIMUM CONNECTION TIME, and know that
the legal minimum is not a comfortable one, particularly with terminal changes,
immigration, or a change from a domestic to an international terminal.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — SEPARATE TICKETS are the single most consequential booking mistake.**
> ⚠️ **On one ticket (or interlined), a missed connection is the airline's problem and
> they rebook you.** **⚠️ On separate tickets, a missed connection is entirely YOUR problem
> — the second airline owes you nothing, and your insurance may not cover it either.**
> **⚠️ If you must self-connect, leave hours, not minutes, and prefer separate days for
> anything critical.**

**⚠️ Alliances and interlining** matter for exactly this reason; ⚠️ **low-cost carriers
generally do not interline.**
**⚠️ Seat selection**: **⚠️ SeatGuru-type resources for the specific aircraft; exit rows;
⚠️ and the genuine trade-off that the back of the aircraft is bumpier and later to
disembark but often emptier.**
**⚠️ Overbooking and denied boarding** — ⚠️ **compensation rules are jurisdiction-specific
and are worth knowing before you're at the desk** (§25 → `travel-groups-long-term-travel-and-handling-disruption`, §26.2 → `travel-reference`).
