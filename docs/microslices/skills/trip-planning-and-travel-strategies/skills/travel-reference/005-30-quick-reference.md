## §30. Quick Reference

### 30.1 Picker
| Question | Where |
|---|---|
| Where should we go? | ⚠️ **Wrong first question. Constraints first** (§3 → `travel-trip-type-destination-constraints-and-budget`) |
| How many places in 10 days? | ⚠️ **Fewer than you think. Count bases** (§3 → `travel-trip-type-destination-constraints-and-budget`, §17 → `travel-pacing-packing-jet-lag-connectivity-and-culture`) |
| When should I book the flight? | ⚠️ **Watch the route; no magic number** (§6 → `travel-airfare-flights-points-accommodation-and-booking-order`) |
| One ticket or two cheaper ones? | ⚠️ **One. Separate tickets carry the risk** (§7 → `travel-airfare-flights-points-accommodation-and-booking-order`) |
| Is a rail pass worth it? | ⚠️ **Price point-to-point first** (§10 → `travel-airfare-flights-points-accommodation-and-booking-order`) |
| Do I need a visa? | ⚠️ **Check the destination's own site, early** (§12 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`) |
| Is my passport valid enough? | ⚠️ **Six months beyond, usually** (§12 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`) |
| Do I need insurance? | ⚠️ **For medical and evacuation, yes** (§13 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`) |
| How dangerous is it there? | ⚠️ **Less than you fear; watch the roads** (§15 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`) |
| The terminal offers my home currency | ⚠️ **Decline. Always** (§16 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`) |
| Itinerary feels packed | ⚠️ **It is. Remove something** (§17 → `travel-pacing-packing-jet-lag-connectivity-and-culture`) |
| Flight cancelled — what now? | ⚠️ **Queue AND call; document; know your rights** (§25 → `travel-groups-long-term-travel-and-handling-disruption`) |
| Travelling to Europe — what's changed? | ⚠️ **EES is live; ETIAS isn't** (§26.1) |
| Am I owed compensation? | ⚠️ **Depends on route jurisdiction, not airline** (§26.2) |

### 30.2 Pre-departure checklist
- [ ] ⚠️ **Passport validity and blank pages checked against destination rules** (§12 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Visa/authorization obtained; TRANSIT countries checked too** (§12 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] Insurance bought early, exclusions read, medical limits checked (§13 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Medication legal at destination, in original packaging, in hand luggage** (§14 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] Vaccines and prophylaxis arranged with lead time (§14 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Two cards, different networks, stored separately; no-FX card** (§16 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Documents scanned, offline AND cloud, copy left with someone** (§12 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
- [ ] Offline maps, tickets and translation downloaded (§20 → `travel-pacing-packing-jet-lag-connectivity-and-culture`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Extra time budgeted at Schengen external borders** (§26.1)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Buffer before anything unmissable** (§17 → `travel-pacing-packing-jet-lag-connectivity-and-culture`)
- [ ] Someone at home has the itinerary (§15 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`)
