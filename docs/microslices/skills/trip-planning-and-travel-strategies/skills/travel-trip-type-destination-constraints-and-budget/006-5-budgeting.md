## §5. Budgeting

```
⚠️ THE CATEGORIES, roughly in order of underestimation
   ⚠️ GROUND TRANSPORT at destination — chronically underestimated
   ⚠️ FOOD — ⚠️ eating out three times a day is a different budget
      from what people imagine
   ⚠️ ACTIVITIES AND ENTRY FEES — add up fast
   ⚠️ THE "ALREADY HERE" TAX — ⚠️ you will spend more because you're
      there and won't be again. Budget for it rather than pretending
   Accommodation · flights · insurance · visas · gear
   ⚠️ CONTINGENCY — 10-20%. ⚠️ Not optional (§25)
```
**⚠️ Total cost, not nightly rate**: ⚠️ **a cheaper hotel 40 minutes out can cost more in
transport and time than a dearer central one.** **⚠️ Similarly a cheap flight at an awkward
hour may require an extra night's accommodation.**
**⚠️ The sunk cost trap**: ⚠️ **having paid for something does not obligate you to do it.**
**Skipping a booked activity because you're exhausted is frequently the right call.**
