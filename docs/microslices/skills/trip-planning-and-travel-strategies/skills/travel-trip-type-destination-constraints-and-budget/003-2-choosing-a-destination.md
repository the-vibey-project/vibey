## §2. Choosing a Destination

**⚠️ Work backwards from constraints (§3), not forwards from a wish list.**
**⚠️ Useful filters**: **flight time and time zones offset against trip length (⚠️ a
long-haul trip under about five days spends most of itself in transit and adjustment);
season (§4); cost level relative to budget; visa friction (§12 → `travel-documents-insurance-health-safety-and-money`); language and
navigability; and ⚠️ how much infrastructure the trip needs versus how much you want.**
**⚠️ Repeat visits are underrated** — ⚠️ **returning somewhere known removes the entire
orientation cost and is often the better answer for a short or restorative trip.**
**⚠️ Beware destination inflation from social media**: ⚠️ **a place that photographs well
may be crowded, small, or a two-hour detour for a five-minute view.** **Check what the
place is like to BE in, not to photograph.**
