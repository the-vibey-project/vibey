## §3. ⚠️ Constraints-First Planning

```
⚠️ THE CONSTRAINTS, in the order they actually bind
   1. ⚠️ TIME — total days INCLUDING travel days. ⚠️ A "week in Japan"
      from Europe or North America is roughly five usable days
   2. ⚠️ MONEY — total, not per-item (§5)
   3. ⚠️ ENERGY — ⚠️ the most-ignored constraint. Health, fitness,
      sleep needs, tolerance for uncertainty, and how you'll be
      feeling AFTER the flight
   4. ⚠️ COMPANIONS — their constraints, honestly gathered (§23)
   5. ⚠️ FIXED DATES — school terms, events, visa windows
   6. ⚠️ DOCUMENTS — passport validity and visa lead times (§12)
      ⚠️ This can eliminate a destination entirely, so check EARLY
```
**⚠️ The travel-day tax**: ⚠️ **count arrival and departure days as roughly half days at
best.** **A "10-day trip" is commonly 8 usable days, and less across time zones.**
**⚠️ The rule of thumb worth adopting**: ⚠️ **decide the number of BASES before the number
of PLACES.** **Each additional base costs a half-day minimum in packing, transit,
check-in and re-orientation** (§17 → `travel-pacing-packing-jet-lag-connectivity-and-culture`).
