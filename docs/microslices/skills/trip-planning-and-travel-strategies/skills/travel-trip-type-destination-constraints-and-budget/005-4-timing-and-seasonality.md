## §4. Timing and Seasonality

**⚠️ Shoulder season is usually the best answer**: ⚠️ **acceptable weather, lower prices,
fewer crowds, and everything still open.** **⚠️ Check that last one — some destinations
substantially close outside peak.**
**⚠️ What to check before fixing dates**: ⚠️ **monsoon and hurricane seasons (which are
seasonal but not deterministic), extreme heat (⚠️ increasingly a real constraint in
Mediterranean and desert summers), public holidays in the DESTINATION (⚠️ Golden Week,
Lunar New Year, Ramadan, August in southern Europe — things close and prices spike),
school holidays in the source markets, and local festivals you'd want to catch or avoid.**
**⚠️ Weather averages hide variance** — ⚠️ **an average temperature tells you little about
the distribution, and "average rainfall" doesn't distinguish an afternoon shower from
three days indoors.**
**⚠️ Overtourism management is now a real planning variable**: ⚠️ **several destinations
have introduced entry fees, timed-entry booking, visitor caps or accommodation
restrictions, and the popular sites in popular cities increasingly require booking weeks
ahead.** **⚠️ Check specific attraction booking windows before assuming walk-up access.**
