## §19. What Cleaning Actually Is

**⚠️ Four factors trade against each other — Sinner's circle — and this is the framework
that explains every cleaning decision:**
```
⚠️ CHEMISTRY · MECHANICAL ACTION · TEMPERATURE · TIME
```
⚠️ **Reduce one and you must increase another.** **Cold-water laundry needs better
detergent or longer time; less scrubbing needs stronger chemistry or a longer dwell.**
**⚠️ This is why "let it soak" genuinely works** — **you're substituting time for
mechanical action** — **and why the most common cleaning-product error is not allowing
DWELL TIME.**
