## §21. Water Hardness

**⚠️ Hard water is dissolved calcium and magnesium**, **and it matters more than most
people realize**: ⚠️ **it deactivates anionic surfactants (forming soap scum, which is
literally the surfactant reacting with calcium), leaves scale on heating elements, and
reduces detergent effectiveness — so hard-water households genuinely need more detergent
or a builder.**
**⚠️ Builders and chelators (sodium carbonate, citrate, EDTA, and formerly phosphates)
sequester those ions** — ⚠️ **which is precisely what phosphate detergents did well, and
their removal for eutrophication reasons (§24 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) made detergents visibly less effective in
hard water.**
