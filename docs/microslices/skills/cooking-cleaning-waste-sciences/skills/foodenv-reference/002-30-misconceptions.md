## §30. Misconceptions

| Misconception | Correction |
|---|---|
| Searing seals in juices | ⚠️ **False. Sear for flavour** (§3 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Well-done meat is dry from losing fat | ⚠️ **Actin denatures ~60°C and squeezes out water** (§3 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Onions caramelize in 10 minutes | ⚠️ **45+ minutes, and it's Maillard plus caramelization** (§4 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Maillard and caramelization are the same | ⚠️ **Different reactions; Maillard needs protein** (§4 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Bread stales from drying out | ⚠️ **Starch retrogradation — faster in the fridge** (§5 → `foodenv-food-science`, §11 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Salt just before cooking | ⚠️ **Well ahead or at the pan; the middle is worst** (§7 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| A tablespoon of salt is a tablespoon of salt | ⚠️ **Brands differ nearly 2× by weight** (§7 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Add tomatoes early to the beans | ⚠️ **Acid slows softening dramatically** (§8 → `foodenv-food-science`) |
| Wash raw chicken | ⚠️ **Aerosolizes contamination; doesn't reduce risk** (§14 → `foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service`) |
| Gloves replace handwashing | ⚠️ **Contaminated gloves transfer just as well** (§14 → `foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service`) |
| Reheating makes old food safe | ⚠️ **Staph toxin is heat-stable** (§12 → `foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service`) |
| Cooking is where food safety fails | ⚠️ **COOLING is** (§12 → `foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service`) |
| Low food-cost % is the goal | ⚠️ **You bank dollars, not percentages** (§17 → `foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service`) |
| Stronger cleaner works better | ⚠️ **Match chemistry to soil type** (§20 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| Vinegar cleans everything | ⚠️ **Useless on grease; etches stone** (§20 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| Hot water for blood stains | ⚠️ **Cooks protein onto fibre. Use cold** (§20 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`, §23 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| Mixing cleaners makes them stronger | ⚠️ **It makes toxic gas** (§22 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| Spray and wipe disinfects | ⚠️ **Dwell time; surface must stay wet** (§22 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| Disinfect a dirty surface | ⚠️ **Organic soil inactivates disinfectants. Clean first** (§22 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| 100% alcohol is the best sanitizer | ⚠️ **60–90% works better** (§22 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| More detergent cleans better | ⚠️ **Residue attracts soil** (§23 → `foodenv-cleaning-chemistry`) |
| Flushable wipes are flushable | ⚠️ **They cause fatbergs and pump damage** (§24 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| Landfills break waste down | ⚠️ **They're designed to entomb; decades-old food is identifiable** (§26 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| "Biodegradable" helps in a landfill | ⚠️ **It doesn't decompose there, and may make methane** (§26 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| The number in the triangle means recyclable | ⚠️ **It's a resin ID code** (§27 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| Put it in the bin and hope | ⚠️ **Wishcycling contaminates whole loads** (§27 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| Recycling is the top green action | ⚠️ **Third tier. Reduce and reuse beat it** (§25 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| All plastics get recycled | ⚠️ **Realistically #1 and #2; the rest lack markets** (§27 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management`) |
| There's one US recycling rate | ⚠️ **Capture vs sorting are different numbers** (§29.1) |
| Biosolid PFAS rules are settled | ⚠️ **Voluntary guidance; actively contested** (§29.2) |
