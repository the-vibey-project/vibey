## §32. Books

| Author | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **McGee** | ***On Food and Cooking*** | ⚠️ **THE food science reference. Start here** |
| **López-Alt** | ***The Food Lab*** | ⚠️ **Tests the folklore. §3 → `foodenv-food-science`'s searing myth among others** |
| **This** | *Molecular Gastronomy* | The chemistry, rigorously |
| **Ruhlman** | *Ratio* | ⚠️ **Baking by proportion rather than recipe** |
| **Katz** | *The Art of Fermentation* | §10 → `foodenv-food-science` |
| **NCHFP / USDA** | ***Complete Guide to Home Canning*** | ⚠️ **Free, and the authority for §10 → `foodenv-food-science`'s botulism risk** |
| **ServSafe** | *Manager* coursework | §12–§14 → `foodenv-food-safety-and-food-service`, and often legally required |
| **Dornenburg & Page** | *The Flavor Bible* | §9 → `foodenv-food-science`, practically |
| **Tchobanoglous et al.** | *Wastewater Engineering* / *Integrated Solid Waste Management* | ⚠️ **The §24–§27 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management` references** |

**⚠️ Also free and authoritative**: **FDA Food Code and your local health department;
USDA FSIS temperature guidance; ⚠️ your municipality's actual recycling list (§27 → `foodenv-wastewater-and-waste-management` — rules
are local and no general source substitutes); and EPA's waste and biosolids pages.**
