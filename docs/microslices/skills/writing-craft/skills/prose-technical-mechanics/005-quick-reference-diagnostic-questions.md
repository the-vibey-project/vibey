## Quick Reference: Diagnostic Questions

When editing prose at the mechanical level, ask these questions in sequence:

**Diction:**
- What is the AS/Latinate ratio? Is the ratio producing the right effect (intimacy/authority)?
- Are there near-synonyms that carry better connotations for this context?
- Are abstractions earning their place, or can they be replaced with concrete particulars?
- What is the phonesthetic texture — proportion of plosives, fricatives, liquids, nasals? Does it match the intended effect?
- Are nouns and verbs as specific as they can be, or are modifiers compensating for vague root words?

**Sentence mechanics:**
- Where does new/emphatic information land? Is it in the stress position (end)?
- What does the topic position signal about perspective and given information?
- Is this a cumulative or periodic structure? Does the choice match the effect needed (discovery vs. arrival)?
- What is the logic of each conjunction? Does *however* actually signal contrast here?
- Is sentence length varied? When did the last short sentence fire?

**Paragraph and argument:**
- Does the topic sentence state an argument, not just a subject?
- Is there a warrant connecting data to claim, or is the inferential leap left unspoken where it shouldn't be?
- Is the opposing view a straw man or a steel man?
- What is the register throughout — and is any shift deliberate or accidental?
