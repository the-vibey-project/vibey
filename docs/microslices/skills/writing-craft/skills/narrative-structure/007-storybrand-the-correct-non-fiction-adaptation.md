## StoryBrand: the correct non-fiction adaptation

Donald Miller's *Building a StoryBrand* (HarperCollins Leadership, 2017) is the dominant marketing adaptation. Seven-part structure: A Character → Has a Problem → Meets a Guide → Who Gives Them a Plan → And Calls Them to Action → That Helps Them Avoid Failure → And Ends in Success.

**The critical inversion**: the **customer is the Hero**, the **brand is the Mentor**, the **product or methodology is the elixir**. This is the rhetorically correct application to non-fiction.

**The most common error**: the brand or author casts themselves as the Hero. This violates the psychological contract on which mythic resonance depends. The reader cannot identify with a Hero who is also the narrator's self-praise.

**The test**: a draft that uses the word "we" more often than "you" in the first two pages probably has the wrong Hero.
