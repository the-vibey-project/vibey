## Audience types and how to adapt

**C-suite readers** want: ROI implications, risk exposure, strategic fit, competitive implications. Give them: quantified business impact, regulatory risk, benchmark comparisons. They will read the executive summary and the conclusion; the body should stand alone for their direct reports.

**Technical evaluators** want: architecture details, integration complexity, security posture, operational requirements. Give them: diagrams, specifications, comparison tables, technical proof points, references to standards.

**Policy analysts** want: regulatory feasibility, precedent, stakeholder impact, implementation challenges. Give them: regulatory citations, cross-jurisdiction comparisons, case studies from comparable implementations.

**Practical rule**: if you do not know who will read this, write for the technical evaluator — they are the most demanding audience and their approval is usually required before a purchase decision reaches the executive.
