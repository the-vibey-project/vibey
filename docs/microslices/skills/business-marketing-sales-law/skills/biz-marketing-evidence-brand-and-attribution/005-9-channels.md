## §9. Channels

**Search** — ⚠️ **demand capture, not creation. High intent, and you're bidding against
everyone else who wants that intent.**
**SEO** — ⚠️ **technical, content, links; slow-compounding; and increasingly complicated
by AI-generated answers reducing click-through.**
**Paid social** — demand creation; **creative is the main lever, not targeting**
(⚠️ **platform algorithms now do most of the targeting, and over-narrow audiences hurt
delivery**).
**Email** — ⚠️ **owned, first-party, the highest-ROI channel for most businesses, and the
one most neglected relative to paid.**
**Content, partnerships, affiliate, PR, events, community, referral** (⚠️ **the strongest
signal when organic, and it degrades when incentivized**).
**⚠️ The general principle**: **owned and earned compound; paid rents attention and stops
the moment you stop paying.** **A business whose growth requires continuously increasing
paid spend does not have a channel, it has a cost.**
