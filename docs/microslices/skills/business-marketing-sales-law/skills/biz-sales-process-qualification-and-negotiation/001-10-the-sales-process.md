## §10. The Sales Process

```
Prospect → Qualify → Discovery → Demo/Proposal → Objections → Negotiate → Close → Expand
```
**⚠️ The most common failure is skipping discovery.** **Presenting before you understand
the problem produces a pitch about your features rather than their situation**, and
⚠️ **the strongest predictor of a lost deal is often that nobody asked what the customer
was actually trying to achieve.**

**⚠️ B2B vs B2C differ structurally, not just in size**: **B2B has multiple stakeholders,
longer cycles, formal procurement, and — critically — a status quo bias where "do nothing"
is the most common competitor.** ⚠️ **Most lost B2B deals are lost to no decision, not to
a competitor.**
**Pipeline mechanics**: ⚠️ **stages must be defined by BUYER actions, not seller
activities** — "demo given" tells you nothing; "customer confirmed budget and timeline"
tells you something. **Coverage ratios, velocity, and conversion by stage.**
**⚠️ Forecasting is where sales organizations lie to themselves**: commit / best case /
pipeline, and ⚠️ **the discipline is applying consistent exit criteria rather than
optimism.**
