## §13. Sales Management

**Compensation**: ⚠️ **base/variable split by role, quota, accelerators, and clawbacks** —
**and comp design is strategy, because people will do exactly what you pay them for.**
⚠️ **Paying on bookings produces bookings, including bad ones; paying on collected revenue
or retained revenue produces different behaviour.**
**Territory and account assignment**, **ramp time** (⚠️ **often 3–9 months in B2B, which
is why hiring plans lag revenue plans**), **specialization** (SDR/AE/CS), **enablement**,
**CRM hygiene** (⚠️ **a CRM nobody updates is worse than none, because it produces
confident wrong forecasts**).
