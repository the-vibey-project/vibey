## §14. Trust and Reputation

**⚠️ §1 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`'s third organizing idea.** ⚠️ **Most negotiations are embedded in continuing
relationships or reputational networks, and single-shot logic misapplied to a repeated game
is the standard failure.**
**⚠️ Two kinds of trust** worth distinguishing: ⚠️ **trust in COMPETENCE (they can deliver)
and trust in INTEGRITY (they will).** ⚠️ **They fail differently and are repaired
differently.**
**⚠️ Trust is asymmetric** — ⚠️ **slow to build, fast to destroy, and hard to rebuild — which
is the practical argument against short-term tactics that succeed.**
**⚠️ Repair** requires ⚠️ **acknowledgement, explanation, and — the part usually missing —
a structural change that makes recurrence less likely; ⚠️ apology without change reads as
management.**
**⚠️ Verification is compatible with trust.** ⚠️ **Contracts, milestones and audit rights are
not accusations; treating verification as an insult is itself a tactic** (§15 → `negot-hard-tactics-multiparty-culture-power-and-remote`).
