## §12. Emotion

**⚠️ Emotion is information, not noise** — ⚠️ **it signals what matters, and suppressing it
entirely removes data both sides need.**
**⚠️ Anger**: ⚠️ **the research finds expressed anger can extract concessions in one-shot
distributive settings — ⚠️ AND it damages relationships, increases impasse and retaliation,
and reduces the other side's willingness to deal again.** ⚠️ **It is a tactic that borrows
from the future** (§14).
**⚠️ Anxiety** is the underrated one: ⚠️ **anxious negotiators make lower first offers,
concede faster and exit earlier — ⚠️ which is why preparation and practice pay in confidence
as much as content.**
**⚠️ Emotional regulation techniques**: ⚠️ **naming the emotion, taking breaks, separating
the person from the problem, and going to the balcony.**
**⚠️ Acknowledging the other side's emotion** costs nothing and does real work — ⚠️ **and
note that acknowledgement is not agreement, which is the distinction that makes it safe.**
