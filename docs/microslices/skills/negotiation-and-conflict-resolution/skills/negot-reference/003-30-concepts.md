## §30. Concepts

```
⚠️ ⚠️ BATNA  ⚠️ what you do if there's NO deal. The source of
   leverage. ⚠️ An actual alternative, not a hope
⚠️ RESERVATION VALUE  ⚠️ derived from BATNA + switching costs
⚠️ ZOPA  ⚠️ the overlap. ⚠️ No overlap = no deal worth doing
⚠️ ⚠️ POSITION vs INTEREST  ⚠️ what they say vs WHY
⚠️ Log-rolling  trade across differently-valued issues
⚠️ MESO  ⚠️ multiple equivalent simultaneous offers — reveals
   priorities without asking
⚠️ Contingent contract  ⚠️ exploits differing forecasts; also
   tests bluffs
⚠️ ⚠️ Negotiator's dilemma  ⚠️ creating needs openness,
   claiming rewards concealment
⚠️ ⚠️ Reactive devaluation  ⚠️ same proposal valued lower from
   the other side — ⚠️ why mediators can carry offers
⚠️ Self-serving fairness bias  ⚠️ both sides sincerely believe
   the fair outcome favours them
⚠️ Glasl  ⚠️ 9 escalation stages; ⚠️ stage determines viable
   intervention
⚠️ ⚠️ Ultimatum game  ⚠️ low offers rejected at cost to the
   rejector, across cultures
⚠️ ⚠️ First-offer meta-analysis  ⚠️ Orr & Guthrie r≈.5 but only
   ~19 effects · ⚠️ 2025 synthesis (N=2,121) replicated BOTH
   benefit AND peril; ⚠️ complexity shrinks the effect;
   ⚠️ anger drives impasse
⚠️ ⚠️ AI pre-mediation (2026)  ⚠️ comparable to human mediators
   on short-term self-report; ⚠️ 36% lower preference-inference
   error · ⚠️ weaker AI negotiating agents lost up to 14% profit
⚠️ Mediators skipping pre-session contact  ⚠️ 34% civil, 61% family
```
