## §32. Quick Reference

### 32.1 Picker
| Question | Where |
|---|---|
| How do I get more leverage? | ⚠️ **Improve your BATNA. Away from the table** (§3 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`) |
| Should I make the first offer? | ⚠️ **Simple + informed = yes. Complex or uninformed = wait** (§6 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`, §28.2) |
| They anchored extreme — now what? | ⚠️ **Don't counter from it. Name it, re-anchor on standards** (§6 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`, §15 → `negot-hard-tactics-multiparty-culture-power-and-remote`) |
| We're deadlocked on price | ⚠️ **Add issues. One number can't be traded** (§2 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`, §8 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`) |
| How do I find the trades? | ⚠️ **Ask why. Use MESOs to reveal priorities** (§4 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`, §8 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`) |
| How much do I reveal? | ⚠️ **Priorities yes, reservation value no** (§9 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`) |
| They keep pushing after agreement | ⚠️ **Nibbling. Reopen the package** (§15 → `negot-hard-tactics-multiparty-culture-power-and-remote`) |
| They won't take a reasonable offer | ⚠️ **Reactive devaluation, or fairness, or face** (§11 → `negot-biases-emotion-fairness-and-trust`, §13 → `negot-biases-emotion-fairness-and-trust`) |
| The other side is much stronger | ⚠️ **BATNA, standards, coalitions — or a structural remedy** (§19 → `negot-hard-tactics-multiparty-culture-power-and-remote`) |
| This conflict keeps escalating | ⚠️ **Diagnose the stage before choosing an intervention** (§21 → `negot-conflict-analysis-mediation-adr-workplace-and-ethics`) |
| Mediate or arbitrate? | ⚠️ **Do you want control or finality?** (§23 → `negot-conflict-analysis-mediation-adr-workplace-and-ethics`) |
| My colleague is impossible | ⚠️ **Would a different person in that role have this conflict?** (§25 → `negot-conflict-analysis-mediation-adr-workplace-and-ethics`) |
| Is this tactic acceptable? | ⚠️ **Would it still work if they knew?** (§27 → `negot-conflict-analysis-mediation-adr-workplace-and-ethics`) |

### 32.2 Preparation checklist
- [ ] ⚠️ **My BATNA, honestly assessed — and can I improve it first?** (§3 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **My reservation value, derived from that BATNA** (§3 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **My estimate of THEIR BATNA — and am I overestimating it?** (§3 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`)
- [ ] My interests, ranked — and their likely interests (§4 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Full issue list. Can I add issues to enable trades?** (§8 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`)
- [ ] An ambitious but justifiable aspiration, not just a floor (§5 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Objective standards I can appeal to** (§13 → `negot-biases-emotion-fairness-and-trust`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **What I'll share, what I won't, and in what order** (§9 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`)
- [ ] Two or three MESOs prepared (§8 → `negot-anchoring-concessions-value-creation-and-closing`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Authority: mine and theirs, established up front** (§17 → `negot-hard-tactics-multiparty-culture-power-and-remote`)
- [ ] Is this relationship repeated? Price the tactics accordingly (§14 → `negot-biases-emotion-fairness-and-trust`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **My walk-away decided in advance, while calm** (§3 → `negot-framings-batna-zopa-interests-and-preparation`, §12 → `negot-biases-emotion-fairness-and-trust`)
