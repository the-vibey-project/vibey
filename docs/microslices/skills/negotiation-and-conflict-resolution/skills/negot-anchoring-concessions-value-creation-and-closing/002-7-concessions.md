## §7. Concessions

**⚠️ The pattern signals more than the amounts.** ⚠️ **Decreasing concession sizes signal
approach to a limit; constant-sized concessions invite continued pushing; a large late
concession suggests there was more available all along.**
**⚠️ Reciprocity is the governing norm** (§13 → `negot-biases-emotion-fairness-and-trust`): ⚠️ **unreciprocated concessions train the
other side to expect more, and the correct response to a non-reciprocal counterpart is to
stop conceding, not to concede faster.**
**⚠️ Label your concessions** — ⚠️ **make clear that it is a concession, that it is costly,
and what you expect in return; ⚠️ an unlabelled concession is often not registered as one.**
**⚠️ Conditional language is the workhorse**: ⚠️ **"if you can do X, then I could consider Y"
— which explores without committing.**
**⚠️ Never concede in response to pressure alone**, ⚠️ **only in response to a reason or a
reciprocal move; otherwise pressure is being rewarded and will continue** (§15 → `negot-hard-tactics-multiparty-culture-power-and-remote`).
