## Where to go next

- **A term you cannot place in Part II** — start at §6 → `ministry-apologetics-arguments-for-god`. Nine
  of the sixteen glossary terms are apologetics vocabulary and most of them resolve there; the two that
  do not are the problem of evil and worldview, both at §9–§11 →
  `ministry-apologetics-evil-objections-and-worldview`.
- **A term about people rather than arguments** — relational evangelism and trust at §1 →
  `ministry-evangelism-persuasion-and-listening`, discipleship at §17 →
  `ministry-teaching-and-discipleship`, the founder bottleneck at §20 → `ministry-church-planting`.
- **A judgement call rather than a definition** — the honest assessments live with their subjects: the
  stated limitation on each theistic argument at §6, the five alternative explanations of the empty tomb
  at §7 → `ministry-apologetics-resurrection-and-scripture`, the theodicies and what each fails to
  address at §9, and the six planting models with what each costs at §19 → `ministry-church-planting`.
- **An ethical question about how you are persuading someone** — go straight to §5 →
  `ministry-evangelism-ethics`. The disclosure test is one sentence long and settles most of them, and
  it is the part of this reference that constrains all the rest.
