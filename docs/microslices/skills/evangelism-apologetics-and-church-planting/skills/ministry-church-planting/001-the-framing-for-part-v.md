## The framing for Part V

Church planting is the establishing of a new Christian community in a specific context. The source
holds two descriptions together: a **theological act** — the church is God's instrument for his
mission — and a **community-building act**, because a church is a community of people who are
*maintained*, not merely founded. That second description sets the shape of this part. Almost all the
failure modes are in sustaining rather than founding: the founder bottleneck, volunteer burnout,
unhandled conflict, and the transition from a group where everyone knows each other to one where they
cannot. A plant that launches well and then fails did not fail at the thing it spent eighteen months
preparing for.
