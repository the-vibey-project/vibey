## §13. Counterpoint

**⚠️ Counterpoint is the combination of independent melodic lines, and it is the historical
foundation harmony was later abstracted from** — **not the other way round.**
**Species counterpoint** (Fux, 1725) — **a graded pedagogy from note-against-note through
florid** — ⚠️ **and it remains an outstanding training method because it isolates voice
leading from harmonic vocabulary.**
**Devices**: **imitation, canon, fugue** (⚠️ **subject, answer — real vs tonal —
countersubject, episode, stretto**), **invertible counterpoint, augmentation, diminution,
retrograde, inversion.**
**⚠️ The conceptual point worth carrying**: **in a genuinely contrapuntal texture the
chords are a BY-PRODUCT of simultaneous lines, not a pre-existing scaffold.** ⚠️ **Analyzing
Bach purely as chord progressions inverts how it was made.**
