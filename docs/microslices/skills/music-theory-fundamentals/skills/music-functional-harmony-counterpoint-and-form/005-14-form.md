## §14. Form

```
BINARY (AB) · ROUNDED BINARY (ABA') · TERNARY (ABA)
⚠️ SONATA FORM   exposition (1st theme–transition–2nd theme in a NEW KEY–
   closing) → development → recapitulation (⚠️ 2nd theme now in the TONIC).
   The form is a KEY drama first and a theme drama second — which is what
   textbook diagrams obscure
RONDO (ABACA) · THEME AND VARIATIONS · FUGUE · STROPHIC
⚠️ 12-BAR BLUES  I-I-I-I-IV-IV-I-I-V-IV-I-V. The most generative form of
   the 20th century
⚠️ POP FORMS  verse–chorus, verse–prechorus–chorus–bridge, and the loop-based
   structures of electronic music where variation is textural, not thematic
```
**⚠️ Formal function matters more than the letter diagram**: **is this passage presenting,
transitioning, developing, or closing?** ⚠️ **Two pieces with the same ABA shape can be
doing entirely different things.**
