## §9. Chords

**Triads**: **major (4:5:6), minor, diminished, augmented.** **Sevenths**: **dominant 7,
major 7, minor 7, half-diminished, fully diminished.** **Extensions**: **9, 11, 13** —
⚠️ **which are just continued stacking of thirds, and which become the primary vocabulary
in jazz** (§17 → `music-analysis-jazz-popular-non-western-and-meaning`).
**Inversions and figured bass** — ⚠️ **figured bass is worth understanding because it
reveals that Baroque musicians thought in terms of intervals above a bass line, not
"chords" as objects.** **The chord-as-object concept is a later abstraction (Rameau).**
**Voicing, spacing, doubling** — ⚠️ **the same chord can sound completely different
depending on register and spacing, and close low voicings turn muddy because of critical
bands** (§3 → `music-claims-harmonic-series-psychoacoustics-and-tuning`).
