## §5. Pitch, Intervals, Notation

**Notation is a technology with a history and its biases show**: ⚠️ **staff notation
encodes pitch and duration precisely, and timbre, articulation, microtiming and intonation
badly or not at all** — **which is exactly why it serves Western art music well and serves
jazz, folk and non-Western traditions poorly** (§18–§19 → `music-analysis-jazz-popular-non-western-and-meaning`).
**Intervals**: **number + quality** (major, minor, perfect, augmented, diminished).
⚠️ **Enharmonic equivalence — that A♯ and B♭ are "the same" — is TRUE ONLY IN EQUAL
TEMPERAMENT** (§4 → `music-claims-harmonic-series-psychoacoustics-and-tuning`), **and the distinct spellings persist because they mean different
things functionally.**
**Inversion, compound intervals, consonance/dissonance classification** (⚠️ **and note
from §3 → `music-claims-harmonic-series-psychoacoustics-and-tuning` that the classification is stylistic**).
