## §2. Yarn and Spinning

**⚠️ Staple (short fibres spun together) vs FILAMENT (continuous).** ⚠️ **Silk and all
synthetics are natively filament; everything else is staple, and synthetics are often cut
into staple deliberately to mimic natural hand.**
**⚠️ TWIST is the variable that does the work**: ⚠️ **more twist gives strength and a
harder, cooler hand; less twist gives softness and loft.** **Crepe yarns are extremely
high-twist, which is what produces their texture.**
**Ply** (⚠️ **plied yarns are rounder, stronger and more balanced than singles — and
singles skew, which matters in knitting**), **and yarn COUNT systems** (⚠️ **confusingly
inverted: in cotton count and worsted count HIGHER = FINER, while in denier and tex
HIGHER = HEAVIER**).
**⚠️ Worsted vs woollen preparation**: ⚠️ **worsted combs fibres parallel for a smooth,
strong, dense yarn; woollen leaves them jumbled for a lofty, insulating, weaker yarn.**
**Same fleece, entirely different fabric.**
