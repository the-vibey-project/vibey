## §1. ⚠️ Fibres

**⚠️ Everything else follows from this table. Learn it and most care and construction
questions answer themselves.**
```
⚠️ CELLULOSIC (plant)
  COTTON   absorbent, comfortable, strong WET (⚠️ unusual — most fibres
     weaken wet), wrinkles, shrinks, no elastic recovery
  LINEN (flax)  ⚠️ very strong, highly absorbent, excellent conductor of
     heat (why it feels cool), creases badly, low elasticity, gets
     softer with washing
  RAYON/VISCOSE  ⚠️ regenerated cellulose — behaves like a weak cotton and
     is MUCH WEAKER WET. ⚠️ The single most common laundering disaster
  LYOCELL/TENCEL  regenerated, stronger wet, closed-loop solvent process

⚠️ PROTEIN (animal)
  WOOL   ⚠️ insulates when wet, resists wrinkles, flame-resistant,
     naturally elastic. ⚠️ FELTS irreversibly with heat + moisture +
     agitation (the scales interlock). Damaged by alkali (§23)
  SILK   strong, lustrous, ⚠️ weakened by sunlight and by alkali;
     water-spots on some weaves
  CASHMERE/ALPACA/MOHAIR  finer, warmer, weaker, more prone to pilling

⚠️ SYNTHETIC
  POLYESTER  ⚠️ strong, hydrophobic, holds a crease, resists wrinkles,
     ⚠️ HOLDS OILY SOIL AND ODOUR because it's oleophilic — that's why
     activewear smells even after washing
  NYLON/POLYAMIDE  ⚠️ the most abrasion-resistant common fibre; elastic
  ACRYLIC  wool-like, ⚠️ pills badly, sheds heavily (§27.1)
  ELASTANE/SPANDEX/LYCRA  ⚠️ degraded by heat, chlorine and oils. It is
     what dies first in a garment blend
  OLEFIN/POLYPROPYLENE  ⚠️ essentially unwettable — solution-dyed only
```
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — the wet-strength inversion is the most useful single fact here.**
> ⚠️ **Cotton and linen get STRONGER when wet; rayon/viscose gets dramatically WEAKER; wool
> loses strength and becomes vulnerable to felting.** **⚠️ This is why you can scrub a
> cotton shirt and why wringing a wet rayon garment destroys it — the fibre is at its most
> fragile exactly when you're handling it hardest.**

**⚠️ Blends inherit the WORST care requirement of their components**, ⚠️ **not an average.**
**A cotton/elastane blend must be washed for the elastane.**
