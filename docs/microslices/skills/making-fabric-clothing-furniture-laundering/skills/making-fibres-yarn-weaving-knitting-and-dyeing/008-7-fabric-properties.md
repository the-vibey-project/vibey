## §7. Fabric Properties

**⚠️ HAND (how it feels), DRAPE (how it falls), GSM or oz/yd² (weight), opacity, thermal
behaviour, moisture management (⚠️ wicking vs absorbency are DIFFERENT — polyester wicks
without absorbing; cotton absorbs without wicking, which is why cotton stays wet).**
**⚠️ Pilling**: ⚠️ **caused by fibre ends migrating and tangling, and made permanent when
the fibre is too STRONG to break off** — **which is why polyester and acrylic pill visibly
and wool eventually sheds its pills.** **Strong fibres pill worse, counterintuitively.**
