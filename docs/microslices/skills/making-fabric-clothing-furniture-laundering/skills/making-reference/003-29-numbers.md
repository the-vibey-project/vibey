## §29. Numbers

```
⚠️ Wearing ease, woven      ~2–4" at the bust · ⚠️ knits: 0 or negative
⚠️ Seam allowance           5/8" (US commercial) · 1 cm (many European)
⚠️ Needle change interval   ~6–8 hours of sewing
⚠️ Hand-sewing thread       ~18" lengths
⚠️ Wood MC for interiors    ~6–8% (kiln-dried, heated space)
⚠️ Tangential movement      roughly 2× radial (why flatsawn cups)
⚠️ Seat height              ~16–18" · depth ~18–22"
⚠️ Moth/beetle freezing     several days at −18°C, thaw and refreeze
⚠️ Microfibre per wash      reported 100,000–12,000,000 (⚠️ NO standard method)
⚠️ Acrylic vs polyester     reported ~5× shedding
⚠️ Cold wash reduction      reported 30–40%
⚠️ Filter retention         reported 80–90% · in-drum bag ~54%
⚠️ Synthetics as primary marine microplastic  ~35% (IUCN)
⚠️ CA PFAS threshold        100 ppm TOF (2025) → 50 ppm (1 Jan 2027)
⚠️ EU PFHxA in apparel      restricted from 10 Oct 2026
```
