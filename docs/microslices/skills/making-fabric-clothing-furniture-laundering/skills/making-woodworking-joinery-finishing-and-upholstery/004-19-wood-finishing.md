## §19. Wood Finishing

```
⚠️ FILM FINISHES  sit ON the wood — protective, repairable-by-recoating
  ⚠️ SHELLAC   fast, non-toxic when cured, ⚠️ dissolved by alcohol,
     excellent as a barrier/sealer coat under almost anything
  LACQUER      fast, sprayed, ⚠️ solvent-heavy
  ⚠️ VARNISH / POLYURETHANE  durable, slow, ⚠️ hard to repair invisibly
  WATER-BASED  low odour, ⚠️ raises the grain (sand between coats)
⚠️ PENETRATING  soak IN — easy to apply and repair, less protective
  ⚠️ OIL (tung, linseed)  ⚠️ "Danish oil" and "teak oil" are marketing
     names, not defined products — read the actual contents
  WAX  ⚠️ a maintenance coat, not a finish on its own
```
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — oily rags from linseed or tung oil can SPONTANEOUSLY COMBUST.** ⚠️ **The
> oil cures by exothermic oxidation, and a crumpled rag insulates the heat.** **⚠️ Spread
> rags flat to dry, or submerge them in water in a sealed metal container.** **This starts
> real fires every year and it is the one genuine safety hazard in this section.**

**⚠️ Surface prep is most of the result**: ⚠️ **sand progressively without skipping grits,
remove all dust, and ⚠️ raise the grain deliberately with a damp cloth before the first
water-based coat.** **⚠️ Blotch-prone woods (pine, cherry, maple) need a conditioner or a
shellac washcoat before stain.**
