## §18. Adhesives and Fasteners

```
⚠️ PVA (yellow/white)  the default. ⚠️ Stronger than the wood itself in
   long grain. Not gap-filling; needs clamping pressure. Type II/III
   for water resistance
⚠️ HIDE GLUE  ⚠️ REVERSIBLE with heat and moisture — which is why
   antiques can be repaired and modern glued furniture often can't.
   Liquid or hot hide; the conservator's choice
POLYURETHANE  ⚠️ foams (the expansion is NOT gap-filling STRENGTH),
   moisture-cured, messy
EPOXY  ⚠️ genuinely gap-filling, waterproof, good for end grain and
   dissimilar materials
CA  fast, brittle, for small repairs
```
**⚠️ Clamping**: ⚠️ **pressure, not squeeze-out, is the objective; even distribution
matters more than force; and ⚠️ a joint that needs heavy clamping to close is a joint
that's cut wrong and will be under permanent stress.**
**Fasteners**: **pilot holes (⚠️ always, in hardwood), pocket screws, knockdown fittings,
and ⚠️ the fact that screws into end grain hold poorly.**
