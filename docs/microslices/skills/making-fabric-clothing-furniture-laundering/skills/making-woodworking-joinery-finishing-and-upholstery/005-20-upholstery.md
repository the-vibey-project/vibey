## §20. Upholstery

**⚠️ The traditional stack, bottom to top**: **frame → webbing → springs (⚠️ coil, tied
eight ways, or sinuous/zigzag) → burlap → edge roll → stuffing → muslin → wadding →
top cover.**
**⚠️ Foam**: **density vs firmness are DIFFERENT properties** — ⚠️ **density predicts
LONGEVITY, firmness predicts feel, and cheap furniture uses low-density foam that feels
fine and collapses within a couple of years.**
**⚠️ Fabric selection for upholstery**: **abrasion resistance measured in double rubs
(⚠️ Wyzenbeek or Martindale — different tests, not interconvertible), cleaning codes
(⚠️ W water-based, S solvent, WS either, X vacuum only), and ⚠️ railroading (running the
fabric sideways to avoid seams on wide pieces).**
**⚠️ Reupholstery is worth it when the FRAME is good** — **hardwood, joined rather than
stapled** — **and rarely otherwise.**
