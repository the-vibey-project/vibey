## §22. ⚠️ How Laundering Actually Works

**⚠️ SINNER'S CIRCLE: four factors that trade off against each other. Reduce one and you
must increase another to get the same result.**
```
⚠️ TEMPERATURE · CHEMISTRY · MECHANICAL ACTION · TIME
```
⚠️ **This is why cold washing works — modern detergents with effective enzymes and
surfactants substitute chemistry for temperature — and why cold washing with an old
detergent and a short cycle disappoints.**
```
DETERGENT COMPONENTS
  ⚠️ SURFACTANTS  lift and suspend soil (hydrophilic head, lipophilic tail)
  ⚠️ ENZYMES      protease (protein), amylase (starch), lipase (fat),
     ⚠️ cellulase (removes fuzz and pills — and gradually thins cotton)
  BUILDERS      soften water so surfactant isn't wasted
  ⚠️ OPTICAL BRIGHTENERS  fluorescent dyes that make white LOOK whiter.
     ⚠️ They don't clean anything, and they build up
  ⚠️ BLEACH  chlorine (⚠️ NOT on wool, silk, elastane) vs OXYGEN
     (percarbonate — gentler, colour-safe, ⚠️ needs warmth to activate)
```
**⚠️ Water hardness matters more than people think**: ⚠️ **hard water wastes surfactant and
deposits minerals that make fabric stiff and grey.** **In hard water use more detergent or
a builder, not more fabric softener.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — fabric softener is actively harmful to several fabric types.** ⚠️ **It
> works by depositing a cationic lubricant film, which destroys the absorbency of towels,
> ruins the wicking of technical activewear, degrades the flame-resistance of children's
> sleepwear, and builds up in microfibre.** **⚠️ For softening, white vinegar in the rinse
> or wool dryer balls do the job without the coating.**

**⚠️ Overloading is the most common laundering error** — ⚠️ **it removes the mechanical
action leg of Sinner's circle entirely**, **and clothes come out unclean regardless of what
you put in the drawer.**
