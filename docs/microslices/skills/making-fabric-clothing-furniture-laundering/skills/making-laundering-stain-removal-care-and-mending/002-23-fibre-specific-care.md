## §23. Fibre-Specific Care

```
⚠️ COTTON/LINEN  tolerate heat and agitation. ⚠️ Shrinkage is the risk
   (unless pre-shrunk); hot drying is what actually shrinks garments
⚠️ WOOL  ⚠️ NEVER heat + moisture + agitation together — that's felting,
   and it is IRREVERSIBLE. Cool water, wool-specific (NEUTRAL or slightly
   acidic) detergent, ⚠️ NO alkali, no wringing. ⚠️ Dry FLAT — wet wool
   is heavy and hanging it stretches the garment permanently
⚠️ SILK  ⚠️ alkali-sensitive; no chlorine bleach; ⚠️ sunlight degrades it.
   Cool water, neutral detergent, avoid prolonged soaking
⚠️ RAYON/VISCOSE  ⚠️ VERY weak wet (§1). Support the weight; don't wring;
   often genuinely dry-clean-only
⚠️ POLYESTER  easy, but ⚠️ holds oils and odour. Wash promptly, use a
   detergent with lipase, avoid fabric softener, and ⚠️ avoid high heat
   which sets oily stains permanently
⚠️ ELASTANE BLENDS  ⚠️ heat kills elastane. Warm wash at most, air dry
DOWN  ⚠️ needs a specific detergent and thorough drying with agitation
   (dryer balls) or it clumps and mildews
```
**⚠️ "Dry clean only" vs "dry clean"**: ⚠️ **the former is a genuine warning (structure,
interfacing, dye, or fibre); the latter is often the manufacturer being cautious.**
**⚠️ Home dry-cleaning kits are a dryer-based refresh, not solvent cleaning.**
