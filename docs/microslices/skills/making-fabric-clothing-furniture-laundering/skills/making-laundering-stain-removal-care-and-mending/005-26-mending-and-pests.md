## §26. Mending and Pests

**⚠️ Mending**: **darning (⚠️ weaving a new patch across a hole), Swiss darning/duplicate
stitch for knits, patching (⚠️ from the inside, on the grain), sashiko and visible mending,
reweaving, and button and zip replacement.**
**⚠️ Reinforce BEFORE the hole**: ⚠️ **thin areas are far easier to save than holes, and
elbow, knee and crotch wear is predictable.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — clothes moths and carpet beetles eat KERATIN, so they attack wool, silk,
> fur and feathers, not cotton or synthetics.** ⚠️ **The larvae do the damage, not the
> adults, and they prefer soiled fabric — sweat, food and body oils are what attract
> them.** **⚠️ Therefore: CLEAN before storing is the single most effective control.**
> **⚠️ Cedar and lavender are repellents at best and do not kill larvae; freezing
> (several days at −18°C, ideally with a thaw and refreeze cycle) or thorough heat does.**
> **Sealed containers and vacuuming the dark undisturbed places — under furniture, closet
> corners — is the rest of it.**
