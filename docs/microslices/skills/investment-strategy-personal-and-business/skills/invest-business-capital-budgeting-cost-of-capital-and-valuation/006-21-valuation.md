## §21. Valuation

**⚠️ Three families, and any serious valuation triangulates:**
⚠️ **DCF (intrinsic — discounted future cash flows, and ⚠️ extremely sensitive to the
terminal value and discount rate, which typically dominate the answer); COMPARABLES
(relative — multiples of similar companies, which imports the market's mispricing along
with its judgement); and ASSET-BASED (floor value, relevant for liquidation).**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — a valuation is a NEGOTIATING POSITION supported by analysis, not a
> discovered fact.** ⚠️ **Every DCF contains assumptions that determine the answer, and
> the honest practice is to show the range and the sensitivities rather than a single
> number.** **⚠️ When someone shows you one number to two decimal places, ask what
> terminal growth rate they used.**
