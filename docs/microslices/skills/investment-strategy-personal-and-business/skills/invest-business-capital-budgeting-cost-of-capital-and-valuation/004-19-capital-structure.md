## §19. Capital Structure

**⚠️ Modigliani-Miller** says that in a frictionless world capital structure doesn't affect
firm value — ⚠️ **which is useful precisely because it tells you that everything real about
the decision lives in the FRICTIONS: taxes, bankruptcy costs, agency conflicts and
information asymmetry.**
**⚠️ The trade-off**: ⚠️ **debt is cheaper (tax deductible, senior claim) and adds FINANCIAL
risk on top of business risk.** **⚠️ The right leverage depends on cash flow stability —
volatile businesses should carry less.**
**⚠️ PECKING ORDER**: ⚠️ **firms tend to prefer internal funds, then debt, then equity,
because issuing equity signals that management thinks the shares are expensive.**
**⚠️ Covenants and refinancing risk** are what actually kill leveraged businesses —
⚠️ **not the interest rate but the need to refinance at a bad moment.**
