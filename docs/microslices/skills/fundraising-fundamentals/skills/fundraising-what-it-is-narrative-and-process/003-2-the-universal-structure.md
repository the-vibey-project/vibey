## §2. The Universal Structure

**⚠️ Whatever you're raising, four things are being decided:**
```
SOURCE       ⚠️ who has capital AND a mandate to deploy it into something like you.
             The mandate part is what founders and EDs miss — a VC with a
             healthcare thesis is not a source for your fintech, at any quality
INSTRUMENT   what they get (§1)
TERMS        ⚠️ price, preferences, control, restrictions, reporting
OBLIGATIONS  ⚠️ what you owe afterwards, for how long, and to whom
```
**⚠️ "Mandate fit" is the highest-leverage filter in every one of the three worlds.**
**A VC's fund size, stage, sector and check size; a foundation's programme areas and
geography; a bond buyer's rating requirement.** ⚠️ **Most rejections are mandate
mismatches dressed as judgements about you**, and **researching mandate before pitching
is the cheapest possible improvement to hit rate.**
