## §17. Post-IPO Raising

```
FOLLOW-ON      ⚠️ a secondary public offering. Primary (company) or secondary
               (existing holders) shares
SHELF / ATM    ⚠️ registered in advance, sold into the market opportunistically.
               Cheap and flexible — the workhorse
PIPE           private placement to institutions at a discount. ⚠️ Fast, and
               signals difficulty raising conventionally
CONVERTIBLE    debt convertible to equity. ⚠️ Low coupon, and dilutive later
RIGHTS ISSUE   ⚠️ existing shareholders get first refusal pro rata. Common in Europe
```
**⚠️ Equity issuance signals.** **Markets generally read a share issue as management
believing the stock is fully valued** — ⚠️ **which is why announced offerings frequently
move the price down**, and why the sequencing and framing of the announcement matters.
