## 16. Research workflow

Frame the question with population, phenomenon, mechanism, comparison, outcome, and time. Build a source map: primary evidence, scholarly synthesis, data, standards, and critiques. Keep a search log. Define inclusion and exclusion criteria. Separate exploratory from confirmatory analysis. Preserve uncertainty and dissent. Report what would falsify or change the conclusion.
