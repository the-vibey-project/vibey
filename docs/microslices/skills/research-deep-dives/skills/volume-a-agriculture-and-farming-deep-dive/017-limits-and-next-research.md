## Limits and next research

This is a completed first-edition synthesis of Volume A, not a substitute for dedicated monographs. The next depth pass should add case studies and quantitative literature reviews for each subfield: soil-health measurement, irrigation economics, pesticide resistance, livestock emissions and welfare, controlled-environment agriculture, farm labor, crop insurance, postharvest loss, autonomous machinery, and climate adaptation. Those questions have different evidence bases and should not be collapsed into one score.
