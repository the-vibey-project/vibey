## 1. What counts as a pest

Agricultural pests include:

- insects and mites;
- weeds and parasitic plants;
- fungal, bacterial, viral, and oomycete pathogens;
- nematodes and other soil organisms;
- rodents, birds, and vertebrate herbivores;
- invasive species and quarantine organisms;
- livestock parasites and disease vectors;
- stored-grain insects, molds, and rodents;
- postharvest pests and contamination organisms.

Diagnosis comes before treatment. Nutrient deficiency, drought, waterlogging, heat, herbicide
injury, compaction, salinity, mechanical damage, and disease can resemble insect feeding. A
wrong diagnosis can make the problem worse, destroy beneficial organisms, waste money, and
delay the correct response.
