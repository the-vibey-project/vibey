## 7. Common failure modes

- **Encyclopedia before drama:** build only the world elements the current story can pressure.
- **Aesthetic culture:** distinctive clothes and invented words without institutions or power.
- **Magic/technology without second-order effects:** ask what it does to work, law, class,
  warfare, family, disability, medicine, and belief.
- **Mystery-box evasion:** promise meaningful answers; do not confuse ambiguity with omission.
- **Trilogy middle-book paralysis:** give volume two an independent moral crisis and ending.
- **Escalation inflation:** larger map, weaker intimacy; escalate choices and consequences.
- **Canon worship:** preserve contradictions only when they generate meaning; otherwise clarify.
- **Adaptation literalism:** translate function, not surface form.
- **Game cutscene domination:** let mechanics, space, and player decisions carry meaning.
- **False choice:** communicate constraints honestly; reactivity can be local and still matter.
- **Cultural extraction:** do not borrow a living people's sacred material as exotic texture.
- **Production fantasy:** budget, schedule, localization, accessibility, performance capture,
  QA, and pipeline constraints belong in the creative design from the beginning.
