## Research brief

Agriculture is not only a biological production process. It is a designed system of land, water, buildings, roads, energy, machines, labor, finance, insurance, processing, storage, and markets. A farm can grow an excellent crop and still fail because a culvert washes out, a pump lacks power, a grain bin spoils the harvest, a cold room cannot pull field heat, a slaughter process contaminates product, or debt payments continue after an uninsured loss.

The central management idea is **whole-system resilience**: design physical assets, operating procedures, capital structure, insurance, and preservation systems together around the farm’s hazards, products, markets, and recovery time.
