## 13. Final synthesis

The strongest common principle across all volumes is not maximal complexity. It is accountable coherence:

- rules produce consequences;
- resources and power shape behavior;
- information is partial and situated;
- maintenance determines survival;
- diversity and redundancy can create resilience;
- values shape what counts as success;
- people deserve agency and dignity;
- uncertainty should be visible;
- systems should be judged by lived outcomes, not only elegant design.
