## Purpose

The earlier volumes studied domains separately. This volume explains how to connect them without collapsing important differences. Agriculture, operating systems, communities, religious institutions, political systems, media worlds, and engineered technologies are all systems, but they have different materials, timescales, evidence standards, and ethical constraints.
