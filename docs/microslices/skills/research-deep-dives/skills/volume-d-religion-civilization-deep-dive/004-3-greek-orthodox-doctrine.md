## 3. Greek Orthodox doctrine

Eastern Orthodox theology centers on the Trinity, incarnation, resurrection, the Church, Scripture within Holy Tradition, the Ecumenical Councils, episcopal communion, divine liturgy, icons, asceticism, saints, and participation in divine life often discussed as theosis. Orthodox ecclesiology is conciliar and does not recognize a universal papal jurisdiction in the Roman Catholic sense.

Orthodoxy often calls sacraments holy mysteries. Seven are commonly counted, but Orthodox sources emphasize that the whole life of the Church is sacramental and mystical rather than limiting sacramentality to a numbered list. Baptism, Chrismation, Eucharist, confession, marriage, ordination, and unction are central mysteries. [OCA Orthodox Faith](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith); [OCA sacraments](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/worship/the-sacraments/the-sacraments).
