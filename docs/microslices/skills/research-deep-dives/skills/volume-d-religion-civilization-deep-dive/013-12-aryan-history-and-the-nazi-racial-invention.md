## 12. Aryan history and the Nazi racial invention

“Aryan” is historically associated with Indo-Iranian linguistic and cultural contexts. European racial theorists turned linguistic relationships into a supposed biological race. Nazi ideology then manipulated the term into a hierarchy, defined belonging and exclusion, and connected it to antisemitism, eugenics, conquest, forced labor, and genocide.

There is no legitimate biological “Aryan race” hierarchy. Research may examine Indo-Iranian languages, texts, migrations, religions, state formation, agriculture, kinship, trade, gender, and law. These are diverse societies over long periods, not one Aryan civilization.

Sources: [USHMM Aryan](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/aryan-1); [USHMM Nazi racism](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/nazi-racism).
