## §25. Community Conflict

**⚠️ Conflict at group scale differs from §17 → `speaking-listening-rapport-difficult-conversations-and-negotiation`** — ⚠️ **it has an audience, factions form
fast, and history accumulates.**
**⚠️ Address early and directly.** ⚠️ **Hoping it resolves is the standard mistake, and the
cost of an awkward early conversation is always lower than the cost of the late one.**
**⚠️ Handle privately where possible, publicly where the community needs to see it
handled** — ⚠️ **and knowing which is a judgement call about whether the harm was public.**
**⚠️ The missing stair problem**: ⚠️ **a community that quietly works around a person who
harms others is protecting the person and sacrificing everyone who encounters them
unwarned.** ⚠️ **This is common, it is a leadership failure, and it is usually rationalized
as keeping the peace.**
**⚠️ Enforcement**: ⚠️ **consistent, proportionate, documented, with a path back where
appropriate — ⚠️ and the willingness to actually remove someone is what makes every other
norm credible.**
**⚠️ Safeguarding** in any community involving children or vulnerable adults is a formal
obligation with legal requirements, ⚠️ **not a matter of judgement or community feeling, and
it needs written policy, trained people and reporting lines established before anything
happens.**
