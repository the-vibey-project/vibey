## §22. ⚠️ Facilitation

> **⚠️ A distinct skill from speaking, frequently assigned to whoever is most senior rather
> than whoever can do it.**
```
⚠️ ⚠️ THE FACILITATOR SERVES THE GROUP'S PROCESS, NOT THE
   OUTCOME. ⚠️ Holding both roles — running the meeting and
   advocating a position — is a conflict, and it should be
   named or separated
⚠️ BEFORE  ⚠️ purpose, desired output, right people, ⚠️ AGENDA
   CIRCULATED IN ADVANCE, pre-reading
⚠️ ⚠️ THE MOST VALUABLE MOVE: DECIDE HOW YOU WILL DECIDE, BEFORE
   DISCUSSING. ⚠️ Consensus, consent, majority, or
   consultative-then-decided — ⚠️ ambiguity about the decision
   rule is the cause of most meeting frustration and most
   post-meeting resentment
⚠️ DURING  ⚠️ start on time · ⚠️ MANAGE AIRTIME actively (⚠️ the
   loudest voice is not the best signal, and quiet people often
   have the objection that matters) · ⚠️ go round to draw people
   in · park tangents visibly · surface disagreement rather
   than smoothing it · ⚠️ name the dynamic when it's the problem
⚠️ AFTER  ⚠️ decisions, owners and dates, circulated. ⚠️ A
   meeting with no recorded decision usually did not have one
⚠️ ⚠️ THE MEETING THAT SHOULD NOT HAVE HAPPENED  ⚠️ if the
   purpose is broadcast, write it down instead
⚠️ ONLINE  harder — ⚠️ shorter, more structured, explicit
   turn-taking, chat as a parallel channel to actually read
```
