## §23. Organizing and Mobilizing

**⚠️ The distinction that matters**: ⚠️ **MOBILIZING activates people who already agree;
ORGANIZING builds power by developing people and relationships over time.** ⚠️ **Mobilizing
is faster and does not compound; organizing is slow and does.**
**⚠️ The one-to-one relational meeting** is the core organizing practice — ⚠️ **a
conversation to understand someone's actual interests and motivations, not to recruit them
on the spot.**
**⚠️ Ladders of engagement**: ⚠️ **give people a first step that costs little, then a real
one; and ⚠️ the most common failure is having no rung between "attend" and "run the
thing."**
**⚠️ Power mapping**: ⚠️ **who decides, who influences them, and what they respond to.**
**⚠️ Coalitions** work on narrow shared interest and fracture on scope creep.
**⚠️ Sustaining momentum** — ⚠️ **celebrating small wins is not sentimentality; it is how
people learn that effort produces outcomes, which is the belief the whole thing runs on.**
