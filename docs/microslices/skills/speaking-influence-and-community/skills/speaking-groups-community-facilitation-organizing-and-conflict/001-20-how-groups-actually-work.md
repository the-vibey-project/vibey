## §20. How Groups Actually Work

**⚠️ Formation stages** (forming, storming, norming, performing) are ⚠️ **a useful shorthand
and not a law — groups cycle, regress on membership change, and some never leave
storming.**
**⚠️ Norms are set early and by behaviour, not by statements** — ⚠️ **what a group tolerates
in its first months becomes what it is.**
**⚠️ Roles emerge** whether or not they are assigned, ⚠️ **and the informal ones — the
connector, the historian, the person everyone actually asks — matter more than the titles.**
**⚠️ The dynamics that reliably cause harm**: ⚠️ **groupthink; the abilene paradox (everyone
going along with something nobody wants); diffusion of responsibility; ⚠️ and the
TYRANNY OF STRUCTURELESSNESS — ⚠️ Freeman's point that a group without formal structure does
not lack power structure, it has an INVISIBLE and unaccountable one, which is worse.**
**⚠️ Size changes everything**: ⚠️ **a group where everyone knows everyone works by
relationship; past that it needs process, and the transition is where most groups fail
without noticing why.**
