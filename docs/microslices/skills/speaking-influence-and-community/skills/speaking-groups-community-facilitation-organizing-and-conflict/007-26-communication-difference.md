## §26. ⚠️ Communication Difference

**⚠️ Communication norms are conventions, not standards** — ⚠️ **and treating one culture's
conventions as universal competence excludes people unnecessarily.**
```
⚠️ WHERE NORMS DIFFER LEGITIMATELY  ⚠️ eye contact · directness ·
   interruption and overlap · silence tolerance · emotional
   expressiveness · high vs low context communication ·
   formality · turn-taking
⚠️ ⚠️ THE DOUBLE EMPATHY PROBLEM  ⚠️ Milton's finding, now
   well-supported: communication difficulty between autistic and
   non-autistic people is MUTUAL and bidirectional — ⚠️ not a
   deficit in one party. ⚠️ Autistic people communicate
   effectively with each other; the breakdown is at the
   interface. ⚠️ This reframes "social skills" from a deficit to
   be remediated into a translation problem with shared
   responsibility
⚠️ PRACTICAL ACCOMMODATIONS THAT COST NOTHING  ⚠️ agendas in
   advance · written follow-up · ⚠️ saying what you mean
   literally · ⚠️ not requiring eye contact · allowing
   processing time before demanding an answer · ⚠️ permitting
   fidgeting and movement · lighting and noise · advance notice
   of changes
⚠️ FOR SPEAKERS  ⚠️ captions · handouts · clear structure ·
   ⚠️ not relying on tone alone to carry meaning · describing
   visuals aloud
⚠️ ⚠️ AND NOTE THE ASYMMETRY: most of these help EVERYONE.
   ⚠️ Clear agendas and written decisions are simply better
   practice, which is the usual pattern with accessibility
```
