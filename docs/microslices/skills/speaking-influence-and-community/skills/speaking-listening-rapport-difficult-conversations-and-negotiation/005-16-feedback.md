## §16. Feedback

**⚠️ Giving**: ⚠️ **specific and behavioural rather than characterological ("the report
missed the deadline twice" not "you're unreliable"); timely; private for criticism, public
for praise; ⚠️ and separate — genuinely separate — performance feedback from evaluation and
compensation conversations, because people cannot hear the first while braced for the
second.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — the "feedback sandwich" is widely taught and works poorly.** ⚠️ **People
> learn the pattern and discount the praise as packaging, and the criticism gets softened
> until the message is missed entirely.** **⚠️ Be direct, be kind, and let the praise be
> real praise at another time.**

**⚠️ Receiving is the harder skill** (§14's identity conversation): ⚠️ **listen fully before
responding, ask for specifics, separate the message from the delivery (⚠️ badly-delivered
feedback can still be true), and say thank you even when it stings.**
**⚠️ Asking for it well**: ⚠️ **"what's one thing I could do differently?" gets an answer
where "any feedback?" gets "no, all good."**
