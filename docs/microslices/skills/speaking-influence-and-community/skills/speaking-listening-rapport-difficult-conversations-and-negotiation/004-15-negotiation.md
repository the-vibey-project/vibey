## §15. Negotiation

**⚠️ Interests versus positions** is the durable insight: ⚠️ **a position is what someone
says they want; an interest is why. ⚠️ Positions conflict far more often than interests do,
and the whole of integrative negotiation is finding the interests underneath.**
**⚠️ BATNA** — ⚠️ **your best alternative to a negotiated agreement — is the source of real
leverage, and improving it before you negotiate is more effective than any tactic at the
table.**
**⚠️ Distributive versus integrative**: ⚠️ **fixed-pie situations exist, and people assume
them far more often than they occur.** ⚠️ **Asking what else matters to them is cheap and
frequently finds trades.**
**⚠️ Anchoring is real and works** — ⚠️ **and note the ethical line: an aggressive first
offer is legitimate; a fabricated justification for it is not** (§19 → `speaking-persuasion-and-the-ethics-of-influence`).
**⚠️ Practical**: ⚠️ **prepare, ask more questions than you expect to, do not negotiate
against yourself, silence after an offer, and be willing to walk — which is only credible
if it is true.**
**⚠️ Relationship-preserving negotiation** matters most where you will meet again, which is
most community and workplace contexts.
