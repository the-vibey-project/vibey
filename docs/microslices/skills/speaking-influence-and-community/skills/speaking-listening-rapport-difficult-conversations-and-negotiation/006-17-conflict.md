## §17. Conflict

**⚠️ Conflict is not failure** — ⚠️ **it is what happens when people who care about
different things work together, and a group with no visible conflict is usually suppressing
it rather than lacking it.**
**⚠️ Sources**: ⚠️ **substantive disagreement, process disagreement, unmet interests,
misunderstanding, values, and personality — and treating a substantive conflict as a
personality clash is a classic and damaging misdiagnosis.**
**⚠️ The styles** (Thomas-Kilmann's framing): ⚠️ **competing, accommodating, avoiding,
compromising, collaborating — ⚠️ and the useful point is that each is right sometimes.
Avoidance is correct for trivia; collaboration is expensive and reserved for what matters.**
**⚠️ De-escalation**: ⚠️ **lower your voice, slow down, acknowledge the feeling before
addressing the content, ask genuine questions, and take a break — ⚠️ physiological arousal
genuinely impairs the reasoning you need, and it takes real time to subside.**
**⚠️ Repair after** is the part most often skipped, ⚠️ **and a specific apology for a
specific thing does more than a general one.**
