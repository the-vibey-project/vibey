## §13. Rapport and Trust

**⚠️ Trust decomposes usefully** into ⚠️ **competence (can you do it), reliability (do you
do what you said), and CARE (are my interests in your calculation)** — ⚠️ **and most trust
failures are the third, mistaken for the first.**
**⚠️ It is built by small kept commitments over time**, ⚠️ **and destroyed asymmetrically —
a single defection undoes a great deal.**
**⚠️ What genuinely helps**: ⚠️ **remembering things about people and following up;
introducing people to each other; attributing credit publicly and in specifics; being the
same person in different rooms; being useful before you need anything.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — deliberate "mirroring" technique is overrated and detectable.** ⚠️ **Natural
> convergence in posture and speech happens by itself when people are genuinely engaged; the
> conscious version reads as odd, and when noticed it costs exactly the trust it was meant
> to build.**

**⚠️ Networking, honestly**: ⚠️ **the transactional version works poorly and feels bad. The
version that works is a long-run practice of being genuinely helpful and interested, with
benefits arriving unpredictably and much later.**
