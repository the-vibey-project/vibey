## §11. Practice and Getting Better

**⚠️ Rehearse aloud, standing, at full volume** — ⚠️ **silent reading is not rehearsal and
does not reveal what breaks.**
**⚠️ Time it**, ⚠️ **and note that live delivery usually runs longer than rehearsal.**
**⚠️ Record yourself.** ⚠️ **Unpleasant and by far the fastest feedback available.**
**⚠️ Deliberate practice** means working a specific weakness, ⚠️ **not repeating whole talks
— pick one thing per outing.**
**⚠️ Feedback**: ⚠️ **ask specific questions ("was the middle section clear?") rather than
"how was it?", which reliably produces "good."**
**⚠️ The venues that give reps**: ⚠️ **speaking clubs, meetups, teaching, church and
community roles, internal brown-bags.** ⚠️ **§2 → `speaking-what-the-field-knows-anxiety-audience-and-structure`'s point stands — volume of exposure is the
mechanism.**
**⚠️ Keep a record** of what worked and what didn't while it's fresh.
