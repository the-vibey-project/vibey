## §7. Delivery

```
⚠️ VOICE  ⚠️ PROJECTION comes from breath support, not strain ·
   ⚠️ PACE — nerves accelerate you, so deliberately slow down ·
   ⚠️ PAUSES are the most underused tool, and they feel far
   longer to you than to the audience · variation in pitch and
   volume · articulation
⚠️ BODY  ⚠️ stillness with purposeful movement beats either
   pacing or rigidity · gestures that arise from the content ·
   ⚠️ EYE CONTACT distributed around the room, in whole
   sentences to one person at a time rather than sweeping
⚠️ ⚠️ AUTHENTICITY BEATS TECHNIQUE. ⚠️ Audiences detect
   performance, and a slightly awkward speaker who clearly means
   it outperforms a polished one who doesn't
⚠️ NOTES  ⚠️ full script (⚠️ risks reading, which kills contact) ·
   bullet notes (⚠️ usually the best compromise) · memorized
   (⚠️ risky — a blank is catastrophic) · ⚠️ know the opening and
   closing verbatim and the middle by structure
⚠️ FILLER WORDS  ⚠️ far less noticeable to audiences than to the
   speaker. ⚠️ The fix is comfort with silence, not policing
⚠️ ⚠️ AND SEE §27.1 BEFORE BELIEVING ANY PERCENTAGE-BASED CLAIM
   ABOUT BODY LANGUAGE
```
