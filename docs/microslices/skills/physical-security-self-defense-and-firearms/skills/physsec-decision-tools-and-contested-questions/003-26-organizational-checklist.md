## §26 Organizational checklist

- Risk assessment on file and reviewed annually
- BTAM team and anonymous reporting channel
- Access control with anti-tailgating culture and visitor management
- Emergency action plan: Run/Hide/Fight training, lockdown hardware compliant with egress codes
- Stop the Bleed kits with AEDs; staff trained
- Periodic authorized physical penetration test with a findings-to-fixes loop
- Liaison with local police, fire and EMS; floor plans shared
- Post-incident recovery plan (EAP, communications, continuity)
