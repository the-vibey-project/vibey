## §27 Free/low-cost training and references

- **FEMA IS-907** *Active Shooter: What You Can Do* (online, free)
- **CISA** Active Shooter Preparedness, Pathway to Violence, Power of Hello, security guides for faith-based organizations and small businesses
- **US Secret Service NTAC** reports on targeted violence and mass attacks
- **Stop the Bleed** (American College of Surgeons) courses
- **Empowerment self-defense** programs (look for EAAA-style curricula for women; programs that include verbal skills and risk recognition)
- **SC SLED** CWP information and approved instructors; SC Code of Laws (scstatehouse.gov) Titles 16 and 23
- **RAND Gun Policy in America** — the most even-handed evidence synthesis available (5th edition)
- **988** Suicide & Crisis Lifeline; National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233; SAMHSA National Helpline 1-800-662-4357
- Books: Gavin de Becker, *The Gift of Fear* (intuition and pre-incident indicators; dated in places); Massad Ayoob, *Deadly Force*; Andrew Branca, *The Law of Self Defense*; Rory Miller, *Meditations on Violence*
