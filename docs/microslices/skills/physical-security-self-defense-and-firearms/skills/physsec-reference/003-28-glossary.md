## §28 Glossary

- **AOJ (Ability, Opportunity, Jeopardy)** — a common teaching framework for when deadly force may be justified: attacker has the means, is positioned to use them, and is acting in a way that places you in imminent danger.
- **BTAM** — Behavioral Threat Assessment and Management.
- **Castle doctrine** — legal presumptions favoring defenders against unlawful forcible entry into homes/occupied vehicles.
- **CPTED** — Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design.
- **CWP** — Concealed Weapons Permit (SC).
- **DGU** — Defensive Gun Use.
- **Disparity of force** — when size, number, skill or other factors let an unarmed attacker pose a deadly threat.
- **Fail-safe / fail-secure** — lock behavior on power loss (unlocked vs. locked).
- **MRTP** — FDA Modified Risk Tobacco Product order.
- **NFA** — National Firearms Act (1934).
- **NICS** — National Instant Criminal Background Check System.
- **OC** — oleoresin capsicum (pepper spray).
- **PPPA** — SC Protection of Persons and Property Act.
- **Schedule I / III** — federal controlled-substance categories; Schedule III has accepted medical use and lower abuse potential.
