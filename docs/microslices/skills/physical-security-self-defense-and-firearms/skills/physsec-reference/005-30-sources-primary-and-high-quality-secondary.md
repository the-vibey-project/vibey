*Method note: sources were prioritized as primary (statutes, court opinions, government reports, peer-reviewed trials) → high-quality secondary (RAND, CRS, professional-society statements) → trade and advocacy sources (used only for current-events facts and flagged where they carry interest). Industry-funded findings (alarm deterrence, TASER effectiveness from the manufacturer) are marked as such. **\[Pack note: the alarm finding is marked (§5); the TASER-vs-OC comparison in §12 is not — see the pack note there.\]** Legal statements reflect research through September 30, 2026 and are not a substitute for counsel.*
