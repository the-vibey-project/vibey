## §11. Glue-Ups

**⚠️ The most stressful ten minutes in woodworking, because it's timed.**
```
⚠️ DRY FIT EVERYTHING FIRST, with clamps, and rehearse the sequence
⚠️ ⚠️ OPEN TIME is the constraint — ⚠️ PVA gives you minutes.
   ⚠️ For complex assemblies use slow-set or hide glue, or break
   the glue-up into STAGES
⚠️ CLAMPING PRESSURE  ⚠️ even, and enough to close the joint — but
   ⚠️ a joint that needs heavy clamping to close is cut wrong and
   will be under permanent stress
⚠️ CAULS and clamping blocks distribute pressure and protect surfaces
⚠️ CHECK SQUARE AND WIND before the glue sets — ⚠️ measure diagonals
⚠️ SQUEEZE-OUT  ⚠️ remove it, and note that dried glue BLOCKS FINISH,
   producing pale patches you won't see until it's too late
```
**⚠️ Long grain to long grain glues stronger than the wood; end grain glues poorly** —
⚠️ **which is why joints exist to create long-grain contact and mechanical interlock.**
