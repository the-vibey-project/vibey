## §12. Wood Finishing

**⚠️ Surface preparation is most of the result** — ⚠️ **sand progressively without skipping
grits, or plane and scrape (§6), which is faster and better where the grain allows.**
**⚠️ Raise the grain deliberately with a damp cloth before water-based finishes.**
**⚠️ Finish families** (covered more fully in a furniture reference): ⚠️ **film finishes
(shellac, lacquer, varnish/polyurethane, water-based) sit on top and protect; penetrating
finishes (oils, oil-varnish blends) soak in and are easy to repair; wax is a maintenance
coat.**
**⚠️ Practical**: ⚠️ **blotch-prone woods (pine, cherry, maple) need a conditioner or
shellac washcoat before stain; test on offcuts from the same board; thin coats beat thick
ones; and dust control during drying matters more than technique.**
**⚠️ SAFETY**: ⚠️ **oily rags spontaneously combust — spread flat or submerge in water in a
sealed metal container.** **⚠️ Solvent vapour needs ventilation, and some finishes need a
cartridge respirator, not a dust mask.**
