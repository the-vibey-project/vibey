## §29. Numbers

```
⚠️ Wood MC for heated interiors  ⚠️ ~6–8%
⚠️ Tangential movement  ⚠️ roughly 2× radial
⚠️ Sharpening angles  ⚠️ ~25° primary, ~30° secondary (bench chisels)
⚠️ Hacksaw  ⚠️ minimum 3 teeth in the cut
⚠️ Tap drill  ⚠️ ~75% thread engagement standard
⚠️ Brazing vs soldering  ⚠️ ~450°C dividing line
⚠️ Caliper realistic resolution  ~0.05 mm · micrometer ~0.01 mm
⚠️ Table saw AIM standard (proposed)  ⚠️ ≤3.5 mm depth of cut at 1 m/s
⚠️ Measured AIM performance  ⚠️ SawStop 1.5–2.8 mm · Bosch 1.8–2.5 mm
⚠️ CPSC rule withdrawn  ⚠️ 20 August 2025
⚠️ Table saw injuries  ⚠️ ~33,400 ED-treated (2015); ~49,000+ the rule
                       would have prevented or mitigated annually
⚠️ Cr(VI) OSHA PEL  ⚠️ 5 µg/m³ 8-h TWA · action level 2.5 µg/m³
⚠️ Cr(VI) NIOSH REL  ⚠️ 0.2 µg/m³ (25× stricter)
⚠️ Iron oxide fume PEL  10 mg/m³ — ⚠️ 1,000× higher than Cr(VI)
⚠️ US workers exposed to Cr(VI)  ⚠️ ~558,000
⚠️ Lung cancer risk  ⚠️ reported +85% in high-exposure welders
```
