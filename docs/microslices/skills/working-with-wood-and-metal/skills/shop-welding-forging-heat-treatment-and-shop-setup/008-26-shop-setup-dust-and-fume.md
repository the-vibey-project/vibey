## §26. Shop Setup, Dust and Fume

```
⚠️ DUST (wood)  ⚠️ the fine invisible fraction is the hazard (§2).
   ⚠️ A shop vac captures chips and NOT the dangerous fraction
   ⚠️ EXTRACTION AT SOURCE > ambient air cleaner > respirator
   ⚠️ HEPA filtration; ⚠️ and note ambient cleaners run AFTER the
   dust is airborne, which is already the failure
⚠️ FUME (metal)  ⚠️ LOCAL EXHAUST VENTILATION at the arc is the
   primary engineering control (§27.2). ⚠️ Positioning your head
   out of the plume is free and effective
⚠️ RESPIRATORS  ⚠️ a nuisance dust mask is NOT respiratory protection
   for fume or fine dust. ⚠️ P2/P3 (or N95/P100), fit-tested,
   and ⚠️ facial hair breaks the seal
⚠️ LIGHTING · power (⚠️ RCD/GFCI protection) · fire extinguisher ·
   first aid · ⚠️ clear floor and defined walkways
```
**⚠️ Small shop reality**: ⚠️ **you cannot do everything, and the honest sequence is to buy
tools as projects demand them rather than in advance.** **⚠️ Spend on the workbench,
workholding, sharpening and extraction before the third power tool.**
