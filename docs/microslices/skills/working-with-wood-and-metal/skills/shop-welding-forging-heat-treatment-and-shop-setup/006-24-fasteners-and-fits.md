## §24. Fasteners and Fits

**⚠️ Thread standards**: **metric coarse and fine, UNC/UNF, ⚠️ and BSP/NPT pipe threads
(tapered, sealed by interference).** ⚠️ **Never force mismatched threads; identify with a
thread gauge.**
**⚠️ A bolted joint works by CLAMPING** (see a manufacturing reference) — ⚠️ **preload
creates friction that carries the load.** **⚠️ Torque specs exist for that reason.**
**⚠️ Fits**: **clearance, transition, interference.** ⚠️ **Press fits are assembled by force
or by THERMAL means — heat the outer part or chill the inner, which is elegant and
avoids damaging either.**
**⚠️ Thread repair**: **helical inserts, oversize tapping, and ⚠️ the observation that a
stripped thread in aluminium is common and preventable with correct torque.**
