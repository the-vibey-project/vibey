## §20. Brazing and Soldering

**⚠️ Joining WITHOUT melting the parent metal** — ⚠️ **filler flows by CAPILLARY ACTION into
a close-fitting joint, so JOINT CLEARANCE is the critical variable and a sloppy gap makes
a weak joint.**
**⚠️ Brazing** (above ~450°C) — ⚠️ **strong, joins dissimilar metals, excellent for cast
iron and for thin tube (bicycle frames), and lower distortion than welding.**
**⚠️ Soldering** (below ~450°C) — **electronics and plumbing.**
**⚠️ Flux does the essential work**: ⚠️ **it removes oxide and prevents re-oxidation, and
the right flux for the metal and temperature is not optional.**
**⚠️ Heat the WORK, not the filler** — ⚠️ **the joint should melt the filler, which is how
you know it's hot enough for the alloy to flow.**
