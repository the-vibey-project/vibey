## §25. Measurement

**⚠️ Tools**: **rule, tape (⚠️ the hook is deliberately loose to account for its own
thickness — that's not a fault), calipers (⚠️ good to ~0.05 mm realistically), micrometers
(⚠️ 0.01 mm and better, with a ratchet for consistent feel), dial indicators, squares,
feeler gauges, and gauge blocks.**
**⚠️ Practical discipline**: ⚠️ **temperature matters at tight tolerances; measuring FORCE
matters with calipers (over-squeezing reads small); zero your instrument before use; and
⚠️ measure the same feature the same way each time.**
**⚠️ In wood, "measure twice cut once" is real; ⚠️ in metal, measure after every cut,
because you cannot put it back.**
