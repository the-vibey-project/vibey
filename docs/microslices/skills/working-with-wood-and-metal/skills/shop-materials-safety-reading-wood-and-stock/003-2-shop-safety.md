## §2. ⚠️ Shop Safety

> **⚠️ Read this before anything else. The injuries in this domain are predictable and
> the mechanisms are specific.**
```
⚠️ THE ACUTE HAZARDS
   ⚠️ TABLE SAW KICKBACK and blade contact (§9) — ⚠️ the single most
      injurious woodworking machine
   ⚠️ ANGLE GRINDER — ⚠️ disc shatter, kickback, and the sparks it
      throws are burning metal that starts fires metres away
   ⚠️ ENTANGLEMENT — ⚠️ NO gloves near rotating machinery (lathes,
      drill presses); no loose sleeves, no jewellery, tie back hair.
      ⚠️ Gloves on a lathe is how people lose hands
   ⚠️ ARC EYE — ⚠️ UV burns the cornea, symptoms appear HOURS later,
      and BYSTANDERS are exposed too (§19)
   ⚠️ HOT METAL looks identical to cold metal
   ⚠️ SWARF and chips — ⚠️ never clear by hand; use a brush, machine off
⚠️ THE INVISIBLE HAZARDS — the ones people ignore
   ⚠️ WOOD DUST is a classified carcinogen (⚠️ hardwood dust
      particularly, associated with nasal and sinus cancers) and a
      respiratory sensitizer. ⚠️ The FINE fraction you can't see is
      the dangerous part
   ⚠️ WELDING FUME — ⚠️ IARC Group 1 carcinogen; hexavalent chromium
      from stainless is severely regulated (§27.2)
   ⚠️ GALVANIZED metal releases zinc oxide when heated — metal fume fever
   ⚠️ NOISE — cumulative and irreversible. ⚠️ Hearing protection every time
   ⚠️ SOLVENTS and finishes (see a materials reference on oily rags —
      ⚠️ linseed and tung oil rags SPONTANEOUSLY COMBUST)
⚠️ THE HIERARCHY (see a civil/industrial reference)
   ⚠️ ELIMINATE the hazardous operation > SUBSTITUTE a safer process >
   ENGINEERING CONTROLS (guards, extraction, riving knife) >
   ADMINISTRATIVE (procedure, training) > ⚠️ PPE LAST
   ⚠️ PPE is the WEAKEST control and it is what hobbyists reach for first
```
**⚠️ The habits that actually prevent injury**: ⚠️ **never reach over or behind a spinning
blade; use push sticks and hold-downs; unplug before changing blades or bits; keep the
floor clear; work at a pace that permits thinking; ⚠️ and stop when tired, because
fatigue-driven shortcuts are the common precondition to most shop injuries.**
**⚠️ Fire**: ⚠️ **have an extinguisher; understand that grinding sparks smoulder in sawdust
and rags for a long time before igniting; and never grind near flammables.**
