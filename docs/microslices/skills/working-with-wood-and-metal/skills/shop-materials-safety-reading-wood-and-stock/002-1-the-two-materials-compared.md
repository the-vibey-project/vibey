## §1. The Two Materials Compared

```
                    WOOD                      METAL
STABILITY      ⚠️ moves with humidity      ⚠️ stable; moves with heat
DIRECTIONALITY ⚠️ ANISOTROPIC (grain)      mostly isotropic
CUTTING        fast, low force             ⚠️ slow, high force, heat
TOLERANCE      ⚠️ ±0.5 mm is often fine    ⚠️ ±0.02 mm is routine
JOINING        glue is stronger than wood  ⚠️ welding melts the parent
MISTAKES       ⚠️ often recoverable        ⚠️ removed metal never returns
HAZARD         ⚠️ dust, kickback, blades   ⚠️ fume, heat, sparks, swarf
```
**⚠️ The habit transfer that hurts**: ⚠️ **woodworkers coming to metal push too hard and too
fast and burn tools; metalworkers coming to wood ignore grain direction and tear out.**
**⚠️ In both, the fix is the same — let the tool cut at its own rate.**
