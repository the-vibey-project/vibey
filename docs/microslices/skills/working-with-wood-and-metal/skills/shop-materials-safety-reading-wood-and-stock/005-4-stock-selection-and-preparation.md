## §4. Stock Selection and Preparation

**⚠️ Board defects to check before buying**: ⚠️ **cup, bow, crook and TWIST — and twist is
the worst because it costs the most thickness to remove.**
**⚠️ Sawing patterns**: **flatsawn (cheap, wide figure, moves most), quartersawn (⚠️ stable,
ray fleck in oak, expensive), riftsawn.**
**⚠️ The four-square process is the foundation of accurate work:**
```
⚠️ 1. ONE FACE FLAT (jointer or hand plane)
⚠️ 2. ONE EDGE SQUARE to that face
⚠️ 3. SECOND FACE PARALLEL (thicknesser/planer)
⚠️ 4. SECOND EDGE PARALLEL (table saw)
⚠️ Everything downstream depends on this. Skipping it means every
   joint fights you
```
**⚠️ Sheet goods**: **plywood (⚠️ dimensionally stable — which is why casework uses it),
MDF (⚠️ flat and its dust is particularly fine and hazardous), particleboard, and veneered
panels.**
