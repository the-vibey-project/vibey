## §11. Heat Illness

```
⚠️ HEAT EXHAUSTION  heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, headache,
   ⚠️ MENTAL STATUS INTACT. Rest, shade, fluids, electrolytes
⚠️ HEAT STROKE  ⚠️ ALTERED MENTAL STATUS + high core temperature.
   ⚠️ A MEDICAL EMERGENCY with a mortality that rises with every
   minute of delay
⚠️ THE DISTINGUISHING SIGN IS MENTAL STATUS, NOT SWEATING —
   ⚠️ "dry skin" is NOT required; exertional heat stroke frequently
   presents with the patient still sweating profusely
⚠️ TREATMENT  ⚠️ COOL FIRST, TRANSPORT SECOND. Cold water immersion
   is the most effective field cooling method where available;
   otherwise wet the skin and maximize airflow, ice to neck,
   armpits and groin
```
**⚠️ Hyponatremia is the mirror-image error and it is mistaken for dehydration**:
⚠️ **drinking large volumes of plain water while losing sodium through sweat produces
confusion, nausea and headache — symptoms that look like dehydration, and giving more
plain water makes it worse and can be fatal.** ⚠️ **Include electrolytes and food on long
exertion, and be suspicious when someone who has been drinking heavily gets worse.**
**⚠️ Wet-bulb temperature is the meaningful heat-stress metric** (see a refrigeration/
psychrometrics reference) — ⚠️ **because evaporative cooling fails as humidity rises.**
