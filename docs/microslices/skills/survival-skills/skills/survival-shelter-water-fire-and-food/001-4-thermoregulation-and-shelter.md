## §4. ⚠️ Thermoregulation and Shelter

> **⚠️ The most important practical section. Hypothermia is the wilderness killer, and
> the conditions that produce it are unremarkable.**
```
⚠️ HEAT LOSS MECHANISMS, and where the leverage is
   ⚠️ CONDUCTION  ⚠️ the ground steals heat FAST. Insulation UNDER you
      matters more than a blanket over you, and this is the most
      commonly missed point in improvised shelter
   ⚠️ CONVECTION  wind. ⚠️ A windbreak is worth more than most insulation
   ⚠️ EVAPORATION  ⚠️ WET is the multiplier. Wet clothing loses heat
      dramatically faster than dry
   RADIATION  head, neck, hands
⚠️ THEREFORE THE PRIORITY IS: DRY → OUT OF WIND → OFF THE GROUND →
   INSULATED
```
**⚠️ Hypothermia is a spectrum and the early signs are cognitive:**
```
⚠️ MILD  shivering, ⚠️ CLUMSINESS and POOR JUDGEMENT (⚠️ the "umbles" —
   stumbles, mumbles, fumbles, grumbles). ⚠️ The impaired judgement
   IS the danger, because the person stops helping themselves
⚠️ MODERATE  ⚠️ shivering STOPS (a serious sign, not an improvement),
   confusion, slurred speech
⚠️ SEVERE  ⚠️ rigidity, unresponsiveness, undetectable pulse
⚠️ PARADOXICAL UNDRESSING occurs in severe cases and is a recognized
   phenomenon, not irrationality
```
**⚠️ Field management principles** (§23.2 → `survival-reference` for current guidance): ⚠️ **handle GENTLY —
rough handling of a severely hypothermic person can precipitate cardiac arrhythmia;
remove wet clothing; insulate including underneath and provide a vapour barrier; add
external heat to the trunk rather than the extremities; give warm sweet drinks ONLY if
fully conscious and able to swallow; ⚠️ and "no one is dead until warm and dead" reflects
the real phenomenon of survival after prolonged severe hypothermia — do not abandon
resuscitation on temperature grounds alone.**
**⚠️ Shelter principles**: ⚠️ **small volume beats large (you are the heater); insulate
below; block wind; ⚠️ and the fastest shelter is usually improvement of existing terrain —
a hollow, a deadfall, a rock overhang — rather than construction.** **⚠️ A large plastic
bag or emergency bivvy is remarkably effective for its weight and is why it appears in
every serious kit list** (§19 → `survival-planning-kit-practice-and-myths-that-kill`).
