## §6. Fire

**⚠️ Fire matters for warmth, morale, signalling (§9 → `survival-navigation-signalling-and-field-medicine`), water treatment and cooking —
in roughly that order.** ⚠️ **Its morale value is genuinely significant and shouldn't be
dismissed** (§2 → `survival-what-kills-psychology-and-priorities`).
```
⚠️ THE THREE REQUIREMENTS  heat, fuel, oxygen — ⚠️ and the near-universal
   failure is FUEL PREPARATION, not ignition
⚠️ THE PROGRESSION  tinder (⚠️ takes a spark) → kindling (pencil-thin
   to thumb-thick) → fuel. ⚠️ Prepare far more than you think, and
   ALL of it, BEFORE striking anything
⚠️ IGNITION  ⚠️ carry a lighter AND a ferrocerium rod AND stormproof
   matches. ⚠️ Redundancy, because they fail differently
⚠️ WET CONDITIONS  ⚠️ standing dead wood beats ground wood; split to
   the dry interior; ⚠️ resinous wood and birch bark light wet;
   build a platform off wet ground
⚠️ FIRE IN WIND AND RAIN  a reflector and a windbreak, and shelter
   the fire lay while it establishes
```
**⚠️ Friction fire is a genuine skill and a poor emergency plan**: ⚠️ **it is difficult
under ideal conditions, extremely difficult when wet, cold and exhausted, and it consumes
energy and time you may not have.** **⚠️ Learn it if you want; do not rely on it.**
**⚠️ Safety and legality**: ⚠️ **check fire restrictions, clear a perimeter, never build
under snow-laden branches or on peat, and ⚠️ carbon monoxide accumulates fatally in
enclosed shelters — never burn inside an unventilated snow shelter, tent or vehicle.**
