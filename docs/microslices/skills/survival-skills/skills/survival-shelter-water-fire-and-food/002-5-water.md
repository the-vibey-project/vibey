## §5. Water

**⚠️ Dehydration degrades judgement before it threatens life**, ⚠️ **which links it back to
§2 → `survival-what-kills-psychology-and-priorities` — the mistake it causes is the danger.**
**⚠️ Finding water**: ⚠️ **descend and follow drainages; look for green vegetation, animal
trails converging, insect activity; collect rain and dew; snow must be MELTED before
consumption (eating snow costs body heat you cannot spare — §4).**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — treatment removes different things and they are not interchangeable.**
> ⚠️ **BOILING kills all pathogens (a rolling boil, longer at altitude) and removes NO
> chemical contamination.** ⚠️ **FILTERS remove protozoa and bacteria by pore size but
> generally NOT viruses.** ⚠️ **CHEMICAL treatment (chlorine dioxide, iodine) handles
> viruses and bacteria well and ⚠️ requires long contact times against *Cryptosporidium*,
> which is notably chlorine-resistant.** **⚠️ UV requires clear water.**
> **⚠️ Solar still and transpiration bag yields are famously poor relative to the effort
> and sweat expended — treat them as a last resort, not a plan.**

**⚠️ What NOT to drink**: ⚠️ **seawater, urine, and alcohol all make dehydration worse.**
**⚠️ In a genuine short-term emergency with clear-running water and no treatment
available, the risk calculus can favour drinking** — ⚠️ **giardiasis has an incubation
period measured in days to weeks, and dehydration acts in hours.** **That is a judgement
call, not a recommendation.**
