## §20. Vehicle and Home

**⚠️ Vehicle**: ⚠️ **in a breakdown or being stranded, STAY WITH THE VEHICLE in almost all
cases — it's shelter, it's visible, and it's what searchers look for.**
**⚠️ Winter vehicle kit**: ⚠️ **blankets or sleeping bags, extra clothing, food and water,
shovel, traction aid, ⚠️ and a candle or small heat source — and if running the engine for
heat, ⚠️ CLEAR THE EXHAUST PIPE OF SNOW, because carbon monoxide entering the cabin is a
recurring cause of death in stranded vehicles.**
**⚠️ Home**: ⚠️ **water, non-perishable food, medications, first aid, light, radio, cash,
documents copied, ⚠️ working smoke and CO alarms (⚠️ the highest-value safety device in
any home by a wide margin), and a fire extinguisher you know how to use.**
