## §21. Practising

**⚠️ Reading this document does not confer any capability.** ⚠️ **The skills that matter
degrade without practice and fail differently under stress.**
**⚠️ What to practise, in priority order**: ⚠️ **wilderness first aid (take a certified
course); navigation with map and compass in real terrain; ⚠️ fire in bad conditions, not
good ones; shelter building against a clock; and ⚠️ using your own kit before you need it.**
**⚠️ Practise TIRED, COLD and IN THE DARK**, ⚠️ **because that is the condition under which
you'll need it, and everything is dramatically harder then.**
**⚠️ Formal training is worth far more than gear**: **wilderness first aid or WFR,
avalanche courses for winter terrain, swiftwater for river travel, and navigation
courses.**
