## §18. Preventive Planning

```
⚠️ BEFORE EVERY TRIP
   ⚠️ Leave an itinerary with a specific overdue time and
      instructions to call SAR (§9). ⚠️ THE highest-value action
   ⚠️ Check weather AND the forecast trend, not a snapshot
   ⚠️ Check conditions (snowpack, river levels, closures, fire)
   ⚠️ Set a TURNAROUND TIME and commit to it out loud
   ⚠️ Match the objective to the WEAKEST member's capability
   ⚠️ Know the bail-out options and where they are
⚠️ THE TEN ESSENTIALS (systems, not items)  navigation ·
   sun protection · ⚠️ INSULATION · illumination · first aid ·
   fire · repair kit and tools · nutrition · hydration ·
   ⚠️ EMERGENCY SHELTER
```
**⚠️ The day-hiker problem is the central finding of §23.1 → `survival-reference`**: ⚠️ **day hikers dominate SAR
statistics precisely because a short outing feels like it doesn't warrant preparation.**
**⚠️ The correction is small: a headtorch, an insulating layer, an emergency bivvy or large
bag, water, and a whistle — on every walk, including the short ones.**
