## §22. ⚠️ Myths That Kill

```
⚠️ "Suck out snake venom" / cut and suck / suction devices —
   ⚠️ NO benefit, causes tissue damage (§23.2)
⚠️ "Apply a tourniquet for snakebite" — ⚠️ concentrates venom and
   worsens local tissue destruction (§23.2)
⚠️ "Rub frostbite to warm it" — ⚠️ causes mechanical tissue damage
⚠️ "Rewarm frostbite immediately" — ⚠️ NOT if refreezing is possible
⚠️ "Give alcohol to warm someone up" — ⚠️ vasodilation increases
   core heat loss
⚠️ "Rub snow on" anything — ⚠️ no
⚠️ "Eat snow for water" — ⚠️ costs body heat. Melt it (§5)
⚠️ "Moss grows on the north side" — ⚠️ unreliable
⚠️ "Follow a river and you'll find help" — ⚠️ true in some terrain,
   fatal in canyons and slot country (§8)
⚠️ "The universal edibility test" — ⚠️ does not detect delayed
   toxins (§7)
⚠️ "Play dead for all bears" — ⚠️ species and behaviour dependent
⚠️ "Drink your urine" — ⚠️ worsens dehydration
⚠️ "You must find food quickly" — ⚠️ almost never true (§7)
⚠️ "Keep moving to stay warm when lost" — ⚠️ expands the search
   area, exhausts you and produces sweat (§2, §9)
⚠️ "Ration water strictly" — ⚠️ ration sweat instead (§14)
⚠️ "A big knife is the essential survival tool" — ⚠️ insulation and
   a whistle outrank it comfortably (§19)
```
