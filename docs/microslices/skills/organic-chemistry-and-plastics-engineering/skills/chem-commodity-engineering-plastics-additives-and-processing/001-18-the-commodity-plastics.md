## §18. The Commodity Plastics

```
⚠️ PE  ⚠️ HDPE (linear, crystalline, stiff — bottles, pipe) ·
   LDPE (branched, soft, film) · LLDPE · UHMWPE (⚠️ extraordinary
   abrasion resistance and impact, hard to process)
⚠️ PP  ⚠️ isotactic (§13). Higher Tm than PE, ⚠️ excellent LIVING
   HINGE behaviour, ⚠️ but Tg near or above 0 °C so it embrittles
   in cold — a very common in-service failure
⚠️ PVC  ⚠️ rigid (pipe, window frames) or flexible depending
   ENTIRELY on plasticizer content (§20). ⚠️ Contains chlorine,
   which complicates incineration and recycling
⚠️ PS  ⚠️ cheap, clear, BRITTLE (Tg ~100 °C, so glassy at room
   temperature). ⚠️ HIPS and ABS are rubber-toughened versions
⚠️ PET  ⚠️ bottles and fibre. ⚠️ Crystallizes controllably (§16),
   ⚠️ and HYDROLYSES if processed wet — drying before moulding is
   mandatory, not optional
⚠️ PU  polyurethanes — foams, coatings, elastomers. Enormously
   versatile family
```
**⚠️ These six or so families dominate volume overwhelmingly** — ⚠️ **the exotic materials
in §19 are a small fraction of tonnage and a large fraction of the literature.**
