## §2. The Elements

```
LINE      ⚠️ direction, weight, character. Contour vs gesture. Line is a
          CONVENTION — there are no outlines in nature, only edges
SHAPE     2D. ⚠️ Positive and NEGATIVE — the shapes between things are shapes
FORM      3D, or its illusion
VALUE     ⚠️ lightness/darkness. THE most important element (§3)
COLOUR    hue, saturation, value (§4)
TEXTURE   actual vs implied
SPACE     ⚠️ depth cues, and the picture plane as a flat thing (§6)
EDGE      ⚠️ hard, soft, lost, found — the most under-taught element of all
```
**⚠️ Edges deserve their own mention because they're where amateur and expert work most
visibly diverge.** **Uniformly hard edges make everything equally insistent and the image
reads as cut-out.** ⚠️ **Varying edge quality — hard at the focal point, softening and
losing edges elsewhere — is how a painter directs attention and creates air**, and it
tracks how vision actually works: **you have sharp acuity only at the fovea.**
