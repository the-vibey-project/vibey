## §7. Medium and Materiality

**⚠️ Medium is not neutral delivery — it constrains and suggests.**
**Fresco** (⚠️ **must be painted into wet plaster, so it demands decisiveness and
sectioning**), **egg tempera** (⚠️ **fast-drying, hatched, luminous, hard to blend**),
**oil** (⚠️ **slow-drying — which is what made blending, glazing, and extended revision
possible, and it changed what painting could depict**), **watercolour** (⚠️ **transparent,
unforgiving, the white is the paper**), **acrylic**, **printmaking** (⚠️ **relief,
intaglio, lithography, screen — and the MULTIPLE is a conceptual fact as much as a
technical one**), **photography**, **sculpture** (**carving is subtractive and
irreversible; modelling is additive and provisional**), **and time-based, installation,
performance and digital media.**
**⚠️ Materiality carries meaning**: **gold ground signals the eternal and non-spatial;
marble signals permanence and cost; the choice to work in cardboard is a statement about
both.**
