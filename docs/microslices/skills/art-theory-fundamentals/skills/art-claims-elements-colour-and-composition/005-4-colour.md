## §4. Colour

**Dimensions**: **hue, saturation/chroma, value/lightness.** **Systems: Munsell (⚠️
perceptually organized and still the most useful for painters), HSL/HSV, CIELAB
(perceptually uniform-ish), RGB/CMYK (device).**

> **⚠️ GOTCHA — red-yellow-blue primaries are a pedagogical fiction, and this is taught in
> schools worldwide.** ⚠️ **RYB cannot mix a large part of the visible gamut — try making
> a clean magenta or a vivid cyan from it.** **The actual situation:**
> - **⚠️ ADDITIVE (light): RGB primaries. This is about the three cone types in your eye.**
> - **⚠️ SUBTRACTIVE (pigment/ink): cyan, magenta, yellow. This is why your printer uses
>   CMYK and not RYB.**
> - **⚠️ Paint mixing is neither, exactly** — **real pigments have complex spectral
>   reflectance, scatter, and are not ideal subtractive filters, which is why mixing
>   behaviour is partly empirical and pigment-specific.**
>
> **⚠️ RYB survives because it produces acceptable results for a limited palette and it's
> historically entrenched, not because it's correct.**

**⚠️ "Colour theory" as commonly taught is a mixture of real perceptual science and
inherited convention, and it's worth knowing which is which:**
```
⚠️ REAL PERCEPTUAL EFFECTS
  Simultaneous contrast — ⚠️ a colour's appearance depends on its surround.
    Robust, dramatic, and the core of Albers' Interaction of Color
  Afterimages and opponent process — red/green, blue/yellow, black/white
  ⚠️ Colour constancy — you perceive surface colour, not the light hitting your
    eye. This is why "the dress" was possible
  Warm advances / cool recedes — ⚠️ a real tendency, partly optical, easily
    overridden by value and context
  Saturation and value interact — ⚠️ you cannot have a very light, very
    saturated blue in pigment. The pigments don't exist

⚠️ CONVENTION DRESSED AS LAW
  "Complementary schemes are harmonious" — ⚠️ a useful default, not a fact
  The 60-30-10 rule and similar formulas — heuristics
  ⚠️ Fixed colour symbolism — HEAVILY culture-dependent (§24). White is
    mourning in parts of East Asia. Blue-for-boys is recent and was
    once reversed
```
**⚠️ The practically decisive point**: **most colour problems are actually VALUE problems
or SATURATION problems.** ⚠️ **Beginners reach for a different hue when what's wrong is
that everything is at the same saturation and mid-value.** **Controlling the saturation
range — keeping most of the picture relatively neutral so a small saturated area can sing
— is the higher-leverage skill.**
