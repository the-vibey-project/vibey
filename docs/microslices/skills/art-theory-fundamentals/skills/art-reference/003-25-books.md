## §25. Books

**⚠️ Start here — looking, not theory:**
| Author | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Berger** | ***Ways of Seeing*** | ⚠️ **Short, political, transformative. The best entry point** |
| **Gombrich** | ***The Story of Art*** | ⚠️ **The standard narrative survey — read knowing §16 → `art-history-modernism-and-the-canon`** |
| **Gombrich** | *Art and Illusion* | ⚠️ **Better book: perception, convention and schema** |

**Formal and craft**
| **Albers** | ***Interaction of Color*** | ⚠️ **§4 → `art-claims-elements-colour-and-composition`'s real perceptual effects, demonstrated** |
| **Arnheim** | *Art and Visual Perception* | Gestalt applied to composition (§5 → `art-claims-elements-colour-and-composition`) |
| **Loomis** | *Creative Illustration* | ⚠️ **Old, practical, excellent on value and composition** |
| **Baxandall** | ***Painting and Experience in 15th-C Italy*** | ⚠️ **The "period eye." A masterpiece of method** (§19 → `art-interpretation-criticism-and-the-art-world`) |

**Aesthetics**
| **Hume** | *Of the Standard of Taste* | ⚠️ **§10 → `art-aesthetics-and-philosophy-of-art`. Twenty pages; still the best treatment** |
| **Kant** | *Critique of Judgment* | ⚠️ **Foundational and hard. Read a guide alongside** |
| **Danto** | *The Transfiguration of the Commonplace* | §11 → `art-aesthetics-and-philosophy-of-art`'s indiscernibles argument |
| **Carroll** | *Philosophy of Art* | ⚠️ **The best contemporary survey of §11–§13 → `art-aesthetics-and-philosophy-of-art`** |
| **Scarry** | *On Beauty and Being Just* | The rehabilitation case (§9 → `art-aesthetics-and-philosophy-of-art`) |

**Interpretation, history and institutions**
| **Panofsky** | *Studies in Iconology* | §17 → `art-interpretation-criticism-and-the-art-world` |
| **Nochlin** | ***Why Have There Been No Great Women Artists?*** | ⚠️ **§16 → `art-history-modernism-and-the-canon`. Essential** |
| **Said** | *Orientalism* | §19 → `art-interpretation-criticism-and-the-art-world` |
| **Bourdieu** | *Distinction* | §10 → `art-aesthetics-and-philosophy-of-art`'s sociological challenge |
| **Thompson** | *The $12 Million Stuffed Shark* | ⚠️ **§22 → `art-interpretation-criticism-and-the-art-world`, readable and disillusioning** |

**Non-Western aesthetics** — ⚠️ **go to primary traditions rather than surveys where you
can**: **Xie He's Six Principles**, **the *Natyashastra* on *rasa***, **and Tanizaki's
*In Praise of Shadows*** (⚠️ **a short, beautiful statement of a genuinely different
aesthetic**).
