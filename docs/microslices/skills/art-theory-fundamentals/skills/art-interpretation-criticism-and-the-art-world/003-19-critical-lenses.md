## §19. Critical Lenses

```
FORMALIST      ⚠️ what's in the work. Blind to context and honest about it
BIOGRAPHICAL   the maker's life. ⚠️ Prone to §20's fallacy
SOCIAL HISTORY OF ART   ⚠️ T.J. Clark et al. — patronage, class, the market,
   material conditions of production
MARXIST        ideology, commodity, labour, whose interests the work serves
FEMINIST       ⚠️ representation, the gaze (Mulvey), authorship, institutions (§16)
PSYCHOANALYTIC ⚠️ Freud/Lacan — desire, the unconscious, the gaze. Explanatorily
   rich and empirically unfalsifiable; use knowingly
POSTCOLONIAL   ⚠️ Said's Orientalism; representation of the other; museums as
   colonial institutions (§22)
RECEPTION      ⚠️ Jauss, Iser — meaning is completed by the viewer; the
   "period eye" (Baxandall) — viewers of 1450 brought different competences
MATERIAL/TECHNICAL   ⚠️ pigment analysis, X-ray, dendrochronology — the closest
   thing to hard evidence in the field, and it settles real disputes
```
**⚠️ The mature use of these is as a set of questions, not a set of verdicts.** **Each
illuminates some works well and others badly.** ⚠️ **The characteristic failure is
mono-lens reading — where the conclusion is determined before the work is examined, and
the work becomes an occasion rather than a subject.** **This is a criticism made vigorously
from inside the discipline, not only from outside it.**
