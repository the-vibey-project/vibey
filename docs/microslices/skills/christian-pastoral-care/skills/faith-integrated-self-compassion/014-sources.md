## Sources

**Primary source.** A ten-page synthesis of evangelical theology of grace, spiritual-formation
writing, clinical research on self-compassion and religious coping, and writing by autistic
Christians. ⚠️ **All personal and case material in the source was excluded**: this reference is about
no individual, and its pacing outline (§11) replaces the source's individual programme with a generic,
optional one. Authors are cited as the source cites them; quotations are limited to a few short
phrases, and everything else is paraphrase.

**Theology and spiritual formation:** Baptist Faith and Message 2000 (Articles III–V); Tim Keller,
including the *Gospel in Life* study; Jerry Bridges (crediting Jack Miller); John Piper; Paul David
Tripp, *New Morning Mercies*; Wayne Grudem, *Systematic Theology*; Ed Welch and CCEF; Scotty Smith,
*Everyday Prayers*; Dallas Willard; Donald Whitney; Mark Vroegop; Elizabeth Lewis Hall (Biola
University); Martin Luther on Sabbath and grace; Robert McGee, *The Search for Significance*; Neil
Anderson, *Victory Over the Darkness* and *Steps to Freedom in Christ*; Curt Thompson, *The Soul of
Shame* and *Anatomy of the Soul*; Diane Langberg; Judith Herman's phase model of trauma recovery.

**Neurodivergent Christian writing:** Lamar Hardwick; Daniel Bowman Jr., *On the Spectrum: Autism,
Faith, and the Gifts of Neurodiversity*; Grant Macaskill and the Centre for Autism and Theology,
University of Aberdeen; Laura Hurley (The Gospel Coalition Australia); Rebekah Drumsta, "Masking for
Jesus"; a writer for the Evangelical Alliance UK; a Liberty University thesis; Southeastern Baptist
Theological Seminary, "Autism and the Church"; Dawn Swayne; Stephen Grcevich (Key Ministry);
*Christianity Today* (May 2024) on ADHD and spiritual practice.

**Clinical research, as the source cites it:** Winders et al. (2020); Luo et al. (2021); Karatzias et
al. (2019); Huang et al. (2025); the 2023 multilevel meta-analysis of 23 RCTs of R/S-adapted
treatment; Koenig (2012); Knabb (2019 workbook; 2020 RCT); Jones, Garzon & Ford (2021); Kashdan et al.
(2006); Israel-Cohen et al. (2015); Wong et al. (2018); Pargament (Brief RCOPE); Worthington (REACH);
the 2024 RCT of trauma therapy and guilt and shame; Raymaker et al. (2020).

**Wider literature added to state evidence strength honestly, marked where used:** Neff's
self-compassion model; MacBeth & Gumley (2012) and Ferrari et al. (2019) on self-compassion; Captari
et al. (2018) on religiously adapted psychotherapy; Ano & Vasconcelles (2005) on religious coping;
Cregg & Cheavens (2021) on gratitude interventions; Wade et al. (2014) on forgiveness interventions;
Tangney's research on shame and guilt; de Jongh et al. (2016) on phase-based trauma treatment; major
clinical guidelines on trauma-focused therapy.
