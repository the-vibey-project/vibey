### 29.2 What's actually well-supported
- [ ] ⚠️ **Correcting genuine deficiencies works, dramatically** (§1 → `nutrition-epistemics-study-design-and-measurement`, §10 → `nutrition-macronutrients-vitamins-minerals-and-supplements`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Fibre intake is low and raising it is well-supported** (§5 → `nutrition-macronutrients-vitamins-minerals-and-supplements`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Industrial trans fat is harmful; its removal was a real win** (§6 → `nutrition-macronutrients-vitamins-minerals-and-supplements`)
- [ ] Dietary patterns beat single-nutrient targeting (§19 → `nutrition-dietary-patterns-weight-regulation-and-life-stages`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Sugar-sweetened beverages are the clearest single adverse item** (§5 → `nutrition-macronutrients-vitamins-minerals-and-supplements`, §24.1 → `nutrition-live-evidence-and-reading-a-claim`)
- [ ] Adherence dominates diet composition (§20 → `nutrition-dietary-patterns-weight-regulation-and-life-stages`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Structural interventions outperform education** (§17 → `nutrition-digestion-energy-balance-appetite-and-food-processing`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Almost everything else is weaker than it sounds**
