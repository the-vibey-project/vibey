## §30. Method

**§1–§23 → `nutrition-epistemics-study-design-and-measurement`, `nutrition-macronutrients-vitamins-minerals-and-supplements`, `nutrition-digestion-energy-balance-appetite-and-food-processing`, `nutrition-dietary-patterns-weight-regulation-and-life-stages` rests on nutritional biochemistry, physiology and the methodological literature on
nutrition epidemiology** — **energy metabolism, nutrient functions and deficiency
syndromes, appetite regulation, and the standard critical apparatus for reading dietary
studies.** ⚠️ **The biochemistry is settled and needed no verification; the deficiency
diseases and their cures remain the field's clearest achievements.**

**Two searches were run in August 2026**, on **ultra-processed foods** and **the
2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines** — ⚠️ **both chosen because they are live, contested, and
consequential, and because both are frequently reported as settled when they are not.**

**Confidence.** **High** in §1–§3 → `nutrition-epistemics-study-design-and-measurement`, which are the load-bearing sections and the ones I'd
most want read. ⚠️ **Healthy-user bias, the substitution problem, and the systematic
non-random error in self-reported intake together explain most of why nutrition findings
reverse — and the vitamin E and beta-carotene cases give the base rate you should carry
into any new observational claim.**

**High** in §12–§13 → `nutrition-digestion-energy-balance-appetite-and-food-processing`'s framing. ⚠️ **Both common positions on energy balance are wrong in
opposite directions, and the useful synthesis is that thermodynamics describes the
accounting while regulation explains the behaviour.**

⚠️ **§24.1 → `nutrition-live-evidence-and-reading-a-claim` is written as a genuine dispute because it is one.** ⚠️ **I've reported the
strongest version of each side, including that *Nature Food* published opposing
Perspectives side by side, and I've deliberately included the point — made by a
critic-of-the-critics — that methodology must be evaluated independently of funding
source.** ⚠️ **My own reading is stated as a reading, and it is that the association is
real while NOVA is too coarse to be the causal variable, with energy density and eating
rate the better-specified candidate.** **⚠️ Note one sourcing caveat: the Harvard
NUTRITION 2026 analysis is reported from conference presentation via secondary coverage,
not from a peer-reviewed publication I verified.**

⚠️ **§24.2 → `nutrition-live-evidence-and-reading-a-claim` is deliberately reported rather than adjudicated, because it is contested
policy with a political dimension.** **I've given what changed, and the objections from
several directions — Harvard's nutrition department, CSPI, the American College of
Cardiology, a low-carb-aligned commentator, and an alcohol industry coalition — because
the pattern of who objects to what is more informative than any single verdict.**
⚠️ **The observation that opposing camps are pointing at the same saturated-fat
inconsistency and cheering different halves is mine.** **⚠️ Sourcing note: several of these
sources are advocacy organizations on one side or another, which I've identified inline
rather than laundering into neutral-sounding citations.**

⚠️ **§22 → `nutrition-dietary-patterns-weight-regulation-and-life-stages` is placed before the diet content deliberately and is not an afterthought.**
**Nutrition references that treat disordered eating as a footnote get the ordering exactly
backwards.**
