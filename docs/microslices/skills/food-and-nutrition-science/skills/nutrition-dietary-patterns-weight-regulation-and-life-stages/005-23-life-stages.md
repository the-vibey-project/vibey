## §23. Life Stages

**⚠️ Pregnancy**: ⚠️ **folate before and during early pregnancy (neural tube defects close
before many people know they're pregnant, which is why fortification works and
supplementation-on-discovery is late); iodine; iron; ⚠️ and specific avoidances for
listeria, mercury and alcohol.**
**⚠️ Infancy**: ⚠️ **breastfeeding is recommended where feasible and formula is a safe
alternative — the framing matters, because guilt-based messaging has documented harms.**
**⚠️ Early allergen introduction** (§18 → `nutrition-digestion-energy-balance-appetite-and-food-processing`). **⚠️ Vitamin K at birth; vitamin D in infancy.**
**⚠️ Childhood**: ⚠️ **growth trajectory over single measurements; ⚠️ neophobia is
developmentally normal and repeated neutral exposure works better than pressure; and
⚠️ pressuring children to eat reliably backfires.**
**⚠️ Older adults**: ⚠️ **anabolic resistance means protein requirements are plausibly
HIGHER, not lower; appetite declines; ⚠️ B12 absorption falls with age; and undernutrition
is a more common clinical problem than overnutrition in frail elderly people.**
