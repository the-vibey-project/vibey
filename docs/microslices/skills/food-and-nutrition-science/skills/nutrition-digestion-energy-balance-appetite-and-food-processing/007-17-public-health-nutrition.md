## §17. Public Health Nutrition

**⚠️ The interventions with the strongest track records are structural, not educational:**
⚠️ **salt iodization, folate fortification, vitamin D in milk, vitamin A supplementation in
deficient populations, and industrial trans fat elimination.**
**⚠️ Contested policy levers**: ⚠️ **sugar-sweetened beverage taxes (⚠️ evidence of reduced
purchasing is reasonably consistent; health outcome evidence is thinner), front-of-pack
labelling, marketing restrictions to children, and reformulation targets.**
**⚠️ Food security and access**: ⚠️ **nutrition outcomes track socioeconomic position
strongly, and advice that ignores cost, time, cooking facilities and food availability
fails predictably** — **which is a recurring criticism of individual-responsibility framing.**
