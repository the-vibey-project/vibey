## §12. ⚠️ Energy Balance

```
⚠️ TDEE = BMR (~60-70%) + TEF (~10%) + activity + NEAT
⚠️ THERMODYNAMICS HOLDS. Energy balance is not in dispute
⚠️ BUT "calories in, calories out" is a poor MODEL of a regulated
   system, because BOTH SIDES ARE DEPENDENT VARIABLES
```
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — the two common errors are opposite and both wrong.** ⚠️ **Error one: "it's
> just calories, so eat less" — this ignores that intake is under strong physiological
> regulation (§13) and that expenditure ADAPTS DOWNWARD during energy restriction
> (adaptive thermogenesis, reduced NEAT).** ⚠️ **Error two: "calories don't matter, only
> hormones" — this violates thermodynamics.**
> **⚠️ The correct synthesis: energy balance describes the accounting; it does not explain
> the BEHAVIOUR or the REGULATION, and interventions work by changing those.**

**⚠️ Measurement caveats**: ⚠️ **food labels carry permitted error margins, Atwater factors
are approximations, activity trackers estimate expenditure poorly, and ⚠️ the doubly
labelled water literature shows self-reported intake is systematically low** (§3 → `nutrition-epistemics-study-design-and-measurement`).
