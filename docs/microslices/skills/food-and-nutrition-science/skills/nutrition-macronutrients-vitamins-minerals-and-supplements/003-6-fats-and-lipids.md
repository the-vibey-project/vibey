## §6. Fats and Lipids

```
SATURATED (SFA) · MONOUNSATURATED (MUFA) · POLYUNSATURATED (PUFA)
⚠️ TRANS  ⚠️ industrial trans fats are the clearest dietary harm ever
   established, and their near-elimination is a real public health win
⚠️ OMEGA-3  ALA (plant) → EPA/DHA conversion is POOR in humans;
   marine sources supply EPA/DHA directly
OMEGA-6  ⚠️ linoleic acid. ⚠️ The "omega-6 causes inflammation" claim
   is much weaker than commonly stated — trials generally do not
   show the predicted inflammatory effect
⚠️ CHOLESTEROL  ⚠️ DIETARY cholesterol has modest effects on SERUM
   cholesterol for most people. ⚠️ This was the basis for removing
   the specific dietary cholesterol limit from guidelines
LIPOPROTEINS  ⚠️ LDL-C, HDL-C, ⚠️ ApoB and Lp(a) as better markers
```
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — saturated fat is the genuinely contested case, and both camps overstate.**
> ⚠️ **The evidence that saturated fat raises LDL-C is strong.** **⚠️ The evidence on hard
> outcomes depends entirely on the REPLACEMENT (§1 → `nutrition-epistemics-study-design-and-measurement`): replacing SFA with PUFA appears
> beneficial; with refined carbohydrate, not.** **⚠️ Meta-analyses disagree, partly by which
> substitutions they model.**
> **⚠️ Meanwhile "saturated fat was never a problem" overstates the revisionist case,
> which is why §24.2 → `nutrition-live-evidence-and-reading-a-claim`'s guidelines retained the 10% limit while simultaneously promoting
> foods high in it — a contradiction critics have flagged.**
