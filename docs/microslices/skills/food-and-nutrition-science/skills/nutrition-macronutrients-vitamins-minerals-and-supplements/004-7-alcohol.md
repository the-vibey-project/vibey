## §7. Alcohol

**⚠️ Ethanol provides ~7 kcal/g; metabolized via alcohol dehydrogenase to acetaldehyde
(⚠️ a carcinogen and the cause of the flushing response in people with ALDH2 variants).**
**⚠️ The J-curve is the important reversal**: ⚠️ **older observational studies suggested
moderate drinkers had lower mortality than abstainers.** **⚠️ Subsequent analyses found
the abstainer group contaminated by "sick quitters" — people who stopped drinking because
of illness — and Mendelian randomization studies generally do not support a protective
effect.** ⚠️ **The current scientific direction is that there is no clearly established
level of alcohol that improves health, and cancer risk rises from low levels.**
**⚠️ Note §24.2 → `nutrition-live-evidence-and-reading-a-claim`'s guidelines weakened the specific numeric limits, which is contested.**
