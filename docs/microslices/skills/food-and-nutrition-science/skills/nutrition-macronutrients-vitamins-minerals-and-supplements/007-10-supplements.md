## §10. ⚠️ Supplements

> **⚠️ The general finding across decades: supplements correct DEFICIENCIES well and
> produce disappointing results in replete populations.**
```
⚠️ GOOD EVIDENCE  folate in pregnancy · B12 where intake is absent ·
   vitamin D where deficient · iron where deficient · iodine ·
   ⚠️ specific clinical situations (bariatric surgery, malabsorption)
⚠️ MIXED / WEAK  omega-3 for cardiovascular prevention · multivitamins
   in well-nourished people · most single antioxidants
⚠️ EVIDENCE OF HARM  ⚠️ beta-carotene in smokers · high-dose vitamin E ·
   ⚠️ antioxidants around training may blunt adaptation
```
**⚠️ The regulatory reality in the US and many jurisdictions**: ⚠️ **supplements are not
approved for safety or efficacy before sale, and independent testing repeatedly finds
products that do not contain the stated amount, or that contain undeclared
pharmaceuticals.** **⚠️ Third-party certification is the practical mitigation.**
**⚠️ Interactions are real** — ⚠️ **St John's wort induces drug metabolism, vitamin K
interacts with warfarin, and high-dose supplements can interfere with medication.**
