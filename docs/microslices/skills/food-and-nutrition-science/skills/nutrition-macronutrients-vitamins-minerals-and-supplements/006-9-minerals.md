## §9. Minerals

**⚠️ Macrominerals (calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chloride) and trace
(iron, zinc, iodine, selenium, copper, manganese, chromium, molybdenum, fluoride).**
```
⚠️ IRON  heme (animal, well absorbed) vs non-heme (plant, poorly
   absorbed; ⚠️ vitamin C enhances, phytates and tannins inhibit).
   ⚠️ Deficiency is the most common nutrient deficiency globally;
   ⚠️ excess is harmful and iron is not a supplement to take casually
⚠️ SODIUM/POTASSIUM  ⚠️ the RATIO matters, and most populations are
   high sodium and low potassium. ⚠️ Sodium reduction lowers blood
   pressure; the magnitude of mortality benefit is contested
⚠️ CALCIUM  ⚠️ supplementation shows weaker fracture benefit than long
   assumed, with some cardiovascular signals debated
⚠️ IODINE  ⚠️ deficiency remains the leading preventable cause of
   intellectual disability worldwide; salt iodization is a triumph
⚠️ ZINC and SELENIUM  narrow windows between deficiency and toxicity
```
