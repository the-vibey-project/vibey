## §5. Carbohydrates and Fibre

```
SIMPLE  monosaccharides (glucose, fructose, galactose), disaccharides
COMPLEX  starch (amylose, amylopectin), ⚠️ RESISTANT STARCH (behaves
   like fibre; ⚠️ cooking and COOLING starchy foods increases it)
⚠️ FIBRE  soluble (viscous — cholesterol and glycaemic effects) vs
   insoluble (bulk and transit). ⚠️ FERMENTABLE fibre feeds the
   microbiome and produces short-chain fatty acids (§14)
⚠️ GLYCAEMIC INDEX  ⚠️ a property measured in isolation, on a fixed
   carbohydrate dose, with wide between-person variability.
   ⚠️ GI of a single food poorly predicts the response to a MEAL,
   which is why GI-based advice has underperformed
```
**⚠️ Fructose**: ⚠️ **metabolized largely in the liver; the concern is dose and context.**
**⚠️ Fruit is not the problem — the fibre, water and volume make overconsumption difficult;
sugar-sweetened beverages deliver the same sugar with no satiety** (§13 → `nutrition-digestion-energy-balance-appetite-and-food-processing`).
**⚠️ Fibre is one of the better-supported nutrients** — ⚠️ **and intake is well below
recommended levels in most Western populations.**
