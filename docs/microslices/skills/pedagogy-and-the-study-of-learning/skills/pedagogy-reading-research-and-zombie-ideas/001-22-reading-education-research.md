## §22. ⚠️ Reading Education Research

> **⚠️ The skill that protects you from most of §23.**
```
⚠️ EFFECT SIZES  ⚠️ Cohen's benchmarks (0.2/0.5/0.8) were never meant
   for education. ⚠️ In education, ~0.1 is meaningful, 0.4 is large
   for a well-run trial, ⚠️ and anything above 1.0 in a real school
   trial should provoke suspicion, not excitement
⚠️ WHAT INFLATES EFFECT SIZES
   ⚠️ Researcher-made outcome measures aligned to the intervention
   ⚠️ Short duration and immediate post-tests
   ⚠️ Small samples · ⚠️ narrow-range populations
   ⚠️ Developer-delivered implementation
   ⚠️ Publication bias
⚠️ THE HATTIE CAUTION  ⚠️ Visible Learning's league table of effect
   sizes is enormously influential and has been substantively
   criticized — for averaging incommensurable meta-analyses
   ("meta-meta-analysis"), for statistical errors identified by
   several reviewers, and for the CLE metric. ⚠️ Use it to generate
   hypotheses, NOT to rank interventions
⚠️ QUESTIONS TO ASK
   Compared to WHAT? · Measured HOW? · For WHOM? · For HOW LONG? ·
   ⚠️ WHO delivered it? · ⚠️ WHO funded it? · ⚠️ Has an INDEPENDENT
   team replicated it at scale?
```
**⚠️ Better sources than a single study**: ⚠️ **the EEF Toolkit, the What Works
Clearinghouse, Campbell and Cochrane reviews** — **and note that even these disagree,
partly because they apply different inclusion standards.**
