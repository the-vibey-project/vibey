## §24. Teacher Expertise

**⚠️ Teacher quality varies substantially and matters** — ⚠️ **but observable credentials
predict it poorly, which is why the policy problem is hard.**
**⚠️ PCK (pedagogical content knowledge)**: ⚠️ **knowing the content is necessary and not
sufficient; the distinctive expertise is knowing how learners typically misunderstand THIS
topic and what representations work** (§10 → `pedagogy-explicit-instruction-worked-examples-and-feedback`).
**⚠️ Development**: ⚠️ **one-off professional development has poor evidence; sustained,
content-specific, coached programmes with follow-up do better.** **⚠️ Instructional
coaching has among the better evidence bases in teacher development, and its constraint —
like tutoring (§18 → `pedagogy-subject-instruction-curriculum-and-classroom-practice`) — is cost.**
**⚠️ The expert blind spot** (§10 → `pedagogy-explicit-instruction-worked-examples-and-feedback`) ⚠️ **means experienced teachers can lose access to what
novices find hard**, **which is why student misconception data is so valuable.**
