## §23. ⚠️ Zombie Ideas

```
⚠️ LEARNING STYLES  ⚠️ The meshing hypothesis — that matching
   instruction to a preferred style improves learning — has been
   tested repeatedly and FAILS. ⚠️ People have preferences; matching
   them does not help. ⚠️ Still believed by large majorities of
   teachers in survey after survey
⚠️ THE LEARNING PYRAMID / "cone of experience" percentages
   ⚠️ Fabricated. The numbers have no traceable empirical source
⚠️ "WE ONLY USE 10% OF OUR BRAINS"  ⚠️ False
⚠️ LEFT-BRAIN / RIGHT-BRAIN learners  ⚠️ Not how lateralization works
⚠️ MULTIPLE INTELLIGENCES  ⚠️ Gardner's theory is not an empirically
   validated model of ability, and ⚠️ Gardner himself has objected to
   its conflation with learning styles
⚠️ BRAIN GYM  ⚠️ No credible basis
⚠️ DIGITAL NATIVES  ⚠️ Young people are not innately skilled with
   technology; the research does not support a generational cognitive shift
⚠️ "TEACH SKILLS, NOT CONTENT" / content-free critical thinking
   ⚠️ Contradicted by §5 — critical thinking is largely domain-specific
⚠️ 70-20-10  ⚠️ No empirical origin (§20)
⚠️ "GOOGLE IT — memorization is obsolete"  ⚠️ Contradicted by §2 and
   §5: you cannot think with information you must look up
```
**⚠️ The diagnostic that catches most of them**: ⚠️ **does the claim have a citable primary
study, or only a diagram and a percentage?** **⚠️ And does it flatter the listener?**
