## §19. Higher Education

**⚠️ The lecture is not inherently bad — an unstructured, non-interactive lecture to
novices is.** ⚠️ **Freeman et al.'s meta-analysis found active learning improved exam
performance and cut failure rates substantially in STEM.**
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — students often RATE active learning lower while LEARNING more from it**,
> ⚠️ **because effortful learning feels less fluent than a polished lecture** (§7 → `pedagogy-reliability-memory-cognitive-load-and-robust-effects`).
> **⚠️ Deslauriers et al. demonstrated this directly: measured learning up, perceived
> learning down.** **⚠️ This is a serious problem for institutions that manage teaching by
> student satisfaction scores.**
**⚠️ Also note**: ⚠️ **student evaluations of teaching correlate weakly with learning and
carry documented biases** — **useful for some things, not a measure of teaching quality.**
