## §17. Classroom Practice

**⚠️ Routines and expectations reduce cognitive load for everyone** (§3 → `pedagogy-reliability-memory-cognitive-load-and-robust-effects`) — ⚠️ **behaviour
management is substantially a design problem, not a personality one.**
**⚠️ What the evidence supports**: **clear expectations taught explicitly, consistency,
high ratios of positive to corrective interaction, ⚠️ momentum and minimal transitions
(lost time is the largest silent cost), and ⚠️ addressing the lowest-level disruption early
rather than escalating.**
**⚠️ Relationships matter and are not a substitute for instruction** — ⚠️ **the honest
framing is that a warm classroom enables demanding teaching, not that warmth alone
teaches.**
**⚠️ Homework**: ⚠️ **effects are small and age-dependent — very weak at primary, larger at
secondary — and QUALITY dominates quantity.** **⚠️ Retrieval-practice homework (§4 → `pedagogy-reliability-memory-cognitive-load-and-robust-effects`) is far
more defensible than "finish the worksheet."**
