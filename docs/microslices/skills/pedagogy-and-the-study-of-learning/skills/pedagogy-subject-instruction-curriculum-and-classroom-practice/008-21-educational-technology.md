## §21. Educational Technology

**⚠️ The historical pattern is remarkably consistent: each new technology is predicted to
transform education, and meta-analyses find small average effects with enormous
variance.**
⚠️ **The variance is the finding — implementation, pedagogy and context dominate the
technology itself.**
```
⚠️ WHAT TECH IS GENUINELY GOOD AT
   ⚠️ Spaced retrieval at scale (§4) — algorithmic scheduling is a
      genuine improvement over a human doing it
   ⚠️ Immediate feedback on well-defined tasks
   Simulation of the inaccessible; adaptive practice sequencing
   Access and accessibility
⚠️ WHAT IT DOESN'T FIX
   ⚠️ Weak curriculum, weak explanation, or weak assessment
⚠️ KNOWN NEGATIVES
   ⚠️ Laptops for note-taking underperform handwriting in several
      studies; ⚠️ multitasking is genuinely costly; ⚠️ the presence
      of devices distracts even when unused
```
**⚠️ The evaluation caution**: ⚠️ **edtech efficacy studies are frequently vendor-funded,
short, and measure engagement or usage rather than learning** (§22 → `pedagogy-reading-research-and-zombie-ideas`).
