## §27. Numbers

```
⚠️ Working memory        ⚠️ ~4 chunks for novel material (not 7±2)
⚠️ Wait time             ⚠️ ~3 seconds transforms answer quality
⚠️ Feedback              ⚠️ ~1/3 of interventions REDUCED performance
⚠️ Education effect size ⚠️ ~0.1 meaningful · 0.4 large · ⚠️ >1.0 suspect
```
