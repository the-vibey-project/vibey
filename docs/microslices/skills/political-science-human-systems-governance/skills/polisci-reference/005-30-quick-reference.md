## §30. Quick Reference

### 30.1 Picker
| Question | Where |
|---|---|
| Why doesn't the obvious public good get provided? | ⚠️ **Free riding and Olson's asymmetry** (§5 → `polisci-collective-action-commons-social-choice-and-voting`) |
| Why do narrow interests win so often? | ⚠️ **Concentrated benefits, diffuse costs** (§5 → `polisci-collective-action-commons-social-choice-and-voting`) |
| Must a shared resource be privatized or nationalized? | ⚠️ **No — see Ostrom's third path** (§6 → `polisci-collective-action-commons-social-choice-and-voting`) |
| Which voting system is best? | ⚠️ **Depends what you value. There's no neutral one** (§8 → `polisci-collective-action-commons-social-choice-and-voting`, §10 → `polisci-constitutional-design-electoral-systems-and-institutions`) |
| Why does this country have two parties? | ⚠️ **Probably the electoral rule** (§8 → `polisci-collective-action-commons-social-choice-and-voting`) |
| Why can't this government pass anything? | ⚠️ **Count the veto players** (§9 → `polisci-constitutional-design-electoral-systems-and-institutions`) |
| Should courts overturn legislatures? | ⚠️ **A genuine normative dispute** (§13 → `polisci-constitutional-design-electoral-systems-and-institutions`) |
| Why did the policy fail in implementation? | ⚠️ **Chain of agreements, plus street-level discretion** (§14 → `polisci-bureaucracy-parties-voter-behaviour-and-polarization`, §24 → `polisci-regimes-state-capacity-corruption-ir-and-field-problems`) |
| Are voters rational? | ⚠️ **Wrong frame. They use heuristics under low information** (§16 → `polisci-bureaucracy-parties-voter-behaviour-and-polarization`) |
| Is polarization increasing? | ⚠️ **Affective, fairly clearly. Ideological, less so** (§18 → `polisci-bureaucracy-parties-voter-behaviour-and-polarization`) |
| Why did war happen when both preferred peace? | ⚠️ **Fearon's three mechanisms** (§23 → `polisci-regimes-state-capacity-corruption-ir-and-field-problems`) |
| Is democracy declining? | ⚠️ **Direction supported; magnitude genuinely disputed** (§26) |
| How much should I trust this index? | ⚠️ **Read its methodology and who coded it** (§25 → `polisci-regimes-state-capacity-corruption-ir-and-field-problems`, §26.2) |

### 30.2 Reading a political claim critically
- [ ] ⚠️ **Is this empirical, contested-empirical, or normative?** (§1 → `polisci-what-the-field-establishes-power-the-state-and-theory`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **What is the actual MEASURE, and who produced it?** (§25 → `polisci-regimes-state-capacity-corruption-ir-and-field-problems`, §26)
- [ ] Is the causal claim identified, or a correlation with a story? (§1 → `polisci-what-the-field-establishes-power-the-state-and-theory`, §25 → `polisci-regimes-state-capacity-corruption-ir-and-field-problems`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **What's the n, and are the cases independent?** (§1 → `polisci-what-the-field-establishes-power-the-state-and-theory`)
- [ ] Does it generalize beyond the countries studied? (§25 → `polisci-regimes-state-capacity-corruption-ir-and-field-problems`)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Would the strongest opposing case be stated this way by its holders?** (§4 → `polisci-what-the-field-establishes-power-the-state-and-theory`)
- [ ] Is a normative conclusion being smuggled in as description? (§15 → `polisci-bureaucracy-parties-voter-behaviour-and-polarization`'s populism note)
- [ ] ⚠️ **Are stated error bars covering all the uncertainty?** (§17 → `polisci-bureaucracy-parties-voter-behaviour-and-polarization`)
