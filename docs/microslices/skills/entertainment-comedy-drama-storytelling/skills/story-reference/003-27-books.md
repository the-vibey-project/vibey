## §27. Books

**⚠️ Weight practitioners over gurus** (§1 → `story-rules-character-structure-and-scene`).
| Author | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **McKee** | *Story* | ⚠️ **The standard. Dogmatic; read critically** |
| **Egri** | ***The Art of Dramatic Writing*** | ⚠️ **1946, and still the best on premise and character** |
| **Yorke** | ***Into the Woods*** | ⚠️ **The most intelligent structure book — asks WHY the shapes recur** |
| **Snyder** | *Save the Cat* | ⚠️ **Useful diagnostic; know what it did to studio film** (§4 → `story-rules-character-structure-and-scene`) |
| **Aristotle** | *Poetics* | ⚠️ **The origin, and short** |
| **Goldman** | ***Adventures in the Screen Trade*** | ⚠️ **§24 → `story-acting-directing-medium-and-industry`. "Nobody knows anything"** |
| **Mamet** | *On Directing Film* | ⚠️ **Severe, clarifying, and arguable** |
| **Johnstone** | ***Impro*** | ⚠️ **§15 → `story-comedy-jokes-improv-standup-and-timing`'s status work. Genuinely great, well beyond improv** |
| **Vorhaus** | *The Comic Toolbox* | §13–§14 → `story-comedy-jokes-improv-standup-and-timing` |
| **Sacks** | ***Poking a Dead Frog*** | ⚠️ **Working comedy writers on process** |
| **Murch** | ***In the Blink of an Eye*** | ⚠️ **§23 → `story-acting-directing-medium-and-industry`. Short and the best thing on editing** |
| **Bogart / Mitchell** | *A Director Prepares* / *Directing Actors* | §21 → `story-acting-directing-medium-and-industry` |
| **Le Guin** | *Steering the Craft* | ⚠️ **Prose-side, and excellent on §8 → `story-dialogue-point-of-view-theme-exposition-and-endings`** |
