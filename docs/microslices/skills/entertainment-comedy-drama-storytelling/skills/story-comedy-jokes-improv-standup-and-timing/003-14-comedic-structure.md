## §14. Comedic Structure

```
⚠️ RULE OF THREE   establish pattern, confirm pattern, BREAK pattern.
   ⚠️ Two isn't enough to establish; four is usually one too many
⚠️ CALLBACK        rewards attention and creates the pleasure of an in-group.
   ⚠️ The most reliable structural device in long-form comedy
⚠️ ESCALATION      each beat raises the absurdity while remaining internally
   logical. ⚠️ The logic is what makes escalation work rather than just noise
⚠️ RUNNER          a repeated element across a piece
⚠️ HEIGHTEN AND EXPLORE  (from improv, §16) — find the unusual thing and
   pursue its implications rather than adding new unusual things
⚠️ COMIC PREMISE / "GAME"  ⚠️ the single most important concept in modern
   comedy writing: identify the one unusual pattern, then play IT
   repeatedly with variation, rather than being randomly funny
```
> **⚠️ GOTCHA — "random" is not a comedic technique, and this is the most common failure
> in amateur comedy.** ⚠️ **Comedy needs INTERNAL LOGIC — the situation is absurd, and
> the characters behave consistently within it.** **The funniest scenes are usually people
> taking an insane premise entirely seriously.** **⚠️ Randomness has no pattern to violate,
> so there's nothing to be incongruous WITH** (§12).
