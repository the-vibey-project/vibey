## §8. Point of View and Narration

```
FIRST PERSON      intimacy; ⚠️ limited to what the narrator knows —
                  and enables the UNRELIABLE NARRATOR
THIRD LIMITED     the modern default in prose
THIRD OMNISCIENT  ⚠️ out of fashion, and capable of things nothing else does
SECOND PERSON     rare; ⚠️ standard in interactive fiction and games
⚠️ CAMERA (screen)  external by default. ⚠️ Interiority must be EXTERNALIZED —
                  which is the fundamental adaptation problem (§22)
```
**⚠️ Psychic distance** (Gardner) — **how close the narration sits to the character's
consciousness, and it can slide within a paragraph.** ⚠️ **Controlling it deliberately is
one of the more advanced prose skills.**
**⚠️ Free indirect discourse** — **third person that takes on the character's idiom and
judgements without quotation** — ⚠️ **is the workhorse of literary fiction and is nearly
invisible when done well.**
