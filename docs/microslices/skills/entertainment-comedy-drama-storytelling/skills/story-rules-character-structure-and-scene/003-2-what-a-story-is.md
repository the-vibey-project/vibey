## §2. What a Story Is

**⚠️ The minimum viable definition: somebody wants something, something stands in the way,
and by the end something has irreversibly changed.**
```
⚠️ CAUSALITY   "and then" is a list; "THEREFORE / BUT" is a story.
   ⚠️ The single most useful diagnostic there is — read your outline and
   insert the connective; if "and then" fits everywhere, you have episodes
   rather than a plot
⚠️ CHANGE      the state at the end differs irreversibly from the start
⚠️ MEANING     the change signifies something (§9)
```
**⚠️ Story vs plot** (Forster's distinction): **"the king died and then the queen died" is
story; "the king died and then the queen died OF GRIEF" is plot** — ⚠️ **causality is what
converts one into the other.**
**⚠️ Fabula and syuzhet** — **the events as they happened versus the order in which they're
told.** ⚠️ **Nearly all narrative sophistication lives in the gap between them:
flashback, dramatic irony, unreliable narration, mystery.**
