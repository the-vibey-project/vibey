## The framing for Part III

Soil is not dirt. It is a living, structured, three-phase system, and treating it as an inert
substrate is the root error behind most degradation. Everything in crop farming is a system with
feedback — maximize yield and lose soil, maximize nitrogen and leach it into the water table,
maximize monoculture and invite pest resistance. The soil and its microbial communities are the
engine; your job is managing that engine.
