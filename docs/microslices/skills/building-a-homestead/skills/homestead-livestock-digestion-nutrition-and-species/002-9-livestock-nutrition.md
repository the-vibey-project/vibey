## §9 Livestock Nutrition

| Nutrient | Key Points |
|---|---|
| Energy | Usually the first limiting nutrient on forage diets. Measured as TDN, ME, or NE. Forage quality falls as plants mature — lignin rises, energy and protein fall. Harvest or graze timing determines quality more than species choice. |
| Protein | For ruminants, the rumen-degradable protein (RDP) / rumen-undegradable protein (RUP) split matters more than total crude protein. RDP feeds the rumen microbes; RUP bypasses to the small intestine. |
| Fiber | NDF (neutral detergent fiber) predicts intake — high NDF means lower intake. ADF (acid detergent fiber) predicts digestibility — high ADF means lower digestibility. Effective fiber is needed for rumen health. |
| Minerals | Calcium:phosphorus ratio matters. Salt is essential. Trace minerals (selenium, copper) have strong regional deficiency patterns. Copper is easily toxic, and sheep are far more sensitive than cattle — **never give sheep cattle mineral mix.** |
| Vitamins | A, D, E are the critical ones for livestock. B vitamins are synthesized in the rumen of mature ruminants. |
| Water | The most important and most neglected nutrient. Clean, accessible, adequate quantity. **Animals will not eat if they cannot drink.** |

> **TEST YOUR FORAGE**
>
> Hay from the same field in different years can differ by a wide margin in energy and protein.
> Feeding "hay" as if it were a constant is how animals lose condition through winter. Get a forage
> analysis (**$20-40**) and formulate rations based on actual values, not assumptions.

### Body condition scoring

Body condition scoring is the practical, free management tool.

- Condition at calving or lambing predicts rebreeding success **better than almost anything else you
  can measure**.
- Thin females do not cycle, and the interval from calving to first estrus lengthens sharply with
  poor body condition.
- Score your animals regularly and adjust feeding accordingly.

Nutrition therefore drives reproduction, the largest economic lever in a livestock operation —
§11–§14 → `homestead-livestock-housing-health-and-grazing`. Forage quality is set by grazing and
harvest timing, which is a grazing-management decision in the same sibling skill. Definitions of
**Body condition score (BCS)** is the practical, free monitoring tool that turns all of the above
into a weekly decision: a subjective score of an animal's fat cover, **typically on a 1–5 or a 1–9
scale** depending on the system in use. Condition at calving or lambing predicts rebreeding success,
which makes it a forward indicator of next season's calf or lamb crop rather than a record of last
season's feeding.
