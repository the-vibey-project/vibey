## §26 The special problems of ancient history

- **Scarcity and survival bias:** most ancient writing is lost. Tacitus' *Annals* 1–6 survive in a single medieval manuscript; much of Livy is gone. We see the ancient world through elite, male, literate, urban, and often victorious eyes.
- **Late manuscripts:** almost all classical texts survive in medieval copies centuries removed from their authors — the same textual-criticism problems as the Bible, with far fewer witnesses.
- **Numbers and rhetoric:** ancient authors routinely inflate army sizes, invent speeches (Thucydides says so openly), and follow genre conventions foreign to modern history-writing.
- **Integrating material and textual evidence:** archaeology and texts answer different questions at different resolutions; forcing them to "match" is a classic error in both directions.
