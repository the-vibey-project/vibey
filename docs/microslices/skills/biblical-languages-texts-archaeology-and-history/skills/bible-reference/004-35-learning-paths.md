## §35 Learning paths

### §35.1 If you want to read the languages

1. **Greek first or Hebrew first?** Greek has more resources and a more familiar grammar for English speakers; Hebrew is smaller and pays off quickly in the Old Testament. Either works.
2. **Hebrew:** Pratico & Van Pelt, *Basics of Biblical Hebrew*; Futato, *Beginning Biblical Hebrew*; or immersion/living-language approaches (Biblical Language Center, Randall Buth). Then graded readers and the *Reader's Hebrew Bible*.
3. **Greek:** Mounce, *Basics of Biblical Greek*; Decker, *Reading Koine Greek*; Black, *Learn to Read New Testament Greek*; immersive options (Polis Institute). Then the *UBS Reader's Greek New Testament*.
4. **Aramaic:** Van Pelt, *Basics of Biblical Aramaic*.
5. **Latin:** Wheelock's *Latin*; for Church Latin specifically, Collins, *A Primer of Ecclesiastical Latin*. Then read the Vulgate Gospels alongside the Greek.
6. **Habit that matters most:** daily reading of real text, even a few verses, beats occasional intensive study.

### §35.2 If you want to understand textual criticism

- Bruce Metzger & Bart Ehrman, *The Text of the New Testament* (4th ed., 2005) — the standard introduction.
- Kurt & Barbara Aland, *The Text of the New Testament* (2nd ed.).
- Peter Gurry & Elijah Hixson (eds.), *Myths and Mistakes in New Testament Textual Criticism* (2019) — corrects common apologetic *and* skeptical errors.
- Tommy Wasserman & Peter Gurry, *A New Approach to Textual Criticism: An Introduction to the Coherence-Based Genealogical Method* (2017).
- David Parker, *An Introduction to the New Testament Manuscripts and Their Texts* (2008).
- Bruce Metzger, *A Textual Commentary on the Greek New Testament* (2nd ed., 1994) — explains the UBS committee's decisions verse by verse.
- Emanuel Tov, *Textual Criticism of the Hebrew Bible* (4th ed., 2022).
- Ellis Brotzman & Eric Tully, *Old Testament Textual Criticism: A Practical Introduction*.
- Ronald Hendel, *Steps to a New Edition of the Hebrew Bible* (2016).

### §35.3 If you want to understand translation

- Eugene Nida & Charles Taber, *The Theory and Practice of Translation* (1969).
- Ernst-August Gutt, *Translation and Relevance* (1991).
- Dave Brunn, *One Bible, Many Versions* (2013) — excellent on why "literal" is complicated.
- Leland Ryken, *The Word of God in English* (2002) — the case for essentially literal translation.
- Gordon Fee & Mark Strauss, *How to Choose a Translation for All Its Worth* (2007).
- David Daniell, *The Bible in English* (2003).
- Adam Nicolson, *God's Secretaries* (2003) — the KJV translators.
- Robert Alter, *The Art of Bible Translation* (2019).
- The **NET Bible's translators' notes** (free online) — the best practical window into translation decisions.

### §35.4 If you want to understand archaeology

- Amihai Mazar, *Archaeology of the Land of the Bible, 10,000–586 B.C.E.* (1990) — still the standard synthesis.
- Israel Finkelstein & Neil Asher Silberman, *The Bible Unearthed* (2001) — the Low Chronology view for general readers.
- William Dever, *What Did the Biblical Writers Know and When Did They Know It?* (2001) and *Beyond the Texts* (2017).
- Jodi Magness, *The Archaeology of the Holy Land: From the Destruction of Solomon's Temple to the Muslim Conquest* (2012).
- Eric Cline, *Biblical Archaeology: A Very Short Introduction* (2009).
- Edward Harris, *Principles of Archaeological Stratigraphy* (2nd ed., 1989).
- Colin Renfrew & Paul Bahn, *Archaeology: Theories, Methods, and Practice* (general archaeology textbook).
- Journals and magazines: *Near Eastern Archaeology*, *BASOR*, *Israel Exploration Journal*, *Tel Aviv*, *Levant*; *Biblical Archaeology Review* for popular access.

### §35.5 If you want to understand historical method

- Marc Bloch, *The Historian's Craft*.
- E. H. Carr, *What Is History?*; R. G. Collingwood, *The Idea of History*.
- C. Behan McCullagh, *Justifying Historical Descriptions* (1984).
- Martha Howell & Walter Prevenier, *From Reliable Sources: An Introduction to Historical Methods* (2001).
- Iain Provan, V. Philips Long & Tremper Longman, *A Biblical History of Israel* (conservative method), alongside Mario Liverani, *Israel's History and the History of Israel* (critical method) — read as a pair.
- Chris Keith & Anthony Le Donne (eds.), *Jesus, Criteria, and the Demise of Authenticity* (2012); Dale Allison, *Constructing Jesus* (2010); Richard Bauckham, *Jesus and the Eyewitnesses* (2nd ed., 2017); E. P. Sanders, *The Historical Figure of Jesus* (1993); Paula Fredriksen, *Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews* (1999).
