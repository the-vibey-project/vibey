## §37 Glossary

- **Apparatus (critical apparatus):** the footnote section of a critical edition listing variants and their witnesses.
- **Autograph:** the original document written by an author (none survive).
- **Back translation:** a literal rendering of a draft translation into a consultant's language for checking.
- **Balk (baulk):** an unexcavated strip between excavation squares, preserving a vertical section.
- **BHS / BHQ:** Biblia Hebraica Stuttgartensia / Quinta — critical editions of the Hebrew Bible.
- **Binyan:** a Hebrew verb stem pattern.
- **CBGM:** Coherence-Based Genealogical Method.
- **Codex:** a book with pages (vs a scroll). Christians adopted the codex early and enthusiastically.
- **Conflation:** combining two variant readings into one.
- **Diplomatic edition:** prints a single manuscript's text, putting variants in the apparatus.
- **Eclectic edition:** prints a reconstructed text drawn from many witnesses.
- **ECM:** Editio Critica Maior.
- **Formal / functional equivalence:** translation that prioritizes source form / target-language meaning and naturalness.
- **Gregory-Aland number:** the catalogue number of a Greek NT manuscript.
- **Harris Matrix:** diagram of stratigraphic relationships.
- **Initial text (Ausgangstext):** the reconstructed text from which the surviving tradition descends.
- **Ketiv/Qere:** "what is written / what is read" in the Masoretic tradition.
- **Koine:** the common Greek of the Hellenistic–Roman world.
- **Lectio difficilior potior:** "the harder reading is preferable."
- **Locus:** the basic excavation unit (a distinct deposit or feature).
- **LXX:** the Septuagint.
- **Masoretes / Masorah:** medieval Jewish scholars who vocalized and annotated the Hebrew text / their notes.
- **MT:** Masoretic Text.
- **NA28/NA29:** Nestle-Aland *Novum Testamentum Graece*, 28th/29th editions.
- **Palimpsest:** a manuscript whose original text was scraped off and overwritten.
- **Provenance:** documented ownership and find history of an object.
- **Recension:** a deliberate revision of a text.
- **Stratigraphy:** study of the sequence of deposits.
- **Tell:** a mound formed by successive settlements.
- **Textus Receptus (TR):** the printed Greek NT tradition from Erasmus to the Elzevirs, underlying the KJV New Testament.
- **Variation unit:** a place in the text where witnesses differ.
- **Vorlage:** the source text a translator worked from.
- **Vulgate:** the Latin Bible associated with Jerome.
