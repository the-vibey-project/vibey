## §29 Why provenance matters

**Provenance** is an object's documented history from discovery to today. An artifact from a controlled excavation comes with its context; an object bought on the antiquities market usually comes with a story that cannot be verified. The market rewards spectacular biblical connections, which creates an incentive for **looting** (which destroys the context of genuine objects) and **forgery** (which manufactures objects to order). International norms treat the **1970 UNESCO Convention** as the key threshold: objects without documented export before 1970 are presumptively suspect.
