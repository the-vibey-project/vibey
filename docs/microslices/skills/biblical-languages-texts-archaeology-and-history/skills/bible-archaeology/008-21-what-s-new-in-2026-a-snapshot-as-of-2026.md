## §21 What's new in 2026 (a snapshot) **\[as of 2026\]**

- **Burnt wooden roof beams from the 586 BCE Babylonian destruction** found in the Givati Parking Lot excavation in the City of David, preserved beneath melted plaster; their tree rings may allow unusually precise radiocarbon work.
- **The first known mold for Judean pillar figurines** (First Temple period) found in the Tower of David excavations in July 2026 — evidence such figurines, sometimes linked to the goddess Asherah, were manufactured in Jerusalem.
- A **standing stone concealed at Tel 'Eton**, argued in a 2026 study to reflect Hezekiah's cultic reforms. **\[new; interpretation debatable\]**
- **Nineveh's Shamash Gate:** new excavation after ISIS-era looting tunnels, publishing evidence of the city's 612 BCE destruction (arrowheads, skeletons, burn layer).
- **Codex H** multispectral recovery (§13 → `bible-textual-criticism`).
- The **Great Isaiah Scroll** went on display in its entirety for the first time since 1968 (reported February 2026).
- A 2026 *Levant* study reassigning a fortress to the Kingdom of Israel in the early 8th century BCE rather than to the Assyrians; a Persian-period mass infant burial at Tel Azekah.
