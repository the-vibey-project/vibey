## §13 Digital tools and new science

- **NTVMR** (New Testament Virtual Manuscript Room, Münster): images, transcriptions, the Liste, the ECM online.
- **Codex Sinaiticus Project**: the whole manuscript digitized and reunited online across its four holding institutions.
- **Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library** (Israel Antiquities Authority): high-resolution multispectral images of the scrolls.
- **Multispectral imaging** recovers erased undertexts in palimpsests. In spring 2026 a University of Glasgow–led team announced recovery of 42 lost pages of **Codex H**, a sixth-century manuscript of Paul's letters taken apart on Mount Athos in the 13th century and recycled as binding material, now scattered across libraries in Italy, Greece, Russia, Ukraine, and France; the recovered text preserves early chapter divisions of Paul's letters that differ from ours. **\[as of 2026\]**
- **AI and radiocarbon dating:** In June 2025, a University of Groningen–led team (Mladen Popović) published *Enoch*, a machine-learning model trained on radiocarbon-dated Dead Sea Scroll samples and handwriting features, in *PLOS One*. Results suggest many scrolls are **older than previously thought**, revising the development of the "Hasmonaean" and "Herodian" scripts; the team argues two biblical fragments (4Q114 Daniel, 4Q109 Qohelet) date to roughly the time of their books' presumed composition. Experts judged Enoch's estimates "realistic" in 79% of tested cases. **\[new; will be debated\]**
- **Computational stemmatology and phylogenetics** borrowed from evolutionary biology are being tested on textual traditions.
