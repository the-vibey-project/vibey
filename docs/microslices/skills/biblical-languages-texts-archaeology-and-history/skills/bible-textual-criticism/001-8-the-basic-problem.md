## §8 The basic problem

We have **no autographs** (original documents) of any biblical book — nor of virtually any ancient literary work. We have copies of copies, made by hand for well over a thousand years before printing. Every hand copy introduces changes. **Textual criticism** is the discipline of collecting the surviving witnesses, classifying their differences (**variants**), and reasoning back toward the earliest recoverable text — while also studying the history of the text as a subject in its own right.

It is also called **"lower criticism"** (establishing the text), as distinct from **"higher criticism"** (questions of authorship, sources, date, and history). "Lower" means foundational, not inferior.
