## 6. What must be disclosed, or could be exposed

- **A lawsuit the filer is pursuing** is an asset the trustee can take an interest in, and exemptions for it are small. The litigation attorney and the bankruptcy attorney need to coordinate **before** filing.
- **A business the filer owns** (an LLC, for example): ownership, accounts and debts must all be listed.
- **Valuations:** informal estimates of a house or car are a starting point; the trustee will form their own.
- **Tax refunds** and any other money expected may count as assets.
