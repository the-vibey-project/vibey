## Sources and caveats

- Court fees: $338 Chapter 7 / $313 Chapter 13 (28 U.S.C. § 1930 plus administrative fees), per MoneyLion and Ascend, 2026.
- South Carolina attorney fees ($1,100–$3,000): tryascend.com, "bankruptcy/south-carolina/cost", 2026.
- South Carolina median income and the means test: recordinglaw.com's means-test calculator (U.S. Trustee Program figures effective April 1, 2026; unchanged as of July 15, 2026).
- South Carolina exemptions: Nolo, Upsolve and DocDraft guides; their figures conflict, so verify against the South Carolina notice effective July 1, 2026.
- Surrender mechanics: Nolo, Lawrence Law, Burns & Hansen.
- Timeline: ntrial.com and Upsolve's South Carolina guide.
- Credit effects and mortgage waiting periods are general guidance, not taken from the sources above. ⚠️ Verify before relying on them.
