## Quick reference

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trinity | Violence/people, chance/army, policy/government — ignore one and the strategy fails |
| Friction / fog | Small difficulties that separate plan from execution / irreducible uncertainty |
| Centre of gravity | Source of an opponent's strength and cohesion — not "the important thing" |
| Culminating point | Where an advancing offensive becomes weaker than the defender |
| OODA | Observe, **Orient**, Decide, Act — Orient is the decisive step |
| Auftragstaktik | Mission command: specify intent and task, delegate method |
| Punishment / denial | Threaten costs / deny the objective — denial is generally more credible |
| Compellence | Demanding visible action; harder than deterrence because it requires humiliation |
| Stability-instability paradox | Stability at the nuclear level enabling conflict below it |
| Abandonment / entrapment | The ally does not come / the ally drags you in |
| Ends, ways, means | The core discipline of grand strategy; the classic failure is ends exceeding means |
