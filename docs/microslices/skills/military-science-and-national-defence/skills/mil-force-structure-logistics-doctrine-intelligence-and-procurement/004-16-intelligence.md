## §16. Intelligence

**⚠️ The cycle**: ⚠️ **direction, collection, processing, analysis, dissemination — and the
observation that the cycle model is tidier than practice.**
**⚠️ The disciplines**: ⚠️ **HUMINT, SIGINT, IMINT/GEOINT, MASINT, and ⚠️ OSINT, whose
importance has grown enormously with commercial satellite imagery and social media —
to the point where open sources now do work that once required national technical means.**
**⚠️ The analytical failures** are well studied: ⚠️ **mirror imaging, confirmation bias,
politicization, and the SIGNAL-TO-NOISE problem — ⚠️ Pearl Harbor and 9/11 both involved
available indicators lost in volume, which is why "we had the information" and "we knew" are
different claims.**
**⚠️ Warning versus surprise**: ⚠️ **strategic surprise usually reflects a failure to BELIEVE
rather than a failure to collect, because the warning contradicted an existing assumption.**
**⚠️ Counterintelligence and deception** — ⚠️ **and the deception literature's finding that
the most effective deception reinforces what the target already expects.**
