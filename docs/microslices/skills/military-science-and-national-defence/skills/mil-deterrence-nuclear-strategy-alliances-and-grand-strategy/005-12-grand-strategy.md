## §12. Grand Strategy

**⚠️ The integration of all instruments toward national objectives** — ⚠️ **diplomatic,
informational, military and economic, with military force usually the most expensive and
least controllable.**
**⚠️ The core discipline is matching ENDS, WAYS and MEANS** — ⚠️ **and the classic failure
is ends that exceed means, which produces either strategic overstretch or quiet
abandonment of stated goals.**
**⚠️ The recurring debate** in any large power's strategy runs between ⚠️ **primacy, deep
engagement, selective engagement, offshore balancing and restraint** — ⚠️ **and this is a
genuine political disagreement about interests and risk tolerance, not a technical question
with a correct answer.**
**⚠️ Geography persists** as a constraint: ⚠️ **the classical geopolitical theories were
overstated and the underlying observation that position, distance and access shape options
remains true.**
**⚠️ The economic base** is the ultimate constraint (§1 → `mil-theory-clausewitz-sun-tzu-and-sea-and-air-power`'s second organizing idea) —
⚠️ **and Kennedy's argument that great powers decline when military commitments outrun their
economic foundation remains the most-cited version of that claim.**
