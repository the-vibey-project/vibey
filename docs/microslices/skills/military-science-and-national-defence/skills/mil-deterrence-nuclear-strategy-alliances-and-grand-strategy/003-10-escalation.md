## §10. Escalation

**⚠️ Escalation ladders and thresholds** — ⚠️ **Kahn's framework, and the practical
observation that thresholds are SOCIALLY constructed and asymmetrically perceived.** ⚠️ **What
one side reads as a proportionate response, the other may read as a major escalation.**
**⚠️ Salami tactics and grey-zone action** exploit this: ⚠️ **each individual step is too
small to justify a response, and the cumulative effect is large.**
**⚠️ Escalation dominance** — ⚠️ **the ability to prevail at each higher rung, which is what
makes threats credible.**
**⚠️ ESCALATION MANAGEMENT** in practice depends on ⚠️ **signalling, off-ramps that let an
opponent stop without humiliation (§8's compellence asymmetry), and communication channels
that survive a crisis.**
**⚠️ The uncomfortable finding from wargaming**: ⚠️ **participants frequently escalate faster
than they predict they will, and inadvertent escalation from misperception is a more common
pathway than deliberate choice.**
