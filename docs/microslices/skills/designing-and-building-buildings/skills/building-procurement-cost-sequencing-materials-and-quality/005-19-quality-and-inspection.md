## §19. Quality and Inspection

**⚠️ Quality is built, not inspected in** — ⚠️ **but verification exists because that
statement is aspirational.**
**⚠️ Mechanisms**: **submittals and shop drawing review, ⚠️ MOCK-UPS (⚠️ the single most
effective quality tool for façades and finishes — build it, test it, agree it, then it's
the benchmark), witness and hold points, special inspections (§14 → `building-accessibility-energy-drawings-bim-and-codes`), and
⚠️ NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING (concrete cube/cylinder tests, rebar scanning, weld UT and
radiography, thermography, air pressure testing).**
**⚠️ Snagging/punch lists and defects liability periods** — ⚠️ **and the honest observation
that latent defects (§8 → `building-structure-foundations-envelope-services-and-fire`'s moisture problems especially) appear years later, after the
defects period has expired.**
