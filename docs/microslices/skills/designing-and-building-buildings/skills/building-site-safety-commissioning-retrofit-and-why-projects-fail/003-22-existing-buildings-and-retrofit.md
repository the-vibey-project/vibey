## §22. Existing Buildings and Retrofit

**⚠️ Most of the buildings that will exist in 2050 already exist, which makes this the
larger part of the work.**
**⚠️ Survey and assessment**: ⚠️ **measured survey, structural appraisal, material testing,
⚠️ and hazardous materials (asbestos, lead paint) surveys BEFORE any work.**
**⚠️ The recurring retrofit hazards**: ⚠️ **unknown structure behind finishes; original
drawings that don't match reality; load path changes from previous alterations nobody
recorded; and ⚠️ CHANGING THE MOISTURE BEHAVIOUR of a historic assembly by insulating it,
which can cause rot in a wall that survived a century** (§8 → `building-structure-foundations-envelope-services-and-fire`).
**⚠️ Heritage constraints** limit intervention and demand reversibility.
**⚠️ Adaptive reuse and the carbon argument** — ⚠️ **retaining structure avoids the embodied
carbon of replacement, which is now a regulated consideration in some jurisdictions**
(§24.2 → `building-reference`).
