## §20. Site Safety

**⚠️ Construction has a disproportionate share of workplace fatalities, and the causes are
concentrated and well known.**
```
⚠️ THE RECURRING FATAL FOUR  ⚠️ FALLS FROM HEIGHT (the largest by far) ·
   struck-by · caught-in/between · electrocution
⚠️ ALSO  ⚠️ excavation collapse (⚠️ a cubic metre of soil weighs
   well over a tonne, and unshored trenches kill quickly) ·
   confined spaces · lifting operations · ⚠️ silica dust and
   asbestos (⚠️ long-latency, and the biggest killer by total
   deaths in some jurisdictions)
⚠️ THE CONTROLS  design out the hazard first (⚠️ prevention through
   design / CDM Regulations make this a DESIGNER duty in some
   jurisdictions) → engineering controls → then PPE
```
**⚠️ The designer's role is real**: ⚠️ **specifying a detail that can only be installed by
someone working at height on a ladder is a design decision with a safety consequence.**
