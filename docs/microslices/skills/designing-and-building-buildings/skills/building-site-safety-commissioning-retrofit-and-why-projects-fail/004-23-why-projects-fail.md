## §23. ⚠️ Why Projects Fail

```
⚠️ COORDINATION FAILURES AT BOUNDARIES  ⚠️ between disciplines,
   between design and construction, between contractor and
   specialist designer. ⚠️ The largest single category
⚠️ INADEQUATE SITE INVESTIGATION (§2)
⚠️ SCOPE CREEP and late changes (§1's cost-influence curve)
⚠️ OPTIMISM BIAS in cost and programme (see a civil/industrial
   reference for the base rates)
⚠️ INCOMPLETE DOCUMENTATION → RFIs, delays, claims (§13)
⚠️ RISK ALLOCATED TO PARTIES WHO CANNOT CARRY IT (§15)
⚠️ COMPRESSED COMMISSIONING (§21)
⚠️ THE HYATT REGENCY PATTERN  ⚠️ a change that looks like a detail
   silently invalidates the original analysis, and nobody re-derives it
```
**⚠️ What correlates with projects going well**: ⚠️ **early contractor and specialist
involvement, a stable brief, adequate design fees and time (⚠️ under-resourced design is
false economy that shows up as construction cost), realistic programmes, and genuine
early-warning culture rather than retrospective claims.**
