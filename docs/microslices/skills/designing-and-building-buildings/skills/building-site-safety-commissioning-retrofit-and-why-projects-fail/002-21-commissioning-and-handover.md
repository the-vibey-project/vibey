## §21. Commissioning and Handover

**⚠️ The step that determines whether the building actually works as designed, and the
step that gets compressed when the programme slips.**
**⚠️ Commissioning**: **testing and balancing, controls verification, ⚠️ integrated systems
testing, and ⚠️ seasonal commissioning (a system tuned in October has not been proven in
July).**
**⚠️ Handover**: **O&M manuals, as-built information, training the facilities team,
warranties, spares.** ⚠️ **Handover documentation quality is chronically poor, and the
operator inherits the consequences.**
**⚠️ Soft landings** — ⚠️ **the designer and contractor remain involved after occupation to
tune the building and close the performance gap** (§12 → `building-accessibility-energy-drawings-bim-and-codes`). **⚠️ It works and it is
frequently cut.**
**⚠️ Post-occupancy evaluation** is ⚠️ **the industry's most obvious missing feedback loop:
buildings are rarely measured against their design intent, so the same mistakes recur.**
