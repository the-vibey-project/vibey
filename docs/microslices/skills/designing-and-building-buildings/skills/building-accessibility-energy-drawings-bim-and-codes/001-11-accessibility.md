## §11. Accessibility

**⚠️ A legal requirement and a design quality issue, and treating it as the former only
produces bad buildings.**
**⚠️ Typical requirements**: **accessible routes and entrances, ⚠️ gradients and ramps,
door widths and clear opening, manoeuvring space, sanitary accommodation, lift provision,
⚠️ means of escape for disabled occupants (refuges and evacuation lifts — frequently
overlooked), signage, acoustics and lighting for sensory impairment.**
**⚠️ Standards**: **ADA and ANSI A117.1 (US), Approved Document M and BS 8300 (UK), and
equivalents elsewhere.**
**⚠️ Universal design is the better framing** — ⚠️ **designing for the widest range of
users produces buildings that work better for everyone, and retrofitting accessibility is
far more expensive than designing it in.**
