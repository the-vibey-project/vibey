## §14. ⚠️ Codes and Permitting

**⚠️ Codes are law where adopted, and adoption is LOCAL** (§24.1 → `building-reference`).
```
⚠️ THE STRUCTURE  model codes (⚠️ IBC/IRC/IFC and the I-Codes;
   Eurocodes; national building regulations) → adopted by
   jurisdiction, ⚠️ frequently WITH AMENDMENTS → interpreted by
   the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ)
⚠️ THEREFORE  ⚠️ the model code is not the law. The ADOPTED and
   AMENDED version in that jurisdiction is, and editions lag —
   a state may still be several cycles behind (§24.1)
⚠️ REFERENCED STANDARDS  ⚠️ codes incorporate standards by reference
   (ASCE 7 for loads, ACI 318 for concrete, AISC 360 for steel,
   NFPA for fire), and the referenced EDITION matters
⚠️ THE PROCESS  permit application → plan review → permits →
   ⚠️ INSPECTIONS at defined hold points → certificate of occupancy
⚠️ SPECIAL INSPECTIONS  independent verification of critical work
   (§19)
```
**⚠️ Alternative means and methods** allow non-prescriptive solutions where equivalence is
demonstrated — ⚠️ **the route for innovation, and it costs time and review.**
