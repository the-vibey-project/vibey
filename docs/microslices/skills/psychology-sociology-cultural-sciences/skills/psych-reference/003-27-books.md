## §27. Books

**⚠️ Epistemics first — read these before the content books:**
| Author | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Ritchie** | ***Science Fictions*** | ⚠️ **The best accessible account of §1–§3 → `psych-epistemic-situation-and-replication-reform`. Start here** |
| **Chivers** | *Everything Is Predictable* | Bayesian reasoning applied to exactly these problems |
| **Meehl** | *"Why Summaries of Research… Are Often Uninterpretable"* | ⚠️ **He diagnosed all of this in 1978. Chilling** |

**Psychology**
| **Kahneman** | *Thinking, Fast and Slow* | ⚠️ **Foundational AND partly superseded — read with §7 → `psych-perception-memory-learning-cognition-and-emotion` in hand** |
| **Barrett** | *How Emotions Are Made* | §8 → `psych-perception-memory-learning-cognition-and-emotion`'s constructionist case, argued strongly |
| **Loftus** | *Eyewitness Testimony* | §5 → `psych-perception-memory-learning-cognition-and-emotion` |
| **Nettle** | *Personality* | ⚠️ **Short, honest Big Five treatment** |
| **Brown, Roediger & McDaniel** | ***Make It Stick*** | ⚠️ **§5 → `psych-perception-memory-learning-cognition-and-emotion`'s learning findings, practically** |

**Sociology and anthropology**
| **Mills** | ***The Sociological Imagination*** | The founding statement of the discipline's move |
| **Goffman** | ***The Presentation of Self*** | ⚠️ **Still the most useful single sociology book** |
| **Bourdieu** | *Distinction* | §17–§18 → `psych-sociology-institutions-culture-and-the-weird-problem`. Difficult and worth it |
| **Granovetter** | *"The Strength of Weak Ties"* (1973) | ⚠️ **One paper, enormous payoff** (§19 → `psych-sociology-institutions-culture-and-the-weird-problem`) |
| **Geertz** | *The Interpretation of Cultures* | §23 → `psych-sociology-institutions-culture-and-the-weird-problem` |
| **Henrich** | ***The Secret of Our Success*** / *The WEIRDest People* | ⚠️ **§24 → `psych-sociology-institutions-culture-and-the-weird-problem`. The best cultural-evolution case** |
| **Scott** | *Seeing Like a State* | ⚠️ **Institutions, legibility, and why schemes fail** (§20 → `psych-sociology-institutions-culture-and-the-weird-problem`) |
