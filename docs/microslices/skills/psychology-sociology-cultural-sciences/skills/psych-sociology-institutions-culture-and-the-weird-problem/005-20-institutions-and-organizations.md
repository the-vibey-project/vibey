## §20. Institutions and Organizations

**⚠️ Institutions are the rules of the game — formal and informal** — **and they are
sticky, path-dependent, and often persist long after the conditions that produced them.**
**⚠️ Isomorphism (DiMaggio & Powell)** — **organizations in a field come to resemble one
another through coercive, mimetic and normative pressure** — ⚠️ **which explains why
competitors converge on the same practices regardless of whether the practices work.**
**Bureaucracy** (Weber) — **the rational-legal form, its genuine efficiency advantages,
and its pathologies: goal displacement, where following the rule replaces achieving the
purpose.**
**Principal-agent problems, loose coupling** (⚠️ **the formal structure and the actual work
can be substantially decoupled, and the formal structure may exist mainly for
legitimacy**), **and organizational culture.**
**⚠️ Normal Accidents (Perrow) vs High Reliability Organizations** — **whether catastrophic
failure is inevitable in tightly-coupled complex systems, or whether some organizations
genuinely achieve reliability.** **Both literatures are worth knowing and they disagree
productively** (see an IT infrastructure reference §20).
