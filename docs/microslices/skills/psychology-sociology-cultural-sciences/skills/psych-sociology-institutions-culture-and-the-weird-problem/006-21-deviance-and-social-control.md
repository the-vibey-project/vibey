## §21. Deviance and Social Control

**Labelling theory** — ⚠️ **deviance is partly produced by the response to it; labels can
become self-fulfilling.**
**Anomie/strain** (Merton) — **mismatch between cultural goals and legitimate means.**
**Social control and differential association.**
**⚠️ Broken windows is a cautionary case study**: **enormously influential in policy, and
the empirical support is much weaker and more contested than its policy uptake implied.**
**⚠️ What the crime literature better supports**: **the CERTAINTY of sanction deters more
than its SEVERITY** — **a robust finding that policy consistently ignores** — **and the
age-crime curve is one of criminology's most stable empirical regularities.**
**⚠️ Moral panics** (Cohen) — **episodic societal alarm about a group or behaviour,
amplified by media, disproportionate to the actual threat.** ⚠️ **A genuinely useful
analytic concept, and one that is itself frequently deployed rhetorically to dismiss
concerns that turn out to be legitimate** — **the label is not an argument** (§25.2 → `psych-reference`).
