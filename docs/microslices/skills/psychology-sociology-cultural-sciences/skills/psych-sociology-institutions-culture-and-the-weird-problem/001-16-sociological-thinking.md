## §16. Sociological Thinking

**⚠️ The core move is that social facts are real and not reducible to individual
psychology.** **Durkheim's demonstration — that suicide rates are stable and patterned
across groups even though each instance is intensely individual — remains the cleanest
statement of why a separate discipline exists.**

```
⚠️ STRUCTURE vs AGENCY   the field's central tension. People act; structures
   constrain and enable; and structures are reproduced BY that action
   (Giddens' structuration; Bourdieu's habitus)
SOCIAL CONSTRUCTION   ⚠️ categories like race, gender, madness and childhood are
   built and maintained socially — which does NOT mean they are unreal
   or lack material consequence. This is the most misread idea in sociology
THE SOCIOLOGICAL IMAGINATION  ⚠️ Mills — linking personal troubles to public issues
LEVELS   micro (interaction) · meso (organizations) · macro (institutions)
```
**⚠️ Emergence is the key conceptual point**: **social phenomena have properties that do
not exist at the individual level.** **Segregation can emerge from mild individual
preferences with no one intending it (Schelling); traffic jams from no jam-causing car.**
⚠️ **Explaining a social outcome by the intentions of individuals is usually a mistake.**
