## §36. Dabrowski's Overexcitabilities and the Overlap Problem

**Kazimierz Dabrowski's Theory of Positive Disintegration (1964)** holds that psychological
tension and anxiety can be necessary for advanced personality development rather than signs
of pathology. Its five **overexcitabilities (OEs)** — psychomotor, sensual, intellectual,
imaginational and emotional — are heightened responses to stimuli read as "developmental
potential".

**The mapping onto neurodivergence the source draws:**

| OE | Overlaps with |
|---|---|
| Psychomotor (surplus energy, restlessness, rapid speech) | ADHD hyperactivity |
| Sensual (intensified sensory experience) | Autistic sensory-processing differences |
| Intellectual (relentless drive to understand) | Autistic deep interests; gifted curiosity |
| Imaginational | — |
| Emotional (intense feeling, deep caring) | Autistic emotional intensity; ADHD emotional dysregulation |

> **⚠️ GOTCHA — the OE construct is weaker than its standing in gifted education suggests.**
> - **[CONTESTED]** Positive Disintegration is a **grand developmental theory** that sits
>   outside mainstream personality science and has had little predictive testing.
> - **[WEAK]** The OEs are measured mainly by self-report questionnaires (the OEQ family)
>   with debated validity. **A 2016 meta-analysis (Winkler & Voight, *Gifted Child
>   Quarterly*) found gifted–nongifted differences that were modest and uneven across the
>   five OEs, largest for intellectual OE** *(wider literature, not the source)* — far from
>   the "defining trait of giftedness" presented in popular material.
> - **The overlap cuts in a direction the advocacy reading tends to skip.** At the 2023 NAGC
>   conference, Chris Wells of the Dabrowski Center argued OEs **cannot be reliably
>   distinguished** from ADHD and autism by behavioural observation **[CLINICAL LORE — a
>   conference position]**. If so, OEs may partly be **re-descriptions of neurodivergent
>   traits** under a non-clinical name. The source's resolution — "both may be
>   simultaneously true", a de-pathologized and a clinical description of the same
>   characteristics — is a reasonable **[INTERPRETATION]**, and ⚠️ **it means an OE framing
>   must never substitute for an autism or ADHD assessment.**
