## §41. Claim Ledger — Every Flagged Claim at a Glance

The robust anchors — *g* and its predictive validity, autism–ADHD co-occurrence, SMPY,
acceleration, the Flynn effect and its Norwegian reversal — are labelled in place above;
this table lists what needs a caveat.

| Claim as it circulates | Label | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Depression, stress and trauma lower measured performance | **MODERATE** | Direction solid; magnitudes vary |
| 2e index scatter (VCI ≫ PSI) | **MODERATE** | Real in clinical samples; scatter also common in norms |
| Masking correlates with IQ; higher-IQ autistic women missed | **MODERATE** | Consistent direction; figures vary |
| Autistic burnout as a distinct syndrome | **MODERATE, emerging** | Defined 2020; growing, mostly self-report |
| Leadership perception peaks near IQ 120 | **MODERATE** | One study; perception, not performance |
| Neural-efficiency, P-FIT, network accounts | **MODERATE** | Group-level, correlational |
| "Gifted brains are wired differently at every level" | **INTERPRETATION** | Continuous correlations restated categorically |
| Masking suppresses IQ by 15–25 points | **WEAK** | No cited study |
| A depleted-state score is "a floor, not a ceiling" | **INTERPRETATION** | Error is two-way; regression to the mean |
| Hollingworth's ~30-point communication range | **CLINICAL LORE** | Case observations; not replicated despite the claim |
| "1 in 44 people are intellectual peers" | **INTERPRETATION** | Arithmetic on the untested range |
| Dabrowski's overexcitabilities as a gifted trait | **CONTESTED / WEAK** | Modest, uneven meta-analytic differences; overlaps neurodivergence |
| Columbus Group's "qualitatively different experience" | **CONTESTED** | Definitional stance, unmeasured |
| Existential depression in the gifted | **CLINICAL LORE** | No measure, no controlled comparison |
| Gifted people are more perfectionistic | **WEAK** | Not in the maladaptive dimension (meta-analysis) |
| Hyper-brain/hyper-body | **WEAK** | Mensa self-report vs base rates; contradicts cohort data |
| "ADHD burnout", "gifted burnout", compound burnout | **CLINICAL LORE** | Community and clinical terms |
| Each burnout makes the next recovery harder | **WEAK** | Self-report |
| Multiple Intelligences | **WEAK** | Contradicted by the positive manifold |
| Systemizing genes as "talent genes" | **CONTESTED** | Genetic correlation real; interpretation disputed |
| Very high IQ tiers (160+, 180+) as precise | **WEAK** | Ceilings, thin norms, ratio-IQ incomparability |
| Reversal makes gifted people rarer | **INTERPRETATION** | Re-norming fixes the proportion by construction |
| US gifted funding "defunded in 2011" | **VERSIONED** | Restored from FY2014 |
| Peer access transforms adult outcomes | **CLINICAL LORE** | Child and acceleration evidence extrapolated |
