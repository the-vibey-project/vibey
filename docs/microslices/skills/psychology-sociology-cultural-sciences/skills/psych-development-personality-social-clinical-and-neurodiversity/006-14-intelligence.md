## §14. Intelligence

**⚠️ This is a domain where the empirical findings are unusually robust and the public
discourse is unusually poor, in both directions.**

**What is well established:**
- **⚠️ The positive manifold**: **performance across diverse cognitive tasks correlates
  positively, yielding a general factor, g.** **This is one of the most replicated
  findings in psychology.**
- **⚠️ IQ tests are reliable and have real predictive validity** for **educational
  attainment, job performance (rising with job complexity), income and health outcomes.**
  ⚠️ **Predictive, meaning at the group-statistical level — not determinative for
  individuals.**
- **⚠️ Substantially heritable in adulthood, with heritability INCREASING with age**
  (the Wilson effect) — **which is counterintuitive and well replicated.**
- **⚠️ The Flynn effect**: **large IQ gains across the 20th century, far too fast to be
  genetic.** ⚠️ **This alone demonstrates powerful environmental effects on measured
  scores**, and **the effect has stalled or reversed in several developed countries.**

**⚠️ What is NOT established, and where public discussion goes wrong:**
- ⚠️ **Heritability is a population statistic about VARIANCE in a specific environment.**
  **It says nothing about immutability, and nothing whatsoever about the causes of
  differences BETWEEN groups.** **This inference is invalid and is the most common error
  in the area.**
- ⚠️ **Environmental effects are large** — **nutrition, schooling, lead exposure, iodine
  deficiency and illness all measurably affect cognitive development, and several have
  produced large population-level changes.**
- **⚠️ g is not "the whole of intelligence" in any everyday sense**, and **multiple
  intelligences (Gardner) is popular but has not held up as a psychometric model.**
- **⚠️ Test scores are influenced by motivation, familiarity and circumstance**, and
  **measurement invariance across groups has to be demonstrated, not assumed.**

**⚠️ The responsible summary**: **g is real, measurable and predictive; it is not destiny,
not fixed, not the sum of human worth, and not a licence for any claim about groups.**
