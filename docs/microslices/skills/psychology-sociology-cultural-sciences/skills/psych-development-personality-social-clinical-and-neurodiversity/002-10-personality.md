## §10. Personality

**⚠️ The Big Five (OCEAN) is one of psychology's genuine successes**: **Openness,
Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism.** ⚠️ **Derived
bottom-up from the statistical structure of trait language and behaviour rather than
imposed by theory, replicated across many cultures and languages, substantially heritable,
reasonably stable in adulthood, and predictive of real outcomes.**
- ⚠️ **Conscientiousness predicts job performance across nearly every occupation and
  predicts longevity.**
- **Neuroticism predicts mental and physical health outcomes.**
- ⚠️ **Traits are continuous distributions, not types.** **There are no "introverts" and
  "extraverts" as discrete kinds — there is a distribution.**
- **Personality changes gradually with age (the maturity principle: conscientiousness and
  agreeableness tend to rise, neuroticism to fall).**

> **⚠️ GOTCHA — the MBTI is not a valid instrument and its ubiquity in corporate settings
> is a genuine embarrassment.** ⚠️ **It has poor test-retest reliability (a substantial
> fraction of people get a different type on retest within weeks), it imposes false
> dichotomies on continuous distributions, its factor structure doesn't hold up, and it
> lacks predictive validity for job performance.** **The same caution applies to
> Enneagram, DISC, colour-based typologies and most commercial personality products.**
> **⚠️ If you need a personality measure, use a validated Big Five inventory.**

**⚠️ The person-situation debate resolved sensibly**: **Mischel showed situations predict
behaviour more than traits in any single instance; traits predict AGGREGATE behaviour
across many instances well.** **Both are true and they operate at different levels.**
**Dark triad** (narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy) — **real constructs**,
⚠️ **and heavily over-applied in popular usage to describe people one dislikes.**
