## §9. Development

**⚠️ Piaget's stage sequence is directionally right and the ages are wrong** — **later
methods showed infants understand far more, far earlier, than his tasks revealed.**
**Vygotsky** — **the zone of proximal development and the social scaffolding of learning;
influential and hard to test rigorously.**
**Attachment** (Bowlby, Ainsworth) — ⚠️ **the secure/insecure distinction has real
predictive validity, though weaker than popular treatments imply, and adult "attachment
style" as a personality typology is a considerable extrapolation from infant research.**
**⚠️ Behavioural genetics is where developmental psychology's most robust and most
uncomfortable findings live** (§14): **nearly everything is partly heritable; shared
family environment explains less of adult outcomes than intuition demands; and
gene-environment correlation means "environment" measures are partly genetic.**
**⚠️ Adolescence**: **real neurological development (prefrontal maturation extending into
the twenties, differential timing of reward and control systems)** — ⚠️ **and popular
"the teenage brain" accounts routinely overstate the determinism.**
**Ageing**: ⚠️ **fluid abilities decline from early adulthood; crystallized abilities hold
or improve into later life.**
