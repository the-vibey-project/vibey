## §11. Motivation

**Intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation; Self-Determination Theory** (autonomy, competence,
relatedness) — ⚠️ **well-supported and among the more durable frameworks.**
**⚠️ The overjustification effect is real but narrower than advertised**: **rewarding an
already-intrinsically-motivating activity can undermine it** — ⚠️ **the effect is
strongest for expected, tangible, performance-independent rewards on tasks the person
already enjoys, and does not generalize to "incentives don't work."**
**Goal setting** (Locke & Latham) — **specific, difficult goals outperform "do your
best"** — ⚠️ **and goals distort behaviour toward what's measured** (Goodhart; see a
business reference §13).
**⚠️ Growth mindset is a cautionary tale worth knowing.** **The original claim was
enormous; large preregistered replications and meta-analyses found effects far smaller
than claimed, concentrated in specific subgroups, and inconsistent.** **Something is
probably there. It is not what was sold to schools.**
