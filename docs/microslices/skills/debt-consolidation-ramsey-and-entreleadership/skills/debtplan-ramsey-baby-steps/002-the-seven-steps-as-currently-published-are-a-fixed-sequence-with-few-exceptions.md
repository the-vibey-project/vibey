## The seven steps, as currently published, are a fixed sequence with few exceptions

The sequence is: (1) a $1,000 starter emergency fund; (2) pay off all non-mortgage debt with the debt snowball; (3) a fully funded emergency fund of three to six months of expenses; (4) invest 15% of household income for retirement; (5) save for children's college; (6) pay off the home early; (7) build wealth and give. Ramsey's own pages recognize few exceptions: Step 2 excludes the mortgage, an optional Step 3b covers a home down payment, retirement saving may be paused up to about two years to build that down payment, and identity-theft coverage may be skipped temporarily if it slows the snowball ([Help Center](https://help.ramseysolutions.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046646012-What-are-the-7-Baby-Steps); [Save for a house](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/real-estate/how-to-save-money-to-buy-a-house); [Insurance](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/insurance/insurance-and-the-baby-steps)). A discrepancy remains unresolved: the main help-center article lists seven steps with no 3b, while the Step 3 page and EveryDollar help describe it.
