## Step 1 is still $1,000, and the page defends that figure openly

The Step 1 page says to save $1,000 "as fast as possible," acknowledges the number has not changed in about 30 years, argues it was always meant as a starter, and suggests finishing in about 30 days. No variant exists for higher earners, and I found no dated change to $2,000; third-party claims of one are unsupported unless a dated Ramsey source appears. The page also says to give 10% of gross first, pay only minimums on debt, pause investing "even up to the company match," and cover the Four Walls (food, utilities, shelter, transportation), citing a 2026 Federal Reserve report that 37% of people could not cover a $400 expense ([Step 1](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps/step-1)). The emergency test is unexpected, necessary, urgent, and not coverable from the budget. The page concedes $1,000 will not cover job loss or major surgery, which is Step 3's job. Critics such as one CFP quoted by FinanceBuzz accept a 3-to-6-month fund but dislike a fixed $1,000 and cutting all enjoyment ([FinanceBuzz](https://financebuzz.com/news/financial-advisors-dave-ramseys-advice-2)).
