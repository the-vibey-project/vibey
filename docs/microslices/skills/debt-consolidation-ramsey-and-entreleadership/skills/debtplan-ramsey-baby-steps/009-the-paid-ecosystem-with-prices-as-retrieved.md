## The paid ecosystem, with prices as retrieved

EveryDollar Premium is **$17.99 per month or $79.99 per year**, with a 14-day trial, US only ([EveryDollar help](https://everydollar.help.ramseysolutions.com/hc/en-us/articles/21544207900685-EveryDollar-Premium-Subscription-Cost)). The official store lists "Financial Peace All Access" at a $99.99 sale price against a $129.99 MSRP, renewing at $119.99, and the FPU class alone at $79.99 ([Ramsey store](https://store.ramseysolutions.com/money/financial-peace-university/financial-peace-all-access)). The Ramsey+ page shows no price; a third-party blog's $129.99 is unverified ([Ramsey+](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/ramseyplus); [Finny blog](https://getfinny.app/blog/everydollar-pricing-2026)). Prices vary by sale date. Ramsey says nearly 10 million people have taken Financial Peace University ([Dave bio](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey)). The free zero-based budgeting method (income minus planned spending equals zero, a $100 to $300 buffer, cash envelopes for hard-to-track categories) works with a spreadsheet at no cost ([Budget guide](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/budgeting/how-to-make-a-budget)).
