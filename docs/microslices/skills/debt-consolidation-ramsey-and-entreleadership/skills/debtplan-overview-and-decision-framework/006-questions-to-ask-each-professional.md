## Questions to ask each professional

For a **South Carolina bankruptcy attorney**: Do I pass the means test with my last six months of income, and how does unemployment compensation count? Which of my assets are within the current (July 1, 2026) South Carolina exemption caps, and what are those caps? What happens to my car and home if I surrender versus keep them, and what does reaffirmation involve? Would paying a particular creditor, taking a new loan, or making a payment before filing create preference or fraud problems? Which of my debts will not be discharged? What is your fee, what does it include, and can it be paid in installments? Chapter 7 or Chapter 13, and why?

For a **nonprofit credit counselor** (check the DOJ approved list and SCDCA license): Are you SCDCA licensed, and what are the exact fees (setup, monthly) against the state caps? Will my creditors actually reduce rates, and what do I need to cancel? Does the plan require closing cards, and what happens to utilization? Can you provide a written budget analysis showing the plan fits my income?

For a **lender or consolidation offer**: What is the APR with fees included? Is there a prepayment penalty? Is the loan secured, and by what? What happens to the rate after the promotional period? Will it appear as a hard inquiry before I accept? Is the offer contingent on me stopping payments to my creditors?

For a **tax professional**: How would a settlement, bankruptcy discharge, or 401(k) distribution affect this year's return? Do I qualify for the insolvency exclusion and Form 982? How should I handle estimated payments on business profit while receiving unemployment?

For **DEW**: Does starting or operating my LLC, even without income, need to be reported? Are expenses counted? How are partial benefits computed against self-employment profit?
