## Offerings and prices: published dates, mostly unpublished prices

The business hub lists the book, a free podcast (Dave Ramsey took over hosting in February 2023 in a caller-driven format; John Felkins is head coach), a Friday newsletter, a free Stages of Business Assessment, the Build a Business You Love book (April 2025), and low-cost items: Build a Business You Love at $29.99, the Career Bundle at $39.99, the book at $17.99, and a Delegation Quick Read at $12.99 ([Ramsey business hub](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/business); [EntreLeadership page](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/business/entreleadership); [Podnews](https://podnews.net/press-release/dave-ramsey-entreleadership)). Paid events: the **Master Series runs November 8 to 13, 2026** at Ramsey headquarters in Franklin, Tennessee (price by call) ([Master Series](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/events/entreleadership-master-series)); the **Summit is May 23 to 26, 2027** at the Gaylord Pacific Resort in San Diego, about 3,000 attendees, pricing by call, aimed at owners with more than five team members ([Summit](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/events/entreleadership-summit); [leadership conferences](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/business/leadership-conferences)). The Winter 2026 Elite Retreat cost **$4,500** ([Ramsey store](https://store.ramseysolutions.com/elite-retreat-winter-2026)). Elite digital membership advertises "two months free ($500 off)" annually, which implies roughly $250 per month, but that is arithmetic on a discount, not a stated price ([Elite](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/business/entreleadership/elite)). Coaching, Summit and Master Series prices are not published, and no program built specifically for solo operators was found.

Company-size claims conflict: Ramsey's media kit says over 1,100 team members and over $200 million in annual revenue; newer Ramsey pages say "$300 million"; and an April 15, 2025 press release (cited from the GlobeNewswire homepage; the article URL was not retained) says a "$250 million-a-year company with over 1,000 employees" ([Media kit](https://ramseysolutions.com/company/newsroom/media-kit); [Ramsey EntreLeadership](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/business/entreleadership); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/)). All are self-reported. A 2015 Bisnow article confirms about $100 million in revenue in 2014 and that Ramsey paid cash for its campus ([Bisnow](https://www.bisnow.com/news/national/other/whats-next-for-dave-ramsey-50045)). Ramsey's revenue comes largely from media, books, and personal-finance products, so it is not clean proof that the book's methods work for a service LLC.
