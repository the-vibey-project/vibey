## Open questions and unverified items

The research could not confirm the following; each is excluded from the findings above or labeled where used.

**Debt consolidation and bankruptcy**
- Whether the South Carolina DMP fee regulation excerpt ($60/$50/$10 per creditor/$70) is the current version of Regulation 28-700, or whether SCDCA has approved lower fees.
- The July 1, 2026 South Carolina exemption amounts. The statute requires adjustment in even-numbered years, and only the July 2024 figures were found; a figure around $80,125 for the homestead on a non-official site is unverified.
- Whether the SC Bankruptcy Court's Chapter 7 fee is $335 (US Courts page) or $338 (SC law-firm sites). A possible $3 administrative-fee difference is conjecture.
- Whether South Carolina's garnishment bar (section 37-5-104) covers medical bills, judgments on non-consumer-credit claims, taxes, child support, or federal student loans; bank-account garnishment and judgment-lien rules were not researched.
- Which accrual date starts the three-year SC limitations clock for a credit card debt (default versus last payment), and whether a DMP payment revives a stale debt; no case was found.
- DMP completion rates, credit-score recovery times after an inquiry, DMP, settlement or bankruptcy, and typical score drops from FICO or the bureaus; only non-FICO estimates (NFCC, NCLC) exist.
- Whether a pre-filing consolidation loan or paying selected creditors raises preference or fraud issues; how bankruptcy treats 401(k) loans.
- Direct Consolidation Loan mechanics, RAP formulas and 2026 default-collection status (studentaid.gov would not load); the loss of federal protections when refinancing to private loans.
- Whether Congress extended the qualified principal residence indebtedness exclusion (H.R. 917 status unreadable); the $600 Form 1099-C threshold (secondary source only); SC treatment of canceled debt.
- Equifax and TransUnion Chapter 13 retention periods; second sources for NerdWallet's balance-transfer list; credit-limit and purchase-interest rules on transfers; current policy-loan and margin rates; 2025 to 2026 updates to payday or title lending data.
- The broader macro picture (inflation, unemployment, forecasts); bankruptcy and foreclosure counts for 2026. The Bankrate personal loan page's title says June 2026 despite October 7 data.
- No academic study gives a verified share of consolidators who re-accumulate debt; no balance-transfer outcome study was found.

**Ramsey and the Baby Steps**
- Dave's on-air statements on bankruptcy, settlement and counseling (no transcripts searched beyond two); primary text of those Ramsey pages was available only as summaries or snippets.
- Whether Step 3b is an official step, and Ramsey+'s price.
- The exact split of the "10,000" millionaire respondents between random and Ramsey-audience samples.
- Independent comparisons of Baby Steps against alternatives from Kitces-style sources beyond those cited; Ramsey's direct replies to named critics.
- Amos: why trial was cancelled and when it will be rescheduled. O'Connor: whether a settlement occurred (dismissal by agreement is verified; settlement is inference). Patrick: why the extension was sought. A 2022 settlement with a former employee is reported by one secondary source; no EEOC action was found, which is not proof none exists.
- Whether the Yahoo-reported July 2026 and April 2026 on-air remarks match the show audio.

**EntreLeadership**
- Whether the numbers $200 million, $250 million and $300 million for company revenue can be reconciled; headcount and the media kit's date.
- Prices for the Summit, Master Series, coaching and Elite digital membership; the "six drivers" fully described beyond the lists above; the "Ramsey Solutions Leadership Model."
- Independent evidence that EntreLeadership methods improve small-business outcomes (none found beyond reviews and company claims); head-to-head studies of debt-free versus leveraged small firms; Fed nonemployer chart values (images only).
- Seven-year survival percentages from the Census working paper; Goodreads and Amazon rating counts; per-chapter themes for Chapters 3, 5, 6, 8 and 10 to 15 beyond title level.
- Whether DEW treats forming a no-income LLC as reportable self-employment, and the current maximum weekly benefit.

**What does not exist (do not rely on it):** an official "Business Baby Steps" list, an "EntreLeadership Pyramid," a "Six Steps" model, a three-word "Excellence, Integrity, Heart" values list, and a separate "2,000-millionaire" Hogan study. A Ramsey quote about not caring about your credit score was not found in a primary source.
