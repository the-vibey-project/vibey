## Glossary

**APR:** annual percentage rate, including fees on many loans. **Avalanche:** paying the highest-rate debt first. **Balance transfer:** moving card debt to a new card, usually with a 0% intro rate and a fee. **Chapter 7:** liquidation bankruptcy that discharges eligible debts. **Chapter 13:** three-to-five-year court-supervised repayment plan. **Deemed distribution:** a 401(k) loan treated as taxable income because it was unpaid. **DMP:** debt management plan, a nonprofit-run repayment plan with reduced rates. **DEW:** South Carolina Department of Employment and Workforce, which administers unemployment benefits. **Discharge:** court order ending personal liability for eligible debts. **Exempt property or income:** assets and income creditors and trustees cannot reach. **Four Walls:** Ramsey's term for food, utilities, shelter, transportation. **HELOC:** home equity line of credit. **Insolvency:** liabilities exceeding the fair market value of assets, relevant to the tax exclusion. **KRA:** Key Results Area, a written definition of a role's results in Ramsey's hiring system. **Means test:** Chapter 7 screen comparing current monthly income with the state median and, above it, disposable income. **QPRI:** qualified principal residence indebtedness. **RAP:** Repayment Assistance Plan for federal student loans, launched July 1, 2026. **Regulation F:** the CFPB's debt-collection rule. **Snowball:** paying smallest balance first regardless of rate. **SmartVestor / RamseyTrusted:** Ramsey's paid referral programs for advisors and local providers. **Statute of limitations:** the time within which a creditor may sue; three years for contract debt in South Carolina. **Utilization:** card balances as a share of limits.
