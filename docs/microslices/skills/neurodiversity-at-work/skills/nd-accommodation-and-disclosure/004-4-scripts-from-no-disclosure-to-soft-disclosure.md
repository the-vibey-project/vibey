## 4. Scripts: from no disclosure to soft disclosure

Ordered from least to most disclosure. Square brackets are fill-in points. `[chat tool]` is your workplace messaging platform.

**4.1 Preference only, no disclosure.** (More in `nd-workplace-communication-scripts` §3.)
> "I retain details more accurately when they are in writing. Could you send the requirements by email or in a [chat tool] message so I can refer back to them while I work?"

**4.2 Soft disclosure, no diagnosis stated (preferred opening).** Modelled on JAN's Dos and Don'ts of Disclosure.
> "I need an adjustment at work because of a medical condition. Specifically, I work most effectively with written instructions, advance agendas, and the ability to use uninterrupted focus blocks. Could we set up a short conversation to discuss?"

**4.3 Disclosure naming the condition (only when you choose to).**
> "I am [autistic / have ADHD / condition]. I am sharing this because I want to request a small set of accommodations that will let me do my best work and reduce the risk of burnout. None of them are expensive or unusual. Specifically, I would like to formalise: [written instructions for assignments]; [advance agendas for meetings]; [flexible scheduling around deep-work blocks]; [the option to keep my camera off during working sessions]; and [one-on-one communication for complex topics]. I am happy to go through the interactive process to refine these."

Tips:
- Lead with the functional need; the label is optional context.
- Keep the list short and concrete; you can add more later.
- If you have more than one condition, you only need to raise what is relevant to the accommodations you are asking for, framed as present functional needs (⚠️ general practice, single-source).
