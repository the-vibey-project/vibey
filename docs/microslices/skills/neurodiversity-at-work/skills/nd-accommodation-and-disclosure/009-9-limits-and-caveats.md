## 9. Limits and caveats

- **Not legal advice.** US federal coverage generally starts at 15 employees and has exceptions; state law can add more. Confirm coverage with HR or counsel before relying on it, especially if any adverse action is in view.
- **Disclosure is personal.** Documented risks (§1.3) are real; the decision and its timing are yours.
- **Evidence limits.** The accommodation evidence base is small and mostly autism-focused (§1.1); the cost data is employer self-report (§1.2); the risk data is qualitative (§1.3).
- **Jurisdiction.** Everything legal here is US-specific (§3.5).
- **Templates are starting points.** Edit until they describe your actual needs in your own words.
