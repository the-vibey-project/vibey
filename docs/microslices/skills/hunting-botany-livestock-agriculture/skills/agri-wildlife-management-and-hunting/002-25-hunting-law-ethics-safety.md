## §25. ⚠️ Hunting: Law, Ethics, Safety

**⚠️ LAW FIRST, and it is genuinely non-negotiable:**
```
⚠️ Regulations are STATE-specific and change ANNUALLY. Your state wildlife
   agency's current published regulations are the authority — not a book,
   not a forum, not this document
⚠️ Licences, tags, permits, hunter education requirement
⚠️ Seasons, legal hours (⚠️ usually tied to sunrise/sunset offsets), bag limits
⚠️ Legal methods and equipment by season (archery/muzzleloader/firearm)
⚠️ Baiting rules — ⚠️ vary enormously and are increasingly restricted for
   disease reasons (§28.2)
⚠️ Written landowner permission; trespass law
⚠️ Tagging and reporting requirements — often immediate, before moving
⚠️ Transport rules, including CARCASS MOVEMENT restrictions (§28.2)
```
**⚠️ FIREARM SAFETY — the four rules, and they are the standard because they are
redundant. Any one of them, followed, prevents the incident:**
```
1. ⚠️ Treat every firearm as loaded
2. ⚠️ Never point the muzzle at anything you are not willing to destroy
3. ⚠️ Keep your finger off the trigger until your sights are on the target
   and you have decided to shoot
4. ⚠️ BE CERTAIN OF YOUR TARGET AND WHAT IS BEYOND IT
```
**⚠️ Rule 4 is the one that kills people in the field.** ⚠️ **Positive target
identification — the whole animal, identified, not a shape or a sound or a movement — is
mandatory, and "I was sure" is what every such incident report says.** **Know where your
hunting partners are. Wear the required blaze orange.**
**⚠️ Tree stand falls cause more hunting injuries than firearms in many jurisdictions**,
**and the mitigation is simple and widely ignored: ⚠️ a full-body harness worn from the
ground up, a lifeline, three points of contact, and inspecting stands and straps every
season.**
**⚠️ Other field safety**: **tell someone your plan and expected return; carry
communication and know your coverage; hypothermia is the most common serious outdoor
medical problem; and ⚠️ dragging a heavy animal is a genuine cardiac risk in unfit
hunters.**

**⚠️ Fair chase and ethics** — **the tradition's own standard: no unfair technological or
positional advantage, respect for the animal, ⚠️ take only shots you are confident of
(§26), make every reasonable effort to recover, use the meat, and respect landowners and
other hunters.** ⚠️ **Legal and ethical are not the same set, and the ethical standard is
generally the stricter one.**
