## §19. Reproduction

**Estrous cycles (⚠️ cattle ~21 days, sheep ~17, pigs ~21), seasonal vs continuous
breeders (⚠️ sheep and goats are short-day seasonal), gestation (⚠️ cattle ~283 d,
sheep/goats ~150 d, pigs ~114 d, horses ~340 d).**
**Tools**: **AI, estrus synchronization, pregnancy diagnosis (palpation, ultrasound,
blood test), embryo transfer, sexed semen.**
**⚠️ Reproductive efficiency is usually the largest economic lever in a cow-calf
operation** — **a cow that doesn't wean a calf costs a full year of maintenance for
nothing.** ⚠️ **And nutrition drives reproduction: thin females don't cycle, and the
interval from calving to first estrus lengthens sharply with poor body condition** (§18).
**⚠️ Dystocia risk is driven substantially by sire selection (birth weight EPDs) and by
heifer size at breeding** — **both manageable in advance.**
