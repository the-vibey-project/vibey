## §8. Plant Pathology

**⚠️ The disease triangle: susceptible host + virulent pathogen + favourable environment.**
⚠️ **Remove any one and there is no disease** — **and environment is usually the one you
can most cheaply influence** (spacing, airflow, irrigation timing, avoiding wet foliage).
```
FUNGAL     ⚠️ the majority. Rusts, smuts, mildews, blights, wilts
BACTERIAL  ⚠️ fire blight, bacterial spot; hard to treat, manage culturally
VIRAL      ⚠️ usually vector-borne (aphids, thrips); no cure — remove plants
NEMATODES · ABIOTIC (⚠️ nutrient, herbicide drift, cold, compaction — and these
   are routinely misdiagnosed as disease, and treated with fungicide uselessly)
```
**⚠️ Monoculture and clonal uniformity amplify epidemics** — **the Irish potato famine
(one clone, *Phytophthora infestans*), southern corn leaf blight in 1970 (a shared
cytoplasmic male-sterility source), and the banana industry's repeated Panama disease
crises are all the same structural lesson.** ⚠️ **Genetic uniformity is an efficiency gain
purchased with correlated risk.**
