## §1. Plant Structure

```
ROOTS    ⚠️ anchorage, water and nutrient uptake, storage. Root HAIRS do
         most of the absorbing; ⚠️ mycorrhizal fungi extend reach enormously (§10)
STEMS    support and transport. ⚠️ XYLEM carries water UP (dead cells, driven
         by transpiration); PHLOEM carries sugars BOTH WAYS (living, pressure-driven)
LEAVES   ⚠️ the photosynthetic organ. STOMATA regulate the fundamental
         tradeoff: open to admit CO₂, and lose water (§3)
FLOWERS/FRUIT/SEED   reproduction (§5)
MERISTEMS ⚠️ where growth happens. Apical (tips) vs lateral (girth) vs
         intercalary (⚠️ at the BASE in grasses — which is exactly why grass
         survives grazing and mowing and most other plants don't). §22
```
**⚠️ Apical dominance** — **the growing tip suppresses lateral buds via auxin** — **is why
pruning or browsing a terminal bud produces bushier growth.** **This single mechanism
underlies pruning, pinching, hedging and much browse response in wildlife habitat.**
