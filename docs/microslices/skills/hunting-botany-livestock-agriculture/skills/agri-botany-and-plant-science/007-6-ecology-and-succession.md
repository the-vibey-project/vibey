## §6. Ecology and Succession

**⚠️ Succession is the organizing concept for both habitat management and weed pressure.**
**Primary (bare rock/sand) vs secondary (after disturbance).** ⚠️ **Early successional
stages are dominated by fast-growing, high-seed, short-lived species — which is exactly
what "weeds" are, and why disturbance creates weed pressure by definition** (§9).
**⚠️ For wildlife this inverts the intuition**: ⚠️ **early successional habitat is what most
game species need** — **deer browse, quail and turkey brood cover, rabbit** — **and mature
closed-canopy forest is comparatively poor habitat for them.** **Which is why habitat
management involves deliberate disturbance: prescribed fire, timber harvest, disking** (§24 → `agri-wildlife-management-and-hunting`).
**Edge effect** (⚠️ **more species at boundaries, and it favours generalists and
predators**), **carrying capacity, and competition.**
