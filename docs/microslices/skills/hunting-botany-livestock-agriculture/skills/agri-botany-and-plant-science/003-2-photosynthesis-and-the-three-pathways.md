## §2. Photosynthesis and the Three Pathways

```
⚠️ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂   (the summary, not the mechanism)
Light reactions (thylakoid) → ATP + NADPH + O₂
Calvin cycle (stroma) → fixes CO₂ using RuBisCO
```
**⚠️ RuBisCO is the most abundant protein on Earth and it is bad at its job** — **it also
binds oxygen, causing photorespiration, which wastes energy.** ⚠️ **Photorespiration gets
worse as temperature rises and as stomata close in heat.** **Two workarounds evolved:**
```
C3   ⚠️ most plants — wheat, rice, soybeans, most trees, cool-season grasses.
     Efficient in cool, moist, moderate light. Suffers in heat
C4   ⚠️ maize, sorghum, sugarcane, warm-season grasses (bermudagrass,
     switchgrass). Concentrates CO₂ around RuBisCO. ⚠️ Better in HEAT and
     HIGH LIGHT and more water-efficient; costs energy, so worse when cool
CAM  ⚠️ cacti, agave, pineapple. Stomata open at NIGHT, storing CO₂ as acid.
     Extreme water efficiency, slow growth
```
**⚠️ Practical consequence that matters for pasture and food plots**: **cool-season (C3)
and warm-season (C4) forages peak at different times of year.** ⚠️ **A pasture of only one
type has a production gap** — **the "summer slump" in cool-season systems is a C3
physiology fact, not a management failure** — **and mixed swards exist to cover it** (§22 → `agri-livestock-nutrition-health-and-welfare`).
