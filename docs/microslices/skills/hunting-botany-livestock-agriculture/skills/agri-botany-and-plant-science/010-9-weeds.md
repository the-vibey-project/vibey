## §9. Weeds

**⚠️ A weed is a plant where you don't want it — an economic category, not a botanical
one.** **Annual vs perennial matters enormously for control: ⚠️ perennials with rhizomes
regenerate from fragments, so tillage propagates them.**
**Control**: **cultural (rotation, competition, cover crops, timing), mechanical, chemical,
biological.**
**⚠️ Herbicide resistance is evolution operating on a very short timescale, and it is now a
first-order agronomic problem.** ⚠️ **Repeated use of a single mode of action selects
resistant biotypes** — **glyphosate-resistant Palmer amaranth and waterhemp being the
best-known** — **and the mitigation is rotating and mixing MODES OF ACTION (herbicide
group numbers), not brand names.** **Many products with different names share a mode of
action.**
