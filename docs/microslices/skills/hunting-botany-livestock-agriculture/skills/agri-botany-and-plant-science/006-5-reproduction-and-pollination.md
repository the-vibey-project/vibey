## §5. Reproduction and Pollination

**Alternation of generations; ⚠️ complete vs incomplete flowers; monoecious vs dioecious
(⚠️ dioecious species need both sexes present — the classic reason a holly or kiwi never
fruits).**
**Pollination**: **wind (grasses, conifers, corn — copious light pollen), insect, bird,
bat, self.** ⚠️ **Self-incompatibility systems exist in many species, which is why some
fruit trees need a compatible pollinizer variety and not just another tree.**
**Seed dormancy and its breaking**: ⚠️ **stratification (cold period), scarification
(abrasion of hard coat), light requirement, and chemical inhibitors.** **Dormancy is an
adaptation, and "the seed didn't germinate" is often "you skipped a required signal."**
**Vegetative propagation** — **cuttings, layering, grafting, tubers** — ⚠️ **produces
clones, which means uniformity and shared vulnerability** (§8).
