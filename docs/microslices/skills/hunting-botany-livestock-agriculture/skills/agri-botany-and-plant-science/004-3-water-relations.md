## §3. Water Relations

**⚠️ Water moves down a potential gradient, and the numbers are extreme**: **soil (~−0.03
MPa at field capacity) → root → xylem → leaf → atmosphere (⚠️ often −50 to −100 MPa).**
**The cohesion-tension mechanism pulls a continuous water column up the plant under
tension.**
**⚠️ The central tradeoff**: **stomata must open for CO₂ and that loses water.**
**Transpiration ratio — hundreds of grams of water per gram of dry matter — means ⚠️ the
overwhelming majority of water a crop uses is spent buying CO₂, not building tissue.**
**⚠️ Wilting point vs field capacity**: **plant-available water is the difference between
them, and it's a soil property (§10 → `agri-soil-fertility-and-crop-production`), which is why sandy soils need more frequent
irrigation regardless of total rainfall.**
**⚠️ Water stress reduces yield most at critical growth stages** — **flowering and grain
fill for most crops** — **so irrigation timing beats irrigation volume.**
