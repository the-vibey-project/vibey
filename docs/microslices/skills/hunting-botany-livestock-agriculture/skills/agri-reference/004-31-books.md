## §31. Books

| Author | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Brady & Weil** | ***The Nature and Properties of Soils*** | ⚠️ **THE soil textbook. §10 → `agri-soil-fertility-and-crop-production`** |
| **Taiz & Zeiger** | *Plant Physiology and Development* | §1–§5 → `agri-botany-and-plant-science` |
| **Magdoff & van Es** | ***Building Soils for Better Crops*** | ⚠️ **Free from SARE. Practical** |
| **Ensminger / Cheeke** | *Feeds and Nutrition* / *Applied Animal Nutrition* | §18 → `agri-livestock-nutrition-health-and-welfare` |
| **Church** | *The Ruminant Animal* | ⚠️ **§17 → `agri-livestock-nutrition-health-and-welfare` in depth** |
| **Grandin** | *Humane Livestock Handling* | ⚠️ **§23 → `agri-livestock-nutrition-health-and-welfare`. Practical and humane** |
| **Smith** | ***Beef Cattle Production*** / breed-society guides | §21 → `agri-livestock-nutrition-health-and-welfare` |
| **Krausman & Cain** | *Wildlife Management and Conservation* | §24 → `agri-wildlife-management-and-hunting` |
| **Leopold** | ***A Sand County Almanac*** / *Game Management* | ⚠️ **The ethical and technical foundation of the field** |

**⚠️ The most useful resources are free and local**: **your state Cooperative Extension
service (⚠️ land-grant university, regionally specific, and genuinely the best first stop
for anything agricultural); your state wildlife agency's regulations and CWD guidance;
NRCS soil surveys and Web Soil Survey; and ⚠️ a REGIONAL field guide with dichotomous
keys for plant ID** (§7 → `agri-botany-and-plant-science`).
