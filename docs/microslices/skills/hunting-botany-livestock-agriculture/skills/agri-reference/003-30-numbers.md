## §30. Numbers

```
SOIL     ⚠️ ideal ~45% mineral / 5% OM / 25% water / 25% air
         ⚠️ optimal pH for most nutrient availability ~6.0–7.0
PLANTS   ⚠️ transpiration ratio: hundreds of g water per g dry matter
         C3 vs C4 — cool-season vs warm-season peak production (§2)
GESTATION ⚠️ cattle ~283 d · sheep/goat ~150 d · pig ~114 d · horse ~340 d
ESTROUS  ⚠️ cattle ~21 d · sheep ~17 d · pig ~21 d
GRAZING  ⚠️ "take half, leave half"; ⚠️ rest period is the key variable
QUARANTINE  ⚠️ 21–30 days for incoming livestock
MEAT     ⚠️ cool carcass below 40°F/4°C fast
         ⚠️ venison whole muscle 145°F/63°C + rest · ground 160°F/71°C
         ⚠️ wild birds 165°F/74°C · bear and wild pig: thoroughly (trichinella)
CWD      ⚠️ incubation ≥16 months; asymptomatic 12–24 months
         ⚠️ detected in 33–37 states depending on counting method (§28.2)
H5N1     ⚠️ dairy cattle confirmed in 19 states; clade 2.3.4.4b (§28.1)
         ⚠️ 36% of retail milk samples had viral RNA (Apr–May 2024) — not
         infectious virus
```
